## Hazie onwe ya na `setup-*`

Ịkwesighi iji aka dee nhazi ngwa ọrụ ọ bụla. OmniRoute na-ebupu iwu `setup-*` kwa CLI kwadoro nke na-agụ ya** ibi ndụ** katalọgụ ụdị sitere na OmniRoute na-agba ọsọ (mpaghara ma ọ bụ nke dịpụrụ adịpụ) wee dee nhazi nke ngwaọrụ na igwe gị:

```bash
omniroute setup-codex        omniroute setup-claude       omniroute setup-opencode
omniroute setup-cline        omniroute setup-kilo         omniroute setup-continue
omniroute setup-cursor       omniroute setup-roo          omniroute setup-crush
omniroute setup-goose        omniroute setup-qwen         omniroute setup-aider
omniroute setup-5dive        omniroute setup-zed
omniroute setup-whycodes
```

Onye ọ bụla na-anabata `--remote <url> --api-key <key>` (hazie ngwá ọrụ mpaghara megide OmniRoute dịpụrụ adịpụ), `--dry-run` (nhụchalụ na-edeghị), na `--port`. Ngwá ọrụ na-enweghị ihe ngosi akpaaka-nchọpụta (Cline, Kilo, Roo, Goose, Aider, Qwen, 5dive, WhyCodes) were `--model <id>` (na `--yes` maka ọsọ na-adịghị emekọrịta ihe). `setup-5dive` bụ otu uzommeputa nke na-adịghị ede n'okpuru `$HOME`: ọ na-ahazi ụgbọ mmiri 5dive agents site na ịde profaịlụ auth nwere mgbọrọgwụ na ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri, ya mere ọ na-emegharị site na `sudo` na enweghị ọnọdụ dịpụrụ adịpụ nke ya. Iji malite CLI nwere env ziri ezi na enweghị nhazi ederede ma ọlị, jiri ihe mmalite `omniroute run <target>` (claude, codex, aider, goose, opencode, qwen, gemini, whycodes — targets and aliases comes from `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs`); ihe nketa kwa ngwa-ebupute `omniroute launch` ( koodu Claude ) na `omniroute launch-codex` (Codex) ka dị. Gemini CLI bụ mmalite-naanị: ọ bụ ebumnuche `omniroute run` mana enweghị uzommeputa `setup-*`/`configure`.

> **Ntụaka n'uju:** Tebụl nna ukwu - ihe iwu ọ bụla na-ede, ọkọlọtọ ọ bụla, mpaghara vs remote, na ngwaọrụ ndị chọrọ suffix `/v1` - bi na ya.** [CLI njikọta](../guides/CLI-INTEGRATIONS.md)** .

### Na-agba ọsọ ndị a n'ime akpa

Iwu `setup-*` e gburu n'ime akpa OmniRoute na-ede n'ime ụlọ nke akpa ahụ, nke onye CLI ọ bụla na-agụghị na nke na-apụ n'anya na akpa ahụ. OmniRoute na-achọpụta nke ahụ wee pụọ `2` na ntuziaka kama ide. Ụzọ abụọ akwadoro n'ihu - wụnye CLI na onye ọbịa na `omniroute connect` n'ime akpa ahụ, ma ọ bụ kechie config dirs wee tọọ `CLI_CONFIG_HOME` (dee profaịlụ `host` ). Iwu `setup-*` ọ bụla, gbakwunyere `omniroute configure` na `omniroute config set`, na-anabata `--allow-container-write` mgbe ị na-ahazi CLIs nke akpa bụ ihe ị pụtara n'ezie; `OMNIROUTE_ALLOW_CONTAINER_CONFIG_WRITE=true` na-eme otu ihe ahụ maka ihe nkesa. Hụ [Docker Nduzi → Ịhazi ngwaọrụ CLI onye ọbịa](../guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#configuring-host-cli-tools-when-omniroute-runs-in-docker).

The dashboard** tinye njedebe** (`POST /api/cli-tools/apply`) na-amanye otu nchekwa ahụ: n'ime akpa, dee ihe ebumnobi ya anaghị ejikọta ya na onye ọbịa ahụ zara.**`422`** na `containerEphemeralTarget: true`, ederede njehie nchekwa na - maka ngwaọrụ nwere uzommeputa onye ọbịa (claude, codex, opencode, cline, kilo, continue) - a `hostSetupCommand` (eg `omniroute setup-opencode`) iji na-agba ọsọ na onye ọbịa kama; ọ dịghị ihe e dere. `dryRun: true` na-aga n'ihu na-arụ ọrụ n'ụdị akpa wee weghachi ụzọ ebumnuche + nhụchalụ na-emetụghị diski. Ọdịnaya nhụchalụ abụghị nhazi nzere iji detuo ma ọ bụ bubata. Tinye na ntinye tool/base URL/API key/model izizi na onye nnabata, ma ọ bụ jiri iwu ntọlite n'akụkụ ndị ọbịa egosiri. Hụ [nchekwa nhazi CLI](../security/CLI-CONFIGURATION.md) maka isi ihe nlegharị anya wee rịọ nkwekọrịta. Omume a bụ ebumnobi na nlọghachi azụ nke `tests/unit/api/cli-tools/apply-container-guard.test.ts` na-echekwa - emela "dozie" 422 site na iwepu onye nche.

## Isi Eziokwu

Katalọgụ ejikọtara ọnụ dị na `src/shared/constants/cliTools.ts` dịka `CLI_TOOLS: Record<string, CliCatalogEntry>`.

Ndenye ọ bụla nwere mpaghara ndị a (akọwapụtara na `src/shared/schemas/cliCatalog.ts`):

| Mpaghara                                        | Ụdị                                                          | Nkọwa                                                          |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `category`                                      | `"code" \| "agent"`                                          | Peeji nke ngwa ahụ na-apụta na ya                              |
| `vendor`                                        | `string`                                                     | Ebe ngwa ahụ si bịa ("Anthropic", "OSS (P. Gauthier)")         |
| `acpSpawnable`                                  | `boolean`                                                    | A pụkwara iji ya dịka ACP Agent (a na-egosi baajị)             |
| `baseUrlSupport`                                | `"full" \| "partial" \| "none"`                              | Ọkwa nkwado endpoint ahaziri iche. `"none"` = ndepụta ọrụ MITM |
| `configType`                                    | `"env" \| "custom" \| "guide" \| "custom-builder" \| "mitm"` | Usoro nhazi                                                    |
| `id`, `name`, `color`, `description`, `docsUrl` | ọkọlọtọ                                                      | Mpaghara ndị bụ isi eji egosi ihe                              |

A **naghị egosi** ndenye ndị nwere `baseUrlSupport: "none"` na peeji dashboard — edebanyere ha na ndepụta ọrụ MITM maka atụmatụ 11 (lee `_tasks/features-v3.8.6/refactorpages/_orchestration/_plan11-mitm-backlog.md`).

### Ọkwa ike (edepụtara na katalọgụ × achọpụtakwara × ahazikwara × enwere ike ịmalite)

Ọ bụghị ngwa niile edepụtara na katalọgụ ka enwere ike ịchọpụta, ịhazi, ma ọ bụ ịmalite. Ọkwa ọ bụla nwere otu
isi mmalite na-ekwupụta ya, ule ndapụkwa na-eme ka ha nọgide kwekọọ:

| Ọkwa               | Ihe ọ pụtara                                                        | Ebe e kwupụtara ya                                                 |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Dị na katalọgụ** | Na-apụta na katalọgụ dashboard (aha, vendor, docs, ụdị nhazi)       | `src/shared/constants/cliTools.ts` (`CLI_TOOLS`)                   |
| **Achọpụtakwara**  | Nchọpụta binary/config, nyocha ahụike, ụzọ config                   | `src/shared/services/cliRuntime.ts` (katalọgụ runtime `CLI_TOOLS`) |
| **Ahazikwara**     | `omniroute configure <cli>` na-akwado ya (usoro ntọlite dị)         | `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` (`configure: true`)                     |
| **A pụrụ ịmalite** | `omniroute run <target>` na-akwado ya (akọwapụtara ntinye env/args) | `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` (`run: true`)                           |

`bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` bụ manifest arụmọrụ bụ isi maka mpaghara iwu CLI:
`run`, `configure` na ndị na-emepụta shell-completion niile na-enweta ndepụta
target ha, mkpebi alias (dịka ọmụmaatụ `kilocode`/`kilo-code`/`kilo_cli` → `kilo`)
na njikọ flag `--model` site na ya. Ihe nche ndapụ
`tests/unit/cli/cli-manifest-drift.test.ts` na-akwado na manifest ahụ, katalọgụ runtime,
katalọgụ UI na mpaghara ọ bụla na-eji ha nọgide na-emekọrịta — target agbakwunyere na
otu mpaghara na-enweghị ndị ọzọ na-eme ka test suite daa kama ikwe ka ndapụ mee nwayọ n’amaghị ama.

## 5. API Nchọpụta N'ìgwè

A na-achịkọta nchọpụta ngwaọrụ niile site n'otu endpoint:

**`GET /api/cli-tools/all-statuses`**

- Nnyocha njirimara: `requireCliToolsAuth(request)` (otu ihe ahụ dị ka route `/api/cli-tools/` ndị ọzọ)
- Na-eweghachi: `Record<toolId, ToolBatchStatus>` (ụdị: `src/shared/types/cliBatchStatus.ts`)
- Atụmatụ: `Promise.all` n'elu ngwaọrụ niile, timeout nke 5s maka ngwaọrụ ọ bụla
- Cache: LRU dị na memory nke config file `mtime` na-edepụta. A na-eme ka cache ghara ịdị irè mgbe mtime gbanwere. A na-emegharị ya mgbe server maliteghachiri.

Ọdịdị response maka ngwaọrụ ọ bụla:

```ts
interface ToolBatchStatus {
  detection: {
    installed: boolean;
    runnable: boolean;
    version?: string;
    command?: string;
    commandPath?: string;
    reason?: string;
  };
  config: {
    status: "configured" | "not_configured" | "not_installed" | "unknown" | "other";
    endpoint?: string | null;
    lastConfiguredAt?: string | null;
  };
  error?: string; // emeziri ka ọ dị nchebe, enweghị stack trace
}
```


`providers add/import/auth/edit/remove` na-ebute API ụzọ, ya mere ha na-arụ ọrụ megide
ọnọdụ mpaghara ma ọ bụ nke dị anya na-arụ ọrụ ugbu a. Ntinye nzere kwesịrị iji
`--credential-stdin` ma ọ bụ `--credential-env`; `--dry-run --json` na-akọ naanị
ọnụnọ/usoro e zoro ụfọdụ akụkụ ya. `providers available` na-agụ katalọgụ OmniRoute;
`providers list/test/test-all/validate` na-ejigide omume SQLite mpaghara ha ma
ha achọghị ka sava na-arụ ọrụ.

Maka node ahaziri iche kwekọrọ na OpenAI ma ọ bụ Anthropic, jikọta nzere na
ID node nke `omniroute nodes add` weghachiri, site n'iji `omniroute keys add "$NODE_ID" --stdin`.
Nke a chọrọ sava na-arụ ọrụ na nkwenye njikwa maka ọnọdụ na-arụ ọrụ ugbu a.
CLI na-eji `POST /api/providers`, nke na-enyocha node ahụ ma detuo ntọala endpoint ya
n'ime njikọ ahụ. Node na-adịghị, ọdịda ikike, ma ọ bụ sava na-adịghị
adị na-eweghachi njehie na-enweghị ịmepụta nzere mpaghara ọzọ.

`nodes add --base-url` na-ahazi endpoint node ahụ; ọ dị iche na adreesị sava
dị na `OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL`. Maka faịlụ OpenAPI, jiri
`omniroute openapi dump --format json --out ./openapi.json`; `--output` zuru ụwa ọnụ
na-ahọrọ usoro ngosipụta CLI, ọ bụghị aha faịlụ ebe a ga-edebe ya.

`auth export` bụ **naanị-mpụga-netwọkụ** (ọ na-agụ SQLite ozugbo, enweghị HTTP route) ma a kpachapụrụ anya mee ka ọ bipụta/dee
ụkpụrụ **ederede nkịtị** `apiKey`/`accessToken`/`refreshToken`/`idToken` — nke ahụ bụ atụmatụ ahụ, ọ bụghị
ntụpọ. Ọ dịghị ihe a na-agụ site na ọdụ data, ọ dịghịkwa ihe a na-ewepụ izo ya ezo, na-enweghị `--force`. Ọkọlọtọ
ịdọ aka ná ntị na-apụta mgbe niile na stderr tupu e wepụta ederede nkịtị ọ bụla. Ọ chọrọ ka edobe `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`.
A na-akọ ubi nke e nweghị ike iwepụ izo ya ezo (igodo ochie, ciphertext mebiri emebi) dị ka
`<field>DecryptFailed: true` kama ịkwụsị mbupụ ahụ dum ma ọ bụ ikpughe njehie dị n'okpuru ya.

### Iwu nta ndị ọzọ

Ndị a na-ewere na sava OmniRoute na-arụ ọrụ, belụsọ ebe e kwuru ihe ọzọ:

```bash
omniroute status                       # Ọnọdụ oge-ngagharị zuru ezu
omniroute logs                         # Zipụta ndekọ arịrịọ n'aga n'ihu (--json, --search, --follow)
omniroute config list                  # Gosipụta ngwa CLI ahaziri

omniroute provider list                # Depụta ndị na-eweta ọrụ dị (aha ọzọ nke providers list)
omniroute provider add                 # Debanye OmniRoute dị ka onye na-eweta ọrụ na ngwa
omniroute keys add | list | remove     # Jikwaa igodo API
omniroute models [provider]            # Depụta ụdịdị (--json, --search)
omniroute combo list | switch | create | delete

omniroute backup                       # Mepụta snapshot nke nhazi + DB
omniroute restore                      # Weghachite site na snapshot gara aga

omniroute health                       # Nkọwa ahụike zuru ezu (breakers, cache, ebe nchekwa)
omniroute quota                        # Ojiji oke nke onye na-eweta ọrụ
omniroute cache                        # Ọnọdụ cache

omniroute mcp status | restart         # Ọnọdụ sava MCP / ịmalitegharị
omniroute a2a status | card            # Ọnọdụ sava A2A / kaadị agent

omniroute tunnel list | create | stop  # Jikwaa tunnel (cloudflare/tailscale/ngrok)
omniroute env show | get <k> | set <k> <v>  # Nyochaa / tọọ env vars (nwa oge)

omniroute test                         # Nnwale ngwa ngwa nke njikọ onye na-eweta ọrụ
```

## Jiri OmniRoute CLI jikwaa amụma inference-key

Jiri ọnọdụ njikwa ekwenyere njirimara (lee [Ọnọdụ Ime Ebe Dị Anya](../guides/REMOTE-MODE.md))
iji ID ya nyochaa ma ọ bụ melite igodo inference dị adị:

```bash
omniroute --output json keys policy show KEY_ID
omniroute keys policy set KEY_ID --unrestricted
omniroute --output json keys policy show KEY_ID
```

`--unrestricted` bụ aha ọzọ maka `--model-access-mode all`. Mmelite ahụ na-ehichapụ
ndepụta ụdị ndị enyere ohere. Ọ na-echekwa scopes, iwu njikọ, ụdị ndị egbochiri, oke ọnụego
na oke ụgwọ, yabụ njikwa ndị ọzọ ahụ ka nwere ike ịjụ arịrịọ. Itinye
`--allowed-models ""` kpọmkwem na-ahọrọkwa `all`; jikọta ya na
`--model-access-mode restricted` iji debe igodo amachibidoro nke na-enweghị ụdị enyere ohere.
Ndepụta na-adịghị efu nke e ji rikoma kewaa na-ahọrọ `restricted` ma ọ bụrụ na akọwaghị ya kpọmkwem.
A ga-ajụ ngwakọta `all` na ndepụta ikike na-adịghị efu nke na-emegiderịta onwe ya tupu izipu arịrịọ.

`--rate-limit 30` na-edobe ọnụ ọgụgụ arịrịọ kwa nkeji ma na-echekwa iwu maka windo oge
ndị ọzọ. `--max-cost 5` na-eme ka oke USD kwa ụbọchị rụọ ọrụ n'agbanweghị oke kwa izu;
`--max-cost 0` na-ewepụ oke kwa ụbọchị ma na-echekwa mgbanyụ/mgbanye oke ojiji dị ugbu a
na oke kwa izu. Akụkụ amụma ndị a hapụrụ agaghị agbanwe. Mgbanwe ọnụego na-ebu ụzọ gụọ
amụma dị ugbu a; zere idezi n'otu oge na nhazi rate-limit nke otu igodo ahụ.

Iwu amụma na-eji `GET`/`PATCH /api/keys/{id}` e ji nkwenye njirimara chebe ma na-asọpụrụ
ọnọdụ na-arụ ọrụ na nhọrọ zuru ụwa ọnụ `--api-key`. Mmepụta JSON nwere naanị akụkụ amụma,
na-enweghị igodo echekwara ma ọ bụ ngosipụta ya e kpuchiri ekpuchi. Nzere e mere naanị maka
inference enweghị ike inye ikike maka mgbanwe njikwa. Nzaghachi 401/403 chọrọ ka enyocha nzere
njikwa na ọnọdụ; ọ naghị egosi na token CLI mpaghara ma ọ bụ salt dara ada.
Lee [Token CLI](../security/CLI_TOKEN.md) maka nkwekọrịta nkwenye njirimara mpaghara dị iche.
