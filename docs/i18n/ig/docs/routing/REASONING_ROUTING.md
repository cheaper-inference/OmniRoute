Iwu ntụgharị maka reasoning na-agbatị usoro ntụgharị model na combo dị ugbu a. Mgbe ọ nweghị iwu nọ n'ọrụ dabara,
omume thinking, suffix, connection-default, na provider-translation dị ugbu a ga-anọgide
n’agbanweghị.

## Nchịkwa

Nchịkwa iwu dị n'okpuru **Settings → Global Routing**. Ihe ndezi API-key na-enye otu
UI nchịkwa ahụ, nke e nyochara ka ọ gosi naanị key ahọrọ.

A na-ekpughe API nchịkwa site na routes ndị a:

- `GET` na `POST` na `/api/settings/reasoning-routing-rules`
- `GET`, `PATCH`, na `DELETE` na `/api/settings/reasoning-routing-rules/[id]`
- `POST` na `/api/settings/reasoning-routing-rules/simulate`

Routes niile na-eji `requireManagementAuth`. A na-enyocha inputs site na schemas ndị dị na
`src/shared/validation/schemas/reasoningRouting.ts`. Simulator anaghị akpọ upstream mgbe ọ bụla.

## Mkpebi Iwu

Ntụle mbụ na-ahọrọ kpọmkwem otu iwu. A na-enyocha scopes n'usoro a:

1. `apiKey`
2. `combo`
3. `model`
4. `global`

N'ime scope, `priority` dị elu na-emeri na mbụ, nke na-esochi ya bụ model dabara kpọmkwem kama glob pattern,
wee bụrụ nhazi kwụsiri ike nke `createdAt` na `id`. A na-agụ `requestTags` naanị site na `metadata.tags`
ma ha na-akwado ndakọrịta `any` ma ọ bụ `all`.

A na-enyocha iwu `connection` naanị mgbe ọ nweghị iwu mbụ meriri ma ahọrọlarị provider connection
doro anya. Ọ nwere ike ịgbanwe naanị effort na budget.

## Mgbalị na mmefu ego

`sourceEffort` na-anabata `any`, `missing`, `none`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max`, na `ultra`. `missing` pụtara na arịrịọ ahụ enweghị mbọ pụrụiche ma ọ bụ ntụgharị echiche ma ọ bụ mmefu ego iche echiche. Ya mere, mgbama naanị mmefu ego dabara na naanị `any`.

`effortMode` nwere ụdị atọ:

- `inherit` na-eme ka onye ahịa na-agba mbọ ka ọ na-ekwe ka ihe nlereanya ma ọ bụ ngwakọta gbanwee.
- `default` na-edobe `targetEffort` naanị mgbe enweghị mgbama echiche doro anya dị.
- `force` jiri `targetEffort` dochie mgbalị pụrụ iche.

N'onwe ya, `budgetAction` nwere ike ịbụ `preserve`, `remove`, ma ọ bụ `set`. `force` na `none` na-ewepụ mgbalị niile a ma ama na mpaghara mmefu ego, wee tọọ `reasoning_effort: "none"`, n'ihi na ụfọdụ ndị na-enye ọrụ na-atụgharị uche mgbe ubi ahụ na-anọghị. A na-edozi ndị na-enye ndị na-ajụ `none` nkịtị na ezipụ na `open-sse/executors/base/reasoningEffort.ts`. Usoro iwu maapụ ya na `low`, ewezuga na arịrịọ ụdị azịza (`input` na-enweghị `messages`), ebe ọ na-edobe `none`. Na Cloudflare Ndị ọrụ AI (`cloudflare-ai`), ụdị ndị na-ajụ `none` (gpt-oss, qwen3.8, nemotron-3, glm-4.7-flash) nweta `low`; Ndị ọrụ ndị ọzọ AI ụdị na-edebe `none`. Ịtụba ogige ahụ abụghị nhọrọ ebe ahụ, n'ihi na mbọ na-adịghị na-eme ọkwa ndabara nke ihe nlereanya ahụ. `none` yana `set` adịghị mma.

Ajụjụ a na-ezubere iche ụdị ama ama na-adakọghị tupu oku na-aga elu. Maka ebumnuche ngwakọta, ewepụrụ ndenye na-adakọghị; ọ bụrụ na ọ nweghị nke fọdụrụ, arịrịọ ahụ na-eweghachi ọkwa `400`. Data ikike amaghi ama na-ewepụta ịdọ aka ná ntị wee hapụ iwu ahụ n'ọrụ.

## Nchekwa na Transports

Source na target model, ma ọ bụ source na target combo, ka nọ n'okpuru iwu API-key
dị ugbu a. Iwu reasoning anaghị agbasawanye ikike model, combo, ma ọ bụ quota.

E tinyere engine ahụ n'ime Chat Completions, Responses, Anthropic Messages, na ụzọ
Codex WebSocket dị n'ime. Ụzọ WebSocket na-anabata naanị Codex target models; enweghị ike
ime combo targets n'ebe ahụ. A na-echekwa mkpebi iwu ahụ n'ime route trace dị ugbu a n'enweghị secrets.

## Nchekwa Data

Migration `src/lib/db/migrations/126_reasoning_routing_rules.sql` na-emepụta
table `reasoning_routing_rules`. Iwu na-ezo aka na API keys, combos, na provider connections echekwara.
Deletes na-ehichapụ iwu ndị metụtara ha. Database access layer dị na
`src/lib/db/reasoningRoutingRules.ts` na-elekọta cache enwere ike ime ka ọ ghara ịdị irè maka request path.

A na-etinye iwu n'ime SQLite backups, full database export, na config-sync bundle.
`reconcileReasoningRulesForSync` na-eme ka imported rules nwere references na-efu kwụsị ịrụ ọrụ ma na-akọ
conflicts ndị ahụ.
