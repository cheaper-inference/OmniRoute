Traffic Inspector bụ ngwa nchọpụta nsogbu okporo HTTPS arụnyere n’ime OmniRoute — ngwa yiri Charles Proxy / mitmweb / HTTP Toolkit nke **maara maka LLM** ma **marakwa maka agent**. Ọ dị na `/dashboard/tools/traffic-inspector` ma na-anata okporo dị ndụ site n’ebe nnweta ruru 5 n’otu oge.

**Ebe ọ dị na dashboard:** `/dashboard/tools/traffic-inspector`
**Otu sidebar:** Ngwaọrụ (mgbe AgentBridge gasịrị)
**Hụkwa:** [`AGENTBRIDGE.md`](./AGENTBRIDGE.md) — AgentBridge bụ usoro nnweta nke 1.

### Nhazi ya n’otu paragraf

`TrafficBuffer` (`src/mitm/inspector/buffer.ts`) bụ ring buffer a na-ekekọrịta nke dị na ebe nchekwa (ọ na-enwe entry 1000 na ndabara, a pụkwara ịhazi ya site na `INSPECTOR_BUFFER_SIZE`). Ebe nnweta niile na-eji `push()` edekọ ihe n’ime ya. Klas buffer ahụ na-eji `kindDetector.ts` kewaa entry ọ bụla n’ụdị (iji chọpụta ma ọ bụ arịrịọ LLM), gbakọọ `contextKey` (akara mkpịsịaka SHA-256 nke system prompt), ma gbasaa ya nye ndị niile debanyere aha na WebSocket site na `globalTrafficBuffer.subscribe()`. Dashboard na-ejikọ site na `GET /api/tools/traffic-inspector/ws` ma nata snapshot ozugbo njikọ gasịrị, emesịa ọ na-anata event `new`/`update`/`clear`.

## §2 Ụdị njide

Traffic Inspector na-akwado **isi mmalite njide 5 n'otu oge**. Enwere ike ịgbanye ma ọ bụ gbanyụọ nke ọ bụla n'onwe ya. Oghere `source` dị na `InterceptedRequest` ọ bụla (`src/mitm/inspector/types.ts`) bụ otu n'ime `"agent-bridge"`, `"custom-host"`, `"http-proxy"`, `"system-proxy"`, ma ọ bụ `"tproxy"`.

### Ụdị 1 — AgentBridge (ndabara, na-arụ ọrụ mgbe niile)

**Isi mmalite:** Ndị njikwa AgentBridge (`src/mitm/handlers/base.ts`)
**Usoro ọrụ:** Oku `intercept()` ọ bụla dị na `MitmHandlerBase` na-akpọ `hookBufferStart()` tupu iziga ya n'ihu, ma na-akpọ `hookBufferUpdate()` mgbe ọ gwụchara. Achọghị nhazi ọzọ ọ bụla — ọ na-arụ ọrụ ozugbo AgentBridge malitere ịrụ ọrụ.
**Oke iru:** Ndị nnọchi anya IDE 9 ahaziri na AgentBridge
**Ndetu:** oghere `source` dị na `InterceptedRequest` = `"agent-bridge"`

### Ụdị 2 — Ndị nnabata ahaziri iche (ntụgharị DNS)

**Isi mmalite:** Ndepụta ndị nnabata onye ọrụ kọwara (`inspector_custom_hosts` table)
**Usoro ọrụ:** Ịtinye onye nnabata site na UI na-agbakwunye `127.0.0.1 <host>` na `/etc/hosts` (ọ chọrọ sudo). Sava MITM AgentBridge dị ugbu a (ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri 443) na-emepụta asambodo SNI n'ike maka onye nnabata ọhụrụ ahụ.
**Oke iru:** Ngwa ọ bụla na-eji onye nnabata agbakwunyere — achọghị mgbanwe nhazi ngwa
**Ndetu:** `source` = `"custom-host"`

Ọmụmaatụ ojiji:

- Nyochaa `api.openai.com` site na skripti Python
- Chọta ma dozie nsogbu na `my-internal-llm.company.com`
- Jide okporo ụzọ sitere na ngwaọrụ mkpanaka dị n'otu netwọkụ ahụ (site na aghụghọ ARP — nke dị elu)

### Ụdị 3 — Onye na-ege HTTP_PROXY ntị (ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri 8080)

**Isi mmalite:** Ngwa ndị na-eji mgbanwe gburugburu `HTTP_PROXY`/`HTTPS_PROXY`
**Usoro ọrụ:** Onye na-ege ntị nke abụọ n'ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri 8080 (`src/mitm/inspector/httpProxyServer.ts`) nke na-arụ ọrụ dịka proxy HTTP/HTTPS doro anya nke ọkọlọtọ. Ọ na-anabata ọwara `CONNECT` (HTTPS) na arịrịọ HTTP kpọmkwem.
**Oke iru:** Ngwa ọ bụla na-erube isi na gburugburu `HTTP_PROXY` — enweghị mgbanwe DNS, enweghị sudo
**Ndetu:** `source` = `"http-proxy"`

```bash
# Njide ngwa ngwa maka otu iwu:
HTTPS_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8080 curl https://api.openai.com/v1/models

# Njide na-adịgide n'ime nnọkọ shell:
export HTTP_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8080
export HTTPS_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8080
```

**Mmachi TLS:** HTTPS na-agafe n'ọwara `CONNECT` nkịtị: onye na-ege ntị na-ebufe byte ezoro ezo n'agbanweghị ha ma na-edekọ naanị metadata (onye nnabata, ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri, oge). Ọ naghị ewepụ izo ya ezo na HTTPS ma ọlị, ọ nweghịkwa ntọala na-agbanye iwepụ izo ya ezo maka ụdị a. Iji jide ahụ HTTPS, zipụ onye nnabata ahụ site na Ụdị 1 (AgentBridge), Ụdị 2 (Ndị nnabata ahaziri iche), ma ọ bụ Ụdị 5 (iwepụ izo ya ezo TPROXY).

**Esemokwu ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri:** Ọ bụrụ na a na-eji ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri 8080, AgentBridge na-eweghachi 409 nwere njehie ahaziri n'ụdị doro anya. Gbanwee ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri site na mgbanwe gburugburu `INSPECTOR_HTTP_PROXY_PORT`.

### Ụdị 4 — Proxy n'usoro niile (nke dị elu, a ga-ahọrọ ịgbanye ya)

**Isi mmalite:** Ntọala proxy nke sistemụ arụmọrụ (ọ na-emetụta ngwa niile dị na kọmputa ahụ)
**Usoro ọrụ:** Ọ na-eji API nke sistemụ arụmọrụ tụgharịa okporo ụzọ HTTP/HTTPS niile ka ha gafee onye na-ege HTTP_PROXY ntị:

- **macOS:** `networksetup -setwebproxy / -setsecurewebproxy`
- **Linux:** `gsettings set org.gnome.system.proxy` + `/etc/environment`
- **Windows:** `netsh winhttp set proxy 127.0.0.1:8080`
  **Oke iru:** Ngwa ọ bụla dị na kọmputa ahụ nke na-erube isi na ntọala proxy nke sistemụ
  **Ndetu:** `source` = `"system-proxy"`

**Usoro nchekwa:**

- Ngụoge mgbanyụ akpaghị aka (ndabara bụ nkeji 30, enwere ike ịhazi ya site na `INSPECTOR_SYSTEM_PROXY_GUARD_MINUTES`)
- A na-echekwa ọnọdụ proxy sistemụ gara aga na DB ma weghachite ya mgbe a laghachiri azụ
- Dashboard na-egosi ọkwa "Na-eweghachi proxy sistemụ" ma ọ bụrụ na onye ọrụ apụọ na peeji ahụ mgbe ọ ka na-arụ ọrụ
- UI na-egosi baajị `⚠ Nke dị elu` + igbe nlele nkwenye doro anya

### Ụdị 5 — Iwepụ izo ya ezo n'ụzọ transperent nke TPROXY (Linux, root, a ga-ahọrọ ịgbanye ya)

**Isi mmalite:** TPROXY nke kernel + ntụgharị ụzọ dabere na iwu (`src/mitm/tproxy/`)
**Usoro ọrụ:** Ọ na-etinye akara na njikọ TCP mpaghara ọhụrụ na-apụ apụ gaa n'ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri ebumnuche (ndabara `443`) n'ime `mangle OUTPUT`; `ip rule` na-atụgharị ngwugwu ndị e tinyere akara ka e nyefee ha n'ime sistemụ mpaghara, ebe ebumnuche `TPROXY` nke `mangle PREROUTING` na-enyefe ha n'aka onye na-ege ntị transperent (**IP_TRANSPARENT**) (ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri ndabara `8443`). Onye na-ege ntị na-akwụsị TLS site na asambodo leaf nke CA na-agbanwe agbanwe nyere **maka aha nnabata SNI ọ bụla mgbe achọrọ ya**, na-ejide mgbanwe data ewepụrala izo ya ezo, ma zipụ arịrịọ ahụ n'ihu gaa na ebe mbụ e bu n'obi mgbe e zoro ya ezo ọzọ.
**Oke iru:** Ndị nnabata ebumnuche **ọ bụla** n'ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri ebumnuche — enweghị aghụghọ `/etc/hosts`, enweghị gburugburu `HTTP_PROXY`, enweghị mgbanwe proxy n'usoro niile. Usoro a na-egbochi achọghị mgbanwe nhazi ọ bụla, mana ọ ga-atụkwasịrịrị CA na-agbanwe agbanwe obi.
**Ndetu:** `source` = `"tproxy"`

**Ihe ndị achọrọ:** Naanị Linux (**IP_TRANSPARENT** dị naanị na Linux), ikike **CAP_NET_ADMIN** (root), na mgbakwunye N-API nke sistemụ nke a ga-eji ngwaọrụ C wuo (`npm run build:native:tproxy`). Mgbe ha adịghị, a na-eme ka bọtịnụ ngbanwe dị na dashboard ghara ịrụ ọrụ, yana nkọwa ngwaọrụ "Iwepụ izo ya ezo TPROXY chọrọ Linux + root + mgbakwunye nke sistemụ". A na-etinye ma na-eweghachi iwu firewall dịka otu azụmahịa zuru oke (ọdịda usoro anaghị ahapụ iwu `mangle` n'azụ), a na-ekpochapụkwa ha mgbe sistemụ malitegharịrị. Usoro mgbochi loop dabere na SO_MARK na-egbochi ozi proxy ahụ zipụrụ n'ihu mgbe e zoro ya ezo ọzọ ka a ghara ijide ya ọzọ.

Nke a bụ nnukwu sistemụ nta nwere ntuziaka onye ọrụ nke ya — lee `docs/security/MITM-TPROXY-DECRYPT.md` (git; anaghị achịkọta ya n'ime `/docs`) maka usoro firewall zuru ezu, CA na-agbanwe agbanwe maka SNI ọ bụla + ihe nrụnye trust-store, ụzọ mpaghara naanị, nkọwa mgbochi loop, na schema nhazi. `GET / POST / DELETE /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy` na-achịkwa bọtịnụ ngbanwe ahụ (rịba ama: ụzọ ahụ dị n'okpuru prefix AgentBridge, ọ bụghị prefix Traffic Inspector).

### 3.2 Ndepụta arịrịọ (pane aka ekpe)

- **Emere ka ọ bụrụ nke mebere** (`useVirtualList` + `ResizeObserver`): na-ejikwa ihe 1000 na-enweghị ịkwụsị ịrụ ọrụ
- **Mpịgharị akpaka** nwere ihe ngbanwe iji kwụsịtụ ya mgbe a na-enyocha ihe
- **Ọnọdụ e ji agba mata**: akwụkwọ ndụ (2xx), odo (3xx), ọbara ọbara (4xx/5xx), ntụ ntụ (ka na-aga n'ihu)
- **Emoji nke agent**: 🔵 Antigravity, 🟢 Copilot, 🟠 Kiro, 🟣 Codex, 🔷 Cursor, 🟤 Zed, 🟡 Claude Code, ⚫ Open Code, 🌐 host ahaziri

## §4 Njirimara ndị maara LLM

### 4.1 Ihe nchọpụta ụdị (`src/mitm/inspector/kindDetector.ts`)

Ọ na-ekewa arịrịọ ọ bụla dịka `"llm"`, `"app"`, ma ọ bụ `"unknown"` site n'iji akara 4:

1. **Ndekọ host** — ihe dị ka aha host API LLM 18 a ma ama (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, Mistral, Together, Fireworks, Cohere, Perplexity, Hugging Face, OpenRouter, xAI, Moonshot, wdg.)
2. **Ụkpụrụ path** — `/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/messages`, `/generateContent`, `/v1/responses`, wdg.
3. **Ọdịdị body** — na-achọpụta mpaghara `messages[]` (OpenAI/Claude), `contents[]` (Gemini), `prompt`, `input`
4. **Ihe ngosi user-agent** — `codex`, `claude`, `gemini`, `antigravity`, `kiro`, `copilot`, `cursor` n'ime eriri UA

Host omenala ndị agbakwunyere site na Mode 2 na-eketa `kind` ha site na ntinye fọm (uru ndabara bụ `"custom"`).

### 4.2 Ngwakọta SSE (`src/mitm/inspector/sseMerger.ts`)

**Mmejuputa clean-room nwere onwe ya.** Ntụgharị event na-agbaso
[usoro server-sent events nke WHATWG](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/server-sent-events.html#parsing-an-event-stream),
ebe mwughachi na-agbaso schema streaming ọha nke [OpenAI](https://platform.openai.com/docs/api-reference/chat/create),
[Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/streaming), na
[Gemini](https://ai.google.dev/api/generate-content#method:-models.streamgeneratecontent).

Ọ na-ewughachi ozi ikpeazụ nke assistant site na event delta SSE raw:

- **Anthropic**: na-achịkọta `content_block_delta` dịka index si dị; na-ahazi `text_delta`, `input_json_delta` (oku tool), `thinking_delta`
- **OpenAI**: na-achịkọta nhọrọ/oku tool nke Chat Completions na ihe mmepụta Responses API
  dịka index si dị
- **Gemini**: na-achịkọta `candidates[i].content.parts`
- **Unknown**: na-eweghachi event raw dịka ha dị

Taabụ Response na-egosi toggle: **"Event raw ↔ Ejikọtara"**.

### 4.3 Ihe nhazi mkparịta ụka (`src/mitm/inspector/conversationNormalizer.ts`)

**Mmejuputa clean-room nwere onwe ya.** A kọwara normalization site na contract black-box
obodo na schema ozi ọha nke OpenAI, Anthropic, na Gemini; ejighị source mmejuputa
sitere upstream mee ihe.

Ọ na-atụgharị usoro ozi OpenAI, Anthropic, na Gemini ka ọ bụrụ otu `NormalizedConversation` tupu rendering:

```ts
interface NormalizedConversation {
  request: NormalizedTurn[]; // ozi / ọdịnaya / prompt sitere na body arịrịọ
  response: NormalizedTurn[]; // nzaghachi assistant (eji sseMerger jikọta)
  contextKey: string | null; // akara mkpịsị aka SHA-256 nke system-prompt
}
```

Ụdị block: `text`, `tool_use`, `tool_result`. Taabụ Conversation na-eji ọdịdị a n'agbanyeghị provider.

### 4.4 Itinye agba na context key (`src/mitm/inspector/contextKey.ts`)

- Na-agbakọ `SHA-256` nke system prompt (ozi mbụ `role:system`, ma ọ bụ mpaghara `system`, ma ọ bụ `systemInstruction` nke Gemini)
- Na-eweghachi prefix hex nwere mkpụrụedemede 12 (`"a3f9c2..."`)
- Frontend na-ejikọta key ahụ na agba HSL a na-ekpebi mgbe niile maka ogwe oke aka ekpe
- **Nyo `"same context"`**: ịpị chip `ctx #a3f` na-agbakwunye filter iji gosi naanị arịrịọ nwere otu akara mkpịsị aka ahụ

Nke a na-eme ka ọ dị mfe iji anya kewapụ "persona" ma ọ bụ ọrụ dị iche iche na-agba n'otu session agent.

### 4.5 Iwepụta metadata LLM

Maka arịrịọ LLM, taabụ LLM Details na-ewepụta:

```ts
interface LlmMetadata {
  provider: string | null; // "openai" | "anthropic" | "gemini" | ...
  apiKind: string | null; // "chat.completions" | "messages" | "embeddings" | ...
  model: string | null; // sitere na body arịrịọ ma ọ bụ nzaghachi
  messages: number; // ọnụọgụ turn
  tokensIn: number | null; // usage.prompt_tokens / usage.input_tokens
  tokensOut: number | null; // usage.completion_tokens / usage.output_tokens
  streamed: boolean; // true ma ọ bụrụ na ọ bụ nzaghachi SSE
  mappedTo: string | null; // header x-omniroute-mapped
  costEstimateUsd: number | null; // atụmatụ ọnụahịa dabere na ọnụahịa OmniRoute
}
```

### 4.6 Filter arịrịọ na-aga n'ihu ozugbo

Mpaghara `status` nke arịrịọ bụ `number | "in-flight" | "error"` — a na-etinye entry dịka
`"in-flight"` ozugbo arịrịọ malitere ma **melite ya n'otu ebe**
mgbe nzaghachi (ma ọ bụ njehie) bịarutere. Toggle **"Live"** nke toolbar
(`liveOnly`, key i18n `trafficInspector.liveOnly`) na-amachi ndepụta ahụ ka ọ bụrụ naanị entry
ndị `status === "in-flight"`, nke na-enye gị ohere ile njikọ ndị mepere emepe ozugbo.

Filter ahụ bụ predicate dị ọcha nke na-arụ ọrụ n'akụkụ client na
`src/lib/inspector/matchesTrafficFilter.ts`:

```ts
if (f.liveOnly && req.status !== "in-flight") return false;
```

Ọnọdụ toggle ahụ dị na `useTrafficFilters` (hook dashboard inspector) ma
na-ejikọta ya na filter ndị ọzọ (profile, host, agent, source, status, context).

### 4.7 Ijikọta arịrịọ na process (Linux)

Na Linux, enwere ike ijikọta arịrịọ ọ bụla a jidere na **process obodo
sitere na ya**. A na-agbakwunye mpaghara nhọrọ abụọ na `InterceptedRequest`:

```ts
pid?: number;          // id nke process sitere na ya (naanị Linux)
processName?: string;  // aha process sitere na ya (naanị Linux)
```

`src/mitm/inspector/processAttribution.ts` na-ejikọta port ephemeral nke _client_
nke njikọ ahụ na PID + aha site na:

1. Ịgụ `/proc/net/tcp` na `/proc/net/tcp6` iji chọta inode socket maka
   port ahụ (`parseProcNetTcpForInode`, parser dị ọcha enwere ike iji fixture nwalee).
2. Inyocha `/proc/<pid>/fd/` maka symlink gaa na `socket:[<inode>]`.
3. Ịgụ aha process site na `/proc/<pid>/comm`.

Cache TTL nke sekọnd 1 na-amachi ọnụ ahịa nyocha procfs mgbe ibu dị elu. Attribution bụ
**best-effort** — ọdịda ọ bụla na-enye `null` ma ọ dịghị mgbe ọ na-egbochi capture. Na
macOS/Windows function ahụ na-eweghachi `null` (stub; nkwado `lsof`/`GetExtendedTcpTable`
bụ ọrụ a ga-eme n'ọdịnihu).

Traffic Inspector na-egosi **okporo ụzọ HTTPS niile e jidere**, gụnyere nkụnyeisi ikike na ahụ arịrịọ. Etinyela usoro njikwa ndị a:

| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| Iwu                               | Etu e si etinye ya                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

### Mmachi ndị amaara

- **Ọnọdụ proxy sistemụ niile** na-emetụta ngwa niile dị na igwe ahụ, gụnyere ndị ahịa VPN na SSO. Jiri ya na ngụ oge nkwụsị akpaka mgbe niile. Ejila ya na igwe ọtụtụ mmadụ na-eji.
- **HTTPS nke ọwara CONNECT**: Ọnọdụ 3 (HTTP_PROXY) na-ejide naanị metadata ọwara maka ebe HTTPS na-aga, ọ naghị ewepụkwa izo ya ezo ma ọlị. Nke a bụ atụmatụ e kpachapụrụ anya mee — iwepụ izo ya ezo ga-achọ ka onye ahịa ọ bụla tụkwasị asambodo MITM obi, ya mere onye na-ege ntị na-eme ka HTTPS nọgide bụrụ nke ezoro ezo site n'otu nsọtụ ruo na nke ọzọ. Jiri AgentBridge, Custom Hosts ma ọ bụ TPROXY decrypt mgbe ịchọrọ ahụ HTTPS.
- **Eriri ederede edobere kpọmkwem n'ụfọdụ kọmponẹntị**: Ụfọdụ kọmponẹntị UI (F7/F8) nwere eriri ederede ole na ole edobere kpọmkwem nke igodo i18n na-ekpuchibeghị. Edekọrọ ihe ndị a dịka Mmachi Amaara na akụkọ oghere i18n; a ga-ebuga ha na usoro na-esote. Eriri ndị emetụtara bụ akara ịchọ mma UI ndị na-achọghị ntụgharị asụsụ maka ojiji arụmọrụ.
