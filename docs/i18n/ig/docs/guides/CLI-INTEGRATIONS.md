Maka ngosipụta nke enwere ike imekọrịta ihe, gburugburu ụmụaka amachibidoro yana ntọlite Gemini na-adịgide adịgide, hụ [nkwekọrịta mmalite CLI](./CLI-LAUNCH-CONTRACTS.md).

OmniRoute na-ebufe ezinụlọ `setup-*` iwu nke na-ahazi koodu CLI (Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, Cline, ...) iji OmniRoute dị ka azụ azụ ya - ya mere ngwá ọrụ na-agwa ya okwu.** otu** ụzọ njedebe na OmniRoute ga-aga n'aka onye na-eweta ya nwere ndaghachi onwe ya. Iwu ọ bụla na-agụ ya** ibi ndụ** katalọgụ ụdị sitere na OmniRoute na-agba ọsọ (mpaghara ma ọ bụ nke dịpụrụ adịpụ) wee dee faịlụ nhazi nke ngwaọrụ ahụ na** nke gị** igwe. A na-atụgharị igodo API site na mgbanwe gburugburu ebe ọ bụla ngwa na-akwado ya. Iwu ndị na-aga n'ihu na faịlụ mpaghara mpaghara ka edepụtara n'okpuru.

Enwekwara ihe mbido - `omniroute run <target>` - nke na-emepụta `claude`, `codex`, `aider`, `goose`, `opencode`, `qwen`, `gemini` ma ọ bụ `whycodes` jiri env ziri ezi agbanyere, na-edeghị nhazi ọ bụla. Ebumnobi na aha ha na-esi n'akwụkwọ pụtara `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` (`claude-code|cc|anthropic`, `codex-cli|openai-codex|openai`, `goose-cli`, `open-code`, `qwen-code`, `gemini-cli`, `why-codes|why`), na `omniroute completion` na-enye otu okwu ebumnuche pụtara ìhè. Ndị na-ebuputa ngwa-ngwa ọ bụla - `omniroute launch` ( Koodu Claude ) na `omniroute launch-codex` (Codex) - ka dị.

Ịbanye n'ụgbọ mmiri dị site n'otu ọnọdụ local/remote. Iwu API-nke dị n'okpuru na-edobe njirimara njikwa dị iche na nzere ndị na-eweta ma ghara ibipụta nzere na mmepụta ahaziri ahazi:

```bash
omniroute providers add glm --credential-env GLM_API_KEY --name work
omniroute providers import ./providers.json --dry-run --json
omniroute providers auth openai
omniroute providers edit <connection-id> --default-model glm/glm-5.2
omniroute providers remove <connection-id> --yes
```

Maka edemede, họrọ `--credential-stdin` ma ọ bụ `--credential-env`; A na-edowe `--credential` maka ojiji mpaghara a na-achịkwa. `providers remove` chọrọ `--yes` n'ọnụ ọnụ na-adịghị emekọrịta ihe, na iwu ise niile na-asọpụrụ ọnọdụ nọ n'ọrụ ma ọ bụ nhọrọ `--base-url`/`--api-key` zuru ụwa ọnụ.

Ndị na-enye ihe ahọpụtara jụrụ prefixes ID, aha ma ọ bụ aha ndị na-eweta ọrụ; jiri ID njikọ zuru oke mgbe ọtụtụ njikọ dakọtara. Mepụta ma dezie iwu gụghachi njikọ echekwara azụ, ma wepụ ya na-egosi na ọ naghịzi agụ ya. Mbubata na-awụpụ ụzọ provider/name dị adị. Ndenye ebubata enweghị ike ịkagbu ebe njedebe njikwa, okirikiri ma ọ bụ nzere njikwa ewepụtara na CLI.

- [VS Koodu Copilot Chat](./VSCODE-COPILOT.md) - ndọtị OmniCopilot ; ọ nwekwara ike ịgbara gị iwu `setup-*` ndị a n'ime onye nchịkọta akụkọ

## Tebụl nna ukwu

Iwu ọ bụla na-asọpụrụ** ọnọdụ nọ n'ọrụ** (tọọ ya na `omniroute connect`, lee [Ọnọdụ ime ime](./REMOTE-MODE.md) ) ma ọ bụ ọkọlọtọ `--remote <url> --api-key <key>` doro anya. Ihe ndetu na ọkọlọtọ (egosiri na isi mmalite iwu):

- `--remote <url>` — weta katalọgụ ahụ site na OmniRoute dịpụrụ adịpụ (na-ewepụ `--port` na ọnọdụ nọ n'ọrụ). `--api-key <key>` na-enye nzere maka ihe nkesa ahụ (ndabere na `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` env var, ma ọ bụ akara ngosi ọnọdụ nọ n'ọrụ).
- `--only <patterns>` - eriri ndị kewapụrụ rikoma; debe naanị IDs ụdị dabara (dịka `--only glm,kimi`). Dị na `setup-codex`, `setup-claude`, `setup-opencode`, `setup-continue`, `setup-cursor`, `setup-crush`, `setup-zed`.
- `--dry-run` — bipụta kpọmkwem ihe a ga-ede na-emetụghị sistemụ faịlụ ahụ aka. Dị na iwu `setup-*` ọ bụla** ewezuga** `setup-cursor` (nke na-adịghị ede faịlụ).
- `--model <id>` - achọrọ (ma ọ bụ họrọ mmekọrịta) maka ngwaọrụ ndị na-enweghị ụdị nchọpụta akpaaka: Cline, Kilo, Roo, Goose, Qwen, Aider, 5dive, WhyCodes. Ngwa ndị ahụ na-anabatakwa `--yes` maka ọsọ na-adịghị emekọrịta ihe (nke chọrọ `--model`). `setup-opencode` na-ewe `--model` ka ịtọọ ụdị ọkwa dị elu nke ndabara.
- `--model <id>` na `omniroute run` na-eso ngwa ngwa ngwa ngwa ngwa ngwa (`bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs`):** onye enyemaka** na-anata `--model openai/<id>` na** oghe koodu** `--model omniroute/<id>` (a na-agbakwunye prefix naanị mgbe id anaghị ebu ya);** qwen** na** gemini** nata id verbatim;** claude** na-enweta ya site na `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`,** ọgazị** site na `GOOSE_MODEL`,** whycodes** site `-P omniroute -m <id>`, na** codex** site `-c model_providers.omniroute.*` args.**Qwen bụ naanị ebumnuche ịgba ọsọ nke siri ike chọrọ `--model`** - `omniroute run qwen` na-enweghị ya na-apụ `2` na mperi doro anya.
- `--port <port>` — ọdụ ụgbọ mmiri OmniRoute mpaghara ( `20128` ndabara, eleghara anya mgbe edobere `--remote` ). Ugbu a na `setup-*` niile na ma ndị mbido.
- Koodu ọpụpụ `omniroute run` : nwa CLI nke onwe koodu ka a na-agbasa ngwa ngwa; `2` = arụmụka ezighi ezi (ebumnuche anaghị akwado, `--model` chọrọ efu, ihe nchekwa akpa); `127` = ọnụọgụ abụọ e lekwasịrị anya adịghị na `PATH`; `130`/`143`/`129` mgbe mmalite nke kwụsịrị site `SIGINT`/`SIGTERM`/`SIGHUP`; `1` = ọdịda mmalite oge ojiri gaa ọzọ.
- Ndị nrụpụta abụọ ahụ (`launch`, `launch-codex`) na-anabata `--profile <name>` ka ịhọrọ profaịlụ nke `setup-claude` / `setup-codex` dere, gbakwunyere pass-through args maka ọnụọgụ abụọ `claude` / `codex` dị n'okpuru.

Ezi ntụziaka ntọala na-ekekọrịtakwa onye na-ahọrọ mmekọrịta:

```bash
# Pick from the active local or remote model catalog and configure the target.
omniroute configure claude
omniroute configure opencode --provider glm
omniroute configure qwen --model qwen/qwen3.8-max-preview --yes
```

`configure` ugbu a na-ekenye aka na ntụziaka ndị a nwalere maka `codex`, `claude`, `opencode`, `qwen`, `aider`, `goose`, `cline`, `continue`, `kilo`, na `5dive`. Naanị IDE, MITM, na ndenye katalọgụ naanị na-eduzi na-anọgide na-asọpụta `setup-*`/ akwụkwọ ntuziaka ma ewepụtaghị ya dị ka ebumnuche mmalite.

> `setup-opencode` bu** Openai dakọtara** njikọ OpenCode. Enwekwara ntinye ngwa mgbakwunye bara ụba - `omniroute setup opencode` - nke na-etinye `@omniroute/opencode-plugin`. Ha bụ iwu dị iche iche; okpokoro n'elu akwụkwọ `setup-opencode`.
>
> Ngwa mgbakwunye na-abịa na ngwugwu abụọ, otu kwa OpenCode isi, n'ihi na ndị na-ebu ibu abụọ na-atụ anya ntinye ntinye dị iche iche: `@omniroute/opencode-plugin` maka OpenCode v1 na `@omniroute/opencode-plugin-v2` maka OpenCode v2. Ihe ngwugwu v2 dị ọhụrụ (`0.1.0`) ma na-agbaso nkwekọrịta nnabata nke ka na-agagharị, ya mere ọ na-agụ ụdị OpenCode mkpụrụ n'ime akwụkwọ ndekọ aha kama iche otu. Wụnye ya site na ịtinye ntinye `plugins` na `opencode.json`; `omniroute setup opencode` ka na-etinye ngwungwu v1. Nhọrọ na usoro nyocha nzere dị na ngwugwu README.

## Ojiji n'ime mpaghara

Mgbe OmniRoute na-agba na `localhost:20128`, naanị mee iwu nhazi maka ngwaọrụ gị. A na-ewepụta katalọgụ ahụ site na sava mpaghara ahụ.

```bash
# Codex: dee profaịlụ maka ụdịdị ọ bụla dakọtara n'ime ~/.codex/
omniroute setup-codex
codex --profile glm52            # jiri profaịlụ emepụtara

# Claude Code: dee profaịlụ nke ụdịdị ọ bụla, wee malite otu
omniroute setup-claude
omniroute launch --profile glm52

# OpenCode: dee onye na-eweta dakọtara na openai nke nwere ụdịdị katalọgụ niile
omniroute setup-opencode
export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY=sk-...  # a na-ezo aka na ya site na {env:OMNIROUTE_API_KEY}, ọ dịghị mgbe a na-edebe ya na diski
opencode -m omniroute/glm/glm-5.2 "..."

# Ngwaọrụ ndị na-enweghị nchọpụta akpaaka chọrọ ka e kwupụta ụdịdị kpọmkwem:
omniroute setup-aider --model glm/glm-5.2
omniroute setup-qwen --model qwen/qwen3.8-max-preview

# Lelee tupu oge eruo n'edeghị ihe ọ bụla:
omniroute setup-continue --dry-run
```

Malite ya n'edeghị nhazi ọ bụla ma ọlị (naanị ntinye env):

```bash
omniroute launch                 # Claude Code → OmniRoute mpaghara
omniroute launch-codex           # Codex CLI → OmniRoute mpaghara
omniroute launch-codex --profile glm52
omniroute run claude --model openai/gpt-5.4
omniroute run codex --model openai/gpt-5.4 --dry-run --json
omniroute run aider --model glm/glm-5.2 -- --message "reply OK"
omniroute run goose --model glm/glm-5.2
omniroute run opencode --model glm/glm-5.2 -- run "reply OK"
omniroute run qwen --model glm/glm-5.2 -- -p "reply OK"
omniroute run gemini --model glm/glm-5.2 -- --skip-trust -p "reply OK"

# Ụzọ iwu akọwapụtara kpọmkwem: nyefee ihe ọ bụla na-abịa mgbe --
omniroute run claude -- --print-system-prompt "review this diff"
```

## Ojiji tere aka

Jiri `--remote` + `--api-key` tụọ iwu nhazi ọ bụla aka na OmniRoute dị tere aka. A na-ewepụta katalọgụ ahụ site na nke dị tere aka; a na-ede nhazi ahụ na kọmputa mpaghara gị.

```bash
# OpenCode megide VPS dị tere aka, debe naanị ụdịdị glm/kimi
omniroute setup-opencode --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx \
  --only glm,kimi
opencode -m omniroute/glm/glm-5.2 "..."   # buru ụzọ mee export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY

# Profaịlụ Codex sitere na katalọgụ dị tere aka
omniroute setup-codex --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx

# Malite CLI ozugbo megide nke dị tere aka
omniroute launch       --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx
omniroute launch-codex --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx
```

Kama ịna-enyefe `--remote`/`--api-key` oge ọ bụla, banye otu ugboro ma kwe ka **ọnọdụ na-arụ ọrụ** nye ha na-akpaghị aka:

```bash
omniroute connect 192.168.0.15        # na-emepụta token nwere oke ikike, na-echekwa ọnọdụ ahụ
omniroute setup-codex                 # ← ugbu a na-eji katalọgụ dị tere aka
omniroute setup-opencode              # ← otu ihe ahụ
omniroute launch                      # ← Claude Code megide nke dị tere aka
```

Lee [Ọnọdụ Tere Aka](./REMOTE-MODE.md) maka ọnọdụ, oke ikike, na njikwa token.

## Ìgwè ndị nnọchi anya 5dive

[5dive](https://5dive.ai) na-agba ìgwè ndị nnọchi anya ide koodu na-arụ ogologo oge, nke ọ bụla bụ unit systemd n'okpuru onye ọrụ Unix nke ya. Ọ bụghị CLI ide koodu n'onwe ya, ya mere ọ dịghị ihe `omniroute run` ga-amalite — `5dive` bụ ebe ezubere maka **nhazi-naanị**.

```bash
omniroute configure 5dive --model failover-demo --yes
omniroute setup-5dive --model failover-demo --auth-profile omniroute --agent worker1
```

Ụdị abụọ ahụ na-ede otu **profaịlụ nkwenye** 5dive, ma oche `claude` ọ bụla e jikọrọ na profaịlụ ahụ ga-ekwurịta okwu na OmniRoute. Ihe atọ metụtara ebe ezubere a kpọmkwem:

- **Ọ na-agba na kọmputa nnabata ìgwè ahụ, dịka root.** Ngwaa 5dive na-arụ ọrụ na unit systemd mpaghara na ndekọ ọnọdụ nke root nwe; enweghị ọnọdụ tere aka. Usoro ahụ na-eji `sudo` maliteghachi onwe ya mgbe ọ bụghịbu root (`--no-sudo` na-agbanyụ nke ahụ ma bipụta iwu ahụ kama).
- **Ebe njedebe ahụ ga-abụrịrị `https://` ma ọ bụrụ na ọ bụghị loopback.** Igodo API nke onye nnọchi anya ahụ na-eso URL ahụ na arịrịọ ọ bụla, 5dive na-ajụkwa ebe njedebe ederede nkịtị dị n'èzí kọmputa ahụ. Adreesị LAN nzuzo abụghị ihe ewepụrụ.
- **Nhọpụta ụdịdị nke oche ọ bụla n'onwe ya nwere ọkwa karịa profaịlụ ahụ.** Profaịlụ ahụ na-ebu `ANTHROPIC_DEFAULT_{OPUS,SONNET,HAIKU}_MODEL`, mana oche ka etinyere na model id mbụ na-ada n'oge ntụgharị mbụ ya site na _"There's an issue with the selected model"_. Nyefee `--agent <name>` (enwere ike ikwughachi ya) iji họrọkwa ụdịdị maka oche ndị ahụ; usoro ahụ na-ebipụta iwu ahụ mgbe ị naghị eme ya.

A na-enye 5dive igodo API ahụ na **stdin** (`--api-key=-`), ya mere ọ dịghị mgbe ọ na-apụta na mmepụta `ps`.

Ịtụ profaịlụ ahụ aka na **combo** OmniRoute kama otu ụdịdị bụ ihe na-eme ka ìgwè ahụ nweta failover onye na-eweta: mgbe ebe njedebe bụ isi kwụsịrị kpamkpam n'etiti ntụgharị n'ime ọsọ e dekọrọ na
[#11578](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/issues/11578), onye nnọchi anya ahụ mechara nzọụkwụ ndị fọdụrụ na fallback ahụ ma ọ dịghị mgbe o gosipụtara nkwụsị ọrụ ahụ.

## Idobe native deps mgbe a na-emelite: `--include=optional`

Mgbe ị ji `omniroute update` emelite (mgbe ị kwadoro ya, ma ọ bụ jiri `--apply`),
OmniRoute na-eji `--include=optional` arụnyere n'ime ya mee nrụnye ahụ:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute@latest --include=optional
```

Nke a abụghị ọkọlọtọ ị na-enye `omniroute update` — onye na-emelite ya na-etinye ya
mgbe niile. Ọ na-eme ka o doo anya na `optionalDependencies` (`better-sqlite3`, `keytar`,
`tls-client`, nchịkọta LLMLingua SLM) ga-adịgide mgbe emelitechara ya ọbụna ma ọ bụrụ na nhazi npm gị
nwere `omit=optional`, nke gaara ewepụ native SQLite
driver na njikọ OS-keyring na-enweghị ọkwa. Iji hụ iwu ahụ kpọmkwem tupu etinye ya n'ọrụ:

```bash
omniroute update --dry-run
# [NWALE NA-ENWEGHỊ MMEJU] Ọ ga-agba: npm install -g omniroute@latest --include=optional
```

Ọkọlọtọ `omniroute update` ndị ọzọ (enyochara na koodu isi): `--check` (jiri exit 1 pụọ ma ọ bụrụ na
ọ bụ nke ochie), `--apply` (wụnye n'ajụghị nkwenye), `--changelog`, `--no-backup`,
`--yes`.

## Google Gemini CLI site na `omniroute run gemini`

E nyochara nkwekọrịta a megide `@google/gemini-cli` 0.50.0: CLI na-asọpụrụ
`GOOGLE_GEMINI_BASE_URL` ma na-eziga `POST /v1beta/models/<model>:generateContent`
(yana `:streamGenerateContent?alt=sse`) na ya — nke dabara kpọmkwem na ihu Gemini
nke OmniRoute (`/v1beta`). `omniroute run gemini` na-ahazi nke ahụ na-akpaghị aka:

- `GOOGLE_GEMINI_BASE_URL` → Base URL OmniRoute na-arụ ọrụ ugbu a (mgbọrọgwụ, enweghị `/v1`);
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` → nzere OmniRoute e kpebiri iji (nhọrọ/env/context);
- **`GEMINI_CLI_HOME` nwa oge e kewapụrụ iche** nke `.gemini/settings.json` ya
  na-ahọrọ nkwenye `gemini-api-key`, ka nnọkọ Google OAuth echekwara (Code Assist)
  ghara imeri mmalite e duziri gaa OmniRoute — a na-ewepụ ya mgbe mmemme kwụsịrị;
- **ịdị ọcha env**: a na-ewepụ `GOOGLE_API_KEY`,
  `GOOGLE_GENAI_USE_VERTEXAI` na `GOOGLE_GENAI_USE_GCA` na env nke child process (ndị a ga-atụgharị
  nkwenye gaa Vertex/Code Assist), ma debe `GEMINI_DEFAULT_AUTH_TYPE=gemini-api-key`
  dịka usoro nkwado ọzọ — ebumnuche `run` ndị ọzọ na-enwetakwa otu
  nhazi ahụ maka mgbanwe ndị nke ha na-emegiderịta;
- ntinye `--model <id>` sitere na `--provider`/`--model`.

```bash
omniroute run gemini --model glm/glm-5.2 -- --skip-trust -p "hello"
```

Nchedo workspace-trust nke Gemini ka na-arụ ọrụ na ọnọdụ headless — nye
`--skip-trust` (ma ọ bụ jiri mmekọrịta kwado ndekọ ahụ) n'onwe gị; launcher ahụ
kpachapụrụ anya ghara ịgafe ya. Launcher a dị iche na **ndebanye ACP**
(`src/lib/acp/registry.ts`, `gemini --acp`), nke ka bụ
njikọ agent-protocol maka `/dashboard/acp-agents`.

## Nnwale anwụrụ ọkụ n'ezie (nhọrọ ịbanye)

Nnwale nlọghachi nke atụmatụ mmalite a pụrụ ịkọ kpọmkwem na-agba na CI (`tests/unit/cli/run-command.test.ts`,
`tests/unit/cli/run-execution.test.ts`). Iji nyochaa faịlụ binary N'EZIOKWU megide sava OmniRoute N'EZIOKWU,
e nwere usoro nnwale a na-ahọrọ ịbanye na ya na
`tests/integration/upstream-cli-smoke.int.test.ts`. Ọ naghị agba ọsọ na-akpaghị aka
(nnwale nta ọ bụla na-amafe ma ọ bụrụ na `RUN_CLI_SMOKE=1` adịghị), ọ na-ebufe nzere site na AHA env-var
(ọ bụghị site na uru ya), na-ewepụ eriri ndị yiri igodo na mmepụta ọ bụla e dekọrọ, na-amafe
ebe ebumnuche ndị binary ha na-adịghị arụnyere, ma na-ekewa ọdịda dịka
auth / upstream / config kama iji naanị boolean:

```bash
RUN_CLI_SMOKE=1 \
OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_BASE_URL="http://localhost:20128" \
OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_MODEL="<provider/model>" \
OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_API_KEY_ENV="OMNIROUTE_API_KEY" \
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/integration/upstream-cli-smoke.int.test.ts
```

Nhọrọ: `OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_TARGETS="codex,opencode,qwen"` na-amachi nnwale ahụ;
`OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_TIMEOUT_MS` na-edochi oke oge 120s maka ebumnuche ọ bụla.
