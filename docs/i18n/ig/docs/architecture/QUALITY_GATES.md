Akwụkwọ a bụ ntụaka kachasị ikike maka ọnụ ụzọ ogo CI niile dị na OmniRoute.
Ọ na-akọwa ọnụ ụzọ ọ bụla, ihe ọ na-enyocha, ọrụ CI ọ na-arụ n'ime ya, ma ọ na-eji
ntọala ratchet ma ọ bụ iwu ngafe/ọdịda, yana ma ọ na-egbochi build ka ọ bụ naanị ndụmọdụ.

Maka nchịkọta dị mkpirikpi na iwu allowlist, lee ngalaba "Quality Gates & Ratchets"
dị na `AGENTS.md`. Maka nyocha dị mkpa, nhazi ọkwa ntozu-oke, na atụmatụ mmegharị
nke otu sistemụ ahụ na-adabereghị na ngwá ọrụ ọ bụla, lee
[Quality Gate Playbook](../ops/QUALITY_GATE_PLAYBOOK.md).

## Ndepụta ọnụ ụzọ na profaịlụ mmezu

### Nnabata onye a na-atụle

Usoro ọrụ CI na Quality Gates nke ọ bụla na-ewepụta mkpebi kwụsiri ike: `Gate / CI` na
`Gate / Quality`. Iwu nnabata ha nwere ụdị akọwapụtara na-edepụta ọrụ upstream niile
dị ka ihe achọrọ ma ọ bụ ihe ndụmọdụ. Ọrụ achọrọ nke metụtara ọnọdụ ahụ ga-aga nke ọma:
nsonaazụ na-efu efu, a kagburu, a mafere, ka na-echere, na nke a na-amaghị enweghị ike
igosipụta PASS. Nkewa ziri ezi nke docs-only ma ọ bụ catalog-only nwere ike ime ka lane
koodu ghara ịdị mkpa; a naghị anabata PR draft dị ka onye a na-atụle. Label `hotfix`
anaghị ewepụ mkpa ihe akaebe.

Usoro ọrụ abụọ ahụ na-ekpuchi PR ndị na-abanye na ngalaba main/release, push gaa `main`,
mmalite aka na merge-group events. Push, dispatch na merge-group na-eme nhọrọ zuru ezu.
Push gaa `release/**` anaghị ebido ha (2026-10-18): mgbanwe ọ bụla emeela matrix ahụ na PR
ya, ma ngwakọta e jikọtara bụ ọrụ Release-Green (`docs/ops/RUNNER_BOX.md` → Lane a na-akwado).
Fork na merge group na-eji runner a na-akwado maka ọrụ ndị gaara ahọrọ runner
self-hosted; a ga-enyocha na ikike runner a na-akwado zuru oke tupu mwepụta.

PR nkịtị na-eme nhọrọ ngwa ngwa. Lane `full-code` na `full-code-e2e` nke
iwu nnabata ahụ (E2E, integration, package artifact, Electron smoke, Bun SQLite, ecosystem
na protocol clients) na-arụ ọrụ naanị na draft queue Mergify ma ọ bụ na mmezu zuru ezu:
`gh workflow run ci.yml --ref <branch>`.

Receipt JSON ọ bụla na-akọwapụta SHA e mere checkout, workflow run na attempt.
CLI na-ajụ mgbe SHA checkout/event adabaghị. Nnwale workflow na-ejikọta ndị otu policy
na ndepụta `needs` nke ọrụ mkpebi ka lane ọhụrụ ma ọ bụ nke ewepụrụ ghara ịpụ n'amaghị ama.
Receipt ndị ahụ na-ekpuchi workflow nke ha, ọ bụghị mbipụta, deployment, ma ọ bụ usoro ime
nke advisory scanner dị adị. Ime ka aha check abụọ ahụ rụọ ọrụ n'ime branch rules bụ
mgbanwe nchịkwa dị iche; ịgbakwunye ọrụ ndị a n'onwe ya anaghị echebe branch.

### Queue njikọta

Label `queue` na-eziga PR na queue Mergify (`.mergify.yml`). Mergify na-emepe draft PR na
branch `mergify/merge-queue/`, CI na-eme nhọrọ zuru ezu n'ebe ahụ, ma PR mbụ na-ejikọta
mgbe `Gate / CI`, `Gate / Quality` na `Merge integrity (changelog + generated skills)` gara nke ọma.

Mergify na-ede nchịkọta queue ya n'ime nkọwa draft ahụ, ngwa ndị ọzọ nwekwara ike idezi ya.
Event `pull_request` `edited` na draft dị otu ahụ na-ewere concurrency group nke ya
(`-queue-edit-<run_id>`) ka ọ ghara ịkagbu mmezu eziokwu nke draft ahụ, ọ na-amafekwa gate na
matrix. Naanị `Merge integrity` ka na-arụ ọrụ. Ọrụ gate e mafere nwere aha check dị iche,
n'ihi na Mergify na-agụ `Gate / CI` e mafere dị ka check dara ada ma wepụ PR ahụ na queue.

Mgbe e wepụrụ ya na queue, push anaghị etinye PR ahụ ọzọ na queue mgbe niile. Tinye comment
`@mergifyio queue` na ya.

### Ndepụta static scan

Ndepụta npm-alias nwere ụdị akọwapụtara na ndị otu static-scan dị na
`config/quality/gate-manifest.json`. Mee `npm run check:gate-manifest` iji nyochaa
aha script na command kpọmkwem megide `package.json`; mgbakwunye, mwepụ na
mgbanwe command na-eme ka ma local hook ma ọrụ nhazi ụdị mgbanwe dị na CI daa.
Alias abụghị ọrụ workflow, matrix instance ma ọ bụ test case: a gaghị egosi ọnụ ọgụgụ
ndị a dịka ihe ndị nwere ike dochie ibe ha.

Jiri `npm run quality:scan -- --list` ma ọ bụ `npm run quality:scan:fast -- --list`
iji lelee alias ndị ahọpụtara n'emeghị ha. Runner na-akpọ npm entrypoint,
ya mere a na-echekwa runtime ya (gụnyere Bun ebe a haziri ya).
Manifest na-edekọ alias ndị na-anọghị na profaịlụ ndị ahụ dị ka ndị a na-akpọ iche,
a machibidoro maintenance command na profaịlụ scan read-only.

Profaịlụ ndị a na-ekpuchi naanị static scan. Ha anaghị akwado product test,
coverage, packaging, external check ma ọ bụ nnabata release zuru ezu nke onye a na-atụle.
Nnabata workflow na-eji `config/quality/admission-policy.json` e jikọtara ya na
`scripts/quality/admission-verdict.mjs`. Profaịlụ release-observer ka dị iche;
nyochaa check na receipt ndị metụtara ha n'onwe ha. Ndepụta nkọwa
dị n'okpuru bụ ntụaka, ọ bụghị ihe akaebe na gate rụrụ ọrụ n'ezie.

Script dị n'okpuru `scripts/check/` (policy gate) na `scripts/quality/` (ratchet engine).
Ebe eziokwu CI si abịa bụ `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.

### Ụzọ ngwa ngwa nke Release PR (`quality.yml`)

`.github/workflows/quality.yml` na-emeju CI na PR main/release, push gaa `main`,
dispatch na merge group. PR na-eji fast check a na-enyocha dịka path. E wepụrụ
duplicate build e gbanyụrụ kpamkpam; ezigbo build/package/boot check ka dị na CI.
Unit suite na vitest na-arụ ọrụ naanị na CI (`Unit Tests (N/8)` na `Vitest`).

| Ọrụ                                              | Oke                                                                                                              | Na-egbochi       |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| `Docs Gates (fast-path)`                         | PR docs/koodu; ntụaka docs API na docs-all                                                                       | Ee               |
| `Fast Quality Gates`                             | PR koodu; static check, typecheck, dashboard typecheck, unit test ndị emetụtara, sibling test echefuru (ndụmọdụ) |
| `No new ESLint warnings`                         | PR koodu; lint guard maara suppression                                                                           | Ee, gụnyere fork |
| `Merge integrity (changelog + generated skills)` | PR na-abụghị draft; changelog na mmekọrịta generated skill                                                       | Ee, gụnyere fork |

#### Akụkọ sibling test echefuru

`npm run check:forgotten-sibling-tests` na-arụ ọrụ dịka nzọụkwụ ndụmọdụ nke `Fast Quality Gates` ma na-ejikwa ọzọ
onye na-edozi mbubata dị n’azụ maapụ mmetụta ule.
Maka modul mmepụta ọ bụla agbanwere, ọ na-akọ usoro njikọ ndị a na-apụghị ịgbanwe agbanwe:
`changed module/symbol -> static consumer -> candidate sibling test` mgbe ule a na-atụle
adịghị na ọdịiche pull request ahụ. A na-edobe nchịkọta Markdown na nsonaazụ JSON dịka
artifact workflow `forgotten-sibling-tests` maka nhazi tupu e mee ka ọ bụrụ ihe mgbochi.

Mbupụgharị barrel na mbubata dynamic bụ naanị nchọpụta maka idozi ihe; ha anaghị emepụta
nchọpụta na-egbochi ihe. Ihe ndị ewepụrụ n’iwu ma nyochaa dị na
`config/quality/forgotten-sibling-allowlist.json`. Ndenye ọ bụla ga-akpọ aha consumer na ule a na-atụle,
nye nkọwa doro anya, ma jikọọ na issue ma ọ bụ pull request GitHub. Ndenye ndị ahaziri n’ụzọ na-ezighị ezi na-eme ka
usoro ahụ daa ma gbochie ya. Ihe ndị ewepụrụ n’iwu enweghị ike izochi ule a na-atụle ehichapụrụ ma ọ bụ ọdịiche na-agbakwunye `.skip`/`.todo`;
ime ka assertion ghara ịdị ike na ụzọ nzuzo ndị ọzọ ka bụ ọrụ nke ọnụ ụzọ
`check:test-masking` nke na-egbochi ihe n’onwe ya.

## Ịchekwa Mbelata Ratchet — ụzọ mgbada (#8584)

Ratchet ahụ bụ naanị ọkara akpaaka, ọ bụkwa ọkara na-ezighi ezi. **Ibulite** cap bụ
ndezi JSON e ji aka eme nke na-ewe sekọnd iri ma bụrụ ụzọ kachasị ọsọ iji tọhapụ PR nke nọ na red.
**Iwetu** otu chọrọ ka mmadụ mee `--update` ma commit nsonaazụ ya — ruo mgbe
ọrụ `bank-ratchet-shrinks` rutere, ọ dịghị workflow mere ya. Nsonaazụ a tụrụ
(2026-07-25): faịlụ frozen 18 adịlarị na ma ọ bụ n’okpuru cap ahịrị 800 maka faịlụ ọhụrụ, nke kacha njọ
dị na 132× (`src/shared/validation/schemas.ts`, ahịrị 19 nwere cap 2,523); oke
mgbagwoju anya si na `1794 → 2169` gafee ihe dịka ndetu rebaseline 37, nwere naanị otu
mbelata (−1); e dekwara “mee ka ọ sie ike site na `--update` na cycle na-esote” ugboro 31 ma mee ya
naanị otu ugboro. Cap nke na-adị ndụ karịa koodu nke mere ka e nwee ya na-eji nwayọ agbanwe
decomposition ọ bụla emechara ka ọ bụrụ ohere uto nye onye ọ bụla ga-edezi faịlụ ahụ ọzọ.

`nightly-release-green.yml` → ọrụ **`bank-ratchet-shrinks`** na-emechi okirikiri ahụ:

|            |                                                                                                                   |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Na-arụ na  | `schedule` (3×/ụbọchị) + `workflow_dispatch` — a kpachapụrụ anya mee ka ọ **ghara** ịbụ `push`                    |

| Na-ebuga   | otu PR na-adị ọhụrụ mgbe niile megide alaka release — a na-emelite ya n’ike, anaghị eziga ọtụtụ PR na-enweghị isi |

A na-achịkọta nchekwa a n’otu ìgwè kama ime ya n’otu push n’otu push n’ihi na ọ chọghị ka e mee ya ozugbo (mbelata

push (`release-green`); ọ bụ naanị nchekwa ka a na-achịkọta n’otu ìgwè.

## Iwu Ndepụta Ikike

Ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ọ bụla nke na-agaghị ada n’ihi mmebi ndị dịbu adị na-eji ndepụta ikike a kpọchiri akpọchi
(dịka, `KNOWN_STALE_DOC_REFS`, `KNOWN_MISSING`, `KNOWN_RAW_SQL`). Iwu ahụ bụ:

**Dozie isi ihe kpatara nsogbu ahụ; jiri ndepụta ikike naanị mgbe mmebi ahụ dịbu adị ma
enweghị ike idozi ya n’otu PR ahụ.**

Mgbe ị na-agbakwunye ntinye na ndepụta ikike:

1. Tinye nkọwa na-akọwa ihe mere e ji mee ya.
2. Rụtụ aka n’okwu nlekota ahụ (dịka, `// #3498 — Njirimara Phase 2, emebebeghị ya`).
3. Wepụ ntinye ahụ n’otu PR ahụ nke na-edozi mmebi ahụ — ntinye ochie nke na-anaghịzi
   egbochi mmebi na-arụ ọrụ bụkwa ntụpọ (mmanye iwu megide ntinye ochie nke 6A.3 ga-eme ka
   ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ahụ daa n’ihi ntinye ndepụta ikike na-enweghịzi ihe ọ metụtara ozugbo etinyere ya n’ọrụ).

**Etinyela** ntinye na ndepụta ikike iji mee ka ule gafee ngwa ngwa. Ọnụ ụzọ nyocha na-acha akwụkwọ ndụ ebe
ndepụta ikike na-aga n’ihu na-eto eto na-enye echiche ụgha banyere ịdịmma.

### Mgbe ọnụ ụzọ nyocha dara na PR gị

1. **Jiri nlezianya gụọ nsonaazụ ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ahụ** — ọ na-agwa gị kpọmkwem faịlụ ma ọ bụ akara nke
   mebiri iwu ahụ.
2. **Dozie mmebi ahụ** — ọtụtụ ọnụ ụzọ nyocha bụ nlele sistemụ faịlụ a kara aka nke na-agafe ozugbo
   koodu ahụ ziri ezi.
3. **Ọ bụrụ na mmebi ahụ dịbu adị** (ya bụ, ọ bụghị gị webatara ya mana ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ahụ
   amalitela ikpuchi ya): gbakwunye ntinye ndepụta ikike nwere nkọwa ihe kpatara ya na okwu nlekota.
4. **Ọ bụrụ na ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ahụ bụ ratchet** (mkpuchi, ịdọ aka ná ntị ESLint, mmegharị otu ihe ugboro ugboro, mgbagwoju anya):
   mgbanwe gị mere ka metrik ahụ ka njọ. Dozie nsogbu dị n’okpuru ya, ma ọ bụ (n’ọnọdụ pụrụ iche) mee
   `npm run quality:ratchet -- --update` ma ọ bụrụ na e bu n’obi mee mgbanwe ahụ ma a nabatara
   mbelata metrik ahụ — mana kọwaa ihe kpatara ya na nkọwa PR.
5. **Ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ndụmọdụ** (`continue-on-error: true`) bụ naanị maka ozi — ha anaghị egbochi
   njikọta mana ha na-apụta na nchịkọta CI. Dozie ha n’agbanyeghị nke ahụ.

#### `check-new-key-coverage` — mgbochi i18n maka igodo ọhụrụ

Ọ bụ nwanne `check-ui-value-drift`. Nke ahụ na-achọpụta uru Bekee nke **edegharịrị**
ebe a hapụrụ ntụgharị-asụsụ ya n'azụ; nke a na-achọpụta igodo Bekee nke **agbakwunyere**
ebe ụfọdụ locale na-enwetaghị ya.

`check-ui-keys-coverage` enweghị ike ịhụ ụdị nsogbu a: ọ na-amanye opekempe pasentị n'otu locale, ebe
igodo iri na otu na-adịghị n'ime ~13,000 na-ahapụ mkpuchi na 99.9%. Pasentị n'otu asụsụ enweghị ike
ịkọwa na "e wepụtara atụmatụ a na-enweghị ntụgharị-asụsụ" — atụmatụ dum nwere ike ịbanye na locale ọhụrụ na-enweghị
ederede ọ bụla ma ọnụọgụ ahụ agaghị agbanwe.

Nsogbu ọ na-anọchi anya bụ nke a: Phase 3 nke Orchestration Canvas tụgharịrị igodo iri na otu ya n'ofe
locale 42 ndị dị n'oge ahụ. Awa ole na ole ka e mesịrị, otu asụsụ EU (#13044) buliri repo ahụ
ruo locale 51, ma ndị ọhụrụ itoolu ahụ (`el`, `et`, `ga`, `hr`, `lt`, `lv`, `mt`, `sl`, `sr`) enwetaghị
ha. `deepMergeFallback` na-eji Bekee dochie igodo na-adịghị, ya mere ụdị ọdịda ahụ bụ
UI a na-atụgharịghị kama UI tọgbọ chakoo — ezigbo nsogbu, nke e mere ka ọ ghara ime mkpọtụ.

Dị ka nwanne ya, ọ **na-eburu diff n'uche**, na-atụnyere Bekee dị na merge base na working
tree, ya mere oghere ndị dịbu adị na-anọgide otu ahụ, mgbochi ahụ achọghịkwa migration tupu agbanye ya.

**Ihe akara `__MISSING__:<english>` anaghị emezu ya (kemgbe 2026-09-17).** Na mbụ, ọ bụ
usoro ndọtị oge e depụtara n'akwụkwọ — runtime na-alaghachi na Bekee ziri ezi — ruo mgbe PR atụmatụ asatọ na
2026-09-16 gbakwunyere igodo 61 ma tinye ihe akara ahụ n'ime locale 65 niile kama ịtụgharị ha: mgbochi a
nabataara ha niile, ọ dịghị ihe gbochiri PR ndị ahụ, mgbe ahụ mgbochi oke ruru nke ezigbo ntụgharị-asụsụ nke na-egbochi
daa n'ọnụ release maka onye ọ bụla (pt-BR 3.2 % > 2.5 % + 0.5). Ugbu a, a na-ewere ihe akara
dị ka ntụgharị-asụsụ na-adịghị. Jiri
`node scripts/i18n/sync-ui-keys.mjs --locale=<codes> --translate-markers --batch-size=40` dozie ọdịda, ma ọ bụ
mee locale niile n'otu oge site na `npm run i18n:translate-new-keys` (`scripts/i18n/translate-new-keys.sh`,
nwere nchekwa mgbe detached, ọ na-ajụ ịmalite ma ọ bụrụ na env `OMNIROUTE_TRANSLATION_*` adịghị). Igodo kwesịrị ịnọ
n'Bekee (aha product/engine/flag e kpọgidere) kwesịrị ịdị na `scripts/i18n/untranslatable-keys.json`,
ọ bụghị n'azụ ihe akara. `vi` machibidoro ihe akara kpamkpam (`tests/unit/i18n-vi-completeness.test.ts`).

#### `check-vitest-exclusions` — mgbochi ule e debere n'akụkụ

Faịlụ dị na ndepụta `exclude` nke `vitest.config.ts` bụ ule na-adịghị agba, ma ọ na-adị ka
mkpuchi nye onye ọ bụla na-agụ tree ahụ. Faịlụ iri isii na abụọ chịkọtara n'azụ nkọwa
`// #8618 — ọdịda dịbu adị; wepụ exclusion a mgbe edoziri ya`. E mechiri Issue #8618 na
2026-08-11 ebe ndepụta ọ na-esochi toro site na ntinye 45 ruo 62, nke ọhụrụ ọ bụla na-eketa nkọwa
na-arụtụ aka n'issue nwụrụ anwụ. Mgbe e mesịrị tụọ ndepụta ahụ otu faịlụ n'otu faịlụ (#13204), **51 n'ime 62
gafere megide tree dị ugbu a na-enweghị mgbanwe ọ bụla na isi mmalite**.

Mgbochi ahụ chọrọ ka exclusion ọ bụla na-eduga na ezigbo faịlụ (a) kpọọ issue eji esochi ya na
(b) pụta na `config/quality/vitest-exclusions.json` yana ọkwa ya a tụrụ, ka ịgbakwunye otu bụrụ
diff a pụrụ inyocha n'ime faịlụ e kenyere ya kama ịbụ naanị otu ahịrị ọzọ n'ime array nwere ntinye 60. N'ebumnuche,
ọ naghị agba ule ndị ewepụrụ ọzọ — nke ahụ na-ewe ~10 nkeji ma kwesị ịdị n'ọrụ oge ụfọdụ; ndekọ
ngwaahịa ahụ na-edekọ mgbe e tụrụ nke ọ bụla na nke ikpeazụ.
