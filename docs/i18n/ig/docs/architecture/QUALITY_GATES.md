Akwụkwọ a bụ ntụaka kachasị ikike maka ọnụ ụzọ ogo CI niile dị na OmniRoute.
Ọ na-akọwa ọnụ ụzọ ọ bụla, ihe ọ na-enyocha, ọrụ CI ọ na-arụ n'ime ya, ma ọ na-eji
ntọala ratchet ma ọ bụ iwu ngafe/ọdịda, yana ma ọ na-egbochi build ka ọ bụ naanị ndụmọdụ.

Maka nchịkọta dị mkpirikpi na iwu allowlist, lee ngalaba "Quality Gates & Ratchets"
dị na `AGENTS.md`. Maka nyocha dị mkpa, nhazi ọkwa ntozu-oke, na atụmatụ mmegharị
nke otu sistemụ ahụ na-adabereghị na ngwá ọrụ ọ bụla, lee
[Quality Gate Playbook](../ops/QUALITY_GATE_PLAYBOOK.md).

## Ndepụta ọnụ ụzọ na profaịlụ mmezu

### Ịnabata onye ga-abanye

Usoro ọrụ CI na Quality Gates nke ọ bụla na-ewepụta mkpebi kwụsiri ike: `Gate / CI` na
`Gate / Quality`. Iwu nnabata ha nwere ụdị nsụgharị na-edepụta ọrụ upstream ọ bụla
dị ka nke a chọrọ ma ọ bụ nke ndụmọdụ. Ọrụ achọrọ nke metụtara ọnọdụ ahụ ga-aga nke ọma:
nsonaazụ na-efu efu, nke a kagburu, nke a mafere, nke ka na-echere, na nke amaghị
enweghị ike igosi PASS. Nhazi ziri ezi nke docs-only ma ọ bụ catalog-only nwere ike ime ka
ụzọ koodu ghara ịdị mkpa; a naghị anabata PR draft dịka onye ga-abanye. Label `hotfix`
anaghị ewepụ mkpa ihe akaebe.

Usoro ọrụ abụọ ahụ na-ekpuchi PR ndị na-abanye na branch main/release, push na `main`, dispatch
eji aka, na ihe omume merge-group. Push, dispatch, na merge-group na-eme nhọrọ zuru ezu.
Push ndị a na-eme na `release/**` anaghị ebido ha (2026-10-18): mgbanwe ọ bụla agbaalarị matrix
na PR ya, ebe nchikota e jikọtara ọnụ bụ ọrụ Release-Green (`docs/ops/RUNNER_BOX.md` → Ụzọ hosted).
Fork na merge group na-eji hosted runner maka ọrụ ndị ga-ahọrọ self-hosted
runner n'ọnọdụ ndị ọzọ; a ga-enyocha na ikike hosted zuru oke tupu rollout.

Receipt JSON ọ bụla na-akọwa SHA e mere checkout, workflow run, na attempt.
CLI na-ajụ checkout/event SHA mismatch. Nnwale workflow na-ejikọta ndị so na policy
na ndepụta `needs` nke ọrụ verdict ka ụzọ ọhụrụ ma ọ bụ nke ewepụrụ ghara ịla n'iyi na nzuzo.
Akwụkwọ nnata ndị ahụ na-ekpuchi usoro ọrụ nke ha, ọ bụghị mbipụta, mbugharị, ma ọ bụ usoro ime nke nyocha advisory dị adị. Ime ka nyocha ndị metụtara ya bụrụ ihe a chọrọ n’iwu branch bụ mgbanwe nchịkwa dị iche; ịkọwa jobs ha anaghị echebe branch n’onwe ya.

`API Route Typecheck` bụ nyocha workflow kwụụrụ onwe ya, nke dị n’èzí ma mkpebi nchịkọta abụọ ahụ ma profaịlụ static nke local full observer. Nyochaa nsonaazụ ya iche maka candidate ahụ kpọmkwem. `.github/workflows/api-route-typecheck.yml` na-agba ọsọ na PR ndị na-abanye na `main` ma ọ bụ `release/**`, na push gaa n’ezinụlọ branch nke ọ bụla n’ime ha, yana manual dispatch. O nweghị path filter. Mgbanwe ebe PR base na-atụ aka na-arịọ nyocha ọhụrụ; ndezi body/title na-eji aha observation-only `Admission not requested / API Route Typecheck` ma enweghị ike dochie admission check. Workflow ahụ ekwupụtaghị merge-group trigger. Mgbasawanye merge-queue ọ bụla chọrọ nyocha a ga-ebu ụzọ hụ na e guzobere mmezu ya maka event ahụ.

### Ikike mmezu UI

CI na-agba ndepụta UI zuru ezu dịka shard matrix `test-vitest` anọ, nke ọ bụla nwere
worker na-erughị ma ọ bụ hà abụọ yana oke oge ọrụ nkeji 15 dị ugbu a. Shard mbụ na-agbakwa
suite MCP/autoCombo Vitest. Nsonaazụ ọrụ achọrọ na-ekpuchi matrix leg ọ bụla; ọdịda
ma ọ bụ nkagbu nke otu shard enweghị ike igosi nnabata onye ga-abanye.

Nightly observer na-edobe receipt exact-candidate dị iche maka shard UI ọ bụla,
gụnyere mmezu MCP na receipt mbụ. A chọrọ receipt anọ niile, log, na
njirimara mmezu kwekọrọ. `npm run test:vitest:ui` nke local ka na-ahọrọ
suite zuru ezu; otu run `--shard` bụ naanị ihe akaebe ezughị ezu.

### Nchịkọta coverage unit

Shard unit iri na abụọ ahụ na-anakọta coverage V8 n'oge mmezu nnwale ha dị ugbu a. Shard
gara nke ọma na-agba `scripts/ci/compact-v8-coverage.mjs` iji pịakọta faịlụ mbụ na-enweghị
imepụta report Istanbul etiti. Ọrụ Coverage ikpeazụ na-enyocha manifest iri na abụọ niile megide
SHA onye ga-abanye, runtime, source root, na hash data, wee gbaa reporter c8 na threshold
coverage ndị dị ugbu a. Input na-edobe usoro filename global nke c8; pre-merging n'otu shard
adịghị mma maka coverage function/block agwakọtara. Shard na-efu efu, nke ahaziri n'ụzọ na-ezighi ezi,
ma ọ bụ nke na-adabaghị na-eme ka nnabata daa. Shard nnwale dara ada na-edobe raw diagnostic coverage
iche; ọ gaghị abanye na merge ikpeazụ.

### Ndekọ nnata nke ọganihu mmezu unit

Iwu `test:unit:ci:shard` a na-ekekọrịta na-edekọ usoro `primary`, `dashboard` na `serial`
site na `scripts/ci/unit-progress-reporter.mjs`. Akụkọ ule nkịtị ka dị
n'ọrụ. Ndekọ nnata dị iche nke nwere naanị metadata na-edekọ mmalite na mmecha faịlụ ọ bụla,
ebe mgbama obi nke sekọnd 30 na-edepụta faịlụ ndị ka na-arụ ọrụ na oge ha jirila. Ọ na-edekọ
naanị metadata mmezu dị na ndepụta nkwenye; anaghị echekwa nkọwa ule, mmepụta, ọdịnaya njehie na
gburugburụ gburugburu dị ka data ahaziri ahazi.

CI na Quality na-edobe faịlụ JSONL dịka `unit-progress-ci-<run>-<attempt>-<shard>` na
`unit-progress-quality-<run>-<attempt>-<shard>`. Oge ngwụcha job nwere ike igbochi mbulite ga-eme emechaa;
mgbama obi ndị e zipụlarị ka bụ ihe akaebe maka nchọpụta nsogbu. Enweghị mgbama obi nwekwara ike igosi
usoro runner egbochiri ma ọ bụ kwụsịrị. Ndekọ nnata ndị a na-echekwa nhọrọ ule,
concurrency, oge ngwụcha na exit status ndị dị ugbu a; ha abụghị mkpebi nnabata candidate.

### Ihe akaebe echekwara na nchịkọta nsonaazụ nnabata

`scripts/quality/validate-release-acceptance.mjs` na-achịkọta atụmatụ e nyere ya na manifests ya; ọ naghị egosi na atụmatụ ahụ kpuchiri usoro ntọhapụ niile. Akụkọ ya na-edobe `releaseAcceptance: false` n'ụzọ doro anya, ọbụna mgbe atụmatụ ahụ bụ `VERIFIED`. Workflow `release-acceptance.yml` ka na-eji data ule shadow, ọ bụghị ezigbo akwụkwọ nnata nke build, package, boot ma ọ bụ promotion.

CLI chọrọ `--evidence-root` iji nyochaa ihe akaebe echekwara. A na-achọta faịlụ n'ụzọ `<evidence-root>/<artifact_id>/<member>` ma gbakọọ hash SHA-256 ha. Ndekọ PASS na-enweghị ihe akaebe, faịlụ na-efu ma ọ bụ nke gbanwere, ụzọ na-adịghị nchebe ma ọ bụ symlink na-egbochi nkwenye. Faịlụ na-efu adịghị ka logs efu nke ọrụ gara nke ọma. Nnyocha SHA/run/attempt ndị dị ugbu a ka nọọrọ onwe ha: bytes dakọtara enweghị ike ime ka akwụkwọ nnata ochie dị irè. Izu oke nke faịlụ abụghị ihe akaebe na arụmọrụ bụ nke ezigbo ya ma ọ bụ na e mere promotion nke artifact.

### Ndepụta static scan

Ndepụta npm-alias nwere ụdị nsụgharị na ndị so na static-scan dị na
`config/quality/gate-manifest.json`. Gbaa `npm run check:gate-manifest` iji nyochaa
aha script na command kpọmkwem megide `package.json`; mgbakwunye, mwepụ, na
command drift na-eme ka ma local hook ma ọrụ change-classification dị na CI daa.
Alias abụghị ọrụ workflow, instance matrix, ma ọ bụ test case: a gaghị
egosi ọnụọgụ ndị a dịka ihe enwere ike iji dochie ibe ha.

Jiri `npm run quality:scan -- --list` ma ọ bụ `npm run quality:scan:fast -- --list`
iji lelee alias ahọpụtara n'ebughị ụzọ mee ha. Runner na-akpọ
npm entrypoint, ya mere a na-echekwa runtime ya (gụnyere Bun ebe ahaziri ya).
Manifest na-edekọ alias ndị na-anọghị na profaịlụ ndị ahụ dịka ndị a na-akpọ iche,
a machibidoro command maintenance na profaịlụ scan read-only.

Profaịlụ ndị a na-ekpuchi naanị static scan. Ha anaghị akwado nnwale product,
coverage, packaging, external check, ma ọ bụ nnabata release zuru ezu nke onye ga-abanye.
Nnabata workflow na-eji `config/quality/admission-policy.json` ejikọtara na
`scripts/quality/admission-verdict.mjs`. Profaịlụ release-observer ka dị iche;
nyochaa check na receipt ndị metụtara ha iche. A kọwara static command nke full observer na
`config/quality/release-green-static-profile.json`, gụnyere
`audit:deps`, nhọrọ mutation-test, na translation check. Loader na-enyocha command npm
megide package.json na nkwupụta CI/Quality e mere model; ọ na-ajụ drift
kama ime shell token dịka argument. Output SKIP/INCOMPLETE nke scanner achọrọ
enweghị ike igosi PASS. Profaịlụ ahụ na-edekọ external exclusion ya ma ọ naghị edochi
hosted coverage, matrix nnwale, package, ma ọ bụ ihe akaebe deployment. Ndepụta prose dị n'okpuru
bụ ntụaka, ọ bụghị ihe akaebe na ọnụ ụzọ gbara ọsọ n'ezie.

Script dị n'okpuru `scripts/check/` (ọnụ ụzọ policy) na `scripts/quality/` (engine ratchet).
Ebe eziokwu CI si abịa bụ `.github/workflows/ci.yml`.

### Ọnọdụ ahụike dọkụmentị ntọala e bipụtara

`.github/workflows/i18n-base-health.yml` na-enyocha ọnọdụ ahụike dọkụmentị mgbe e zipụrụ mgbanwe ndị ahọpụtara
na `main` na `release/v*`, nakwa mgbe e ji aka kpalite ya. Ihe nzacha ya na-ekpuchi dọkụmentị,
Markdown dị na mgbọrọgwụ, `llm.txt`, ọnọdụ/nhazi ntụgharị na ngwa i18n ndị metụtara ya.
Ọrụ naanị ya, `Published base documentation health`, na-eji Ubuntu a kwadoro n'eluigwe ojii ma nwee
oke oge nkeji ise. Ọ na-eme nkwado naanị ọgụgụ ma na-edepụta nnata nchọpụta na-enweghị
ịwụnye ndabere, ịtụgharị asụsụ ma ọ bụ ịnabata ọnọdụ.

`scripts/i18n/check-base-health.mjs` chọrọ ka ọnụọgụ nke ihe ndị na-ekwekọghị ekwekọ gbasara source-hash, missing-target, target-hash,
stale-translation na section-cache bụrụ efu. Ọ na-enyochakwa oke dọkụmentị/locale isi dị ugbu a
na ebe e dekọrọ na o si pụta. Ọnọdụ na-ezighi ezi, mkpuchi na-ezughị ezu
ma ọ bụ onye nyocha a kwụsịrị n'etiti ọrụ na-emepụta `INCOMPLETE`; ngbanwe e gosipụtara na-emepụta `FAIL`. Ha abụọ
na-eweghachi ọnọdụ ọpụpụ na-egosi ọdịda. A na-ejikọta nnata ahụ na SHA nke checkout, workflow, run na
attempt, ma na-echekwa ya dịka `i18n-base-health-<sha>-<run>-<attempt>`.

Nke a bụ nlele nọọrọ onwe ya nke ntọala e bipụtara. Ọ naghị agbakwunye event PR ma ọ bụ
merge-group, nyocha PR a chọrọ, alias npm ma ọ bụ onye otu profaịlụ static-scan.
Iwu PR dị iche iche dị ugbu a na nhazi ndụmọdụ nke onye nyocha dị n'okpuru
ka dị iche iche. Nbugo gara nke ọma enweghị ike ịgbanwe nsonaazụ ahụike dara ada ka ọ bụrụ PASS.

### Ụzọ ngwangwa PR release (`quality.yml`)

`.github/workflows/quality.yml` na-emeju CI na PR main/release, push na `main`,
dispatch, na merge group. PR na-eji fast check ndị a nyochara site na path. E wepụrụ duplicate build
nke agbanyụrụ kpamkpam; ezigbo check build/package/boot ka dị na CI.

| Ọrụ                                              | Oke                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Na-egbochi        |
| ------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| `Docs Gates (fast-path)`                         | PR dọkụmentị/koodu; ntụaka dọkụmentị API na docs-all                                                                                                                                                                  | Ee                |
| `Fast Quality Gates`                             | PR koodu; nlele static, typecheck, typecheck dashboard, ule unit metụtara mgbanwe                                                                                                                                     | Ee                |
| `Forgotten sibling tests`                        | PR koodu; a na-esochi modulu ndị gbanwere ruo ndị na-eji ha n'ụzọ static na ule sibling ndị nwere ike ịdị mkpa; a na-akọ ụzọ barrel na dynamic-import dịka nchọpụta ndụmọdụ, tinyere mwepu allowlist e zoro aka na ha | **Ndụmọdụ**       |
| `Vitest (fast-path)`                             | PR koodu; nchịkọta ule vitest ngwa ngwa                                                                                                                                                                               | Ee                |
| `Unit Tests fast-path`                           | PR koodu; nchịkọta ule unit nke kewara ụzọ 4                                                                                                                                                                          | Ee                |
| `No new ESLint warnings`                         | PR koodu; ihe nche lint nke maara suppressions                                                                                                                                                                        | Ee, gụnyere forks |
| `Merge integrity (changelog + generated skills)` | PR ndị na-abụghị draft; mmekọrịta changelog na skill ndị emepụtara                                                                                                                                                    | Ee, gụnyere forks |

#### Akụkọ ule sibling e chefuru

`npm run check:forgotten-sibling-tests` na-ejigharị import resolver dị n'azụ map mmetụta ule.
Maka modulu production ọ bụla gbanwere, ọ na-akọ usoro ndị a pụrụ ịtụkwasị obi
`changed module/symbol -> static consumer -> candidate sibling test` mgbe ule ahụ nwere ike ịdị mkpa
adịghị na diff pull-request ahụ. A na-edobe nchịkọta Markdown na nsonaazụ JSON dịka
artifact workflow `forgotten-sibling-tests` maka calibration tupu e mee ka ọ bụla bụrụ ihe na-egbochi.

Barrel re-exports na dynamic imports bụ naanị nchọpụta resolution; ha anaghị emepụta
nchọpụta na-egbochi. Mwepu ndị enyochala dị na
`config/quality/forgotten-sibling-allowlist.json`. Ntinye ọ bụla ga-akpọ consumer na ule candidate,
nye ezigbo ihe kpatara ya, ma jikọọ issue GitHub ma ọ bụ pull request. Ntinye ndị emebighị nke ọma na-eme ka
usoro mechie. Mwepu enweghị ike izochi ule candidate ehichapụla ma ọ bụ diff na-agbakwunye `.skip`/`.todo`;
ime ka assertion ghara isi ike na ụdị masking ndị ọzọ ka bụ ọrụ nke gate

#### `check-ui-value-drift` — ọnụ ụzọ ntụgharị ochie

Ọ na-achọpụta otu ndaghachi azụ i18n nke ọnụ ụzọ ndị ọzọ enweghị ike ịhụ n'ụzọ nhazi: e degharịrị uru Bekee
ma ntụgharị ndị sitere na Bekee _gara aga_ ka dị, ya mere
ndị ọrụ na-abụghị ndị Bekee ka na-agụ ederede e ji obi ike dee ma nke na-ezighi ezi ugbu a.

Nke a rutere n'ezie na mmepụta. E degharịrị `oauthModal.googleOAuthWarning` mgbe e tinyere onye enyemaka nbanye Antigravity
(#5203); **39 n'ime asụsụ mpaghara 43** ka nwere ederede na-agwa ndị na-ahụ maka sistemụ ka ha "detuo
URL zuru ezu ma mado ya n'okpuru" — usoro nke na-agaghị agwụcha maka onye na-eweta ahụ. Achọpụtaghị ya
ruo #8463 n'ihi na:

- `sync-ui-keys` na-etinye naanị igodo ndị **na-adịghị**, ọ naghị etinye ndị **ochie**;
- `check-ui-keys-coverage` na-agụ ọnụnọ igodo, ya mere a na-agụ ntụgharị ochie dị ka nke ekpuchiri;
- `check-translation-drift` na-esochi oyiri akwụkwọ `docs/i18n/<locale>/**.md` —
  ọ naghị agụ `src/i18n/messages/*.json`. Ọ na-egbochi na job `docs-sync-strict` kemgbe
  mmekọrịta ọzọ nke 2026-09: dezie isi akwụkwọ → `npm run i18n:run -- --files=<doc>` (n'ogo ngalaba, dị ọnụ ala).

**Ọ na-ele ọdịiche anya, ọ bụghị baseline.** Ọ na-atụnyere `en.json` dị na merge base na
working tree; maka igodo ọ bụla uru Bekee ya gbanwere, asụsụ mpaghara ọ bụla ka nwere
ntụgharị a na-emetụghị aka bụ nke ochie. Nke a kpachaara anya **kpọchie ụgwọ dịbu adị** — ọdịiche
enweghị ike igosi Bekee ochie nke ntụgharị dịla ogologo sitere na ya, ya mere ọnụ ụzọ ahụ na-enyocha
naanị ihe mgbanwe ugbu a metụrụ. Nhọrọ ọzọ (baseline hash maka igodo ọ bụla) ga-achọ
faịlụ ewepụtara nke ruru ~600 KB, okpukpu 3 nke baseline kachasị ukwuu dị ugbu a, na-agbanwe na PR i18n ọ bụla.

E nwere ụzọ abụọ iji mezuo ya:

1. melite ntụgharị ndị o metụtara, ma ọ bụ
2. tọọ ha ka ha bụrụ `__MISSING__:<new english>` — mgbe ahụ runtime ga-ewepụta Bekee e meziri
   (`src/i18n/request.ts::deepMergeFallback`, #7258), a ga-etinyekwa igodo ahụ n'ahịrị ntụgharị.

Ọ bụrụ na **pụtara** eriri ahụ gbanwere, họrọ **ịgbanwe aha igodo ahụ**: igodo ọhụrụ enweghị ike iketa
ntụgharị ochie. Nke ahụ bụ usoro #8463 jiri.

```bash
npm run i18n:check-value-drift          # nke siri ike (nke CI na-agba)
npm run i18n:check-value-drift:warn     # naanị mkpesa
BASE_REF=origin/release/vX.Y.Z npm run i18n:check-value-drift
```

Ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ngbanwe-uru na igodo-ọhụrụ na-eji otu ntụnyere anaghị agbanwe agbanwe sitere na
`scripts/i18n/resolve-ui-diff-base.mjs`. Usoro ọrụ ahụ na-enyefe otu SHA ntọala, ọnụ-ntọala na
onye a na-atụle nye nyocha abụọ ahụ. Mmegharị PR/ntọala, push na merge-group na-eji ọnọdụ
mmemme ha. Izipụ alaka onye a na-atụle chọrọ kpọmkwem PR ya mepere emepe ma ọ bụ ntinye

na-enweghị ntinye ahụ na-atụnyere commit ikpeazụ na nne na nna mbụ ya; ọ bụghị nyocha
ụgwọ ntụgharị asụsụ niile e ketara. Ịkpọ ya na kọmputa mpaghara chọrọ ntọala ntụnyere e nyere

**Ụdị koodu ọhụrụ (Clean-as-You-Code) — kemgbe 2026-08-30, naanị ụzọ ọsọ ọsọ PR**

Na event `pull_request`, `quality.yml` na-enyefe `--base-ref <PR base SHA>` na `check:file-size`,
`check:complexity-ratchets` na `check:dead-code`. Maka `check:complexity-ratchets` na
`check:dead-code`, ọnụ ụzọ ahụ na-atụnyere HEAD na merge-base **naanị n'ime faịlụ ndị PR
metụrụ** (`scripts/check/newCodeMode.mjs`: a na-emepụta merge-base n'ime
`git worktree` nwa oge, ESLint/knip na-agba ebe ahụ na na HEAD, wee gbakọọ ọdịiche nke ọnụ ọgụgụ
kwa faịlụ):

- **na-egbochi** — PR tinyere mmebi cyclomatic/cognitive ma ọ bụ dead exports na faịlụ ndị ọ gbanwere
  (`complexityNewCode=`, `cognitiveComplexityNewCode=`, `deadExportsNewCode=` n'ime log);
- **ndụmọdụ** — mkpokọta zuru ụwa ọnụ ma e jiri ya tụnyere ntọala a kpọchiri akpọchi. Mgbanwe e ketara anaghị eme ka
  PR na-enweghị ihe o mere nwee akara uhie; a na-edekọ mgbanwe ahụ maka mmezi n'oge nhazi ntọhapụ ma ọrụ
  ohere fọdụrụ na-enyocha ya.

`check:file-size` na-eji base ref dịka iwu nke ya si dị (`scripts/check/check-file-size.mjs`, #8522):

## Iwu Allowlist

Ọnụ ụzọ nyocha ọ bụla na-agaghị ada n’ihi mmebi ndị dịbu adị na-eji allowlist akpọchiri akpọchi

   ọnụ ụzọ ahụ daa n’ihi ndenye allowlist na-enweghị ihe ọ metụtara ozugbo etinyere ya n’ọrụ).

### Mgbe ọnụ ụzọ nyocha dara na PR gị

Ederede ochie ebe a kpọrọ `check:cycles` gate “akwụkwọ ndụ akwụkwọ ndụ, ahọpụtara nke ọma” ma kwado ka ọ nọgide na-egbochi n'ihi na `check:circular-deps` (dpdm) kọrọ okirikiri 91. Akwụkwọ ndụ akwụkwọ ndụ ahụ bụ **akwụkwọ ndụ akwụkwọ ndụ ụgha**: `check:cycles` nyochara subdirectory 5 (faịlụ 450), dakọtara naanị static `import|export … from`, ma tụfuo specifier `@/` na `@omniroute/open-sse/` niile, ya mere ọ pụghị ịhụ okirikiri dynamic-import + alias ndị jupụtara na repo ahụ. #### `check-new-key-coverage` — mgbochi i18n maka key ọhụrụ

Ọ bụ nwanne `check-ui-value-drift`. Nke ahụ na-achọpụta uru Bekee e **degharịrị**
mgbe a hapụrụ nsụgharị ya n'azụ; nke a na-achọpụta key Bekee e **tinyeere ọhụrụ**
mgbe ụfọdụ locale enwetaghị ya ma ọlị.

`check-ui-keys-coverage` enweghị ike ịhụ ụdị nsogbu a: ọ na-amanye opekempe pasent maka locale ọ bụla,
ma key iri na otu na-adịghị n'ime ihe dị ka leaf 13,000 na-ahapụ coverage na 99.9%. Pasent maka asụsụ

ịbanye na locale ọhụrụ n'enweghị ederede ọ bụla ma ọnụ ọgụgụ ahụ agbanweghị ma ọlị.

Ihe merenụ ọ na-anọchite anya ya: Phase 3 nke Orchestration Canvas tụgharịrị key iri na otu ya n'ime
locale 42 dị n'oge ahụ. Awa ole na ole ka e mesịrị, batch asụsụ EU (#13044) mere ka repo ahụ nwee
locale 51, mana ndị ọhụrụ itoolu ahụ (`el`, `et`, `ga`, `hr`, `lt`, `lv`, `mt`, `sl`, `sr`) enwetaghị
ha ma ọlị. `deepMergeFallback` na-etinye Bekee n'ọnọdụ key na-adịghị, ya mere ụdị ọdịda ahụ bụ UI
