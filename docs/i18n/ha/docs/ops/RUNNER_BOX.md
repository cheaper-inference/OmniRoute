Pool ɗin da ake karɓar baƙuncinsa da kai yana aiki a kan na’urar **.113** (`tomni-proxmox-113`). Tun daga 2026-10-18, kowane
runner na OmniRoute yana da **matsayi guda**, lakabi guda, da kasafin ƙwaƙwalwa/CPU da aka daidaita bisa abin da nauyin aikinsa
ya nuna a ma’auni. Slot ɗin Turbopack yana samun abin da build ɗin ke buƙata; sauran slots kuwa an daidaita su bisa
mafi girman amfani da aka auna. Na’urar tana yin overcommit ne kawai idan build na Turbopack ya zo lokaci guda da build na webpack na Release-Green
da aikin lint; swap na host (16 GB) yana ɗaukar wannan, kuma saboda Builds na pull-request suna gudana a kan hosted runners,
slot ɗin Turbopack ba ya aiki a mafi yawan lokaci:

wani aiki da ke gudana ba. Ana sauya lakabai ta runners API (`gh api -X DELETE repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels/<label>` /
`gh api -X POST repos/<repo>/actions/runners/<id>/labels -f 'labels[]=<label>'`), shi ma kai tsaye.

## Shigar da mai tsaftacewa (sau ɗaya, a kan na’urar)

```bash
scp scripts/ops/runner-janitor.sh root@192.168.0.113:/opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh
ssh root@192.168.0.113 'chmod +x /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh; apt-get install -y lsof'
# cron (root): kowane minti 30, a rubuta log zuwa /var/log/runner-janitor.log
*/30 * * * * MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=8 /opt/omniroute-ops/runner-janitor.sh >> /var/log/runner-janitor.log 2>&1
```

Ana buƙatar `lsof`: mai tsaftacewar yana tabbatar da cewa babu abin da ke amfani da wata hanya ta hanyar ɗaukar hoto guda na fayilolin da suke buɗe
kafin ya cire ta, kuma idan babu wannan kayan aikin ba ya cire komai kuma yana faɗin haka
(exit 1). Da farko a gwada kowane canji da `--dry-run` — yana buga ainihin abin da zai
yi ba tare da taɓa komai ba.

Abin da yake yi a kowane aiki: yana share ragowar namu (`runner-*`, `omniroute-*`,
`next-build*`, `e2e-build.tar.gz`) bayan **awa 3 a kan tmpfs** da awa 24 a kan faifan
`_work/_temp`; yana kashe `next-build` da ya wuce minti 75 (babu wani job da ke ɗaukar wannan tsawon — a
2026-08-27 wani ya yi minti 70 bayan GitHub ya bayyana cewa job ɗinsa ya ɓace); yana goge
checkouts masu awa 48 na runners waɗanda unit ɗinsu ya **tsaya**; yana aika faɗakarwa idan faifa ya kai ≥ 85 %, memory PSI
`full/avg60` ya kai ≥ 10 %, da kuma idan listeners sun fi `MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS` yawa (tare da
rarrabuwar omniroute/other). Exit 1 = ana buƙatar kulawa; karanta log ɗin.

Dokar minti 75 ba ta gano build wanda GitHub ya riga ya daina jiran job ɗinsa ba. Lokacin da
`Runner.Listener` ba zai iya sabunta job ɗinsa ba, `_diag/Runner_*.log` yana nuna
`POST request to …/renewjob timed out after 100 seconds`, API yana nuna job ɗin a matsayin `completed`
(failure) kusan minti goma bayan sabuntawa na ƙarshe da ya yi nasara, kuma `next-build` yana ci gaba da aiki
ba tare da job da ke haɗe da shi ba (2026-10-18: Build na #17066 ya gaza da 19:43 kuma build ɗinsa har yanzu
yana riƙe da 40 GB bayan minti biyar). Tabbatar da ref a cikin `_diag/Worker_*.log` mafi sabo, sannan
a aika SIGTERM ga tsarin build kaɗai ta PID — `next-build`, `npm run build`, `build-next-isolated.mjs` da
workers na `_work`. Kada a taɓa `Runner.Listener`, `RunnerService.js` ko `Runner.Worker`:
worker yana bayar da rahoton cewa an soke job ɗin, kuma listener yana komawa online da kansa.

## Units na runner: KillMode

Tsohon saitin runner na `KillMode=process` yana barin `Runner.Worker → npm → next-build`
suna aiki lokacin da aka tsayar ko aka sake kunna unit — build marar uwa yana ci gaba da cin RAM da
CPU ba tare da job da ke haɗe da shi ba. Kowane unit na OmniRoute yana da drop-in
(`/etc/systemd/system/actions.runner.diegosouzapw-OmniRoute.<name>.service.d/10-killmode.conf`)
mai ɗauke da `KillMode=mixed`: SIGTERM ga listener da farko, sannan SIGKILL ga dukkan cgroup a
`TimeoutStop`. Zai fara aiki a sake kunna unit ɗin na gaba — a sake kunna **runner ɗaya a
kowane lokaci, sai lokacin da ba ya aiki**, tare da binciken rashin aiki da sake kunnawa a cikin command ɗaya.

## Dokokin aiki

- **Ginin Turbopack guda ɗaya a lokaci guda — ana tilasta hakan ta hanyar lakabi.** Duk aikin da ke gudanar da cikakken
  `next build` na Turbopack yana nufin `[self-hosted, omni-build]`, kuma **`omniroute-113-5`** kaɗai ke ɗauke da wannan
  lakabi: `ci.yml` `Build`, `npm-publish.yml` `publish`, `nightly-v4-build`, `preview-artifact`,
  ginin a11y na dare (#16853) da kuma **amd64** na `docker-publish.yml` (webpack a cikin BuildKit —
  Turbopack ya durƙushe a can, gudu na 33253576569). Bangaren arm64 na Docker yana ci gaba da kasancewa a `ubuntu-24.04-arm`.
  amd64 na `docker-publish` yana amfani da rukunin daidaita lokaci na `heavy-build-main` tare da `Build` na `ci.yml`
  (`cancel-in-progress: false`). Dole ne Docker Engine ya kasance a kan `omniroute-113-5`. Gurbin Turbopack
  na biyu ba zai samu sarari a cikin 78 GB ba (2 × 33–37 GB tare da sauran abubuwan da ke kan na’urar); gurbi na biyu yana da aminci ne kawai ga
  gine-ginen webpack.
- **Gine-ginen buƙatar haɗawa suna gudana a kan sabis ɗin da aka tanada (2026-10-18).** `Build` na `ci.yml` yana isa `omni-build` ne kawai don
  `push` da `workflow_dispatch`; kowane `Build` na `pull_request` (daga asalin ma’ajiyar ko fork) da `merge_group`
  yana gudana a kan `ubuntu-latest` tare da webpack. Lokacin da aka tura kowace PR daga asalin ma’ajiyar zuwa gurbin, wani Ginin Turbopack
  ya riƙe shi na minti 29–58, Gine-gine 18 suka jira a layi har zuwa awa 18, kuma Ginin push na layin fitarwar
  kansa ya samu gurbin sau ɗaya ne kawai cikin ayyuka 40. A kan sabis ɗin da aka tanada, wannan Ginin PR ɗin yana ɗaukar minti 8–12 tare da sauran
  ayyukan PR ɗin. Turbopack, mai haɗa kunshin da `npm-publish` ke fitarwa, yana ci gaba da samun gwaji a kan layin fitarwa ta hanyar
  ginin a11y na dare na tsohon reshe (04:41 UTC), ta kowane Ginin push ko dispatch da
  ya isa gurbin, da kuma ta `npm-publish` kanta. `tests/unit/build/heavy-build-queue.test.ts`
  yana tabbatar da sharaɗɗan ƙofar guda huɗu.
- **Kada a taɓa saita girman gurbin Turbopack ƙasa da buƙatarsa da aka auna.** A 2026-10-18 da ƙarfe 18:06 an
  rage gurbin daga 56G/60G zuwa 40G/44G don samar wa `omni-release` sarari. Matakin compile ya tsaya a kan
  `MemoryHigh` tsawon duk gudunsa (memory PSI `full` 30–43 %), kuma `Runner.Listener`, wanda
  ke amfani da cgroup ɗin tare da shi, ya kasa sabunta aikinsa. An maido da 56G/60G na mai shi daga 2026-10-17 a
  ranar nan. Idan aka saita ƙasa da wannan ƙimar, ginin ba ya faduwa; sai dai ya yi ta fama da musayar ƙwaƙwalwa, kuma aikin ya ɓace.
- **Release-Green yana da gurbin kansa (2026-10-18).** Kafin lokacin, duk tabbatarwar Release-Green guda biyu suna gudana
  a kan `omni-build`: a kowane push (~10/awa, an soke 97 cikin gudanawar push 100 a tsakiyar aiki cikin rana ɗaya)
  da cikakkun bincike guda uku na kusan awa 2 a rana (binciken 05:29 ya jira gurbin na awa 6) — an ɗora wa gurbin gini
  guda ɗaya aiki fiye da ƙarfinsa sau da dama, kuma Gine-ginen PR daga asalin ma’ajiyar sun jira na awanni.
  `release-green` da `main-green` yanzu suna nufin `omni-release` (`omniroute-113-6` kaɗai) tare da
  `OMNIROUTE_USE_TURBOPACK=0`, kuma gudu da ya fara ba ya sake samun sokewa saboda sabon push (har yanzu
  gurbin da ke jira yana haɗe cunkoson zuwa guda ɗaya). `tests/unit/build/runner-capacity-routing.test.ts`
  yana tabbatar da hanyar turawa, sauyawar webpack da kuma cewa babu wani abu a kan `omni-release` da ke yin gini da Turbopack.
- **Rukunin ayyuka masu sauƙi: `omni-light`.** `omniroute-113` da `omniroute-113-2` suna gudanar da ayyukan dare waɗanda ke buƙatar
  `next build` na backend kaɗai (~5–6 GB; webpack) da `lint-guard` na `quality.yml`. `omniroute-113`
  yana da `MemoryHigh=4G` har zuwa 2026-10-18 — ginin promptfoo ya shafe minti 54 yana ta dawo da ƙwaƙwalwa
  a can; 8 GB yana kawar da rage saurin.
- **An rage listeners da gangan.** An kashe units guda huɗu na OmniRoute a 2026-08-29 — kowane ƙarin
  listener wani mai amfani ne ga kasafin ƙwaƙwalwar da ke sama. Iyakar janitor ita ce 8 (`MAX_ACTIVE_RUNNERS=8` a cikin cron): yana ƙirga
  kowane `Runner.Listener` da ke kan na’urar, kuma OmniHeuris + OmniMind suna ƙara guda biyu a kan namu guda biyar.
- **Ayyukan Unit da Vitest suna nan a kan masu gudanarwa da aka masauƙanta.** `ci.yml` yana tura shards na unit da aikin Vitest zuwa
  `[self-hosted, omni-ci]` ne kawai idan canjin ma'ajiya `vars.USE_VPS_TEST_RUNNER` ya kasance
  `true`, kuma ko a lokacin sai don gudanarwar `pull_request` daga ma'ajiya ɗaya; forks, `merge_group` da turawa
  suna nan a kan `ubuntu-latest` (`tests/unit/vps-runner-variable-scope.test.ts`). Canjin yana
  `false`. A 2026-10-28 listeners guda uku na `omni-ci` masu cores 4 sun ɗauki 250–270 s don `npm ci`, kuma kowane
  shard na unit ya kai iyakar aiki ta mintuna 30, idan aka kwatanta da ~634 s ga shard da aka masauƙanta, yayin da nauyin na’urar ke
  17–24. An goge listeners ɗin. Kada a sake kunna canjin ba tare da listeners da aka tsara girmansu don
  shard na unit ba.
- **Kada a taɓa tsaftace `/tmp` ko `_work` da hannu yayin da kowane runner ke aiki.** Yin
  dubawa-sannan-sharewa tare da tazara tsakanin matakan biyu shi ne yadda wani aikin Build mai gudana ya rasa
  `_work` ɗinsa a 2026-08-27. Janitor yana yin dubawa da cirewar a mataki ɗaya;
  a bar shi ya yi.
- Tsayar da runner a tsakiyar aiki yana soke aikin (an gani kai tsaye): a yi `systemctl stop` kawai
  lokacin da listener ɗinsa ba shi da ɗan-aikin `Runner.Worker` — kuma a yi hakan cikin umarni guda ɗaya.
- Kada workflows su ajiye artefacts a cikin `/tmp` (RAM ne). A sauke zuwa
  `$RUNNER_TEMP` (a kan faifai, na kowane runner) — artefact ɗin `next-build` mai girman 1.3 GB ya ɗauki minti 27–32
  kafin saukarsa zuwa tmpfs, amma minti 2 kawai don ɗora shi daga faifai.
- VPS na `.15` na homologation ne kawai — ba ya taɓa gudanar da CI runners.

## Layin da aka masauƙanta (masu gudanarwa da GitHub ke masauƙanta)

Ma'ajiyar tana ƙarƙashin ƙungiyar `cheaper-inference`, a kan shirin **Team**: **ayyukan da aka masauƙanta 60
masu gudana a lokaci guda**, waɗanda kowace ma'ajiya a cikin ƙungiyar, kowace duba ta PR,
kowace workflow da turawa ke kunna wa da kuma saitin tsoho na CodeQL suke amfani da su tare. An auna alkaluman da ke ƙasa
a kan shirin **Pro** (ayyukan da aka masauƙanta 40 masu gudana a lokaci guda). An auna a 2026-10-18 yayin
gangamin haɗewa (~haɗewa 10/awa a kan `release/v3.8.52`): **an ƙirƙiri gudanarwar workflow 2,500 cikin
sa'o'i 24**, turawa 44 zuwa reshen cikin sa'o'i 6, **ayyukan da aka masauƙanta 106 suna jiran layi** yayin da dubawar PR ke jira na
sa'o'i kuma aka haɗe PR ba tare da su ba. Workflow tara ne suka kunna a duk lokacin turawa zuwa reshen:

| ga kowace turawa zuwa `release/v*`          | ayyukan da aka masauƙanta | sakamako yayin cunkoson                                                  | tun daga 2026-10-18                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------: | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `ci.yml` (cikakkiyar matrix)                |               har zuwa 30 | turawa ta gaba ta soke shi, 24 cikin 24 a rana                           | ba a fara shi kan turawar `release/**` ba (gudanarwar PR + Release-Green)            |
| `quality.yml`                               |                        ~6 | an soke, 23 cikin 23                                                     | ba a fara shi kan turawar `release/**` ba                                            |

| `Lock released branch`, `OpenSSF Scorecard` |                         2 | gudanarwa ɗaya a layin jira ga kowace turawa                             | gudanarwa ɗaya ga kowane ref (`tests/unit/build/hosted-lane-concurrency.test.ts`)    |
