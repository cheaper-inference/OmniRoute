## npm Amintaccen Bugawa (tsoho tun v3.8.51) — da aka shirya akan buƙata, kai tsaye azaman koma baya

`npm-publish.yml` buga ta** npm Amintaccen Bugawa (OIDC)** ta tsohuwa: aikin `stage-npm` (github-hosted) yana musanya alamar GitHub's id-token don ɗan gajeren lokacin npm don wannan gudu — babu alamar npm mai tsayi a cikin sirrin ma'ajiyar, babu saurin 2FA, tabbatarwa a haɗe. Wato ketare takunkumin npm yanzu da alamun da suka tsallake 2FA ke yin ritaya; yana mayar da cikakken atomatik kwarara aikin yana da har zuwa v3.8.48 yayin da yake kiyaye garantin WS1.3 (alamar da aka leke ba za ta iya buga shi kaɗai ba — babu alama).

**Saitin lokaci ɗaya (mai shi):** npmjs.com → kunshin `omniroute` → Saitunan → _Trusted Publisher_ → GitHub: mai shi `diegosouzapw`, repo `OmniRoute`, aikin aiki `npm-publish.yml` (muhalli: babu). Har sai wannan ya wanzu, matakin atomatik ya kasa tare da `ENEEDAUTH`: sake aikawa da `publish_mode=staged` (a ƙasa) ko `direct`.

### Bugawa mai tsari (kan buƙata — `publish_mode=staged`)

Gudun aiki na npm-buga ba ya bugawa kai tsaye: yana yin takalmin ƙwallon ƙwallon (`check:pack-boot`) sannan yana gudanar da `npm stage publish` — ainihin bytes suna fakin akan wurin rajista,** ba za a iya shigarwa ba** har sai mai shi ya yarda. Ƙofar mutum 2FA ta koma AFTER hujja, ba a gabanta ba.

**Gudun mai shi bayan tafiyar aikin ya tafi kore:**

1. `npm stage list omniroute` — nemo id ɗin mataki (kuma an buga shi a cikin taƙaitaccen aikin).
2. Tabbatar da tsararrun bytes (an shawarta): `npm stage download <id>`, sannan shigar da wasan kwal ɗin da aka zazzage cikin prefix na ɗan lokaci sannan a buga shi (`npm run check:pack-boot` yana sarrafa fakiti ɗaya → shigar da hukuncin boot ɗin → a cikin CI).
3. `npm stage approve <id>` — 2FA ta IS buga. `npm stage reject <id>` ya watsar.
4. Tashar yanar-gizon bayan-buga: mai tabbatarwa bayan bugu (WS1.4 na shirin v3.8.49) yana shigar da sigar da aka buga daga wurin rajistar jama'a a cikin akwati mai tsafta kuma ta sanya shi.

**Komawar gaggawa:** `workflow_dispatch` tare da `publish_mode=direct` yana dawo da gadon `npm publish` nan da nan (amfani kawai idan bayyana kansa yayi kuskure; rubuta dalilin).

**Ƙarfafa lokaci ɗaya (mai shi, npmjs.com):** saita Amintaccen Mawallafin don `omniroute` a yanayin mataki-kawai don haka alamar da aka ɗora ta daɗe ba za ta iya `npm publish` kai tsaye daga ko'ina ba — CI zai iya kawai mataki; kawai 2FA na mai shi ya saki.

**Littafin wasan kwaikwayo mai karye (ba a canzawa):** `npm deprecate omniroute@<bad> "<reason> — use <fixed>"` a matsayin tsoho reflex (mintuna, mai juyawa); `npm unpublish` kawai a cikin taga 72h/no-dependents kuma bai taɓa zama farkon motsi ba. Docker: kar a sake rubuta tambarin sigar — rollback yana sake nuna `latest` zuwa mafi kyawu na ƙarshe.

**Docker Hub `latest` (da ake buƙata akan kowane tsayayye SemVer buga):** aikin `docker-publish` dole ne ya sanya alama** duka biyu** `X.Y.Z` kuma, lokacin da `should-promote-latest.sh` ya yarda wannan shine mafi girman tsayayyen SemVer, `:latest` tare da** iri guda** . Bayan aikin: Hub `latest` narkewa yayi daidai da sabon narkar SemVer kuma `last_updated` ya motsa. Kar a bar `:latest` akan ginin da aka dade yayin sakin bayanan suna magana game da gyare-gyare waɗanda kawai ke kan git. Rubuta saurin farawa amfani da `:latest`; GitOps ya kamata ya ci gaba da pinning `X.Y.Z`. Duba [tashoshin sakin Docker](../guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#release-channels) da #10317.

**ma'ajiyar kungiyar Docker Hub (`cheaperinference/omniroute`):** Docker Hub ba zai iya motsa wurin ajiya tsakanin wuraren suna ba, don haka `diegosouzapw/omniroute` yana adana tarihin ja da kowane saki, da aikin `mirror-org-dockerhub` na `docker-publish` kowace sigar da aka fitar (da `:latest` lokacin da aka inganta) daga `ghcr.io/cheaper-inference/omniroute` zuwa ma'ajiyar kungiya. Yana buƙatar asirin `DOCKERHUB_ORG_USERNAME` / `DOCKERHUB_ORG_TOKEN` ; ba tare da su ba aikin yana tsayawa kore tare da "Docker Hub org madubi ya tsallake" gargadi kuma sauran rajista suna bugawa akai-akai. Don buga sigar da aka saki a can daga baya, gudu** Ayyukan → Mirror Docker hoto zuwa Docker Hub org → Gudun aikin aiki** tare da `version=X.Y.Z` (da `promote_latest` don mafi girman juzu'i): yana kwafin hotunan da ke akwai, babu sake ginawa.

## Hanyar Gaggawa ta Hotfix (lakabi `hotfix`)

PR mai lakabin `hotfix` yana tsallake babban tsarin gwaje-gwajen CI (E2E mai kashi 9, coverage ratchet,
quality-gate, quality-extended) kuma yana riƙe da matakan tantancewa masu sauri kuma masu ƙarfi: build,
sassan unit, integration, vitest, lint/typecheck, docs-sync, `check:pack-artifact`
da gwajin fara aiki na tarball (`check:pack-boot`). Manufa: samun koren sakamako cikin ≤15min maimakon ~33min.

**Ka'idar shiga — dole ne duka huɗun su cika (an tsara bisa hanyoyin gaggawa na Chromium/VS Code/Node):**

1. **Muniwar matsala**: production ya lalace — artifact da aka wallafa yana faɗuwa yayin fara aiki / gyaran
   tsaro / matsalar ta shafi kowane mai amfani da release ɗin. "Mai muhimmanci" ba yana nufin "ya lalace" ba.
2. **Iko**: mai repository ne kawai ke sanya lakabin `hotfix`. Lakabin SHI NE
   amincewar — kada a taɓa sanya shi da kai a campaign PR.
3. **Shaida**: jikin PR yana haɗa hanyar zuwa cikakken babban gwajin baya da ya yi kore (suite ɗin da
   jobs ɗin da aka tsallake za su sake tabbatarwa) tare da gwajin gyaran da da farko ya gaza sannan ya yi nasara.
4. **Iyaka**: cherry-pick kawai — mafi ƙarancin gyara, babu refactors, babu ƙarin canje-canje marasa alaƙa.

Za a sake tabbatar da ɓangaren coverage/ratchet da aka tsallake a cikakken gwaji na gaba a
reshe na release (ci gaba da tabbatar da release mai kore) — hanyar tana tsallake JIRA kawai, ba tabbatarwa ba.
Canje-canjen da suka shafi tests kawai (duk fayiloli suna ƙarƙashin `tests/`, babu ko ɗaya ƙarƙashin `tests/e2e/`) suna tsallake tsarin E2E
ta atomatik, ba tare da wani lakabi ba.

| `dist/`   | Za'a iya jigilar kaya npm bundle — wanda `assembleStandalone` ya harhada | A'a (wanda aka gagara) |

> **Bayanin mai aiki:** littafin jagorar hoton VPS mai nisa ya rage `/usr/lib/node_modules/omniroute/app/`. Sai kawai** in-repo** gina fitarwa motsi (`app/` → `dist/`). Ƙirƙirar ƙwarewar rsync `dist/` abun ciki cikin `app/` dir — mai nisa babu canje-canjen hanyar VPS da ake buƙata.

**Gudun gini guda ɗaya:**

```
npm run build:release
  └─ rm -rf .build dist          (clean)
  └─ next build → .build/next/   (intermediates)
  └─ assembleStandalone          (copies standalone + static + public + natives → dist/)
  └─ writes dist/BUILD_SHA       (HEAD sentinel)
```

Shin NOT gudanar da `npm run build` yana biye da `npm run build:cli` daban don tura — amfani da `npm run build:release` wanda ke sake gina + sentinel mai tsabta a cikin umarni ɗaya.
