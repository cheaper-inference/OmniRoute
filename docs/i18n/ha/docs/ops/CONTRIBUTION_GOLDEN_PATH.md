Yi amfani da wannan jagorar don zaɓar mafi ƙanƙantar amintaccen zagayen haɓakawa don pull request. Ba ya
maye gurbin takardun gine-gine da tsaro na takamaiman ɓangare da aka haɗa a ƙasa; yana haɗa kowane nau'in
canji da aka saba yi da kwangilolinsa, takamaiman bincike, da ɗaukar nauyin CI.

## Hanyar da kowane canji ke bi

1. **Zaɓi reshen tushe kafin gyarawa.** Nemo reshen `release/v*` mafi girma da ke aiki sannan ka ƙirƙiri reshe daga
   ƙarshensa. Nufi wannan reshen, ba `main` ba. Idan an dakatar da canje-canje saboda sakin siga, kada ka nufi reshen da aka
   dakatar; yi amfani da zagayen aiki na gaba da aka bayyana a
   [Tsarin Reshe da Saki](BRANCHING_MODEL.md).
2. **Ambaci kwangilolin.** Gano kowane katalog, schema, abin da aka samar, API na jama'a, ko mahadar
   mai amfani da canjin ya shafa. Teburin da ke ƙasa yana ba da mafi ƙarancin saitin farawa.
3. **Rubuta ko sabunta takamaiman gwaje-gwaje.** Canje-canjen samarwa a `src/`, `open-sse/`, `electron/`, ko
   `bin/` suna buƙatar gwaji mai sarrafa kansa a cikin PR ɗin. Gudanar da mafi ƙanƙantar fayilolin gwaji da ke tabbatar da
   halayen, sannan ka gudanar da takamaiman matakan binciken da aka lissafa.
4. **Bar CI ya gudanar da faffadan matrix.** Cikakkun sassan gwajin unit, Vitest, matakin ɗaukar gwaji, da
   ginin samarwa suna gudana a kan PR. Gudanar da faffadan rukunin gwaji a gida kawai idan gazawar takamaiman gwaji ta nuna
   tasiri mai faɗi ko kuma idan canjin ya ratsa ƙananan-tsaruka da dama.
5. **Daidaita kafin bita.** Ɗauko reshen tushe mai aiki, bincika sabbin commits ɗinsa da bambancin naka da
   shi, sannan ka yi rebase ko merge na reshen tushe bisa tsarin aikin mai ba da gudummawa. Warware rikice-rikicen fayilolin da aka
   samar da na katalog daga tushensu, sake samar da su, sake gudanar da takamaiman zagayen, sannan ka tabbatar cewa
   PR har yanzu yana nufin reshen saki mai aiki.
6. **Rubuta shaidu.** A cikin samfurin PR, jera umarnin da aka gudanar, kowane fayil ɗin gwaji da aka ƙara ko aka canja,
   ƙaura ko tutocin fasali, da duk wani ingantawa na CI kaɗai da har yanzu ake jira.

## Hanyoyi mafi dacewa bisa nau'in canji

Umarnin da ke ƙasa su ne mafi ƙarancin takamaiman bincike, ba izinin tsallake gwajin da ke rufe
halayen da ka canja kai tsaye ba.

### Mai samarwa

**Kwangiloli**

- Ma'anar mai samarwa a `src/shared/constants/providers/` da haɗuwarta a
  `src/shared/constants/providers.ts`.
- Samfura da iyawa a `open-sse/config/providerRegistry.ts` ko fayilolin rajistar da aka ware daga gare shi.
- Zaɓin executor/translator, saitin OAuth ko API-key, kadarorin dashboard, da takardar
  bayanin mai samarwa da aka samar idan ya dace.
- Shaidun shiga na jama'a dole ne su yi amfani da `resolvePublicCred()`; amsoshin kuskure dole ne su yi amfani da mataimakan
  kuskure da aka tsabtace na gama-gari. Duba `docs/security/PUBLIC_CREDS.md` (git; ba a haɗa shi cikin `/docs` ba) da
  [Tsabtace Kuskure](../security/ERROR_SANITIZATION.md).

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
npm run check:provider-consistency
npm run check:provider-assets
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/provider-translate-path-golden.test.ts
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/<provider-or-executor>.test.ts
npm run gen:provider-reference   # idan katalog ya canja; yi commit na bambancin da aka samar
npm run lint
```

Haka kuma gwada kowane rukunin buƙata da abin ya shafa: chat, Responses, hotuna, embeddings, sauti, ko bidiyo.
Binciki bambance-bambancen katalog da golden da aka samar a matsayin canje-canjen kwangila; kada ka karɓe su ba tare da dubawa ba.

### Jagorantar hanya

**Kwangiloli**

- Ƙimomin dabarun jama'a da metadata na UI a `src/shared/constants/routingStrategies.ts`.
- Aikawa da tsara jeri a ƙarƙashin `open-sse/services/combo.ts` da `open-sse/services/combo/`.
- Combo schemas, adanawa, yanayin juriya, iyawar samfuri, da abubuwan sarrafawa na API/UI.
- [Injin Auto-Combo](../routing/AUTO-COMBO.md) da takardun juriya idan hali ya canja.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/combo-<behavior>.test.ts
npm run test:combo:matrix        # canje-canjen dabara ko aikawa
npm run check:known-symbols      # canje-canjen rajistar dabara
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da gwaje-gwajen mocked-upstream masu tabbataccen sakamako a gida. Gwaje-gwajen combo na kai-tsaye suna buƙatar shaidun shiga kuma
na hannu ne, ba madadin CI ba.

### UI / UX

**Kwangiloli**

- Iyakar route/page na Next.js da shared component a ƙarƙashin `src/app/` da
  `src/shared/components/`.
- Tsarukan amsar API, yanayin lodawa/wofi/kuskure, halayen madannai da screen-reader,
  tsarin shimfiɗa mai daidaitawa, jigo, da faɗaɗa locale.
- Rubutun tushen UI na Turanci a `src/i18n/messages/en.json`; kada a saka sabon rubutun da mai amfani zai gani kai tsaye a cikin lamba.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
node --import tsx --test tests/unit/dashboard/<feature>.test.ts
npx vitest run --config vitest.config.ts tests/unit/ui/<component>.test.tsx
npm run check:dashboard-typecheck
npm run lint
```

Gudanar da manhajar don canje-canjen mu'amala ko gani sannan ka duba duka kunkuntar da faffadan faɗin allo. CI yana gudanar da
ginin samarwa da faffadan rukunin gwaje-gwaje; har yanzu halayen gani suna buƙatar takamaiman gwajin component, Playwright,
ko rubutaccen binciken hannu da ya dace da canjin.

### i18n

**Kwangiloli**

- `src/i18n/messages/en.json` shi ne tushen UI; `config/i18n.json` shi ne tushen locale.
- Katalog na CLI suna zama daban a ƙarƙashin `bin/cli/locales/`.
- Kiyaye ICU placeholders da tags daidai. Kada a fassara sunayen samfur/mai samarwa/model,
  sunayen protocol da header, umarni, masu ganowa na code/JSON, URLs, environment variables, ko
  keɓaɓɓun kalmomi kamar `OmniRoute`, `OAuth`, `MCP`, da `A2A`. Jerin tushen na yanzu yana
  `scripts/i18n/glossary/protected-terms.json`.

**Takamaiman zagaye**

```bash
npm run i18n:sync-ui:dry
npm run i18n:check-ui-coverage
npm run i18n:check-value-drift
npm run i18n:check-glossary
npm run check:cli-i18n          # idan rubutun/katalog na CLI suka canja
npm run lint
```

Wannan jagora ne ga tsarin da ake da shi, ba gayyata don faɗaɗa kayan aikinsa ko tsarin key ba.
Kiyaye gyare-gyaren i18n su zama ƙanana kuma takamaimai yayin da ake tsara tsarin da zai maye gurbinsa. Kada a gudanar da umarnin fassara
da ke kiran ayyukan waje sai idan aikin ya buƙaci fassarorin da aka samar a sarari kuma
ka binciki bambancin da aka samu.

### CLI

**Kwangiloli**

- Umarnin jama'a da tutoci a cikin `bin/cli/`, umarnin API da aka samar, lambobin fita, stdout/stderr da
  siffofin fitarwa na JSON, halayen saituna/muhalli, da fayilolin da aka kunshe.
- Rubutun da masu amfani ke gani a CLI dole ne su yi amfani da tsarin i18n na CLI kuma a daidaita kundin `en`/`pt-BR`.
- A kiyaye Node a matsayin runtime da ake tallafawa da kuma yarjejeniyar binary da aka wallafa.

**Zagayen da aka mayar da hankali a kai**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/cli/<command>.test.ts
npm run check:cli-i18n
npm run build:cli             # sauye-sauyen CLI da aka samar/aka haɗa
npm run check:pack-policy     # sauye-sauyen abin da package ke fallasawa
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da ainihin umarnin a cikin kundin bayanai na wucin gadi idan hali ya dogara da parsing, fayiloli, ko matsayin
fita. CI yana aiwatar da ƙarin binciken artifact na package da na tsarin ecosystem.

### Rumbun bayanai

**Yarjejeniyoyi**

- Module na domain a ƙarƙashin `src/lib/db/`; a shigo da takamaiman module kai tsaye (an cire tsohon matakin sake-fitarwa na `localDb.ts`).
- Migrations na SQL masu lambobi kuma masu idempotent a ƙarƙashin `src/lib/db/migrations/`, amincin transaction, halayen
  haɓakawa, indexes, da duk wani caller da schema ya shafa.
- Routes da handlers ba sa taɓa aiwatar da raw SQL kai tsaye.

**Zagayen da aka mayar da hankali a kai**

```bash
npm run check:migration-numbering
npm run check:db-rules
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/db/<domain>.test.ts
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/db/migration-<number>.test.ts
npm run lint
```

Gwada sabon rumbun bayanai da kuma haɓakawa daga schema na baya yayin ƙara migration. Gwaje-gwajen rumbun bayanai
dole ne su rufe handles kuma su kira `resetDbInstance()` yayin tsaftacewa. Gudanar da `npm run test:bun:db` kawai idan
hanyar adapter ta Bun mai best-effort ta sauya; Node ne har yanzu madogara ta hukuma.

### Ginawa / turawa

**Yarjejeniyoyi**

- Manifests/lockfile na root da workspace, `scripts/build/`, haɗa standalone na Next.js, abubuwan da ke cikin package na `dist/`,
  metadata na dandamalin Electron, workflows na CI, da sentinels na turawa.
- Ranges na Node da ake tallafawa da amfani da Bun da aka amince da shi a cikin `CLAUDE.md` dole ne su ci gaba da kasancewa yadda suke.
- Kada a bibiyi artifacts na gini; manufofin dependencies, lasisi, workflow, da package suna aiki.

**Zagayen da aka mayar da hankali a kai**

```bash
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/unit/build/<behavior>.test.ts
npm run check:build-scope
npm run check:lockfile         # sauye-sauyen dependency ko lockfile
npm run check:pack-policy      # sauye-sauyen abin da package da aka wallafa ke fallasawa
npm run lint
```

Yi amfani da `npm run build` a gida kawai idan sauyin ya shafi compilation, haɗa standalone, assets,
ko bundling na runtime. Yi amfani da `npm run build:release` kawai don tabbatar da release/turawa. Ginin CI shi ne
sigina na ƙarshe na dandamali daban-daban; sauye-sauyen Electron na takamaiman dandamali suna buƙatar ginin da ya dace da aka mayar da hankali a kai
ko shaidar smoke.

## Skriptocin masu haɓakawa don na’ura da ake rabawa

Masu taimako guda biyu na `scripts/dev/` suna tsara zagayen aikin gida a devbox inda wasu zaman aiki ke gudana:

```bash
scripts/dev/typecheck-touched.sh --base release/vX.Y.Z   # fayiloli daga merge-base(HEAD, base)..HEAD
scripts/dev/typecheck-touched.sh src/a.ts tests/unit/b.test.ts   # waɗannan fayilolin kaɗai
scripts/dev/new-worktree.sh fix/1234-slug release/vX.Y.Z  # keɓantaccen worktree, mai aminci don aiki a layi ɗaya
```

- `typecheck-touched.sh` yana yin typecheck ne kawai ga fayilolin `.ts/.tsx` da aka taɓa ta hanyar
  `tsconfig.touched.tmp.json` na wucin gadi (`extends ./tsconfig.json`, `types: ["node"]`, `noEmit`,
  `incremental: false`) ƙarƙashin heap na 6 GB, don haka yana maye gurbin `tsc -p tsconfig.json`
  na duk aikin wanda ke fuskantar OOM a na’ura mai nauyin aiki. Yana buga kurakuran da ke cikin fayilolin da aka taɓa tare da layin taƙaitawa guda ɗaya, sannan ya fita da
  `1` idan akwai kurakurai a fayilolin da aka taɓa, `2` idan kurakuran suna cikin fayilolin da aka shigo da su ne kawai ko kuma tsc ya mutu
  ba tare da bayanan bincike ba (karanta log ɗin da ya ambata ƙarƙashin `_artifacts/typecheck-touched/`), `0` idan
  komai yana da tsabta. Ana cire tsconfig na wucin gadi koyaushe; kada a taɓa yin commit ɗinsa.
- `new-worktree.sh` yana jera ayyukan da ke gudana lokaci guda a `<git-common-dir>/omniroute-new-worktree.lock`
  (yana jira har zuwa 600 s ta tsohuwa, tare da saƙo da ake iya gani; taken skript ɗin yana bayyana
  yadda ake sauya wannan) kuma yana
  mayar da reshen da ya ƙirƙira idan `git worktree add` ya gaza kafin worktree ya wanzu, don haka sake gwadawa
  baya buƙatar `git branch -D` da hannu. `node_modules` da aka haɗa da hard-link da kwafin `.husky/_` ba su canza ba.

## Zagayen candidate na gida

Gwaje-gwajen source ba za su iya tabbatar da cewa artifact ɗin da aka shirya a matsayin package zai fara aiki ba: jerin packaging, dependencies da aka rage,
da native binaries suna gaza ne kawai bayan an shigar da tarball kuma an fara shi. `npm run dev:candidate`
(`scripts/dev/candidate.mjs`) shi ne ɓangaren gida na tsarin gina-sau-ɗaya / tantancewa / ciyarwa-gaba daga
RFC #8084: yana gina candidate guda ɗaya, yana tantance ainihin wannan artifact, yana ciyar da shi gaba ta hanyar sauya
sunayen directory, kuma yana mayar da shi baya idan slot ɗin da aka ciyar gaba ya gaza health check.

```bash
npm run dev:candidate -- run --dry-run --json  # buga shirin, kada a canza komai
npm run dev:candidate -- run                   # ginawa + tantancewa + ciyarwa gaba, mayarwa baya ta atomatik
npm run dev:candidate -- build                 # npm pack + shigarwa cikin _artifacts/candidate/<id>/
npm run dev:candidate -- validate --id <id>    # farawa a port da babu mai amfani da shi, /api/health + /v1/models
npm run dev:candidate -- promote --id <id>     # <id> → current, current → previous
npm run dev:candidate -- rollback              # musanya current da previous
npm run dev:candidate -- run --from-tarball <file.tgz>  # sake amfani da tarball da aka gina a wani wuri (CI)
```

- **Gina sau ɗaya.** `build` yana shirya current tree a matsayin package (yana buƙatar `dist/server.js`, don haka fara gudanar da
  `npm run build:release`) ko kuma yana kwafe `--from-tarball`, sannan ya shigar da tarball cikin
  keɓantaccen npm prefix, saboda tarball ba ya ɗauke da `node_modules`. id ɗin shi ne gajeren sha na `HEAD`
  (`-dirty` idan tree ɗin yana da canje-canje na gida) ko `tgz-<sha256>` don tarball. Ana sake amfani da id mai tsabta wanda
  aka riga aka gina maimakon sake gina shi; miƙa `--force` don sake gina shi.
- **Tantance package, ba source ba.** `validate` yana fara CLI da aka shigar
  (`serve --port <free port>`) tare da sabon `DATA_DIR=<candidate>/data`, sirrika na bogi, yayin da aka cire
  `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`, `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY` da `INITIAL_PASSWORD` na mai gudanarwa daga
  environment, don haka yana duba keyless loopback posture na sabuwar shigarwa. Yana jira
  `GET /api/health` ya mayar da 200, yana buƙatar `GET /v1/models` ya mayar da 200, yana dakatar da process group,
  sannan yana adana hukuncin a `validation.json` tare da hash na tarball.
- **Ciyar da artifact ɗin nan ɗin gaba.** `promote` yana ƙin candidate da ba shi da tantancewa mai nasara ga
  hash na current tarball ɗinsa. Active slot na tsohuwa shi ne `_artifacts/candidate/current`; `--target <dir>`
  yana zaɓar wani directory a filesystem ɗin nan ɗin, kuma previous slot ɗinsa shi ne `<dir>.previous`.
  Kowane rename atomic ne, kuma gazawa a tsakiyar aiki tana juyar da rename ɗin da aka riga aka gudanar.
- **Mayar baya.** `run` yana sake tantance slot ɗin da aka ciyar gaba sannan ya sake musanya `current` da `previous` idan
  wannan dubawar ta gaza. Ba a taɓa ciyar da candidate da ya gaza tantancewar farko gaba ba.

Ana rubuta komai ƙarƙashin `_artifacts/candidate/` da git ke yin watsi da shi; ba a taɓa shigarwar OmniRoute
da ke akwai da data directory ɗinta ba. CLI da aka ciyar gaba shi ne
`_artifacts/candidate/current/prefix/bin/omniroute`; fara shi da `DATA_DIR` naka lokacin da kake amfani
da shi. Exit codes: `0` nasara, `1` tantancewa ko ciyarwa gaba ta gaza, `2` kuskuren amfani ko build ya ɓace.

## Zagayen gida idan aka kwatanta da CI

| Gudanar a gida don kowane gyara                                                              | CI yana samar da sigina mai faɗi                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gwaje-gwajen halayya kai tsaye da matakan tantance rukuni da ke sama                         | Cikakken rukunin gwaje-gwajen naúra da aka rarraba da gwaje-gwajen jere      |
| `npm run lint`                                                                               | Rukunin gwaje-gwajen Vitest da matakan ɗaga inganci/ɗaukar gwaji             |
| Duba nau'i ko ginawa kawai idan kwangilar da abin ya shafa ta buƙaci hakan                   | Gina na samarwa, tsaro, takardu, dogaro, da matakan manufofin PR             |
| Mu'amala ta hannu/dubawa kai tsaye kawai idan sarrafa-kai ba zai iya tabbatar da halayyar ba | Haɗakar ayyuka daban-daban da gwaje-gwajen dandamali da tsarin aiki ya saita |

Nasarar zagaye da aka mayar da hankali a kai hujja ce game da kwangilar da aka canza, ba tabbaci ba ne cewa gwaje-gwajen CI marasa alaƙa
za su yi nasara. Haka kuma, kada a sa kowane gyaran gida ya jira cikakken jadawalin gwaje-gwajen ma'ajiyar.

## Jerin dubawa don daidaitawa

Kafin neman bita:

- Tabbatar cewa tushen PR har yanzu shi ne reshen `release/v*` mafi girma da ke aiki.
- Ɗauko wannan tushe sannan ka duba commits da aka haɗa tun bayan da ka ƙirƙiri reshenka.
- Duba `git diff <active-base>...HEAD` don gano sauye-sauyen bazata ko waɗanda aka samar ta atomatik.
- Warware rikice-rikicen kundin bayanai da takardun da aka samar ta hanyar sabunta tushe da sake samar da sakamako.
- Sake gudanar da kowane gwaji/matakin tantancewa da aka mayar da hankali a kai wanda aka jera a bayanin PR bayan daidaitawa.
- Kada ka taɓa raunana sharuɗɗan tabbatarwa ko cire gwaje-gwajen da ake buƙata kawai don dacewa da tushen da ya motsa.

Don ƙa'idodin daskarewar fitarwa da sake nufi, yi amfani da
[Tsarin Reshe da Fitarwa](BRANCHING_MODEL.md). Don cikakken jerin CI, yi amfani da
[Manunin Matakan Inganci](../architecture/QUALITY_GATES.md).
