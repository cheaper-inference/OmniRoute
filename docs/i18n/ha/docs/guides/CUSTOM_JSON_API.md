Kullin JSON API na al'ada yana haɗa ƙarshen kasuwancin JSON wanda baya magana da OpenAI ko Anthropic yarjejeniya. OmniRoute ya cika samfurin buƙatun JSON, aika shi zuwa cikakkiyar maƙasudin URL, karanta amsa daga hanyar amsawa kuma ya mayar da ita ga abokin ciniki a tsarin na abokin ciniki (Kammala Taɗi, Amsoshi, Saƙonni, ...).

Kumburi ne mai jituwa na OpenAI tare da `apiType: "custom-json"`; id dinsa yana da siffar `openai-compatible-custom-json-<suffix>`. Ba a samu fom ɗin dashboard ba tukuna. Ƙirƙiri kuma gyara waɗannan nodes ta hanyar mai bada-node API da ke ƙasa.

## Ƙirƙiri kumburi

`POST /api/provider-nodes` (Management auth):

```json
{
  "type": "openai-compatible",
  "apiType": "custom-json",
  "name": "Business API",
  "prefix": "biz",
  "baseUrl": "https://api.example.com/v1/answer",
  "customHeaders": { "X-Tenant": "demo" },
  "customJson": {
    "method": "POST",
    "authType": "api-key",
    "authHeader": "X-Service-Key",
    "requestTemplate": {
      "project_id": "demo-project",
      "question": "{{prompt}}",
      "history": "{{messages}}"
    },
    "responsePath": "data.answer"
  }
}
```

- `baseUrl` ni** cikakke** karshen URL. Babu wani abu da aka haɗa (ba `/chat/completions`, babu `/models`) kuma, ba kamar sauran nodes masu jituwa na OpenAI ba, babu tsoho URL. Dole ne ya zama `http(s)`, ba tare da shigar da takaddun shaida ba ko `#fragment`, kuma yana wuce daidaitaccen gadin URL mai fita kamar kowane kumburin mai bayarwa, duka lokacin da aka ajiye kumburi kuma akan kowace buƙata.
- Kullin da ke aika maɓallin haɗin (`authType` `bearer` ko `api-key`, gami da tsoho) dole ne yayi amfani da `https://`. Ana karɓar `http://` na fili kawai don madauki na madauki (`localhost`, `127.0.0.0/8`, `::1`) ko tare da `authType: "none"`, wanda ba ya aika maɓalli. Adireshin LAN masu zaman kansu (`10.x`, `172.16-31.x`, `192.168.x`) ba a keɓance su ba: duk wanda ke wannan hanyar sadarwar zai iya karanta maɓallin. Ajiye irin wannan kumburin yana gazawa tare da `400`, kuma kumburin da aka adana a baya yana kasa rufewa akan kowace buƙatu da saƙo iri ɗaya, kafin a aika kowace buƙata. Don maɓalli mai maɓalli akan LAN, sanya shi a bayan TLS (misali wakili na baya).
- an inganta `customJson` da Zod; an ƙi maɓallan da ba a san su ba. Kowane filin na zaɓi ne:

| Filin             | Darajoji                                        | Tsohuwar                                               |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `method`          | `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH`                          | `POST`                                                 |
| `authType`        | `bearer`, `api-key`, `none`                     | `bearer`                                               |
| `authHeader`      | sunan kai da ake amfani dashi lokacin `api-key` | `X-API-Key`                                            |
| `requestTemplate` | JSON (≤ 64 KB, zurfafa ≤ 32)                    | `{ "model": "{{model}}", "messages": "{{messages}}" }` |
| `responsePath`    | hanyar filin, ko `$` ga dukkan jiki             | `choices[0].message.content`                           |

- Sirrin yana tsayawa a cikin maɓallin API na haɗin, ba a cikin samfuri ba. `bearer` aika `Authorization: Bearer <key>`; `api-key` aika `<authHeader>: <key>`. Babban taken auth ba zai iya zama `Host`, `Content-Type`, `Content-Length`, `Connection`, `Transfer-Encoding`, `Authorization`, `Cookie` ko shugaban hop-by-hop. Tare da `none`, haɗi yana buƙatar maɓalli.
- Ƙarin masu kai tsaye suna amfani da `customHeaders` na kulli na yau da kullun (tabbaci ɗaya da kowane kulli mai jituwa; ba a yarda da manyan kanun labarai a wurin ba).

`PUT /api/provider-nodes/<id>` tana karɓar `customJson` iri ɗaya kuma ta sake rubuta `baseUrl` da saitin akan kowane haɗin da ke akwai na kumburi. Yin watsi da `customJson` yana kiyaye tsarin da aka adana; canza `apiType` zuwa `chat` ko `responses` yana cire shi daga kumburi da haɗin gwiwa.

Sannan ƙara haɗi zuwa kumburi kamar yadda aka saba (`POST /api/providers` tare da node id) kuma nemi `<prefix>/<model>`, misali `biz/assistant-v1`. Ƙarshen ƙarshen ba ya fallasa babu jerin samfuri, don haka ƙara ƙirar ƙira da hannu.

## Neman samfuri

Samfura a sarari JSON. Ƙimar kirtani na iya ƙunsar alamomin `{{placeholder}}` ; Babu wani abu da aka kimanta ko aiwatar da shi, kuma ba a taɓa canza maɓallan abu ba.

| Mai riƙe wuri        | Daraja                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `{{prompt}}`         | Rubutun sabon saƙon mai amfani (ɓangarorin rubutu waɗanda aka haɗa tare da sabbin layi) |
| `{{messages}}`       | Tattaunawar a tsarin taɗi na OpenAI                                                     |
| `{{model}}`          | Id ɗin samfurin da aka nema, ba tare da prefix ɗin mai bayarwa ba                       |
| `{{stream}}`         | Koyaushe `false` — ƙarshen ƙarshen yana amsawa tare da takaddar JSON ɗaya               |
| `{{request.<path>}}` | Filin buƙatun fassarar (tsarin taɗi OpenAI ).                                           |

- Ƙimar da ta kasance daidai mai wuri ɗaya tana kiyaye nau'in JSON (`"{{messages}}"` ya zama tsararru); an saka mai sanya wuri a cikin dogon igiya azaman rubutu.
- Mai riƙe da wuri wanda bai yanke komai ba ya gaza buƙatar buƙatun tare da HTTP 400 maimakon aika kaya mara cika.
- Hanyoyi suna amfani da alamar digo da fihirisar lambobi (`data.items[0].text`). `__proto__`, `prototype` da `constructor` an ƙi su a maɓalli da hanyoyi.

## Martani

Ƙimar a `responsePath` ta zama amsa mataimaki: igiyoyi suna wucewa, wasu ƙima JSON ana jera su. Filin da ya ɓace ko `null`, ko jikin da ba JSON ba, yana dawo da HTTP 502. Ana wuce ta cikin matsayi na kuskure. Ga abokan ciniki masu yawo OmniRoute yana jira gabaɗayan martanin JSON sannan ya fitar da shi azaman abun ciki guda ɗaya tare da ƙarewa; wannan ba alamar yawo bane. Ba a tsara kiran kayan aiki da filayen amfani ba.

## Ba a sake kunnawa ba

Buƙatar kasuwanci na iya samun illolin ko da ta gaza. OmniRoute don haka aika sau ɗaya:

- babu sake gwadawa na sufuri kuma babu turawa mai zuwa;
- babu sake gwadawa akan wani haɗin (account fallback) — an dawo da gazawar kamar yadda yake kuma haɗin ba a sanyaya ba;
- babu koma baya ga maƙasudin haɗin gwiwa na gaba, koda don amsa fanko ko gazawa.

Rarraba buƙatun abokin ciniki, da fa'idodin haɗakarwa waɗanda ke kiran ƙira da yawa a sarari, har yanzu suna kiran ƙarshen abin da kansa.

## Gwajin kumburi

Ajiye haɗi, haɗin** Gwaji** maɓalli da bayanan kula da lafiyar baya ba su taɓa kiran ƙarshen ƙarshen ba: suna tabbatar da saitin da aka adana kawai kuma suna ba da rahoton haɗin kamar ba a iya tabbatarwa ba.

`POST /api/provider-nodes/validate` tare da `"apiType": "custom-json"` da filayen node (`baseUrl`, `apiKey`, `customJson`, `customHeaders` na zaɓi da `modelId`) suna aika ainihin buƙatun guda ɗaya, tare da `ping` azaman sabon rubutun mai amfani. Yana amsa `{ "valid": true }` ko matsayi na sama (ba zai taɓa jikin sama ba).

## Adana

Ana adana saitin a cikin `provider_nodes.custom_json_config_json` ( ƙaura `240_provider_node_custom_json_config.sql`) kuma ana kwafi cikin kowace haɗin `providerSpecificData.customJson`.

Aiwatar da: `open-sse/utils/customJson.ts` (tabbatawa da gwaji), `open-sse/executors/customJson.ts` (mai zartarwa), `open-sse/services/combo/noReplayLatch.ts` da `src/sse/handlers/chat/responsePolicies.ts` (babu sake kunnawa).
