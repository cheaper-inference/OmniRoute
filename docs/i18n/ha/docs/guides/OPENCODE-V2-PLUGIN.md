`@omniroute/opencode-plugin-v2` yana sanya dukkan kundin OmniRoute ɗinka — models, combos da
auto-combos — cikin mai zaɓar model na OpenCode v2, tare da sunayen nuni, farashi da
kasafin free-tier.

Kunshi ne daban da `@omniroute/opencode-plugin` saboda OpenCode v1 da v2 suna loda
plugins ta hanyar ƙa'idoji daban-daban. Zaɓi wanda ya dace da babban sigar OpenCode ɗinka; babu abin da
suke rabawa a tsakaninsu, don haka haɓaka ɗaya ba zai taɓa tilasta haɓaka ɗayan ba.

## Abubuwan da ake buƙata

- OpenCode v2.
- Ƙofar OmniRoute da za a iya isa (`http://localhost:20128` ta tsohuwa).
- Node.js 22 ko 24.

## Shigarwa

Ƙara plugin ɗin zuwa `opencode.json`:

```json
{
  "plugins": [
    {
      "package": "@omniroute/opencode-plugin-v2",
      "options": {
        "providerId": "omniroute",
        "baseURL": "http://localhost:20128"
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

Daga nan models za su bayyana a matsayin `omniroute/<provider>/<model>`; `providerId` ne ke ƙayyade wannan prefix da
id na haɗin da OpenCode ke adana credential a ƙarƙashinsa.

## Credentials

Plugin ɗin yana neman mabuɗin gateway a wurare uku, bisa wannan jeri:

1. **Credential ɗin da ka haɗa a OpenCode.** Plugin ɗin yana rajistar integration, don haka
   tsarin auth na OpenCode zai iya adana mabuɗin. Babu abin da zai shiga `opencode.json` — wannan ya fi dacewa.
2. `apiKey` a cikin zaɓuɓɓukan plugin, domin yin override na kowane project. Wannan yana sanya mabuɗin cikin fayil da
   wataƙila kake commit.
3. `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` a cikin environment.

Idan babu ɗaya daga cikin ukun, kundin zai zama fanko kuma plugin ɗin zai faɗi hakan sau ɗaya lokacin farawa maimakon
barinka da mai zaɓe fanko ba tare da wani bayani ba.

### Management token wani mabuɗi ne daban

Combos, lafiyar provider da enrichment (sunayen nuni, farashi, da kasafin free-tier) suna zuwa ne daga
endpoints na `/api/*` na gateway, waɗanda yawancin deployments ke karewa da token na **management**
maimakon mabuɗin inference:

```json
"options": {
  "baseURL": "http://localhost:20128",
  "managementReadToken": "<management read token>"
}
```

Idan ba a saita shi ba, `managementReadToken` zai koma amfani da `apiKey`. Idan gateway ya ƙi wannan fallback,
har yanzu za a wallafa kundin, amma da raw model ids maimakon sunayen nuni, ba tare da canonical
alias dedupe ba, ba tare da farashi ba, kuma ba tare da combos ba. Plugin ɗin zai yi gargaɗi sau ɗaya ga kowane endpoint idan hakan ta faru,
yana bayyana endpoint ɗin da abin da aka rasa — don kada mai zaɓe da ya ragu inganci ya kasance abin mamaki.

## Yadda kundin ke zama sabo

Ana kawo kas ɗin a kasala kuma an adana shi don `modelCacheTtlMs`, kuma hoton faifai yana adana katalogin da aka sani na ƙarshe lokacin da ƙofa ba ta isa ba — abin kashewa ba ya kashe ku sai sabo. Ana buga samfura da combos da zarar sun isa; auto-combos, jerin masu ba da sabis da haɓaka haɓakawa lokacin da suka sauka, don haka jinkirin ƙarshen ƙarshen ba zai iya yin garkuwa da duk wanda aka yi garkuwa da shi ba.

Sunaye suna bin `Provider - Model[ (Editor)][ · budget][ (route)]`: ana nuna mai bayarwa a duk lokacin da id ɗin ya ɗauki ɗaya (marar slash-less id ba tare da prefix ba), babban `🆓` yana yin alamar shigarwar kyauta kuma kasafin kuɗin su na kyauta ya tsaya a matsayin ƙaramar `· 1M tokens/month`, editan sunan `Editor: Model` bayyananne yana matsawa zuwa madaidaicin `combo - <slug>` (babu editan da aka zaci ba tare da wasu sunaye kyauta ba). Shigarwa guda biyu waɗanda ke raba suna suna samun ƙaramar hanya — `(no-think)` — don su kasance masu bambanta. `providerTag` (tsoho `true`) yana jujjuya bacin edita mai biyo baya.

Babu umarni na wartsake da hannu: umarnin OpenCode v2 samfuri ne na gaggawa, ba kira baya ba, don haka plugin ɗin ba zai iya fallasa ɗaya ba. Wartsakewa suna bin TTL; Ana buƙatar mai watsa shiri don sake lodawa kawai lokacin da kasidar ko abin rufewa ya canza a zahiri, ba sau ɗaya a sake sabuntawa ba.

## Kiran kayan aiki a Gemini

Gemini yana ƙin gabaɗayan request idan tool declarations ɗinsa suna ɗauke da `$schema` ko `additionalProperties`, yana amsawa da `400 INVALID_ARGUMENT`. Plugin ɗin yana cire waɗannan keywords daga tool schemas da aka nufa ga Gemini model na wannan provider, yana kuma barin duk sauran requests ba tare da taɓa su ba. Tool da ke ɗauke da `$ref` ana tura shi ba tare da an taɓa shi ba maimakon a cire wani abu, domin cire reference ɗin zai faɗaɗa schema ɗin zuwa “karɓi komai”.
Saita `"geminiSanitization": false` don kashe shi.

## Shirya matsala

| Alama                                     | Dalili                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Picker yana nuna babu samfurin OmniRoute  | Babu maɓalli da aka warware (duba gargaɗin farawa), ko ƙofa ba ta isa ba                                                             |
| Raw model ids, babu combos, babu farashin | Ƙarshen abubuwan gudanarwa sun ƙi alamar — saita `managementReadToken`                                                               |
| Gajere ko fanko `freeOnly` catalog        | Babu alamar gudanarwa ko alamar da aka ƙi — karanta layin taƙaitawar wartsake (ƙididdigar `unmatched-free` ) a cikin log ɗin runduna |
| Wani zaman manne da `opencode-<id>/...`   | The v1 plugin buga `opencode-<id>`; v2 yana buga `<id>` bare, don haka sake zabar samfurin a ƙarƙashin `<id>/...`                    |

Dole ne masu canjin yanayi su isa tsarin uwar garken buɗe code: lokacin da mai watsa shiri ke gudana azaman sabis, harsashin da ke gudanar da `opencode models` da yanayin uwar garken sun bambanta, don haka fitar da maɓallin inda uwar garken ya karanta ta.
