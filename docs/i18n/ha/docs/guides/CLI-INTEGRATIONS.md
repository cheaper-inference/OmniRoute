Don bayanin aiwatarwa da aka raba, ƙayyadaddun yanayin yara da saitin Gemini mai tsayi, duba [kwangilar ƙaddamar da CLI](./CLI-LAUNCH-CONTRACTS.md).

OmniRoute tana jigilar dangin `setup-*` umarni waɗanda ke saita lambar CLI (Codex, Claude Code, OpenCode, Cline,…) don amfani da OmniRoute azaman — na baya don haka kayan aikin yayi magana da** daya** hanyoyin ƙarshe da OmniRoute zuwa ga mai bada dama tare da faɗuwar atomatik. Dokokin saitin suna amfani da mahallin OmniRoute na gida mai aiki ko nesa kuma saita kayan aiki a kunne** ku** inji. Kayan girke-girke na tushen katalogi suna samo samfuri daga uwar garken; girke-girke na samfur guda ɗaya suna karɓar `--model` ko faɗakarwa don samfurin ID. Ana nusar da maɓallin API ta hanyar canjin yanayi a duk inda kayan aiki ke goyan bayansa. An lura da umarnin da ke ci gaba da fayil ɗin mahalli na kayan aiki a ƙasa.

Katalogin da umarnin sarrafa kansa sun ƙunshi nau'ikan kayan aikin daban-daban:

| Surface                        | Kidaya | Abin da ake nufi                                                                 |
| ------------------------------ | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Katalojin kayan aiki           | 39     | Rubuce-rubucen kayan aiki da wakilai, gami da haɗin gwiwar hannu da IDE          |
| `omniroute run <target>`       | 8      | Kaddamar da adaftan da ke ba da maƙasudin ƙarshen, takaddun shaida da samfuri    |
| `omniroute configure <target>` | 12     | Shirye-shiryen daidaitawa mai mu'amala da ke samun goyan bayan umarnin `setup-*` |
| `setup-*` umarni               | 16     | Umarnin saitin rajista, gami da saitin editan guided/manual                      |

Ƙididdiga suna nuna [bayanan kayan aiki](../../config/cli-tools-manifest.json) da [yin rajistar umarni](../../bin/cli/commands/registry.mjs) akan 2026-11-17. Shigar da kasida baya nufin adaftar ƙaddamarwa. Dubi [kundin kayan aikin CLI](../reference/CLI-TOOLS.md) don cikakken kaya.

Haka kuma akwai na'ura mai kunnawa — `omniroute run <target>` — wanda ke haifar da `claude`, `codex`, `aider`, `goose`, `opencode`, `qwen`, `gemini` ko `whycodes` tare da alluran env na dama, ba tare da rubuta komai ba. Maƙasudai da laƙabinsu sun fito ne daga fitattun `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` (`claude-code|cc|anthropic`, `codex-cli|openai-codex|openai`, `goose-cli`, `open-code`, `qwen-code`, `gemini-cli`, `why-codes|why`), kuma `omniroute completion` yana ba da kalmomi iri ɗaya da aka samo asali. `omniroute launch <tool>` yana fara kayan aikin ƙididdigewa da aka goyan baya tare da alamar zaman ƙaddamarwa-kowa-ni-ɗani da jerin ƙirar da aka gina daga masu samarwa da kuka haɗa, kuma ta atomatik fara ƙofar gida lokacin da babu wanda ke gudana (duba [LAUNCH.md](./LAUNCH.md) ). Abubuwan ƙaddamar da kayan aiki na gado — `omniroute launch` ba tare da sunan kayan aiki ba (Lambar Claude ) da `omniroute launch-codex` (Codex) — suna nan.

Ana samun shigar da mai bayarwa daga mahallin local/remote iri ɗaya. API ɗin-umarni na farko da ke ƙasa suna kiyaye amincin gudanarwa daban da takaddun shaida na mai bayarwa kuma kada a taɓa buga takaddun shaida a cikin ingantaccen fitarwa:

```bash
omniroute providers add glm --credential-env GLM_API_KEY --name work
omniroute providers import ./providers.json --dry-run --json
omniroute providers auth openai
omniroute providers edit <connection-id> --default-model glm/glm-5.2
omniroute providers remove <connection-id> --yes
```

Don rubutun, fi son `--credential-stdin` ko `--credential-env`; ana ajiye `--credential` don amfanin gida mai sarrafawa. `providers remove` yana buƙatar `--yes` akan tashar da ba ta hulɗa ba, kuma duk umarni biyar suna girmama mahallin aiki ko zaɓin `--base-url`/`--api-key` na duniya.

Masu zaɓen mai bayarwa sun ƙi maƙasudin prefixes ID, sunaye ko sunayen masu bada sabis; yi amfani da cikakken haɗin ID lokacin da haɗi da yawa suka dace. Ƙirƙiri da shirya umarni karanta ajiyayyun haɗin baya, kuma cirewa yana tabbatar da cewa ba za a iya karantawa ba. Shigowa ya tsallake nau'in provider/name na yanzu. Shigar da aka shigo da shi ba zai iya ƙetare ƙarshen ƙarshen gudanarwa, mahallin ko bayanan gudanarwa da aka kawo zuwa CLI.

Don lokaci ɗaya, saitin tushe da aka rubuta da hannu na haɗin kai biyu mafi arziki, duba nutsewar kowane kayan aiki:

- [Tsarin Code Claude](./CLAUDE-CODE-CONFIGURATION.md)
- [Codex CLI](./CODEX-CLI-CONFIGURATION.md)
- [Yanayin nesa](./REMOTE-MODE.md) — yana fitar da OmniRoute mai nisa (VPS / Tailnet) daga kwamfutar tafi-da-gidanka
- [VS Code Copilot Taɗi](./VSCODE-COPILOT.md) — ƙarar OmniCopilot ; yana kuma iya gudanar da waɗannan umarni `setup-*` a gare ku daga cikin editan

Kowane umarni yana girmama shi** mahallin aiki** (saitin da `omniroute connect`, duba [Yanayin Nisa](./REMOTE-MODE.md) ) ko tutocin `--remote <url> --api-key <key>` bayyananne. Bayanan kula akan tutoci (an tabbatar a tushen umarni):

- `--remote <url>` — yi amfani da OmniRoute mai nisa (ya soke `--port` da mahallin aiki). `--api-key <key>` yana ba da takaddun shaidar wannan uwar garken (tsoho zuwa `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` env var, ko alamar mahallin aiki).
- `--only <patterns>` — waƙafi masu raba igiyoyi; ajiye IDs kawai samfurin da ya dace (misali `--only glm,kimi`). Akwai akan `setup-codex`, `setup-claude`, `setup-opencode`, `setup-continue`, `setup-cursor`, `setup-crush`, `setup-zed`.
- `--dry-run` — buga ainihin abin da za a rubuta ba tare da taɓa tsarin fayil ba. Akwai akan kowane umarni `setup-*`** sai dai** `setup-cursor` (wanda baya rubuta fayil).
- `--model <id>` — da ake buƙata (ko karɓa ta hanyar sadarwa) don kayan aikin da ba su da samfurin ganowa ta atomatik: Cline, Kilo, Roo, Goose, Qwen, Gemini, Aider, 5dive, WhyCodes. Waɗannan kayan aikin kuma suna karɓar `--yes` don gudanar da ba tare da haɗin gwiwa ba (wanda sannan yana buƙatar `--model`). `setup-opencode` yana ɗaukar `--model` don saita tsoho samfurin babban matakin.
- `--model <id>` akan `omniroute run` yana biye da wayoyi na kowane manufa (`bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs`):** mataimaki** yana karbar `--model openai/<id>` kuma** bude code** `--model omniroute/<id>` (ana ƙara prefix ne kawai lokacin da id ɗin bai riga ya ɗauka ba);** qwan** kuma** gemini** karbi id a baki;** claude** yana samun ta hanyar `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`,** goshi** ta hanyar `GOOSE_MODEL`,** whycodes** ta `-P omniroute -m <id>`, da** codex** via `-c model_providers.omniroute.*` args.**Qwen shine kawai makasudin gudu wanda mai wuya ya buƙaci `--model`** — `omniroute run qwen` ba tare da shi yana fita `2` tare da kuskure bayyananne.
- `--port <port>` — tashar tashar OmniRoute ta gida (tsohuwar `20128`, ba a kula da ita lokacin da aka saita `--remote` ). Gabatar da duk `setup-*` da duka masu ƙaddamarwa.
- Lambobin fita `omniroute run` : lambar fita na yaron CLI ana yada shi da baki; `2` = muhawara mara inganci (manufa mara tallafi, batan `--model` da ake buƙata, mai gadin akwati); `127` = maƙasudin binary baya cikin `PATH`; `130`/`143`/`129` lokacin da ƙaddamarwar ta ƙare ta `SIGINT`/`SIGTERM`/`SIGHUP`; `1` = sauran gazawar ƙaddamar da lokacin gudu.
- Masu ƙaddamarwa guda biyu (`launch`, `launch-codex`) suna karɓar `--profile <name>` don zaɓar bayanin martaba da `setup-claude` / `setup-codex` ya rubuta, tare da wucewa ta hanyar args don tushen `claude` / `codex` binary.

Hakanan ana raba mai zaɓin hulɗa ta tsarin girke-girke:

```bash
# Pick from the active local or remote model catalog and configure the target.
omniroute configure claude
omniroute configure opencode --provider glm
omniroute configure qwen --model qwen/qwen3.8-max-preview --yes
omniroute configure gemini
omniroute configure whycodes
```

`configure` a halin yanzu tana ba da wakilai ga girke-girke da aka gwada don `codex`, `claude`, `opencode`, `qwen`, `gemini`, `aider`, `goose`, `cline`, `continue`, `kilo`, `5dive`, da `whycodes`. IDE-kawai, MITM, da shigarwar kundin jagora-kawai sun kasance bayyane `setup-*`/gudanar hannu kuma ba a gabatar da su azaman maƙasudin ƙaddamarwa ba.

> `setup-opencode` ni** budeai mai nauyi mai nauyi** hadewar OpenCode. Hakanan akwai ingantaccen haɗin haɗin plugin ɗin — `omniroute setup opencode` — wanda ke shigar da `@omniroute/opencode-plugin`. Umarni ne daban-daban; teburin da ke sama takardun `setup-opencode`.
>
> Plugin ya zo a cikin fakiti biyu, ɗaya a kowane babban OpenCode, saboda masu ɗaukar kaya biyu suna tsammanin wuraren shigarwa daban-daban: `@omniroute/opencode-plugin` don OpenCode v1 da `@omniroute/opencode-plugin-v2` don OpenCode v2. Kunshin v2 sabo ne (`0.1.0`) kuma yana bin kwangilar mai masaukin baki wanda har yanzu yana motsawa, don haka yana karanta zuriyar OpenCode a cikin daftarin kasida maimakon ɗauka ɗaya. Shigar da shi ta ƙara shigarwar `plugins` zuwa `opencode.json`; `omniroute setup opencode` har yanzu yana shigar da kunshin v1. Zaɓuɓɓuka da odar neman shaidar shaidar suna cikin fakitin README.

## Amfani na gida

Yayin da OmniRoute ke aiki a kan `localhost:20128`, kawai gudanar da umarnin saiti na
kayan aikinka. Ana ɗauko katalog ɗin daga sabar gida.

```bash
# Codex: rubuta profile ɗaya ga kowane model da ya dace cikin ~/.codex/
omniroute setup-codex
codex --profile glm52            # yi amfani da profile da aka ƙirƙira

# Claude Code: rubuta profiles na kowane model, sannan ƙaddamar da ɗaya
omniroute setup-claude
omniroute launch --profile glm52

# OpenCode: rubuta provider mai dacewa da openai tare da duk models na katalog
omniroute setup-opencode
export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY=sk-...  # ana ambatonsa ta {env:OMNIROUTE_API_KEY}, ba a taɓa ajiye shi a faifai ba
opencode -m omniroute/glm/glm-5.2 "..."

# Kayan aikin da ba su da ganowa ta atomatik suna buƙatar a fayyace model:
omniroute setup-aider --model glm/glm-5.2
omniroute setup-qwen --model qwen/qwen3.8-max-preview

# Yi samfoti ba tare da rubuta komai ba:
omniroute setup-continue --dry-run
```

Ƙaddamar ba tare da rubuta wani config kwata-kwata ba (allurar env kawai):

```bash
omniroute launch                 # Claude Code → OmniRoute na gida
omniroute launch-codex           # Codex CLI → OmniRoute na gida
omniroute launch-codex --profile glm52
omniroute run claude --model openai/gpt-5.4
omniroute run codex --model openai/gpt-5.4 --dry-run --json
omniroute run aider --model glm/glm-5.2 -- --message "reply OK"
omniroute run goose --model glm/glm-5.2
omniroute run opencode --model glm/glm-5.2 -- run "reply OK"
omniroute run qwen --model glm/glm-5.2 -- -p "reply OK"
omniroute run gemini --model glm/glm-5.2 -- --skip-trust -p "reply OK"

# Takamaiman hanyar umarni: tura duk abin da ya zo bayan -- kai tsaye
omniroute run claude -- --print-system-prompt "review this diff"
```

## Amfani daga nesa

Nuna kowane umarnin saiti zuwa OmniRoute na nesa da `--remote` + `--api-key`. Ana
ɗauko katalog ɗin daga wurin nesa; ana rubuta config ɗin a kan na'urarka ta gida.

```bash
# OpenCode da ke aiki da VPS na nesa, riƙe models na glm/kimi kawai
omniroute setup-opencode --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx \
  --only glm,kimi
opencode -m omniroute/glm/glm-5.2 "..."   # fara export OMNIROUTE_API_KEY

# Profiles na Codex daga katalog na nesa
omniroute setup-codex --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx

# Ƙaddamar da CLI kai tsaye da wurin nesa
omniroute launch       --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx
omniroute launch-codex --remote http://192.168.0.15:20128 --api-key oma_live_xxx
```

Maimakon bayar da `--remote`/`--api-key` kowane lokaci, shiga sau ɗaya ka bar
**mahallin da ke aiki** ya samar da su ta atomatik:

```bash
omniroute connect 192.168.0.15        # ƙirƙiri scoped token, ajiye mahallin
omniroute setup-codex                 # ← yanzu yana amfani da katalog na nesa
omniroute setup-opencode              # ← iri ɗaya
omniroute launch                      # ← Claude Code da ke aiki da wurin nesa
```

Duba [Yanayin Nesa](./REMOTE-MODE.md) don mahallai, scopes, da sarrafa tokens.

## 5dive agent fleets

[5dive](https://5dive.ai) yana gudanar da ɗimbin wakilai masu yin rikodin dogon aiki, kowane ɗayan tsarin naúrar ƙarƙashin mai amfani da Unix. Ba coding din CLI bane, don haka babu wani abu da `omniroute run` zai kaddamar — `5dive` shine** saitin-kawai** manufa.

```bash
omniroute configure 5dive --model failover-demo --yes
omniroute setup-5dive --model failover-demo --auth-profile omniroute --agent worker1
```

Dukansu nau'ikan suna rubuta guda 5 dive** auth profile** , kuma duk kujerar `claude` da ke daure zuwa wannan profile sai yayi magana da OmniRoute. Abubuwa uku sun keɓance ga wannan manufa:

- **Yana gudana akan rundunar jiragen ruwa, a matsayin tushen.** 5dive's fi'ili suna aiki a kan tsarin gida na gida da dirka mallakar tushen tushe; babu yanayin nesa. Girke-girke yana sake aiwatarwa ta hanyar `sudo` lokacin da bai riga ya samo tushe ba (`--no-sudo` yana kashe wannan kuma ya buga umarnin maimakon).
- **Dole ne maƙasudin ƙarshe ya zama `https://` sai dai idan an sake dawowa.** Maɓallin API na wakilin yana hawa wanda URL akan kowace buƙata, kuma 5dive ya ƙi ƙarshen ƙarshen akwatin. Adireshin LAN mai zaman kansa ba keɓantacce bane.
- **Kowane wurin zama fil ɗin samfurin kansa ya zarce bayanin martaba.** Profile ɗin yana ɗauke da `ANTHROPIC_DEFAULT_{OPUS,SONNET,HAIKU}_MODEL`, amma har yanzu wurin zama da aka liƙa a kan ƙirar ƙira ta gaza juyowar sa ta farko tare da _"Akwai matsala tare da ƙirar da aka zaɓa"_. Wuce `--agent <name>` (maimaituwa) don haɗa kujerun kuma; girke-girke yana buga umarnin lokacin da ba ku yi ba.

Ana mika maɓallin API zuwa 5 nutsewa a kunne** stdin** (`--api-key=-`), don haka baya bayyana a cikin fitarwar `ps`.

Nuna bayanin martaba a OmniRoute** haduwa** maimakon nau'i guda ɗaya shine abin da ke samun masu samar da jiragen ruwa: lokacin da farkon ƙarshen ya tafi tsaka-tsakin tsaka-tsaki a cikin gudu da aka rubuta akan [#11578](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/issues/11578), wakilin ya gama ragowar matakansa a kan koma baya kuma bai taba fitowa ba.

## Kiyaye native deps yayin sabuntawa: `--include=optional`

Lokacin da kuka sabunta da `omniroute update` (bayan tabbatarwa, ko tare da `--apply`),
OmniRoute yana gudanar da shigarwar tare da `--include=optional` da aka riga aka haɗa:

```bash
npm install -g omniroute@latest --include=optional
```

Wannan **ba** flag ba ne da za ku tura wa `omniroute update` — mai sabuntawa ne
koyaushe yake amfani da shi. Yana tabbatar da cewa `optionalDependencies`
(`better-sqlite3`, `keytar`, `tls-client`, tarin LLMLingua SLM) sun ci gaba da
kasancewa bayan sabuntawa ko da saitin npm ɗinku yana da `omit=optional`, wanda
in ba haka ba zai cire native SQLite driver da haɗin OS-keyring a ɓoye. Don ganin
ainihin umarnin kafin aiwatar da shi:

```bash
omniroute update --dry-run
# [GWADAWA KAWAI] Za a gudanar da: npm install -g omniroute@latest --include=optional
```

Sauran flags na `omniroute update` (an tabbatar a cikin tushe): `--check` (fita da
1 idan ya tsufa), `--apply` (shigar ba tare da tambayar tabbatarwa ba), `--changelog`,
`--no-backup`, `--yes`.

## Google Gemini CLI ta hanyar `omniroute run gemini`

An tabbatar da ƙa’idar aiki da `@google/gemini-cli` 0.50.0: CLI yana mutunta
`GOOGLE_GEMINI_BASE_URL` kuma yana aika `POST /v1beta/models/<model>:generateContent`
(da `:streamGenerateContent?alt=sse`) zuwa gare shi — daidai da shimfiɗar Gemini
ta asali ta OmniRoute (`/v1beta`). `omniroute run gemini` yana haɗa wannan ta
atomatik:

- `GOOGLE_GEMINI_BASE_URL` → Base URL na OmniRoute mai aiki (tushe, babu `/v1`);
- `GEMINI_API_KEY` → bayanan shaidar OmniRoute da aka warware (zaɓi/env/context);
- **`GEMINI_CLI_HOME` na ɗan lokaci kuma keɓantacce** wanda `.gemini/settings.json`
  ɗinsa yake zaɓar auth na `gemini-api-key`, don kada zaman Google OAuth da aka
  adana (Code Assist) ya taɓa rinjayar ƙaddamarwar da aka karkatar zuwa OmniRoute
  — ana cire shi bayan fita;
- **tsabtar env**: ana cire `GOOGLE_API_KEY`, `GOOGLE_GENAI_USE_VERTEXAI` da
  `GOOGLE_GENAI_USE_GCA` daga env na child process (waɗanda za su karkatar da auth
  zuwa Vertex/Code Assist), sannan a saita
  `GEMINI_DEFAULT_AUTH_TYPE=gemini-api-key` a matsayin ƙarin kariya — sauran
  manufofin `run` suna samun irin wannan kulawa ga nasu variables masu karo da juna;
- shigar da `--model <id>` daga `--provider`/`--model`.

```bash
omniroute run gemini --model glm/glm-5.2 -- --skip-trust -p "hello"
```

Har yanzu kariyar amincewa da workspace ta Gemini tana aiki a yanayin headless —
ku tura `--skip-trust` (ko ku amince da directory ɗin ta hanyar hulɗa) da kanku;
launcher ɗin da gangan ba ya ketare ta. Wannan launcher ya bambanta da
**rajistar ACP** (`src/lib/acp/registry.ts`, `gemini --acp`), wadda har yanzu ita
ce haɗawar agent-protocol don `/dashboard/acp-agents`.

## Gwajin hayaki na ainihi (na zaɓi)

Ana gudanar da gwajin koma-bayan tsarin ƙaddamarwa mai tabbataccen sakamako a cikin CI (`tests/unit/cli/run-command.test.ts`,
`tests/unit/cli/run-execution.test.ts`). Don tabbatar da binaries na AINIHI a kan sabar
OmniRoute ta AINIHI, akwai tsarin gwaji na zaɓi a
`tests/integration/upstream-cli-smoke.int.test.ts`. Ba ya taɓa gudana ta atomatik
(kowane ƙaramin gwaji ana tsallake shi sai idan `RUN_CLI_SMOKE=1`), yana miƙa bayanan shiga ta SUNAN env-var
(ba ta ƙimarsa ba), yana ɓoye jerin haruffa masu kama da maɓalli daga duk wani fitarwa da aka adana, yana tsallake
manufofin da ba a shigar da binary ɗinsu ba, kuma yana rarraba gazawa zuwa
auth / upstream / config maimakon ƙimar boolean kawai:

```bash
RUN_CLI_SMOKE=1 \
OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_BASE_URL="http://localhost:20128" \
OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_MODEL="<provider/model>" \
OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_API_KEY_ENV="OMNIROUTE_API_KEY" \
node --import tsx/esm --test tests/integration/upstream-cli-smoke.int.test.ts
```

Na zaɓi: `OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_TARGETS="codex,opencode,qwen"` yana taƙaita gwajin;
`OMNIROUTE_SMOKE_TIMEOUT_MS` yana maye gurbin iyakar lokacin daƙiƙa 120 ga kowace manufa.
