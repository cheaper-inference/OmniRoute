Kwangilar `config/cli-tools-manifest.json` tsaka tsaki ta bayyana canonical IDs, laƙabi, yan takarar binary, girke-girke na saitin, jayayyar ƙira da iyawa. Adaftar Node mai bayyana a cikin `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` da adaftar TypeScript a cikin `src/shared/constants/cliIntegrationManifest.ts` karanta fayil iri ɗaya. Lokacin aiki, katalogi, daidaitawa, kammalawa, janareta da rajista na baya ana bincika tare ta `tests/unit/cli/cli-manifest-drift.test.ts`. Kasancewa kataloji ba yana nufin za a iya ƙaddamar da kayan aiki ko daidaita shi ba. Musamman, 5dive ya rage saitin-kawai.

## Yanayin yara da yanayin rayuwa

`omniroute run`, `omniroute launch` da `omniroute launch-codex` sun gaji jerin izinin dacewa OS/terminal kuma a yi musu allurar shaidar da aka zaɓa. Ba a gaji mai ba da alaƙa da bayanan sirri ta tsohuwa. `--inherit-env` a sarari yana maido da cikakken gadon iyaye-muhalli; yi amfani da shi kawai lokacin da zaɓin tsari na ɓangare na uku ya kamata ya karɓi waɗannan ƙimar. Wannan tace muhalli ne, ba akwatin yashi OS ba: har yanzu tsarin yana gudana tare da izinin tsarin fayil ɗin mai kira.

Ana tura siginar katsewa da ƙarewa ga yaro. Mai ƙaddamarwa yana jiran taron kusa da yaron kafin cire saitin wucin gadi. `OMNIROUTE_CHILD_SIGNAL_TIMEOUT_MS` tana sarrafa lokacin alheri kafin ƙarshen tilastawa (tsoho 5000 millise seconds).

`omniroute launch <tool>` (duba [LAUNCH.md](./LAUNCH.md) ) yana bin ƙa'idodin muhalli iri ɗaya da isar da sigina, kuma yana ƙarawa:

- **Gateway ta atomatik farawa.** Don manufa ta gida, lokacin da babu amsa kofa akan tashar jiragen ruwa, mai ƙaddamarwa yana farawa `omniroute serve --headless --no-open` daure zuwa `127.0.0.1` ƙarƙashin tsarin girbi da aka ware. Kowane ƙaddamar da gudu yana riƙe da haya a cikin `DATA_DIR/launch/leases/` (ƙara bugun zuciya kowane s 15, tsayawa bayan 45 s ko lokacin da aikinta ya tafi). Mai girbi yana dakatar da ƙofar da ya fara da zarar babu haya da aka yi rayuwa don alheri mara aiki (`OMNIROUTE_LAUNCH_IDLE_GRACE_MS`, tsoho 10000 millise seconds). Ana amfani da ƙofar da mai amfani ya fara amma bai daina ba. `--keep-gateway` ko `omniroute gateway keep` suna juya ƙofar ƙaddamarwa zuwa na al'ada; `--no-start` yana maido da tsohuwar halayen gazawa idan ƙasa. Ana binciken makasudin nesa kawai.
- **Kaddamar da zaman.** Kowane ƙaddamarwa yana ƙaddamar da alamar zaman `olx_` na ɗan gajeren lokaci daga shaidar shaidar iyaye (`POST /api/launch/sessions`), tana bugun shi yayin da kayan aiki ke gudana, karanta taƙaitaccen amfani kuma ya soke shi bayan kayan aikin ya fita. Alamar ƙaƙƙarfan juzu'i ce ta iyayenta: izini iri ɗaya, iri ɗaya ko mafi ƙanƙantar kasidar, gajeriyar rayuwa, kuma ba ita ce shaidar dashboard ba. Kayan aikin yana karɓar alamar zaman, ba za a taɓa samun shaidar mahaifa ba, kuma ana yiwa buƙatun alamar `launch:<tool>:<sessionId>` sai dai idan an ba `--tag`. Ƙofar ba tare da zaman API ta kasa tare da `GatewayTooOld` maimakon faɗuwa zuwa ƙaddamar da ba a cika ba.
- **Saitin ƙaddamarwa.** Ana rubuta fayilolin saitin kayan aiki zuwa kundin adireshin ɗan lokaci mai zaman kansa (yanayin 0700, prefix `omnirouter-`) kuma a cire bayan yaron ya fita. `SIGKILL` na mai ƙaddamarwa yana barin littafin a baya; `omniroute launch --cleanup` yana cire irin waɗannan kundayen adireshi da zarar sun girmi kwana ɗaya.

## Gemini m saitin

```bash
omniroute setup-gemini --remote https://omni.example --model provider/model --yes
omniroute configure gemini --model provider/model --yes
omniroute run gemini --model provider/model --dry-run --json
```

Umarni biyu na farko suna saita wannan injin don zaɓaɓɓen uwar garken gida ko na nesa. Saita yana adana saitunan JSON maras alaƙa, yana zaɓar auth maɓalli Gemini API, kuma ya rubuta ƙarshen ƙarshen da kuma takaddun shaida zuwa fayil `.gemini/.env` mai zaman kansa. Ya ƙi JSON mara inganci maimakon a sake rubuta shi. Ƙarshen URLs dole ne ya zama HTTP(S), ba tare da shigar da takaddun shaida ba, sigogin tambaya ko guntuwa. Hanyoyi masu busassun gudu, ba ƙididdiga masu inganci ba.

`GEMINI_CLI_HOME` ko `--gemini-home` suna zaɓar tushen gida mai dagewa. Ainihin `run gemini` yana ƙirƙira kuma daga baya ya cire nasa gida na ɗan lokaci. Duk wani yanayi baya ƙetare binciken amintattun wuraren aiki na Gemini.

## Codex profiles

`configure codex` tana rubuta `<name>.config.toml` mai zaman kansa tare da ƙira da madaidaicin mai bada OmniRoute. Ana amfani da takaddun shaida ta hanyar `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY`, ba a taɓa sakawa ba. Umurnin ƙaddamarwa yana saita ƙirar da aka zaɓa a babban maɓalli `model`, ba cikin `model_providers`.

Fayilolin bayanin martaba daban sune kwangilar Codex da aka rubuta na yanzu; suna rufe tsarin mai amfani lokacin da aka zaɓa tare da `codex --profile <name>`. Duba [bayanin bayanin martaba na hukuma](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/config-file/config-advanced#profiles) da [jagorar OmniRoute Codex](./CODEX-CLI-CONFIGURATION.md).

## Hermetic umurnin kisa

Tare da bayyananniyar `DATA_DIR`, saita `OMNIROUTE_CLI_SKIP_DEFAULT_DATA_ENV=1` yana hana farawa daga faɗuwa zuwa ga tsoffin bayanan bayanan mai amfani. Wannan yana da amfani ga keɓe gwaje-gwaje. Ba ya kashe sauran fayilolin yanayi da aka zaɓa a sarari.

## Aiki-directory `.env`

CLI yana karanta fayilolin `.env` a cikin wannan tsari, kuma fayil na farko don saita maɓalli ya yi nasara: bayyanannen `DATA_DIR`, tsohuwar kundin bayanan bayanai, kundin adireshi na yanzu, sannan kundin adireshi. Masu canjin Shell koyaushe suna nasara.

Kundin tsarin aiki na iya zama na wani (majiya mai tushe, ma'ajin da ba a cika kunshin ba), kuma `omniroute serve` yana wuce yanayin CLI zuwa uwar garken da kowane kayan aiki da uwar garken ke gudanarwa. Don haka `.env` a can yana iya saita waɗannan maɓallan kawai, wanda aka jera a cikin `bin/cli/utils/untrustedCwdEnv.mjs`:

| Maɓalli                         | Ƙimar da aka karɓa                                                                |
| ------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `REQUIRE_API_KEY`               | `true`, `1` ko `yes` a kowane hali, ƙimar uwar garken yana bi da su kamar a kunne |
| `LANG`, `LC_ALL`, `LC_MESSAGES` | har zuwa haruffa 64, lambobi, `_`, `.`, `@` ko `-`                                |
| `LANGUAGE`                      | guda, da `:`                                                                      |
| `TZ`                            | har zuwa haruffa 64, lambobi, `_`, `+`, `-` ko `/`, ba a farawa da `/` ba.        |
| `NO_COLOR`, `FORCE_COLOR`       | har zuwa haruffa 16 da lambobi                                                    |

Kowace darajar `REQUIRE_API_KEY` ta karɓa tana kunna rajistan, don haka fayil ɗin ba zai iya kashe cak ɗin da kansa ba. Ba ya yanke shawarar sakamakon, ko da yake: uwar garken yana karanta `REQUIRE_API_KEY` override da aka adana a cikin ma'ajin bayanai kafin yanayin, yayin da gargadin farawa game da sauraron duk musaya ba tare da maɓallin API ba yana karanta yanayi kawai. Tare da abin da aka adana na `false`, kundin tsarin aiki `REQUIRE_API_KEY=true` yana barin API a buɗe ya ɓoye wannan gargaɗin. Ka'idar `TZ` ta fi POSIX kunkuntar : tana kin `.`, `:`, `,` da `<>`, don haka tsarin `:Area/City` da ka'idoji kamar `EST5EDT,M3.2.0,M11.1.0` dole ne a sanya su a cikin harsashi ko a cikin `<DATA_DIR>/.env`.

Dole ne sunaye su yi daidai daidai, a cikin babban harka. Duk wani layi, darajar da ta gaza cak ɗin ta, da sunan da ba a sarari ba haruffa ASCII, lambobi da `_` (misali wanda ke da NUL byte ko harafi mai kama). CLI yana buga gargaɗi ɗaya wanda ke sanya ma maɓallan da ba zai iya saitawa ba da kuma wanda ke ba da izinin maɓallai ya yi watsi da su, kowanne da abin da zai canza (`TZ (expected a zone name such as Europe/Berlin)`, `lang (write the name as LANG)`). Dukansu baya buga ƙimar. Hanyoyin fayil a cikin waɗannan saƙonnin, da kuma hanyar `.env` hanyar `omniroute doctor`, ana tserewa lokacin da suke ɗauke da haruffan sarrafawa ko bidi. Ƙuntatawa baya aiki lokacin da kundin tsarin aiki shine kundin bayanai ko kundin adireshin kunshin.

`PORT`, `API_PORT` da `DASHBOARD_PORT` basa cikin lissafin. Ba tare da tushen URL daga tushen mafi girma ba, CLI yayi magana da `localhost:<PORT>`, kuma duk abin da ya saurara a wurin yana karɓar maɓallin API CLI alamar mahallin da alamar injin CLI (`x-omniroute-cli-token`, duba [CLI_TOKEN.md](../security/CLI_TOKEN.md) ), wanda uwar garken gida ya karɓa don kiran gudanarwa daga loopback. Tashar tashar jiragen ruwa daga kundin adireshin aiki zai bar fayil ɗin ya zaɓi mai sauraro, don haka saita tashoshin jiragen ruwa a cikin harsashi, a cikin `<DATA_DIR>/.env` ko tare da zaɓin `--port` na umarni waɗanda ke da ɗaya (`serve`, `launch`, `run`).

Fassarar ba ta da ma'anar `export`. Layi kamar `export LD_PRELOAD=...` suna suna `export LD_PRELOAD` mai canzawa; daga directory ɗin aiki ana tsallake shi, kuma a cikin wasu fayilolin `.env` yana saita wannan ainihin sunan, wanda babu abin da ya karanta.

CLI ya haɗa alamar mahallin da aka adana (alamar shiga ko gadon API ) kawai tare da asalin mahallin: makirci, masauki da tashar jiragen ruwa, idan aka kwatanta da `URL.origin`. Wannan ya shafi buƙatun da aka yi ta hanyar CLI's HTTP abokin ciniki (`--base-url`, `OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL`, `omniroute connect <new-url>`) da `launch`, `launch codex`, `configure` da `setup-*` umarni waɗanda ke rubuta tushen URL da maɓalli a cikin wani tsarin kayan aiki. An nuna wa wata uwar garken tare da `--remote`, suna amfani da `--api-key` ko `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` bayyananne idan an saita ɗaya, kuma babu alamar mahallin. Kwatancen daidai ne, don haka `localhost` da `127.0.0.1`, ko mai masaukin baki tare da kuma ba tare da ɗigo ba, ƙidaya azaman sabar daban-daban kuma ba su sami alamar mahallin ba. URL tare da userinfo, ja da baya ko farar fata ba su taɓa yin daidai ba, saboda sauran URL parsers (Python's `urllib` da `httpx`, alal misali) na iya karanta wani masauki daban daga gare ta. Don buƙatun CLI ya aika da kansa, wannan cak ɗin ya ƙunshi tsari da iko kawai, don haka id mai sarari a hanya ko tambaya yana kiyaye alamar.
