## Abin da yake (da abin da ba shi ba)

OmniRoute yana danganta kuɗin USD na kowace buƙata ga kowane kammalawa ta hanyar ninka adadin token
da farashin samfurin. Waɗannan lambobi ne ke samar da bayanan dashboard na **Kuɗaɗe**,
CLI na `omniroute cost` / `omniroute usage`, fitarwar CSV/JSON, da kasafin kuɗin kowane mabuɗin API.

> **“Kuɗin” da ke dashboard mai bin diddigin tanadi ne, ba takardar kuɗi ba.** OmniRoute ba ya taɓa cajin ku
> — yana tura buƙatunku zuwa masu samar da sabis da kuka riga kuka haɗa (rajistocinku,
> matakan kyauta, da maɓallan API). “Jimillar kuɗi ta $290” da ta taru gaba ɗaya daga samfuran
> kyauta tana nufin kusan **$290 da ba ku biya ba** ga API mai kuɗi. Adadin _ƙiyasi_ ne
> na abin da zirga-zirgar iri ɗaya za ta ci a daidaitattun farashin kasuwa, domin ku iya ganin inda
> amfaninku ya fi yawa da kuma yawan kuɗin da turawa zuwa masu samar da sabis masu rahusa/kyauta ke adana muku.

An bayyana wannan ma’anar kai tsaye a cikin [README](../../README.md) na aikin (“‘kuɗin’ da ke dashboard
mai bin diddigin tanadi ne, ba takardar kuɗi ba”).

Saboda lambar ƙiyasi ce:

- Ta dogara da teburin farashin da OmniRoute yake da shi ga kowane samfuri. Samfurin da ba shi da shigarwar
  farashi yana bayar da kuɗin `0` (yana bayyana a matsayin layin “Tsoho / Kyauta” a cikin mai binciken).
- Zirga-zirgar matakin kyauta da ta rajista har yanzu tana tara _ƙiyastaccen_ kuɗi — wannan shi ne
  adadin da kuke adanawa, ba adadin da ake bin ku ba.

## Yadda aka kiyasta farashi

### Tushen farashi

Farashin yana fitowa daga teburin farashin da aka haɗa a cikin [`src/lib/db/settings/pricing.ts`](../../src/lib/db/settings/pricing.ts), a cikin wannan tsari na fifiko (** mafi girma na farko** ):

1. **Mai amfani ya soke** farashin — da aka saita a cikin dashboard ko ta hanyar `PATCH /api/pricing` (sararin sunan `pricing` ).
2. **model.dev daidaitawa** farashin — da aka samo daga `https://models.dev/api.json` (sunan `models_dev_pricing` ).
3. **LiteLLM sync** farashin — da aka samo daga LiteLLM ta jama'a `model_prices_and_context_window.json` (sunan `pricing_synced` ).
4. **Hardcoded Predefinicións** farashin — ya shigo cikin [`src/shared/constants/pricing/`](../../src/shared/constants/pricing/).

Don provider/model iri ɗaya , ƙima daga yadudduka masu fifiko suna sake rubuta filayen da suka dace a cikin ƙananan yadudduka; filayen da ba su da babban Layer har yanzu suna iya fitowa daga ƙaramin Layer. Ana gyara oda koda lokacin da ayyukan daidaitawa masu zaman kansu ke gudana a lokaci guda. `GET /api/pricing?includeSources=1` yana dawo da `pricing` da `sourceMap`, wanda kowane nau'in tambarin su (`user`, `modelsDev`, `litellm`, `default`) sun gano babban fifikon fifiko mai ɗauke da wannan ƙirar.

**LiteLLM sync** yana shiga, an kashe shi ta tsohuwa. Matsalolin muhallinta (duba [`.env.example`](../../.env.example) ) sune:

| Env var                 | Tsohuwar  | Manufar                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `PRICING_SYNC_ENABLED`  | `false`   | Kunna aikin daidaita farashin LiteLLM baya.                                            |
| `PRICING_SYNC_INTERVAL` | `86400`   | Tazarar daidaitawa a cikin daƙiƙa (kullum).                                            |
| `PRICING_SYNC_SOURCES`  | `litellm` | Zaɓin tushen don wannan daidaitawa kawai; a halin yanzu `litellm` kawai ake tallafawa. |

**model.dev daidaitawa** ya bambanta da daidaitawar LiteLLM kuma an kashe shi ta tsohuwa. Kunna shi a cikin Saituna (models.dev sync) ko amfani da waɗannan masu canji:

| Env var                    | Tsohuwar          | Manufar                                                                                                              |
| -------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `MODELS_DEV_SYNC_ENABLED`  | _(Ba a saita ba)_ | Cire saiti yana biye da saiti; `1/true/on/yes` yana kunnawa da kashe `0/false/off/no` (ko da Saituna sun kunna shi). |
| `MODELS_DEV_SYNC_INTERVAL` | `86400`           | Default model.dev tazarar daidaitawa a cikin daƙiƙa (kullum).                                                        |

`PRICING_SYNC_SOURCES` yi** ba** kunna model.dev; yana sarrafa aikin daidaita LiteLLM kawai. Share ƙetare mai amfani baya share ko dai da aka daidaita sararin sunan; Layer na gaba da ake samu na farashi ya zama mai tasiri. Don samfurin mara farashi, duba saitunan daidaitawa biyu da `sourceMap` kafin ƙara jujjuyawar hannu.

### Tsarin farashi

Ana ƙididdige farashi akan kowane buƙatun daga ƙidayar alama da ƙimar alama-miliyan a [`src/lib/usage/costCalculator.ts`](../../src/lib/usage/costCalculator.ts) (`computeCostFromPricing` / `calculateCost`):

- **Alamomin shigarwa** (a cire cache karantawa da alamun ƙirƙira cache) ƙimar × `input`.
- **Alamu na karanta cache** ƙimar × `cached` (ya koma ga ƙimar shigarwa).
- **Alamu na ƙirƙira cache** ƙimar × `cache_creation` (ya koma ga ƙimar shigarwa).
- **Alamar fitarwa** `output` ×
- **Alamun dalilai** ƙimar × `reasoning` (ya koma ga ƙimar fitarwa).

Ana fassara duk farashin a matsayin USD a kowace alamar 1,000,000. Matsayin sabis Codex "sauri"/"fififici" ko "flex" matakin sabis yana aiwatar da yawan farashi (`getCodexFastCostMultiplier`) — misali lalƙwalwa ana ƙididdigewa akan ragi na 50%, wanda aka bayyana kamar** m tanadi** a cikin dashboard.

Sunaye na ƙila an daidaita su da farko (an cire prefixes na masu bayarwa kamar `openai/` ko `accounts/fireworks/models/` ) don haka har yanzu layuka na tarihi sun yi daidai da farashi.

### Yadda ake kashewa ana yin rikodin

- Ana ƙididdige farashin kowane-buƙata bayan amsa kuma an rubuta wuta-da-manta don haka ba zai taɓa ƙara latency ga abokin ciniki ba. An tsara amfani da keɓaɓɓen keɓaɓɓen ƙididdiga akan alamar madauki na gaba ta hanyar [`src/lib/quota/spendRecorder.ts`](../../src/lib/quota/spendRecorder.ts).
- API-key kashe ana adanawa kuma ana zubar dashi cikin batches ta hanyar [`SpendBatchWriter`](../../src/lib/spend/batchWriter.ts) (tsoho na 60s flush tazara, buffer-shigarwa 1,000). Mai daidaitawa ta hanyar:

  | Env var                             | Tsohuwar | Manufar                                            |
  | ----------------------------------- | -------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
  | `OMNIROUTE_SPEND_FLUSH_INTERVAL_MS` | `60000`  | Rike tazarar cikin millise seconds.                |
  | `OMNIROUTE_SPEND_MAX_BUFFER_SIZE`   | `1000`   | Matsakaicin shigarwar da aka adana kafin a watsar. |

Adadin farashin dashboard sune** ba** karanta daga adadin dala-jeri-jeri da aka adana — ana ƙididdige su akan tashi daga ƙididdige ƙididdiga da kuma tebur farashin yanzu duk lokacin da ƙarshen binciken ya gudana. Wannan yana nufin gyara farashin da ba daidai ba (da sake daidaitawa) yana sabunta kididdigar farashin tarihi a baya.

## Dashboard: shafin Kuɗaɗe

Shafin **Kuɗaɗe** yana a `/dashboard/costs`
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/costs/`).
Babban kallonsa shi ne shafin **Bayanin Kuɗaɗe**
(`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/costs/CostOverviewTab.tsx`),
wanda ke loda komai daga `GET /api/usage/analytics`.

Abubuwan da yake nunawa:

- **Katunan kashe kuɗi** — ƙiyasin kuɗin da aka kashe na _Yau (1d)_, _7d_, _30d_, da zangon
  da aka zaɓa. Mai zaɓin zango: `7d`, `30d`, `90d`, `all`.
- **Manyan ma'aunai** — buƙatu a cikin zangon, masu samarwa masu aiki, samfura masu aiki, matsakaicin
  kuɗi ga kowace buƙata.
- **Mai Binciken Kuɗi** — tebur mai iya jerantawa/tacewa wanda aka haɗa bisa **mai samarwa**, **samfuri**,
  **maɓallin API**, **asusu**, ko **matakin sabis**, tare da kuɗi, buƙatu, token, matsakaicin
  kuɗi/buƙata, da kaso na jimilla %.
- **Amfani da token** — jimillar / token na shigarwa / token na fitarwa da rabon shigarwa:fitarwa.
- **Ingancin zaɓin hanya** — adadin komawa madadin, ƙimar komawa madadin, da ɗaukar samfurin da aka nema.
- **Hasashen wata-wata** — yana hasashen kuɗin da za a kashe zuwa ƙarshen wata daga matsakaicin kuɗin yau da kullum na kwanan nan.
- **Kwatanta lokuta** — canjin % tsakanin rabin farko da na biyu na zangon.
- **Jadawali** — yanayin kuɗin yau da kullum, rabon mai samarwa (da'ira), manyan masu samarwa, manyan samfura, kuɗi
  bisa maɓallin API, kuɗi bisa asusu, tsarin amfani na mako-mako, da taswirar zafin ayyuka.
- **Fitarwa** — sauke zangon yanzu a matsayin **CSV** ko **JSON** (maɓallan suna bayyana
  da zarar akwai bayanan kuɗi da ba sifili ba).

Lokacin da babu zirga-zirgar da aka sanya wa farashi, layuka suna nuna alamar "Tsoho / Kyauta" maimakon `$0`,
domin nuna tsarin bibiyar tanadi.

### Ƙananan shafukan Kuɗaɗe masu alaƙa

Yankin Kuɗaɗe kuma yana ɗauke da (duk a ƙarƙashin `/dashboard/costs/`):

- **Farashi** (`/dashboard/costs/pricing`) — duba da sauya farashin kowane samfuri (yana nuna
  shafin Farashi na bai ɗaya).
- **Kasafin kuɗi** (`/dashboard/costs/budget`) — saita iyakokin kashe kuɗi ga kowane fage (yana nuna
  shafin Kasafin kuɗi na bai ɗaya).
- **Rabon Ƙa'ida** (`/dashboard/costs/quota-share`) — tafkunan ƙa'ida na bai ɗaya da kallon saurin
  ƙarewa.

## API karshen

Duk waɗannan suna buƙatar auth gudanarwa (loopback/JWT, ta `requireManagementAuth`) sai dai idan an lura.

### Amfani & ƙididdigar farashi

| Hanya | Ƙarshen Ƙarshe           | Manufar                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/analytics`   | Cikakken nazarin cost/usage : taƙaitawa, yanayin yau da kullun, ta provider/model/API key/account/tier. Tambaya: `range`, `startDate`, `endDate`, `apiKeyIds`, `presets`. |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/utilization` | Yin amfani da keɓaɓɓen keɓaɓɓen mai bayarwa akan lokaci. Tambaya: `range` (`1h`/`24h`/`7d`/`30d`), `provider`.                                                            |

| `GET` | `/api/usage/call-logs`   | Rukunin rajistan kira na kowane-buƙata (samfuri, alamu, farashi, jinkiri, matsayi).                                                                                       |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/quota`       | Matsayin rabon mai bayarwa.                                                                                                                                               |
| `GET` | `/api/usage/proxy-logs`  | Dokokin neman wakili.                                                                                                                                                     |

### Kasafin kudi

| Hanya  | Ƙarshen Ƙarshe           | Manufar                                                                                                 |
| ------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET`  | `/api/usage/budget`      | Takaitaccen farashi + duba kasafin kuɗi don maɓalli API ɗaya (ƙirar tambayar `apiKeyId` da ake buƙata). |
| `POST` | `/api/usage/budget`      | Saita daily/weekly/monthly USD yana iyakance madaidaicin faɗakarwar + don maɓallin API.                 |
| `GET`  | `/api/usage/budget/bulk` | Takaitattun bayanai na kasafin kuɗi a cikin maɓallan API.                                               |

| `POST`   | `/api/pricing/sync`     | Fara aiki tare da hannu daga kafofin waje (LiteLLM).                                                                                                               |
| `GET`    | `/api/pricing/sync`     | Halin daidaitawa na yanzu.                                                                                                                                         |
| `DELETE` | `/api/pricing/sync`     | Share farashin da aka daidaita LiteLLM kawai (`pricing_synced`); model.dev data ware.                                                                              |

## CLI

CLI na OmniRoute yana samar da umarnin kuɗi, amfani, da farashi (waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a cikin
[`bin/cli/commands/registry.mjs`](../../bin/cli/commands/registry.mjs)).

### `omniroute cost`

Rahoton kuɗi da aka tara daga `/api/usage/analytics`.

```bash
omniroute cost                          # kwanaki 30 da suka gabata, an haɗa bisa mai samarwa
omniroute cost --period 7d              # kwanaki 7 da suka gabata
omniroute cost --group-by model         # haɗa bisa provider | model | combo | api-key | day
omniroute cost --since 2026-06-01 --until 2026-06-13
omniroute cost --api-key <key> --limit 50
```

Ginshikai: rukuni, buƙatu, tokens masu shiga/fita, kuɗi (USD), da % na jimilla. Ana buga layin
jimillar gaba ɗaya a ƙarshe (ana ɓoye shi da `--quiet` ko `--output json`).

### `omniroute usage`

```bash
omniroute usage analytics --period 30d [--provider <id>]   # taƙaitaccen kuɗi na kowane mai samarwa
omniroute usage logs [--limit 100] [--follow] [--api-key <k>] [--search <q>]
omniroute usage quota [--provider <id>] [--check]
omniroute usage utilization [--api-key <k>]
omniroute usage history [--limit 100]
omniroute usage proxy-logs [--limit 100]

# Kasafin kuɗi
omniroute usage budget list
omniroute usage budget get [scope]
omniroute usage budget set <amount> [--scope global] [--period monthly]
omniroute usage budget reset [scope]
```

### `omniroute pricing`

```bash
omniroute pricing list [--provider <p>] [--model <m>] [--limit 200]
omniroute pricing get <model>
omniroute pricing sync [--provider <p>] [--force]   # POST /api/pricing/sync
omniroute pricing diff [--model <m>]
omniroute pricing defaults show
omniroute pricing defaults set [--input <p>] [--output <p>] [--cache-read <p>] [--cache-write <p>]
```

> `pricing defaults show` yana karanta `GET /api/pricing/defaults`. Don gyara farashin kowane model
> dabam, yi amfani da shafin dashboard na **Farashi** ko `PATCH /api/pricing`.

## Warware Matsaloli

- **Duk kuɗaɗe suna nuna $0 / "Tsoho / Kyauta".** Model ɗin da ake amfani da su ba su da bayanin farashi.
  Kunna daidaitawar waje (`PRICING_SYNC_ENABLED=true`) sannan gudanar da `omniroute pricing sync`, ko
  saita farashin da hannu ta shafin Farashi / `PATCH /api/pricing`.
- **An sa wa wani model na tarihi farashi ba daidai ba.** Gyara farashin (sake rubuta shi ko sake daidaitawa) — ana
  sake lissafa kuɗi daga adadin token a duk lokacin karanta bayanan nazari, don haka ƙiyasin yana sabuntawa har zuwa bayanan da suka gabata.
