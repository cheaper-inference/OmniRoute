`copilot-m365-web` (wanda aka fi sani da `m365copilot`) yana aika buƙatun taɗi na OpenAI-format ta ingantaccen zaman Microsoft 365 Copilot a `m365.cloud.microsoft/chat`. Wannan haɗin kai ne na ɗan ƙasa mara hukuma: ba a buƙatar tsarin wakili na daban ko na sama da ake buƙata. Mai bayarwa ID, laƙabi da haɗin da aka shigo da su yanzu ba su canzawa. `copilot-web` shine mai bayarwa daban don sabis na mabukaci a `copilot.microsoft.com`.

> **Sabo ga masu samar da kuki na Yanar Gizo?**
>
> Karanta**`docs/getting-started/WEB-COOKIE-GUIDE.md`** don tsarin saitin gabaɗaya, jagorar tantancewa, iyakoki, da warware matsalar kafin bin wannan takamaiman jagorar mai bayarwa.
>
> **Don shigo da kayan gado:** Tabbacin wannan mai bada shine** ba kuki ba** . Kar a kwafi cookies daga `m365.cloud.microsoft`. Tabbacin ma'aunin tambaya ne WebSocket URL da ɓangaren hanya, dukansu suna karantawa daga shafin yanar gizo DevTools.

## Abubuwan da ake bukata

- A asusun Microsoft 365 tare da samun damar zuwa Copilot a `m365.cloud.microsoft/chat`
- Mai bincike na tushen Chromium (Chrome, Edge) don binciken DevTools WebSocket
- Samun damar samfurin da aka nema akan wannan asusun; samuwa ya bambanta ta mai haya da lasisi

## Shiga na asali (asusun aiki ko makaranta)

Zabi**Microsoft 365 Copilot → Connect** da shirya ƙoƙarin shiga. Dashboard ɗin yana amfani da kwararar lambar izini tare da PKCE; baya tambayar Microsoft kalmar sirri ko lambar MFA.

1. Bude a** blank browser tab** , sannan a bude**DevTools → Network** kuma kunna** Ajiye log** . Yi haka kafin kewaya zuwa Microsoft: Windows SSO na iya ƙarewa nan take.
2. Kwafi URL izini da aka nuna ta hanyar maganganun haɗin kuma kewaya zuwa gare ta a cikin shafin da aka shirya. Cika zaɓin asusun Microsoft ko MFA idan an buƙata.
3. A cikin bayanan hanyar sadarwa da aka adana, nemo buƙatun `https://login.microsoftonline.com/common/oauth2/nativeclient` kuma kwafi cikakken sa** Neman URL** .
4. Manna wancan URL a cikin maganganun haɗin kuma haɗa. Kar a liƙa ɗan lambar ko URL adireshin `wrongplace` na ƙarshe: kiran da ake buƙata shine kewayawa na wucin gadi.

Kowane ƙoƙari yana ƙare bayan minti goma kuma ana iya amfani dashi sau ɗaya kawai. Sabar tana duba yanayinta, mai tabbatar da PKCE da maƙasudin tantancewa kafin musanya lambar. Bayan ƙarewa ko gazawar musayar, fara sabon ƙoƙari. Ƙoƙari ba zai iya tsira daga sake kunna sabar ba.

Alamu suna amfani da shagon shaidar haɗin kai da ke akwai da kuma hanyar wartsakewa. Sake tabbatar da haɗi yana buƙatar ainihin Microsoft tenant/object iri ɗaya, ba imel ɗaya kawai ba. Yana adana naming/settings mai aiki kuma yana maye gurbin bayanan da aka shigo da su da ba a daɗe ba. Shiga na asali don asusun work/school ne; Hanyar shigo da gadon ta kasance don haɗin kai da goyan bayan jeri na bene.

Cikakkun kiran sake kiran URL shaida ce ta lokaci ɗaya. Kar a taɓa haɗa shi a cikin hotunan kariyar kwamfuta, rajistan ayyukan, saƙonnin goyan baya ko aikatawa. Wannan kwarara baya buɗe mashigar bincike akan uwar garken, bincika tarihin burauzar ku, ko fara sabar dawo da kiran gida.

## Kame shaidar (hanyar WebSocket )

1. Shiga a `https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat` kuma fara tattaunawa.
2. Bude**DevTools → Network** .
3. Tace**WS** (WebSocket).
4. Danna haɗin Chathub WebSocket. Nemo URL wanda ya fara da `wss://substrate.office.com/m365Copilot/Chathub/`.
5. Daga ta** Neman URL** , kwafi:
   - Ƙimar ma'aunin tambayar `access_token` ( JWT ɗan gajeren lokaci).
   - Yankin hanyar Chathub bayan `/Chathub/` (misali `<oid>@<tenant>`).

Cikakkun URL yana da siffa:

```
wss://substrate.office.com/m365Copilot/Chathub/<oid>@<tenant>?...&access_token=<jwt>
```

6. A cikin OmniRoute, zaɓi**Microsoft 365 Copilot** , zaɓi zaɓin API-key/import na gado, kuma liƙa takaddun shaida a cikin tsari:

```
access_token=<jwt>; chathubPath=<oid>@<tenant>
```

7. Danna** Gwaji Haɗin** kuma** Ajiye** .

## Kame shaidar (shigo da HAR )

Maimakon kwafin dabi'u da hannu daga WebSocket URL, zaku iya fitar da fayil DevTools HAR :

1. Bude**DevTools → Network** .
2. Fara ko ci gaba da tattaunawar Microsoft 365 Copilot (wannan yana haifar da haɗin WebSocket ).
3. Danna-dama a cikin cibiyar sadarwar kuma zaɓi** Ajiye duka azaman HAR tare da abun ciki** .
4. A cikin OmniRoute, yi amfani da** Shigo da fayil .har** maɓalli akan maganganun haɗin mai badawa.

OmniRoute tana duba HAR na Chathub WebSocket URL, ta fitar da `access_token` da `chathubPath`, kuma ta cika filayen shaidar kai tsaye. Shigo da HAR yana yanke ƙarshen JWT kuma yana nuna sauran alamar rayuwa.

> **Kar a taɓa yin ainihin fitarwar HAR, alamar samun dama, ko sabunta alamar.** Ƙimar gwaji da takaddun shaida dole ne koyaushe su kasance masu riƙe da wuri.

## Alamar rayuwa da wartsakewa

`access_token` yana ɗan gajeren lokaci (yawanci**~ 75 min** ). Ba tare da alamar annashuwa mai amfani ba, dole ne ku sake ɗaukar shaidar bayan ƙarewar.

### Sake sabuntawa ta atomatik tare da `refreshToken`

A cikin masu samar da Microsoft 365** Ƙara haɗi** ko** Gyara haɗi** zance, bude** Saitunan ci gaba → Alamar Refresh (na zaɓi)** . Manna alamar refresh Microsoft OAuth don iyakar `substrate.office.com/sydney` kuma ajiye. An rufe abin da aka shigar da shi kuma ba a taɓa cika shi da alamar da aka adana ba. Lokacin gyarawa, bar shi babu komai don kiyaye alamar da ke akwai; manna sabon alamar don maye gurbinsa.

Sa hannu na asali yana samun alamar shakatawa lokacin da Microsoft ta dawo ɗaya. Don shigo da kayan gado, yi amfani da kyautar lambar izini Microsoft mai dacewa; wannan mai bada baya aiwatarwa ko yin alƙawarin kwararar lambar na'urar. Tare da daidaita alamar shakatawa, OmniRoute yana duba `access_token` kafin buƙatun kuma yana sabunta shi kawai lokacin da yake buƙatar shakatawa. Nasarar wartsakewa mai nasara kuma tana ci gaba da jujjuya alamar wartsakewa, gujewa sake kamawa da hannu yayin da alamar wartsake ta ci gaba da aiki.

Idan ba'a saita `refreshToken` ba, dole ne ku sake ɗaukar shaidar WebSocket da hannu bayan kowace ƙarewar.

### Zabin matakin

| Mataki       | Laƙabi      | Surface                 |
| ------------ | ----------- | ----------------------- |
| `individual` | _(default)_ | Mai amfani M365 Copilot |
| `edu`        | `included`  | Ilimi M365 Copilot      |
| `enterprise` | `work`      | M365 Copilot don Aiki   |

Saita matakin ciki** Babban Saituna → Copilot matakin** . Sa hannu na asali bai saita shi ba, don haka asusun aiki dole ne ya zaɓi `enterprise` (alias `work`) da asusun ilimi `edu`, kuma lokacin amfani da ƙididdiga samfurin IDs: waɗanda ke zaɓar sautin nasu, yanayi da nau'in lasisi, amma zaɓin wakili da kira har yanzu suna fitowa daga matakin matakin, kuma rashin daidaituwa na mutum ɗaya na iya gazawa kan ɗan haya na aiki. Canza matakin baya ba da lasisin ƙira. Bayyananniyar ƙetare ƙa'idodin ƙa'idodi suna ɗaukar fifiko kuma yakamata a yi amfani da su kawai lokacin da kuka san ƙimar waya da ake buƙata.

## Model da kwatance

Katalojin na asali ya ƙunshi duk rubuce-rubucen IDs na tsaye da na asali guda huɗu IDs. Katalogin ya tsaya tsayin daka kuma mai zaman kansa na asusu, ba binciken haƙƙin asusu ba: hanyoyin da aka biya da kuma laƙabi masu dacewa suna nan a jera su ko da asusu mai haɗin kai ba zai iya amfani da su ba. Alamun nunin ƙira sun ƙetare sunan mai ba da sabis da kowane samfurin wadatar tukunyar jirgi; mai bada ya rage**Microsoft 365 Copilot** . Alamun alama** Laƙabi** ko** Legacy** suna riƙe abubuwan gano su da taswirar yarjejeniya, ba garantin sigar ƙirar tarihi ba. Wakili mai zaman kansa** Shigo daga / samfuri** umarnin ba ya aiki ga wannan ɗan asalin mai bada WebSocket.

| Model iyali       | Canonical IDs                                                                                    |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Na atomatik       | `m365-copilot`                                                                                   |
| GPT-5.5 / GPT-5.6 | `gpt-5.5`, `gpt-5.5-think-deeper`, `gpt-5.6`, `gpt-5.6-think-deeper`                             |
| Tun da farko GPT  | `gpt-5.4`, `gpt-5.4-quick`, `gpt-5.3`, `gpt-5.3-think-deeper`, `gpt-5.2`, `gpt-5.2-think-deeper` |
| GPT-6             | `gpt-6-think-deeper`, `gpt-6-sol`, `gpt-6.1-sol`                                                 |
| Claude Sonnet     | `claude-sonnet`, `claude-sonnet-think-deeper`, `claude-sonnet-5`, `claude-sonnet-5.5`            |
| Claude Opus       | `claude-opus`, `claude-opus-4.8`                                                                 |

Laƙabi kamar `auto`, `quick`, `think-deeper`, da kuma laƙabin sigar ana kuma rijista. `copilot-m365` da ake dasu , `copilot-m365-claude-opus`, `copilot-m365-gpt-5-6-reasoning` da `copilot-m365-gpt-5-5-chat` sun kasance masu inganci. Rubuce-rubucen IDs yana buƙatar prefix na mai bada (`copilot-m365-web/claude-opus` ko `m365copilot/claude-opus`): da yawa kalmomi na ƙayyadaddun kalmomi ne ko wasu sunaye na masu siyarwa, don haka babur `claude-opus` yana ci gaba da tafiya kamar yadda yake yi ba tare da wannan mai bayarwa ba. Rijista ba alƙawarin da Microsoft ke ba da wannan samfurin ga kowane asusu ba. IDs na tsaye yana bin [kasidar ƙa'idar yarjejeniya](https://github.com/cramt/m365-copilot-proxy/blob/main/packages/core/src/copilot.ts) ta jama'a (taswirar ƙirar-zuwa-sautin da aka fitar da samfuran sa na sane da metadata). Duk bayanan martaba suna riƙe da `availability: "unverified"`; haɗa a cikin kasidar ba jarrabawar lissafi ce mai nasara ba.

- Ƙirar lasisin ƙirar ƙira ta dawo** 403** `m365_model_not_licensed`, ba 502 ba. Makullin kewayawa wanda connection/model guda biyu, yana barin samfura masu lasisi akan haɗin haɗin.
- `InvalidCopilotLicense` ƙin lasisin matakin asusun, an ruwaito shi daban azaman `m365_account_not_licensed`.
- Makullin mai amfani ya dawo** 429** `m365_throttled` da huce alakar. Mahimmanci-shiga gajiyar gajiyar dawowa** 429** `priority_access_exhausted` kuma abin ƙira ne. Sakamakon `OutOfCredits` tare da dawowar `errorCode: "ReasoningTurnExhausted"`** 429** `reasoning_turns_exhausted` a maimakon haka, kuma mai ƙira, ba tare da da'awar sake saiti ba. An ba da rahoton a kan mai haya ɗaya mai aiki don rufe sautunan "Ka yi tunani mai zurfi" yayin da Sautunan Sautunan sauri suka ci gaba da yin hidima; Wannan iyakar abin lura ne na mabukaci, ba wani abu da wannan ma'ajiyar ta sake haifarwa ba. Har ila yau, yin gyare-gyare yana ɗaukar sauran rubuce-rubucen Yi tunanin zurfafa ƙira na `gpt-5.5` da `gpt-5.6` daga juyawa akan waccan haɗin don sanyi iri ɗaya, kuma yana barin Sautunan Sauri da kowane gazawa akan nasa ƙirar. An adana alamun sake gwadawa; sake saitin da ya ɓace ba a tallata shi azaman sanannen lokacin sake saiti.
- A Copilot aminci ƙi (`Disengaged`) ya dawo** 400** `provider_safety_rejection`, lakabin tsaida aminci mai bayarwa. Ba zai taɓa yin la'akari da lafiyar haɗin yanar gizo ba, SDKs abokin ciniki baya sake gwadawa 400, kuma tare da kunna hanyar haɗin gwiwar `PROVIDER_SAFETY_STOPS_TERMINAL` yana tsayawa a can maimakon sake kunna buƙatar akan wani manufa.
- Buƙatun kan haɗin kai ɗaya ana jera su ta hanyar semaphore na asusun da ke akwai. Fi son koma baya na fifiko maimakon a layi daya; serialization kadai baya kawar da iyakar yawan zance na Microsoft.

## Nemi hali da iyaka

- Kayan aiki sune** m-emulated** , ba kiran aikin Microsoft na asali ba. Buƙatun da aka kunna kayan aiki yana amfani da tsararraki guda ɗaya; ba ya kashe wani ƙarni yana zaɓar kayan aiki. Kayan aikin da aka bayyana kawai tare da ingantattun siffofi masu goyan bayan hujja ana mayar dasu azaman kira.
- Amsa mai tsayi da aka tsara tare da misali guda ɗaya an kiyaye shi azaman larabci. Wannan heuristic ne, ba hujjar niyya ba; aikace-aikace dole ne har yanzu aiwatar da nasu izinin kayan aiki.
- Shigar da hoto yana aiki kamar yadda abokin ciniki na gidan yanar gizo ke aika shi. Kowane bangare na hoton (`image_url` a matsayin bayanan URL ko na jama'a HTTPS URL, ko Amsoshi `input_image`) ana loda su zuwa `/m365Copilot/UploadFile` a cikin tattaunawar juzu'i tare da alamar taɗi, saƙon yana nuna shi tare da bayanin `ImageFile` ; rubutun yayi alamar inda ya zauna (`[image 1]`, …). Sabon tattaunawa yana loda kowane hoto a cikin tarihi, ci gaba ɗaya ne kawai sabbin hotunan saƙon. Har zuwa 10 PNG, JPEG, GIF ko WebP hotuna na aƙalla 10 MiB kowanne ( MiB 40 kowace buƙata); ana karanta tsarin daga bytes. ana samun HTTPS URLs ta hanyar jama'a-kawai, mai gadin hoto mai nisa mai ratsa DNS. An kasa uploading ya kasa cika bukatar (** 502** `m365_image_upload_failed`,** 400** `m365_image_rejected` ko `m365_image_invalid`); juyawa baya tafiya ba tare da hoton da aka ba shi ba. Sauran sassan da ba na rubutu ba (audio, fayiloli) ana ƙi su a sarari, ba a jefar da su cikin shiru ba.
- Buƙatun biyan kuɗi yana ci gaba da tattaunawa iri ɗaya kuma yana aika saƙonnin bayan amsa ta baya. Dole ne ya mika wannan amsa daidai: haɗi iri ɗaya, bayanin martabar samfuri da saitin kayan aiki; tarihin da bai canza ba ( sarkar sha256 akan kowane sako); da kuma ids na kiran kayan aikin amsa, ko rubutun sa har zuwa farar fata. Duk wani abu (tarihin da aka gyara, wani zama mai saƙon farko iri ɗaya, amsa daban, tattaunawa marar aiki na tsawon mintuna 30 ko kusa da tashar saƙon Copilot 600, haɗi ba tare da id ba) yana fara sabon tattaunawa tare da tarihin gabaɗayan. Ba a taɓa ci gaba da juyowar da ba ta yi nasara ba, kuma ana sake aika `InternalError` marar abun ciki sau ɗaya a cikin sabon tattaunawa (** 502** `m365_internal_error` idan ya sake kasa). Ana kiyaye ci gaba a cikin ƙwaƙwalwar sarrafawa: sake farawa yana fara sabon tattaunawa.
- Kowane juyi yana gudana a cikin taɗi na wucin gadi na Copilot(`disableMemory=1`): tattaunawar tana kiyaye mahallinta yayin da take gudana kuma ba ta taɓa fitowa a tarihin taɗi na asusun. Gwaje-gwajen samfuri, Gwaji Duk da gwaje-gwajen haɗe-haɗe don haka na iya bincika wannan mai bada ba tare da barin tattaunawa a baya ba.
- Juya baya yana da lokacin aiki na daƙiƙa 120 mara amfani da iyakacin ƙayyadaddun ranar ƙarshe. Amsoshi na ƙarshe kawai, tacewar ci gaba da sokewa ana gudanar da su ta hanyar ɗan ƙasar transport/composer.
- Amsoshin da ke tushen yanar gizo suna kiyaye tushen su. Copilot tana yin alamar layi mai layi tare da haruffa masu sarrafa amfani mai zaman kansa kuma yana aika kafofin azaman `references`; kowace alamar ta zama mahaɗin Markdown mai lamba (`[1](https://…)`, ko URL bare lokacin da Copilot yayi amfani da alamar a matsayin maƙasudin hanyar haɗi) tare da madaidaicin bayanin `url_citation` (`message.annotations`, ko `delta.annotations` akan guntun da ke ɗauke da hanyar haɗin). An watsar da tushen da bai taɓa zuwa ba, don haka alamomin ba su taɓa isa ga abokin ciniki ba. Lokacin yawo, rubutu bayan ambaton yana jira har sai tushen sa ya iso.
- Takaitacciyar dalilin Copilot(`ChainOfThoughtSummary`), lokacin da samfurin ya aika ɗaya, ana mayar da shi azaman `reasoning_content`, ba a matsayin ɓangare na amsar ba. Takaitacciyar da ke sake rubutawa kanta tana daina yawo ba tare da gaza juyowa ba.
- Kafin kowane abun ciki, gazawar licence/throttle tana riƙe da HTTP 403/429 koda lokacin da aka nemi yawo. Bayan an fara abun ciki, gazawa sune kurakuran SSE, ba a taɓa samun nasara ba. Sake rubutawa na sama wanda ba za a iya wakilta shi ta hanyar rafi-kawai kawai kuskuren rafi ba; martanin kayan aiki mara yawo da buffered yana riƙe da rubutu na ƙarshe.
- Lokacin da Copilot ta ƙi haɗin kai kanta, ana ba da rahoton amsarta kamar haka:** 401** `m365_unauthorized` don shigarwar da aka ƙi ko ta ƙare (sa hannu kuma),** 429** tare da `Retry-After` ɗin sa don maƙuwa, da gazawar musafaha in ba haka ba, ba zai taɓa gazawar haɗin kai ba.

## Ƙarfin hoto

`POST /v1/images/generations` tare da `model: "copilot-m365-web/m365-copilot-image"` (alias `m365copilot/m365-copilot-image`) ya zana tare da ginanniyar kayan aikin hoto na Copilot, kamar yadda abokin gidan yanar gizo ke yi. Kowane hoto juyi Copilot ɗaya ne: ya fita tare da zaɓin hoton abokin ciniki na gidan yanar gizo yana saita kuma ya bayyana `GenerateGraphicArt`, kuma hoton ya isa kan shigarwar ci gaba yana nuna wani kayan tarihi akan sabis na Zane na Microsoft. Wannan kayan aikin ya ƙi alamar taɗi, don haka OmniRoute ya ƙirƙira alamar Mai ƙira daga alamar refresh ta haɗin kuma zazzage bytes; Ana aika alamar zuwa `designerapp.officeapps.live.com` kawai .

- Yana buƙatar haɗin sa hannu na asali (alamar wartsakewa); Alamar liƙa ta kasa da** 400** kafin a kashe kowane juyi.
- `n` shine 1–4, juyi daya kowanne. `size` kawai ke jagorantar daidaitawa (fadi, tsayi ko murabba'i), wanda Copilot yana karantawa daga faɗakarwa; Copilot yana zaɓar girman sa.
- Ana mayar da hotuna ta layi: `b64_json` ta tsohuwa, data URL don `response_format: "url"`.
- Hotunan da aka ƙirƙira suna zana kan ƙaramin kasafin kuɗi na yau da kullun, daban da taɗi. Lokacin da aka yi amfani da shi, buƙatar ta kasa da** 429** . Juyi da ke amsawa ba tare da hoto ba ya gaza da** 502** (ko** 400** /** 503** lokacin da aka karanta rubutun Copilot a matsayin ƙi ko matsalar iya aiki); sakon yana cewa.

## Tsarin tsaro

- **Haɗin kai mara izini.** Wannan mai badawa yana jujjuya-injiniyoyi ƙa'idar BizChat WebSocket. Yana iya karya ba tare da sanarwa ba lokacin da Microsoft ya canza wurin ƙarshe.
- Tutar `subscriptionRisk: true` tana nuna cewa haɗin kai ya dogara da ƙarshen ƙarshen mara izini. Hane-hane na asusun yana yiwuwa amma ba a tabbatar ba.
- Alamar shiga JWT ce ta ɗan gajeren lokaci. Bi da shi kamar kalmar sirri: kar a yi, shiga, ko raba shi.
- Alamar wartsakewa tana ba da damar ci gaba zuwa saman Microsoft 365. Ajiye shi da kulawa iri ɗaya da maɓallin API.

## Shirya matsala

### Tabbatarwa ya gaza nan da nan

- Tabbatar cewa kun kwafi daga**WebSocket URL** , ba daga buqatar XHR/Fetch ba. Tabbacin yana cikin ma'aunin tambayar `access_token` na haɗin Chathub WebSocket — ba a cikin taken `Authorization: Bearer`.
- Tabbatar cewa `chathubPath` ya ƙunshi cikakken ɓangaren `<oid>@<tenant>` daga hanyar URL.

### Alamar zata ƙare bayan ~ mintuna 75

Ana sa ran wannan. Ba tare da `refreshToken`, alamar shiga ba ta daɗe ba. Ko dai:

- Sake ƙwace takardar shaidar daga DevTools, ko
- Sanya** Babban Saituna → Refresh token (na zaɓi)** a cikin Ƙara ko Gyara maganganun haɗi don sabuntawa ta atomatik.

### Gwajin Haɗin kai ya wuce amma hira ta dawo 401

Don haɗin da aka ƙirƙira tare da** Shiga da Microsoft** , Haɗin Gwaji yana duba ƙarshen damar alamar shiga kuma yana sabunta alamar da ta ƙare tare da adana alamar refresh, don haka gwajin wucewa yana tabbatar da alamar refresh har yanzu yana aiki.

Don takaddun shaida da aka liƙa, Haɗin Gwaji yana duba sifar shaidar kuma, lokacin da alamar ta ɗauki ƙarewa, cewa alamar da mai badawa zai yi amfani da ita bai ƙare ba (alamar da aka adana ta haɗin haɗin ya sami babban manna; Alamar wartsake tana ƙidaya azaman sabuntawa). Alamar da aka soke kafin ta ƙare har yanzu ta ci jarabawar; hira sai ta dawo** 401** `m365_unauthorized`. Amfani** Shiga tare da Microsoft** sake, ko kuma sake kama sabon takardar shaidar.

### Copilot baya bayyana a cikin jerin masu badawa

Tabbatar an kunna `copilot-m365-web` a ciki** Dashboard → Masu bayarwa** . Ana iya ɓoye mai ba da sabis ta tsohuwa a wasu matakan.
