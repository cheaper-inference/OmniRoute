Matsewar OmniRoute an gina ta ne bisa kwangilolin injuna. Wani yanayi na iya tafiyar da injin guda ɗaya kai tsaye
(`caveman` ko `rtk`) ko kuma tsayayyen jerin matakai da aka tara wanda ke aiwatar da injuna da yawa bisa tsari.

## Hanyoyi

| Hanyar       | Hanyar injin                                                                        | Shigarwa da aka yi niyya                                    |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| `off`        | babu                                                                                | Kiyaye umarni daidai                                        |
| `lite`       | Caveman lite helpers                                                                | Tsaftacewa mai ƙarancin haɗari koyaushe                     |
| `standard`   | Caveman                                                                             | Takaita umarni na harshe na halitta                         |
| `aggressive` | Caveman + history/tool taƙaitaccen bayani                                           | Dogon tattaunawa                                            |
| `ultra`      | Caveman + pruning helpers                                                           | Maido da iyakokin mahallin                                  |
| `rtk`        | RTK                                                                                 | Terminal, shell, gini, gwaji, da fitarwa na git             |

### Fasalin matsewa na OmniGlyph

Injin `omniglyph` (kunshin `omniglyph`, 1.4.0+) yana karɓar fasalin ma'ana mai suna, wanda aka saita
a duniya ta hanyar `omniglyph.profile` a cikin saitunan matsewa ko kowane mataki ta hanyar
saitin mataki na pipeline mai tarin yawa:

| Fasali        | Iyaka                                                                                                     |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `aggressive`  | Tsoho. Manufar da aka auna rasit ɗin da aka buga — tsarin hotuna, takardun kayan aiki da tarihin mai yawa |
| `balanced`    | Yana kiyaye yanayin rayuwa na asali, yana kare juyawa 8 na ƙarshe, yana rufe tsohon tarihin da aka rufe   |
| `coding-safe` | Yana kiyaye iko, tsarin kayan aiki da fitarwa na kayan aiki kai tsaye, yana kare juyawa 12 na ƙarshe      |
| `passthrough` | Hanyoyi ba tare da canzawa ba; an tsallake injin                                                          |

Fasalin shine **rufi, ba bene ba**: `mergeCompressionProfileOptions` a cikin kunshin
yana ƙin barin mai kira ya sake buɗe hanyar da fasalin ya rufe, don haka `preserveSystemPrompt: false`
na kowane mataki ba zai iya sake kunna matsewar tsarin a ƙarƙashin `coding-safe` ba.

An auna akan wannan codebase: `coding-safe` da `balanced` suna ɗaga `minCompressChars` zuwa iyakar sa
kuma suna kiyaye tsarin, tsarin kayan aiki da sakamakon kayan aiki na asali, don haka zaman da bai tara
tarihi ba tukuna yana tsayawa a `below_min_chars` kuma injin baya canza komai. Wannan shine dalilin
da yasa tsoho shine `aggressive` maimakon mafi aminci fasalin.

Kunshin yana warware iyakar samfurin sa da fasalin sa daga saitin muhallin sa. OmniRoute baya taɓa
ba da ikon yanke shawara: adaftar yana haɗa ƙofar samfurin zuwa mafi ƙuntatawar iyakar kunshin,
don haka saitunan muhallin mai masaukin baki na iya iyakance jerin izini kawai, ba zai taɓa faɗaɗa shi
fiye da rasit ɗin da OmniRoute ya auna ba.

## Rijistar Injin

Rijistar yana cikin `open-sse/services/compression/engines/registry.ts`. Injuna suna samar da ƙa'ida guda da suke bi tare:

- `id`: tsayayyen id na inji kamar `caveman` ko `rtk`
- `apply(text, config)`: tsohuwar hanyar aiwatarwa da tsarin bututun da aka jera ke amfani da ita
- `compress(input, config)`: babbar hanyar aiwatarwa wadda ke mayar da rubutu + ƙididdiga
- `getConfigSchema()`: tana mayar da tsarin da ya yi kama da JSON-Schema na ingantaccen saitin config
- `validateConfig(config)`: tana mayar da `{ valid, errors[] }`

Yin rajista yana amfani da `registerCompressionEngine(engine)` (ko `registerEngine` don lokuta na ci-gaba),
wanda ke kiran `assertValidEngine()` da `validateConfig(defaultConfig)` kafin karɓa.
Yi amfani da `unregisterCompressionEngine(id)` don cire inji yayin da tsarin ke gudana.

`strategySelector.ts` yana rajistar injunan da ke zuwa tare da tsarin kafin matsawa ya gudana. Wannan yana ba samfoti,
matsawar lokacin aiki, yanayin jeri, gwaje-gwaje, da injuna na gaba damar amfani da hanyar aiwatarwa iri ɗaya.

### Matsawar bayanin MCP (mai alaƙa)

Wani rijista na daban yana matsa metadata na bayanin kayan aikin MCP a matakin rijista — duba
`open-sse/mcp-server/descriptionCompressor.ts` da [MCP-SERVER.md](../frameworks/MCP-SERVER.md). Yana sake amfani da
ƙa'idojin Caveman amma yana aiki a kan metadata na kayan aiki, ba kan payload na buƙata ba.

### Ƙarin injunan da ke zuwa tare da tsarin

Baya ga Caveman, RTK, da LLMLingua-2, rijistar yana zuwa da wasu ƙwararrun injunan da ba sa jawo asarar bayanai /
na tsari (waɗanda tsarin bututun da aka jera, filin gwaji, da gwaje-gwaje ke amfani da su):

| Inji          | Id              | Abin da yake yi                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CCR           | `ccr`           | Content-Compress-Retrieve (H4): yana maye gurbin manyan tubalan rubutu masu jere da manazarta masu adireshin abun ciki, don a aika tubalan da aka maimaita/manya sau ɗaya sannan a yi nuni da su daga baya. |
| headroom      | `headroom`      | SmartCrusher (H3 + N5): matsawar bayanan tabula ba tare da asara ba ga payload na JSON-array masu tsari iri ɗaya zuwa sifar ginshiƙai ta `[N rows]`.                                                        |
| ionizer       | `ionizer`       | Ɗaukar samfuran layuka daga kai/tsakiya/ƙarshe don manyan tubalan masu tsari iri ɗaya, tare da adana ɓangaren tsakiyar da aka cire a matsayin manazarar CCR mai adireshin abun ciki.                        |
| session-dedup | `session-dedup` | Cire maimaituwar abun ciki tsakanin zagaye bisa adireshin abun ciki (wanda TokenMizer ya yi wahayi): yana cire rubutun da aka riga aka gani a zagayen baya na session ɗin guda.                             |

**Umarni na ƙa'idar dawo da CCR (#8033):** a karon farko da CCR ya maye gurbin ≥1 tubali a cikin
buƙata, injin yana saka saƙon `system` guda ɗaya da ba ya canza sakamako idan aka maimaita shi a farko (yana farawa da
alamar sentinel ta `[CCR protocol]`) domin koya wa mai kira yarjejeniyar alama → kayan aiki: abin da
alamar `[CCR retrieve hash=<24hex> chars=N]` take nufi, cewa dole ne a kwafi hash ɗin ba tare da wani sauyi ba
(duk haruffan hex 24 — kuskuren kwafin hash shi ne mafi yawan dalilin rashin samun "block not found"),
da kuma cewa alamar `[dedup:ref sha=...]` tana nufin "duba baya cikin tarihi", ba "kira kayan aikin"
ba. Ana saka bayanin **kawai idan `tools[]` da mai kiran ya bayyana sun tabbatar cewa zai iya
isa ga `omniroute_ccr_retrieve` da gaske** (`callerSupportsCcrRetrieve()` a cikin
`open-sse/services/compression/engines/ccr/protocolInstruction.ts`) — mai kira na yau da kullum
mai dacewa da OpenAI wanda ba shi da wannan kayan aikin ba zai taɓa samun umarnin kiran abin da
ba zai iya isa gare shi ba. Ana tabbatar da rashin maimaitawa ta hanyar bincika tarihin saƙonni don neman sentinel
kafin sakawa, don haka buƙatun zagaye da yawa (waɗanda ke sake kunna saƙonnin baya) ba sa tara
bayanin sau ɗaya a kowane zagaye.

## Caveman

Yanayin Caveman yana mai da hankali kan taƙaita ma’anar rubutu na yau da kullum:

- yana kiyaye tubalan lamba, URLs, JSON, hanyoyin fayil, da tsararrun bayanai
- yana cire kalmomin cike gurbi, shakku, maimaita mahallin bayani, da dogayen jimlolin haɗawa
- yana goyon bayan fakitin ƙa’idojin fayil masu la’akari da harshe a cikin `open-sse/services/compression/rules/`
- ana ci gaba da samunsa ta tsoffin yanayin `standard`, `aggressive`, da `ultra`

Fuskar dashboard ɗin ita ce `Dashboard -> Context & Cache -> Caveman`.

Rahotannin Caveman na upstream sun nuna raguwar token ɗin fitarwa da `~75%`, matsakaicin tanadin fitarwa na `65%` a gwaje-gwajen ma’auni
tare da kewayon `22-87%`, da kayan aikin matse shigarwa na `~46%`. OmniRoute yana amfani da adadin Caveman na ɓangaren shigarwa
yayin rubuta bayanin tanadin prompt/mahalli da aka jera tare; yanayin fitarwa na Caveman ya kasance wata siffa ta daban
ta halayyar amsa.

## RTK

Yanayin RTK yana mai da hankali kan fitarwar umarni da kayan aiki:

- yana gano nau’ikan fitarwa kamar `git status`, `git branch`, `git diff`, Vitest/Jest/Pytest,
  gwaje-gwajen Cargo/Go, gina TypeScript/Vite/Webpack, ESLint, bincike/shigarwar npm, rajistan ayyukan Docker,
  `find`/`grep` na shell, jerin sawun stack, da rajistan ayyuka na gama-gari
- yana amfani da matatan JSON guda 49 daga `open-sse/services/compression/engines/rtk/filters/`
- yana goyon bayan bututun aiki na bayyanawa irin na RTK: cire ANSI, sauyawa, tsallake sauran aiki idan fitarwa ta dace,
  cire/riƙe layuka, yanke tsawon kowane layi, yanke head/tail/max-line, da koma-bayan idan babu komai
- yana goyon bayan matatan aikin da ke buƙatar amincewa a `.rtk/filters.json` da matatan gama-gari a
  `DATA_DIR/rtk/filters.json`
- yana cire jerin ANSI, hayaniyar ci gaba, maimaitattun layuka, da rubutun samfurin da ba ya taimakawa
- yana kiyaye kurakuran da za a iya ɗaukar mataki a kansu, gargaɗi, taƙaitawa, fayilolin da aka canza, da mahallin ƙarshen fitarwa
- zai iya, idan an zaɓa, riƙe danyen fitarwa da aka ɓoye bayanansa domin dawo da bayanai/gyara matsala ta hanyoyin gudanarwa
  masu tantance sahihanci

Fuskar dashboard ɗin ita ce `Dashboard -> Context & Cache -> RTK`.

Cikakkun bayanan aiki don matatu na musamman, amincewa, tabbatarwa, da dawo da danyen fitarwa suna cikin
[`RTK_COMPRESSION.md`](./RTK_COMPRESSION.md).

Rahotannin RTK na upstream sun nuna tanadin `60-90%` wajen matse fitarwar umarni. Misalin README ɗinsa ya nuna zaman Claude Code
na mintuna 30 yana raguwa daga token `~118,000` zuwa `~23,900`, wato an tanadi `79.7%`.

## LLMLingua-2 (Datsewa Bisa Ma’ana)

Yanayin LLMLingua-2 yana yin **datse token bisa ma’ana** a kan rubutu ta amfani da ƙaramin
mai rarraba token na ONNX, yana cike gurbin injunan Caveman da RTK masu dogaro da ƙa’idoji:

- yana matse rubutu ne kawai a saƙonnin da ba na tsarin ba; ba a taɓa canza tubalan lamba masu shinge da sauran
  abubuwan da ake kiyayewa
- yana gudanar da backend na `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` (ONNX ta `@huggingface/transformers`) a cikin
  worker thread, don haka hasashen model ba zai taɓa toshe event loop na buƙata ba
- ana iya **jerawa tare da wasu** (`stackPriority` 35): a cikin bututun aiki mai jerin injuna yana gudana bayan
  injunan tsari (CCR, session-dedup, headroom, Caveman) amma kafin `ultra`, domin
  datsewa bisa ma’ana ya fi tasiri a kan rubutun da aka riga aka matse ta fuskar tsari — misali
  `rtk -> caveman -> llmlingua`
- yana **fail-open idan kowane kuskure ya faru** (rashin optional deps, ƙaddamar da worker, loda model, inference,
  ko timeout) → ana dawo da rubutun asali ba tare da canji ba, ba a taɓa dawo da kuskure ba

Wurin engine: `open-sse/services/compression/engines/llmlingua/`. Fuskar dashboard ɗin
ita ce `Dashboard -> Context & Cache -> LLMLingua`.

### Model-model

Tsohon model na asali shi ne **TinyBERT** (`atjsh/llmlingua-2-js-tinybert-meetingbank`, ~57 MB,
mai sauri). Ana samun model **BERT-base** mai inganci mafi girma (`Arcoldd/llmlingua4j-bert-base-onnx`,
~710 MB) ta filin `model` na saitunan engine. `@huggingface/transformers`
yana sauke zaɓaɓɓen model a hankali daga HuggingFace Hub zuwa
`${DATA_DIR}/models/llmlingua` a kiran farko (`modelStore.ts`); maimakon haka, saitin
`modelPath` na override yana nuna masa kwafin gida (shigarwa marar intanet / keɓantacciyar hanyar sadarwa).

### Optional dependencies & shigarwa lokacin buƙata

Tarin runtime peer na LLMLingua da za a iya cirewa **na zaɓi ne**. An ayyana package guda biyu a matsayin
`optionalDependencies` a cikin `package.json` kuma ginin production yana barinsu a matsayin **external**
(`scripts/build/prepublish.ts` ba ya haɗa su cikin bundle):

| Package              | Siga (pin) | Bayanan kula                                          |
| -------------------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` | `2.0.5`    | Package na shiga; yana ayyana sauran a matsayin peers |
| `js-tiktoken`        | `^1.0.20`  | Mai tokenization                                      |

An ɗaure `@huggingface/transformers` a `^4.2.0` (ana amfani da shi tare da hanyar embeddings ta gida kuma
ana haɗa sawunsa cikin standalone bundle); `@atjsh/llmlingua-2@2.0.5` yana buƙatar sa a matsayin peer da
`"^3.5.2 || ^4.0.0"`, don haka ana goyon bayan Transformers.js v3 da v4 duka. Tun daga 2.0.4,
`@atjsh/llmlingua-2` baya buƙatar `@tensorflow/tfjs`, wanda ya cire mafi girman mai ƙara
girma guda ɗaya (TensorFlow.js) daga tarin SLM. Package biyu na sama kaɗai ne peers na SLM
da za a iya cirewa. Daidaitaccen `npm install` (dev) yana shigar da tarin zaɓin kai tsaye sai dai idan an tsallake
optional dependencies.

**Dalilin shigarwa lokacin buƙata:** package da aka wallafa a npm, standalone bundle, da Docker image
ana tura su **ba tare da** waɗannan deps ba domin su kasance ƙanana. Idan babu su, matakin tantance dependency
na worker (gwajin resolve na `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` a cikin `worker.ts`) yana gaza kuma engine ɗin
yana **fail-open a ɓoye** — zaɓin LLMLingua ba ya yin komai (ana dawo da rubutu ba tare da canji ba, ba a
rubuta kuskure a log ba). Don kunna shi a muhalli da aka rage, shigar da tarin zaɓin:

```bash
# ɗaure zuwa sigogin da aka ayyana a package.json optionalDependencies
npm install @atjsh/llmlingua-2@2.0.5 js-tiktoken
```

Cire `@tensorflow/tfjs` (2.0.4+) yana kawar da wanda a baya ya fi ƙara girma da ~800 MB
— sauran girman ya ƙunshi runtimes na transformers.js + onnxruntime-node,
tare da model na TinyBERT (~57 MB) da ake saukewa a amfani na farko (ba ta npm ba).

Ga kowane muhalli:

- **Ci gaba / `npm install`** — ana shigar da shi ta atomatik sai dai idan ka wuce `--omit=optional`
  (ko `--no-optional`). Babu wani mataki da ake buƙata.
- **npm na duniya (`npm i -g omniroute`) / mai zaman kansa** — gudanar da umarnin shigarwa na sama a cikin
  kundin package ɗin da aka shigar, ko sake shigarwa ba tare da barin optional deps ba.
- **Docker** — ƙara umarnin shigarwa a cikin wani derived image layer; image ɗin da aka wallafa
  an tsara shi ya zama siriri.
- **VPS (PM2)** — shigar a cikin `node_modules` na manhajar, sannan sake kunna process ɗin domin
  worker ya sake binciken gate ɗin.
- **Raw Next standalone (`npm run build` → `.build/next/standalone/server.js`)** — standalone
  trace ɗin ba ya ɗauke da worker KO optional deps, don haka engine ɗin yana yin fail-open ba tare da sanarwa ba.
  `scripts/build/colocate-standalone.mjs` yana sake amfani da dukansu biyun (worker esbuild +
  optional-dep closure zuwa cikin standalone tree); yana gudana ta atomatik ta hanyar
  `postbuild` npm hook bayan kowane build. Idempotent ne, kuma yana yin fail-soft idan deps ba su nan.

**Tabbatar yana aiki:** idan an zaɓi LLMLingua, rubutun prose na ainihi yana raguwa (engine ɗin
yana daina yin fail-open), kuma request na farko yana jawo sauke model zuwa
`${DATA_DIR}/models/llmlingua`. Da gangan gate ɗin yana binciken `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` kaɗai —
sauran peers ɗin ESM-only ne kuma `require.resolve` yana jefa kuskure a kansu ko da suna nan — don haka
worker zai ci gaba da yin fail-open idan wani peer ba ya nan da gaske a lokacin `import()`.

## Bututun Aiki Masu Jere

Yanayin jere yana gudanar da matakan bututun aiki bi da bi. Tsoho shi ne:

```txt
rtk -> caveman
```

Yi amfani da wannan don zaman wakilin tsara lamba inda umarni ya haɗa fitowar umarni da rubutun ɗan Adam ko na mataimaki. RTK yana fara rage rajistan ayyukan kayan aiki masu hayaniya, sannan Caveman ya matse sauran harshe na yau da kullum.

Ana saita matakan bututun aiki da `stackedPipeline` a cikin saitunan matsewa ko ta hanyar haɗe-haɗen matsewa.

Lokacin da injunan biyu suka rage kaya ɗaya da ya cancanta, tanadin yana taruwa:

```txt
combined = 1 - (1 - tanadin RTK) * (1 - tanadin shigarwar Caveman)
average  = 1 - (1 - 0.80) * (1 - 0.46) = 89.2%
range    = 1 - (1 - 0.60..0.90) * (1 - 0.46) = 78.4-94.6%
```

## Tacewar Bishiyar Samun Dama ta MCP

Tacewar wayo ta bishiyar samun dama ta MCP wani mataki ne na matsewa bayan aiwatarwa wanda ke aiki a kan **sakamakon kayan aikin** MCP, ba a kan umarni ko mahallin ba. Tana nufin ɗimbin bayanan bishiyar samun dama da hotunan yanayin burauza masu tsawo da kayan aiki irin su Playwright, computer-use, da sabar MCP ta sarrafa burauza ke mayarwa.

### Abin da take yi

1. **Cire hayaniya** — tana cire shigarwar generic/text marasa komai (`- generic:`, `- text: ""`)
2. **Naɗe ’yan’uwa** — idan layuka a jere ≥ `collapseThreshold` (tsoho 30) maimaitawar tsari ne, tana naɗe su zuwa layukan farko `collapseKeepHead` (tsoho 10) + taƙaitaccen adadi + layukan ƙarshe `collapseKeepTail` (tsoho 5)
3. **Kiyaye ref** — ba a taɓa anka na `[ref=eXX]` da Playwright/computer-use ke buƙata
4. **Yanke tilas** — idan rubutun bayan naɗewa har yanzu ya wuce `maxTextChars` (tsoho 50,000), tana yanke shi tare da alamar jagorar kewayawa domin wakilin ya iya ci gaba da aiki

### Wurin inji

```txt
open-sse/services/compression/engines/mcpAccessibility/
  index.ts            ← wurin shigar smartFilterText()
  collapseRepeated.ts ← tsarin lissafin naɗe ’yan’uwa
  constants.ts        ← DEFAULT_MCP_ACCESSIBILITY_CONFIG
```

### Saitawa

`compression.mcpAccessibility` ne ke sarrafa ta a cikin saitunan gama-gari (ƙaura 056). Saitin tsoho:

```json
{
  "enabled": true,
  "maxTextChars": 50000,
  "collapseThreshold": 30,
  "collapseKeepHead": 10,
  "collapseKeepTail": 5,
  "minLengthToProcess": 2000
}
```

Ana amfani da tacewar ne kawai ga bayanan sakamakon kayan aiki waɗanda `type` ɗinsu yake `"text"` kuma tsawonsu ya wuce `minLengthToProcess`. Ba ta shafar matse umarni ko bayanan buƙata.

### Tanadin da ake tsammani

60–80% a sakamakon kayan aikin hoton yanayin burauza, gwargwadon sarƙaƙƙiyar shafin. Tsarin lissafin naɗewa O(n) ne bisa yawan layuka kuma yana ƙara jinkiri marar yawa.

## Abubuwan buƙatu / jagorar mai aiki (CCR &-deup-deup)

CCR (`ccr`) da `session-dedup` maye gurbin ko goge rubutu kuma su bar a** alama** a baya. Wannan sashe shine bayanin mai aiki don haɗa abokin ciniki sama, karanta alamomin biyu, da maidowa ko gano abin da ya ɓoye. An rufe injiniyoyin kowane injin a sama; wannan shine Layer "yadda zan gudanar da shi".

### Transport & wuraren ƙarshe

Sabar MCP da ke fallasa kayan aikin CCR ana iya isa ta hanyoyi uku. Tsohuwar tushe URL shine `http://localhost:20128` (`OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL`):

| Sufuri                                    | URL / umurnin                           | Bayanan kula                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| HTTP mai gudana (abokin ciniki, shawarar) | `http://localhost:20128/api/mcp/stream` | Abokan ciniki MCP na ƙarshe suna magana da.                                                                                                               |
| Legacy SSE singleton                      | `http://localhost:20128/api/mcp/sse`    | Tsofaffin jigilar singleton. Yana** yana rufewa** lokuta masu gudana; yi amfani da abokan cinikin `streamable-http` akan `/api/mcp/stream`, ba wannan ba. |
| gidan rediyo                              | `omniroute --mcp`                       | Don tsarin IDE MCP wanda ke haifar da tsari. Babu tutar `--dev` ; `omniroute --mcp` shine wurin shiga stdio.                                              |

### Matakan ajiya (abin da ya tsira daga sake farawa)

CCR a** mataki biyu** kantin sayar da, ba a cikin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya-kawai:

- **Zafafan matakin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya** — per-block cap 2 MiB, per-principal 16 MiB, duniya 64 MiB. Shigar da tsoho zuwa 24 h TTL (mafi girman kwanaki 7). Cikakken dawo da MCP daga ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya an rufe shi a 256 KiB; Har yanzu ana iya samun duk wani abu mafi girma ta hanyar kewayon / grep.
- **Matsayin SQLite mai dorewa** (`ccr_blocks`, `COMPRESSION_CCR_DURABLE_STORE`, a kan ta tsohuwa) — yana goyan bayan matakin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya don haka toshe ya tsira daga korar LRU, sake farawa, ko dawo da saukowa a wani misali. Layin yana kiyaye TTL na toshe, don haka shingen da ya wuce TTL ɗinsa ya ɓace daga duka matakan biyu.

Masu gadi guda uku suna kiyaye iyakar — mai ɗorewa mai ɗorewa-kawai lokacin da:

1. toshe ya ƙare** 512 KB** (`MAX_DURABLE_BLOCK_BYTES`);
2. lokacin aiki shine lokacin gudu na girgije (babu diski na gida);
3. `COMPRESSION_CCR_DURABLE_STORE=false`.

Don haka "toshe ya ɓace" ba labarin sake kunnawa ba ne: toshe sub-512 KB a kan wani yanki na gida, lokacin da ba na girgije ba yana tsira daga sake kunnawa ta hanyar matakin mai ɗorewa har sai TTL ɗinsa ya ƙare. Duba [Maganar Muhalli](../reference/ENVIRONMENT.md) don ma'auni.

### Abubuwan buƙatun abokin ciniki

- **Maɓalli (stdio):** mai kiran stdio ba ya ɗauke da shaidar kowane buƙatun, don haka yana tabbatarwa da `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` (wanda aka saita a cikin `env` uwar garken MCP ). Idan ba tare da shi ba mai kiran ba a san sunansa ba kuma kayan aikin da ke buƙatar shugaban makaranta na iya ƙasƙanta.
- **`tools[]` shine sharadin allura.** An yi alluran umarnin dawo da tsari CCR (#8033).** kawai** lokacin da buƙatun da aka yi tallan `tools[]` ya tabbatar abokin ciniki zai iya kai ga `omniroute_ccr_retrieve` a zahiri. Wasan wasa ne na bi-biyu wanda ke daure da mai raba (`__`, `.`, `/`, `:`) — bai taba zama rukunonin rukunin igiya — ba don haka sunan da aka raba suna kamar `mcp__gateway__omniroute_ccr_retrieve` har yanzu ana kirgawa. `tools[]` da ba ya nan ko bai dace ba yana nufin ba a yi wa umarnin _ba_ allura ba, ta ƙira.
- **`codemode:false` (abokan ciniki OpenCode ).** OpenCode ya gaza zuwa Yanayin Code, wanda ke tallata kayan aikin sa na asali kawai zuwa samfurin kuma** ba** saka kayan aikin MCP akan jerin `tools[]` na asali. Saita `codemode: false` akan shigarwar uwar garken MCP domin ana tallata kayan aikin `omniroute_ccr_*`. Wannan a** abokin ciniki** saitin, ba OmniRoute daya ba. Ba ya samun alluran umarnin CCR har yanzu: OpenCode suna suna `<server>_<tool>` kayan aiki, don haka uwar garken da ake kira `omniroute` ta fallasa `omniroute_omniroute_ccr_retrieve`, kuma `_` baya ɗaya daga cikin masu raba daidai.
- **Shugabanni.** An keɓance ma'ajiya da dawo da su kowane ingantacciyar shugaba (kowane maɓallin API ). Maɓalli yana ganin tubalan sa kawai.

### Iyaka maidowa & pagination

Ana yanke sakamakon rubutun kayan aikin MCP a kusa da `mcpAccessibility.maxTextChars` (tsoho `50000`). Maido da `full` na toshe an sanya shi a 256 KiB —** babu cikakken maidowa mara iyaka** . Lokacin da toshe `chars=N` yana da girma, shafi shi maimakon ja da duka:

- Ka'idodin yatsan hannu:**`chars > 40000` → amfani da `mode`** , ba komai ya cika ba.
- `mode:"grep"` — regex `pattern` madaidaicin layi ta layi, har zuwa layukan daidaitawa 1,000 (`unique: true` drops repeats), mafi arha don "nemo layin da nake buƙata."
- `mode:"lines"` — kewayon layi na 1; yana buƙatar duka `start` da `end`.
- `mode:"head"` / `mode:"tail"` — first/last `n` layi.
- `mode:"stats"` — layi, hali, da byte ƙidaya ba tare da jiki ba.

Yi amfani da `mode:"lines"` tare da `start`/`end` don yin tafiya mai tsayi a cikin shafuka maimakon neman duka abu lokaci guda.

### Sunan kayan aiki

A halin yanzu ana kiran kayan aikin maido da `omniroute_ccr_retrieve` (prefix ɗin `omniroute_` ƙwararriyar aikin kwanan wata ce don karon sunan uwar garken). Sunan kayan aiki mai ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun kayan aiki da gyara don sakamakon MCP JSON da aka yanke tsakiyar kirtani ana bin diddigin su azaman masu biyo baya; wannan shafin yana tsara halin yanzu, da aka aika kawai.

### Zagayowar rayuwa

Streamable-HTTP zaman mara amfani da uwar garken girbe su; `OMNIROUTE_MCP_SESSION_IDLE_MS` ne ya daidaita taga mara aiki , yana komawa `DEFAULT_MCP_SESSION_IDLE_MS` a cikin `open-sse/mcp-server/httpTransport.ts`. Alamar zaman girbi mara aiki shine HTTP 404 tare da `Unknown Mcp-Session-Id` — maganin shine sake haɗawa ko sake farawa zaman,**ba** don juya maɓalli, canza iyaka, ko sake suna kayan aiki.

## Iyaka da abubuwan da aka cire

**Ba a taɓa matsa embeddings ba.** `open-sse/handlers/embeddings.ts` ba ya taɓa kiran kowane
engine na matsawa — jikin buƙata/amsa yana wucewa kai tsaye zuwa executor ba tare da an taɓa shi ba.
Wannan tsarin gini ne a halin yanzu (embeddings da chat completions suna da handlers daban-daban), ba
binciken lokacin aiki ba, amma yana nufin damuwar karkatar da vector da ke cikin #8034 ba ta da wata fuskar fallasa
a hanyar embeddings.

**Tatar cirewa ta kowane model/endpoint (#8034).** Don chat completions, mai gudanarwa zai iya ambata
model ids / wuraren `provider/model` waɗanda ba za a taɓa matsawa ba — kariya mai amfani idan
daga baya aka haɗa matsawa kusa da wata hanya da ke maƙwabtaka da embeddings, kuma tana da amfani gaba ɗaya
ga duk wani model wanda prompt ɗinsa ke da muhimmancin daidaituwar byte-for-byte (deterministic evals, prefixes
masu tasirin cache, da sauransu).

- Filin saiti: `exclusions?: string[]` a kan saitin matsawa na duniya baki ɗaya
  (`GET`/`PUT /api/settings/compression`), ana adana shi ta hanyar namespace na matsawa na `key_value` da ake da shi
  (`src/lib/db/compression.ts`) — babu sabon table.
- Shafin dashboard: **Dashboard → Compression → Exclusions**
  (`/dashboard/compression/exclusions`).
- Tsarin pattern: `*` ne kaɗai wildcard. Ana tserewa kowane sauran regex metacharacter a cikin pattern
  kafin matching, don haka `gpt-5.6` yana dacewa da ainihin wannan rubutu kawai, ba zai taɓa dacewa da `gpt-5x6` ba
  (mai kariya daga ReDoS, mai iyaka, babu nested quantifiers). Patterns suna yin matching ba tare da la’akari da
  bambancin manyan da ƙananan haruffa ba, a kan bare model id da kuma haɗin `provider/model` — `gpt-5-6`, `openai/gpt-5-6`,
  da `openai/*` duk suna aiki, kuma `*` shi kaɗai yana cire kowane model.
- Matching: `isCompressionExcluded()` / `normalizeCompressionExclusions()` a cikin
  `open-sse/services/compression/exclusions.ts`. `chatCore.ts` yana bincika target da aka cire
  nan da nan bayan warware saitunan matsawa, **kafin kowane engine ya fara aiki**, kuma yana ɗaukar matching
  daidai kamar an kashe matsawa a duniya baki ɗaya — jikin buƙatar yana kasancewa
  iri ɗaya byte-for-byte tabbatacce. Ana rubuta tsallakewar ta hanyar `writeCompressionSkip(..., "excluded")` domin
  a iya ganinta a nazarce-nazarce.
- Tsohon ƙima (jerin da babu komai/babu shi): daidai yake da halayen kafin #8034 — babu abin da aka cire.

## Sanannun iyakoki

- **LLMLingua-2 (SLM) yana buƙatar zaɓaɓɓun dependencies su kasance a wuri guda.** Worker ɗin yana aiki ne kawai a cikin
  production build idan an haɗa `@atjsh/llmlingua-2` + peers a wuri guda cikin
  `dist/node_modules` (duba `scripts/build/colocateOptionals.mjs`, #4286). Idan babu su,
  engine ɗin zai fail-open (ya mayar da ainihin rubutun). Yanzu warware wurin worker ba ya dogara da
  `import.meta.url` (yana mutuwa a cikin standalone bundle) — yana jingina ne ga runtime
  cwd / `argv[1]`.
- **Language packs na Caveman na `de` / `fr` / `ja` ba su cika ba.** Suna zuwa da ƙa’idojin `context` +
  `filler` + `structural`, amma babu packs na `dedup` / `ultra`, saboda haka ƙarfin `ultra`
  bai fi na `full` ba ga waɗannan harsunan (suna amfani da ƙa’idojinsu kaɗai — babu
  silent fall-back zuwa ƙa’idojin Turanci na `dedup`/`ultra`, wanda zai gurɓata rubutun wasu harsuna).
  `en` / `es` / `id` / `pt-BR` sun cika. Ana maraba da gudummawar `dedup.json` + `ultra.json`
  ga packs ɗin da ba su cika ba.
- **Stacked telemetry yana lissafa engines da suka yi compression ne kawai.** Matakin stacked-pipeline wanda
  engine ɗinsa ya yi aiki amma ya samar da tanadin 0 % yana mayar da `stats:null`, don haka ba ya bayyana a cikin
  `engineBreakdown` — ba za a iya bambanta shi da matakin da aka tsallake ba. Bambance
  "ya yi aiki, 0 %" da "an tsallake" zai buƙaci sauyi ga breakdown-model, kuma an ɗage shi zuwa gaba.
