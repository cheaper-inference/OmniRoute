> Ajiye 15-95% na mahallin da ya cancanta ta atomatik. Don taƙaitaccen bayani, duba [sashen Matsewa na README](../README.md#%EF%B8%8F-prompt-compression--save-15-95-eligible-tokens-automatically).

## Bayani Gabaɗaya

OmniRoute yana aiwatar da bututun matse umarni mai tsarin sassa wanda ke gudana **tun da wuri** kafin buƙatu su isa ga masu samarwa na sama. Wannan yana nufin ana samun tanadin token ɗinka ba tare da ka lura ba — babu buƙatar canza tsarin aikinka.

```
Buƙatar Abokin Ciniki
  → Mai Zaɓar Dabarar Matsewa
    → Akwai saitin combo na musamman? → Yi amfani da saitin combo
    → An kai ma'aunin farawa ta atomatik? → Yi amfani da yanayin auto
    → Yanayin tsoho? → Yi amfani da saitin gama-gari
    → A kashe? → Tsallake matsewa
  → Yanayin Matsewa da Aka Zaɓa
    → A kashe: Babu matsewa
    → Lite: Amintaccen tsaftace sarari/tsarawa (~15%)
    → Standard: Cire kalmomin cikewa irin na Caveman (~30%)
    → Aggressive: Tsufar tarihi + taƙaitawa (~50%)
    → Ultra: Tacewa bisa ƙa'ida + rage tubalan lamba (~75%)
    → RTK: Tacewar fitarwar terminal/kayan aiki mai fahimtar umarni (kewayon 60-90% a sama)
    → Stacked: Bututun injuna da yawa mai bin tsari, yawanci RTK sannan Caveman (kewayon cancanta 78-95%)
  → Buƙatar da Aka Matse → Mai Samarwa
```

## Yanayin Matsewa

### A kashe

Ba a yi amfani da matsewa ba. Dukkan saƙonni suna wucewa ba tare da canji ba.

### Yanayin Lite (~15% tanadi, <1ms jinkiri)

Yanayin da ya fi aminci — gyaran tsari. Rage sakamakon kayan aiki yana haifar da asara, saboda haka tsohuwar hanya
(ba tare da `allow-lossy`, `engine:lite` ko zaɓin suna-haɗe ba) tana amfani da lite tare da
`compressToolResults: false`: sakamakon kayan aiki na baya yana isa ga mai samarwa ba tare da canji ba.

| Dabara                   | Bayani                                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `collapseWhitespace`     | Haɗa layukan da ba komai a jere da sararin da ke ƙarshen layi                                     |
| `dedupSystemPrompt`      | Cire saƙonnin tsarin da suka maimaitu                                                             |
| `compressToolResults`    | Takaita sakamakon kayan aiki na baya zuwa iyakar lite (mai haifar da asara: sai da izinin buƙata) |
| `removeRedundantContent` | Cire umarnin da aka maimaita                                                                      |
| `replaceImageUrls`       | Taƙaita bayanan hoton base64 na URI                                                               |

**Mafi dacewa da:** Amfani na ko da yaushe, da ayyukan da aminci ke da matuƙar muhimmanci.

### Yanayin Standard (~30% tanadi)

An samo wahayi daga [Caveman](https://github.com/JuliusBrussee/caveman) — yana cire kalmomin cike da jimlolin da suka yi tsawo yayin da yake kiyaye ma'ana:

- Yana cire kalmomin cike ("don Allah", "Ina tsammani", "a taƙaice", "a zahiri")
- Yana taƙaita jimloli masu tsawo ("domin" → "zuwa", "sakamakon" → "saboda")
- Yana cire sassauta magana ta ladabi ("Za ka damu ne idan...", "Idan za ka iya yiwuwa...")
- Dokokin regex sama da 30 da aka tsara don buƙatun shirye-shirye

**Mafi dacewa da:** Ayyukan shirye-shirye na yau da kullum, da ƙungiyoyin da ke kula da kuɗi.

### Yanayin Aggressive (~50% tanadi)

Gudanar da tari mai wayo don dogayen zaman aiki:

- **Tsufar Saƙonni** — ana ƙara matse tsofaffin saƙonni a hankali
- **Matsawar Sakamakon Kayan Aiki** — ana takaita ko ɓoye dogayen sakamakon kayan aiki (layukan farko/ƙarshe,
  tace layukan da suka dace, da taƙaita maɓallan JSON)
- **Kare Mutuncin Tsari** — yana tabbatar da cewa ma'auratan `tool_use` + `tool_result` suna nan daidaitattu
- **Sanin Girman Mahallin** — yana mutunta iyakokin alamomin kowane samfurin

**Mafi dacewa da:** Zaman gyaran kurakurai masu tsawo, da manyan rumbunan lamba.

### Yanayin Ultra (~75% tanadi)

Matsawa mafi girma don yanayin da alamomi suke da matuƙar muhimmanci:

- **Yanke Bisa Hasashe** — yanke alamomin rubutu bisa maki
- **Kiyaye Tsari** — tubalan lamba masu shinge, lambar cikin layi, URLs da masu gano abubuwa
  ana sanya su alamar riƙewa sannan a sake haɗa su ba tare da canji ba; ba a taɓa yanke su ba
- **Matakin SLM na zaɓi** — ƙaramin samfurin gida zai iya gyara yankan idan an tsara shi
- Mai zaman kansa daga Yanayin Aggressive: ba ya gudanar da tsufar saƙonni, matsawar sakamakon kayan aiki
  ko taƙaitaccen madadin (rashin nasara kawai a matakin SLM zai iya tura aikin madadin ta aggressive)

**Mafi dacewa da:** Lokacin da kuke ci gaba da kai wa iyakokin mahallin.

### Yanayin RTK (60-90% na iyakar upstream)

An tsara yanayin RTK don dogayen sakamakon kayan aiki da ke bayyana a zaman wakilan shirye-shirye:

- Yana gano rukunin umarni/sakamako kamar `git status`, `git diff`, `git log`, masu gudanar da gwaje-gwaje,
  gina TypeScript/Vite/Webpack, ESLint/Biome/Prettier, npm audit/installs, rajistar Docker, sakamakon
  kayan aikin gine-gine, da kuma sakamakon shell na gama-gari
- Yana amfani da fakitin tace JSON daga `open-sse/services/compression/engines/rtk/filters/`
- Yana shigo da matatun RTK TOML schema v1 daga fayilolin `filters.toml` na aiki ko na duniya, tare da ingancin
  gwajin cikin layi da sarrafa amana ga fayilolin aikin
- Yana ƙunshe da matatu 56 da aka gina a ciki tare da samfuran tabbatarwa cikin layi
- Yana cire jerin sarrafa ANSI, sandunan ci gaba, layukan da suka maimaitu, da hayaniyar da ba ta buƙatar aiki
- Yana kiyaye gazawa, kurakurai, gargaɗi, fayilolin da aka canja, taƙaitawa, da wutsiyar dogon sakamako
- Yana goyan bayan matatun aikin da ake sarrafa amana, matatun duniya, da zaɓin dawo da ɗanyen sakamako da aka ɓoye

**Mafi dacewa da:** Zaman wakilai masu amfani da shell, gini, gwaji, git, grep, da rubuce-rubucen sakamakon fayiloli.

### Yanayin Stacked (78-95% na iyakar da ta cancanta)

Yanayin Stacked yana gudanar da injunan matsawa da yawa cikin tsari tabbatacce. Tsohuwar bututun aiki ita ce:

```txt
RTK -> Caveman
```

Wannan tsari yana fara taƙaita sakamakon tashar/kayan aiki, sannan ya yi amfani da taƙaita ma'ana ta Caveman ga
ragowar buƙatar rubutu ta halitta. Ana iya tsara bututun Stacked a duniya ko ta hanyar haɗin matsawa da aka
sanya wa haɗin hanyoyin turawa.

**Mafi dacewa da:** Mahallin gauraye mai manyan rajistan ayyukan kayan aiki tare da umarnin mutane ko taƙaitawar mataimaki.

## Lissafin Tanadin Upstream

OmniRoute yana bayyana tanadin matsawa daga tushe biyu: ma’aunin gwajin ayyukan upstream da kuma
haɗin injunan OmniRoute da kansa.

| Tushe   | Adadin README na upstream da aka yi amfani da shi a nan                                                                                   |
| ------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Caveman | `~75%` raguwar token na fitarwa, matsakaicin tanadin fitarwa na gwaji `65%`, kewayon `22-87%`, da kayan aikin matsawar shigarwa na `~46%` |
| RTK     | Tanadin fitarwar umarni na `60-90%`; zaman misali na token `~118,000 -> ~23,900`, ko tanadin `79.7%` (`~80%`)                             |

Ga bayanan kayan aiki/mahalli masu karo da juna, haɗin OmniRoute na asali yana jera injunan kamar haka:

```txt
RTK -> Caveman
```

Haɗaɗɗen tanadin na ninkawa ne, ba na tarawa ba:

```txt
haɗaɗɗe = 1 - (1 - tanadin RTK) * (1 - tanadin shigarwar Caveman)
matsakaici = 1 - (1 - 0.80) * (1 - 0.46) = 89.2%
kewayo     = 1 - (1 - 0.60..0.90) * (1 - 0.46) = 78.4-94.6%
```

Wannan adadin `78-95%` yana aiki ne idan RTK da Caveman dukansu za su iya rage bayanan shigarwa/mahalli iri ɗaya.
Yanayin fitarwar martanin Caveman daban yake: idan an kunna shi, yi amfani da tanadin fitarwa na Caveman (`65%`
a matsakaici, `~75%` a babban adadi, kewayon `22-87%`). Jimillar tanadin kuɗin amfani ta dogara da haɗin prompt/fitarwar ku.

### Abin da "cancanta" yake nufi a zahiri

Babban kewayon 15-95% gaskiya ne, amma yana aiki ne kawai ga bayanai masu **maimaituwa ko yawan surutu** — layukan
kuskure masu maimaituwa, log na build da ke cika wuri da gargaɗi iri ɗaya, ko fitarwar `grep`/karanta-fayil mai girma fiye da kima. Wannan
**ba** yana nufin kowace buƙata za ta tanadi adadin haka ba.

An tabbatar ta hanyar gwaji (`tests/unit/compression/stacked-compression-tool-result-savings.test.ts`): gudanarwar
`stacked` (RTK + Caveman) a kan tubalin `tool_result` mai tsarin Anthropic wanda ya ƙunshi layukan kuskure iri ɗaya guda 300
ya samar da **tanadin token na 95.93% / tanadin haruffa na 96.26%** — daidai cikin kewayon da aka tallata.
Amma idan aka gudanar da wannan pipeline ɗin a kan fitarwar kayan aiki ta yau da kullum, wadda ba ta da maimaituwa (jerin sakamakon `grep` mai tsabta,
ɗan gajeren karatun fayil, rubutun tattaunawa na yau da kullum), daidai yake samar da **tanadi kusa da sifili**, saboda
babu wani abu mai maimaituwa da za a cire, kuma `validateCompression()` (`validation.ts`) yana ƙin aika
sake-rubutawar da za ta cire ko sauya tubalan lamba, URLs, kanun labarai, nau’ikan sigogi, ko masu gano constants na ALL-CAPS.

Wannan hali ne da ake tsammani kuma mai aminci, ba bug ba ne: zaman coding wanda galibi yake karantawa/grep na fayiloli masu tsabta zai
ga ɗan ƙaramin jimillar tanadi ko da an kunna matsawa gaba ɗaya, yayin da zaman da ya ci karo da loop mai gazawa
ko linter mai yawan surutu zai ga cikakken kewayon 78-95% a kan wannan zirga-zirgar. Kada ku ɗauki ƙaramin
kason jimillar tanadin zama guda a matsayin hujjar cewa an saita matsawa ba daidai ba — da farko ku bincika ko
ainihin fitarwar kayan aikin tana da maimaituwa.

## Tsarawa

### Dashboard

Je zuwa `Dashboard → Context & Cache`:

- **Caveman** — zaɓin yanayi, fakitin harshe, samfoti, da tsoffin saitunan duniya
- **RTK** — samfotin tace umarni, saitunan tsaron RTK, da kundin tacewa
- **Compression Combos** — bututun injuna masu suna da aka sanya wa haɗin routing
- **Auto-Trigger Threshold** — kunna matsawa ta atomatik idan adadin tokens ya wuce ƙayyadadden mataki

### Sake-kebantawa ta kowane Combo

A cikin `Dashboard → Context & Cache → Compression Combos`, sanya combo na matsawa ga
combo na routing:

```txt
Combo: "free-tier-fallback"
  Compression Combo: "coding-agent-stack"
  Pipeline: RTK -> Caveman
  Targets:
    1. if/kimi-k2.7-code
    2. if/qwen3.8-max-preview
```

Wannan yana ba ka damar amfani da matsawa mai matakai a kan masu samar da sabis na kyauta/na
coding, yayin da kake riƙe da yanayin lite a kan biyan kuɗi.

Wannan sanya **Per-Combo Override** ya bambanta da override na
**routing-combo compression mode** (Default/Off/Lite/Standard/Aggressive/Ultra/Codex Responses — tsarin
filin yana kuma karɓar `rtk`, `stacked` da `omniglyph`) — wannan override ba ya zaɓar wani
bututun compression-combo mai suna; kawai yana saita filin `compressionMode` da
`resolveCompressionPlan` ke dubawa. Ana iya saita shi ko dai a kan katin combo
(`Dashboard → Combos`) ko, tun daga #6760, ga kowane combo na routing a cikin jerin
"Assign to routing" da ke cikin `Dashboard → Context & Cache → Compression Combos`, kusa da
akwatunan zaɓin sanya bututu da aka bayyana a sama. Dukkan waɗannan wurare suna adanawa ta
wurin endpoint ɗin `PUT /api/combos/{id}` iri ɗaya.

### Override na kowace buƙata

Aika taken buƙata `x-omniroute-compression` don override tsarin matsawa na buƙata guda ɗaya. Yana
da fifiko mafi girma — yana rinjayar override na combo na routing, bayanin martaba mai aiki,
auto-trigger, da Default na panel. Ana watsi da ƙimomin da ba a sani ba (ba a taɓa ƙin buƙatar ba),
kuma babban maɓallin kunnawa na duniya har yanzu yana iko da komai: idan an kashe matsawa a
duniya, taken ba zai iya kunna shi ba. Ƙimomi:

| Ƙima          | Tasiri                                                                                                                            |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `off`         | Babu matsawa ga wannan buƙatar.                                                                                                   |
| `default`     | Bayanin martabar Default da panel ya samo (yana watsi da bayanin martaba mai aiki). Ana barin injunan da ke rasa bayanai a kashe. |
| `safe`        | Daidai yake da rashin saka taken.                                                                                                 |
| `allow-lossy` | Yana riƙe da tsarin ma'aikacin wannan buƙatar, har da taƙaitawa, matatun dacewa, da sake-rubuta salo.                             |
| `engine:<id>` | Inji guda ɗaya idan an kunna shi, misali, `engine:rtk`. Wannan shi ne zaɓin kunna wannan injin don kowace buƙata.                 |
| `<combo>`     | Combo mai suna, ana fara dacewa da suna (ba tare da la'akari da manya da ƙananan haruffa ba), sannan da id.                       |

Ba tare da `allow-lossy`, `engine:<id>`, ko combo mai suna ba, ba a amfani da injunan da ke rasa
bayanai. Lokacin da aka rage darajar Default, auto-trigger, ko override na routing mai rasa
bayanai, OmniRoute yana riƙe da matakan da aka gane marasa rasa bayanai daga bututun da aka
tsara mai matakai, cikin tsarin da aka tsara. Yana komawa zuwa `session-dedup → lite` ne kawai
idan babu wani mataki marar rasa bayanai da aka tsara da ya tsira.

Samfotin **Effective pipeline** na Settings panel yana nuna tsarin da ake amfani da shi a al'ada.
Lokacin da auto-trigger zai iya aiki, samfotin ɗaya yana kuma nuna tsarin da zai yi aiki bisa
sharaɗi, domin daidaita panel da ƙudurin runtime da ba shi da header.

Ana mayar da tsarin da aka yi amfani da shi a cikin taken amsar
`X-OmniRoute-Compression: <mode>; source=<source>`, inda `<source>` yake ɗaya daga cikin
`request-header`, `routing-override`, `active-profile`, `auto-trigger`, `default`, ko `off`.

### API

```bash
# Samo saitunan matsawa
curl http://localhost:20128/api/settings/compression

# Sabunta saitunan matsawa
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/settings/compression \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"defaultMode":"stacked","autoTriggerMode":"stacked","autoTriggerTokens":32000}'

# Duba takamaiman bayanan RTK/stacked
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/compression/preview \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"mode":"rtk","messages":[{"role":"tool","content":"npm test output here"}]}'

# Jerin fakitin tace RTK
curl http://localhost:20128/api/context/rtk/filters

# Gwada RTK kai tsaye tare da metadata na umarni na zaɓi
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/context/rtk/test \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"command":"npm test","text":"FAIL tests/example.test.ts\nError: boom"}'
```

## Abin da Ake Karewa

Injin matsewa **koyaushe yana kiyaye:**

- ✅ Tubalan lamba (masu shinge da na cikin layi)
- ✅ URLs da hanyoyin fayil
- ✅ Tsarukan JSON da bayanai masu tsari
- ✅ Masu ganowa da alamomin fasaha da aka kare
- ✅ Kalmomin lissafi
- ✅ Ma'anar kiran kayan aiki/ayyuka
- ✅ Umarnin tsarin (a yanayin lite)

Maido da ɗanyen fitarwa na RTK yana ɓoye maɓallan API na gama-gari, bearer tokens, Slack tokens, AWS access keys,
kalmomin sirri, tokens, da sirrika kafin a adana komai.

### Cache-Aware Compression

Wasu masu bayarwa (kamar Anthropic tare da caching da sauri) tallafi** sauri caching** , wanda ke ba su damar adana sassa na hanzari don rage farashi da latency. Lokacin da aka kunna caching, matsananciyar matsananci na iya zahiri** ciwo** aiki saboda yana canza alamun cache, yana lalata cache ɗin.

Tsarin `cachingAware.ts` yana warware wannan ta hanyar** gano mahallin caching** kuma** daidaita dabarun matsawa** bisa ga haka.

#### Yadda yake aiki

1. **Gano mahallin caching** — Yana bincika jikin buƙatun don alamun `cache_control`
2. **Gano masu samar da caching** — Yana bincika idan mai bada manufa yana goyan bayan caching
3. **Daidaita dabara** — Rage darajar `aggressive`/`ultra` zuwa `standard` don masu samar da caching
4. **Tsallake saurin tsarin** — tsarin faɗakarwa yawanci ana adana su, don haka kar a matsa su

Mai taimakon dabara kuma yana dawo da tutar `deterministicOnly`, amma maginin shirin yana cinye dabarar — kawai babu abin da ke karanta tuta a yau.

#### Misali Code

```ts
import {
  detectCachingContext,
  getCacheAwareStrategy,
} from "@omniroute/open-sse/services/compression/cachingAware";

const body = {
  model: "anthropic/claude-sonnet-4.5",
  messages: [{ role: "user", content: "Hello" }],
  cache_control: { type: "ephemeral" }, // ← Cache marker
};

const ctx = detectCachingContext(body, { provider: "anthropic" });
// → { hasCacheControl: true, provider: "anthropic", targetFormat: null, isCachingProvider: true }

const strategy = getCacheAwareStrategy("aggressive", ctx);
// → { strategy: "standard", skipSystemPrompt: true, deterministicOnly: true }
```

#### Lokacin amfani

Matsi-sane da matsawa shine** ko da yaushe a kan** — ba saitin da ake bukata. Yana farawa a duk lokacin da matsawa ke kunne kuma mai bada manufa yana goyan bayan caching da sauri (Anthropic, OpenAI, da sauransu); ba a buqatar bayyanannun alamomin `cache_control` — mai bada caching shi kaɗai ke haifar da raguwa, kuma alamomi kaɗai ba sa taɓa yin (ganowar alamar tana ciyar da cache telemetry, ba shawarar dabarun ba).

OpenRouter yana ƙidaya a matsayin mai ba da caching kawai don ƙirar `anthropic/*` : Buƙatun lambar Claude suna kiyaye wuraren karya `cache_control` (4 a mafi yawan) kuma mai gadi yana kare prefix, yayin da kowane dangin OpenRouter (`openai/`, `google/`, `qwen/`,…) suna tsigewa kuma ba a tsare su.

### Cigaba Tsufa

Dogayen tattaunawa suna tara saƙo da yawa, amma tsofaffin juyi sun zama marasa dacewa. module `progressiveAging.ts`** yana wulakanta saƙon ta hanyar nisa** (nisa da aka auna daga ƙarshen zance). Tare da abubuwan da aka aika (`verbatim: 2, light: 2, moderate: 3`):

- **Juyawa 2 na ƙarshe (nisa ≤ 2)** : Ci gaba da magana
- **Nisa 3** : Caveman matsawa (cire filler)
- **Distance 4 +** : Takaitattun saƙonnin mataimaka; saƙonnin masu amfani sun rage zuwa layin farko, wanda aka rufe a haruffa 120; sauran rawar da ba a taba ba. Ƙaddamar da tsarin, saƙonnin da suka riga sun tsufa da kuma sabon saƙon mai amfani koyaushe ana kiyaye su da baki ba tare da la'akari da nisa ba. Babu wani abu da aka sauke kai tsaye, kuma rukunin `light` ba shi da isassun abubuwan da aka aika (`light` daidai `verbatim`).

#### Misali Code

```ts
import { applyAging } from "@omniroute/open-sse/services/compression/progressiveAging";

const messages = [
  { role: "system", content: "You are a helpful assistant" },
  { role: "user", content: "What is 2+2?" },
  { role: "assistant", content: "4" },
  // ... 50 more turns ...
];

const { messages: aged, saved } = applyAging(messages, {
  verbatim: 3, // last 3 turns: verbatim
  light: 8, // distance <= 8: lite compression
  moderate: 20, // distance <= 20: caveman compression
  fullSummary: 5, // required by the type, not read by the banding code
  // distance > 20: summarized (assistant) / first line kept (user)
});

// saved = number of tokens saved
```

Ci gaban tsufa shine** ko da yaushe a kan** don yanayin `aggressive` — mataki na 2 ne na `compressAggressive()`. Yanayin Ultra baya gudanar da shi. Yana da tasiri musamman ga:

- Zaman coding na dogon lokaci

#### Yadda allura ke aiki

`applyOutputStyles()` (`open-sse/services/compression/outputStyles/apply.ts`) yana warware zaɓin akan kasidar (ids ɗin da ba a san su ba da kuma salon da ba su dace ba an jefar da su, ba za a taɓa yin kuskure ba; zaɓin da ya warware babu salo ya bar jikin ba ya canzawa, an tsallake shi azaman `no_styles`), yana haɗa umarnin da aka zaɓa a cikin tsari na kasida, yana ƙara sashin iyakokin.** sau ɗaya** (tare da jumlar aminci, `SAFETY_BOUNDARIES` ko fassararsa, lokacin da aka zaɓi `less-code` ko `ponytail` ), kuma ya fara toshe tare da alamar idempotency guda ɗaya (`[OmniRoute Output Styles]`), don haka sake nema babu-op. Lokacin da harshen da aka warware (duba zaɓin Harshen da ke ƙasa) yana da fassarar, ana allurar koyarwar gida maimakon Ingilishi.

On a body with a non-empty `messages` array, the idempotency check runs before the content bypass: when the `[OmniRoute Output Styles]` marker is already in the top-level `system` field (a string or a content-block array) or in a system message with string content, the body is left unchanged as `already_applied` and no keyword check runs. Otherwise a content bypass (`shouldBypassCavemanOutputMode()` in `open-sse/services/compression/outputMode.ts`) checks the text of the last three messages, whatever their role, and skips the styles for the whole turn when that text matches its security, irreversible-action or clarification keywords, or an order-sensitive sequence: `first`, `then`, `after that`, `before`, `rollback` or `backup` followed within 240 characters by `delete`, `drop`, `migrate`, `deploy` or `release`. The bypass runs while the **Auto-Clarity Bypass** toggle (`cavemanOutputMode.autoClarity`, on by default) is on; turning the toggle off skips the keyword check.

Lokacin da hanyar wucewa ta ba da damar juyawa ta hanyar, `placeSystemInstruction()` (fayil iri ɗaya), wanda ba ya haifar da sabon `messages[0]`, ya sanya toshe a farkon waɗannan ya samo:

1. Babban saƙon tsarin tare da abun ciki na kirtani: an haɗa toshe bayan rubutun sa.
2. Filin babban matakin `system` : an haɗa toshe bayan rubutun kirtani, ko ƙara shi azaman sabon toshe rubutu zuwa tsararrun toshe abun ciki.
3. Sakon tsarin farko na farko tare da abun ciki na kirtani: an haɗa toshe bayan rubutun sa.
4. Babu ɗayan abubuwan da ke sama: toshe yana shiga cikin sabon saƙon tsarin a ƙarshen `messages`.

A jikin da ba tare da tsararrun `messages` ba (ko tare da fanko), babu abin da ke wucewa da ke gudana kuma ba a tuntuɓar filin `system` na sama. Ana haɗe katangar bayan rubutun filin `instructions`, sai dai idan wannan filin ya riga ya ƙunshi alamar `[OmniRoute Output Styles]`, a cikin wannan yanayin an bar jikin ba canzawa kamar `already_applied`. Lokacin da jiki ba shi da kirtani filin `instructions` amma yana ɗaukar `input` (kirtani ko tsararru), toshe ya zama `instructions`, yana maye gurbin duk wani ƙimar da ba kirtani ba. Jikin da ba shi da filin `instructions` kirtani ko kirtani ko tsararru `input` ba a canzawa kuma a tsallake shi kamar `no_messages`.

`compressToolResult()` a cikin `open-sse/services/compression/toolResultCompressor.ts` yana matsawa rubutun sakamakon kayan aiki tare da** 5 dabaru** . Yana gwada su a cikin wannan tsari, kuma dabarar da aka kunna ta farko wacce binciken ya yi daidai da abun ciki ya yanke shawarar sakamakon:

1. **`fileContent`** : abun ciki na layuka 3 ko sama da haka wanda aƙalla layi ɗaya, watsi da jagorar shigarwa, yana farawa da `import `, `export `, `function `, `class `, `const `, `let `, `var ` ko `return ` (keyword plus a space), ko tare da `if`, `for` ko `while` biye da `(` ko ` (` 5, tsakiya tare da 20s na ƙarshe tare da 0s.
2. **`grepSearch`** : abun ciki tare da aƙalla layi ɗaya na nau'in `<path>:<digits>:`, inda rubutun da ke gaban mallaka na farko ba shi da farar fata, yana kiyaye waɗannan layin kawai, a mafi yawan 30, sannan ƙidaya kowane ƙarin matches da jerin fayilolin da suka dace; duk sauran layi an sauke. Ɗayan irin wannan layi ya isa ya haifar da dabarun, don haka layin log da ya fara da tambarin lokaci kamar `12:30:45` kuma yana ƙidaya.
3. **`shellOutput`** : fitarwa wanda ya ƙunshi jerin ANSI CSI (`ESC[` sai lambobi ko semicolons sai harafi, kamar yadda a cikin lambobin launi) ko `$` wanda ke biye da farar sararin samaniya a ko'ina cikin rubutun ya rasa waɗannan jerin (sauran tserewa, kamar `ESC[?25l` ko jerin lakabin taga OSC, ana kiyaye su) kuma yana kiyaye layuka 50 na ƙarshe, tare da maimaita layi. Domin wannan cak yana gudana kafin `json` da `errorMessage`, JSON ko kuskuren fitowar da ke dauke da irin wannan `$` ba ya isa gare su yayin da ake kunna `shellOutput`.
4. **`json`** : lodin JSON sama da harufa 2,000 wanda ke farawa da `{` ko `[` (bayan farin sarari na zaɓi) kuma an taƙaita fassarar abubuwa sama da 7 yana adana abubuwa 5 na farko da na ƙarshe 2 da jimillarsa, kuma abu yana riƙe da maɓallai 20 na farko, tare da kowane abu mai gida ko qear, maye gurbinsa da `{…N keys}` (na wani ma'ajin ƙima). alamar `_remaining_<N>_keys` yana ƙirga maɓallan sun wuce 20 na farko. Ana kwafi ƙima mai ƙima gaba ɗaya, don haka abu mai maɓalli 20 ko ƙasa da haka ba tare da ƙima ba ana sake saka — kaɗan kaɗan ya sami haruffa kuma ya tsaya baya canzawa.
5. **`errorMessage`** : fitowar da ta ƙunshi, a ko'ina kuma a cikin kowane harafi, `error:`, `error ` (kalmar ta biyo bayan sarari, kamar yadda yake cikin `no error found`), `[error]`, `exception:`, `exception `, `[exception]` ko `traceback` suna kiyaye layin farko, layi 10 na gaba da 3 na ƙarshe, tare da alamar `… [N frames elided] …` a maimakon layin da ke tsakanin su. Alamar tana bayyana ne kawai lokacin da fiye da layukan 13 suka bi layin farko, don haka fitowar kuskure na layi 14 ko ƙasa da haka ba a taƙaice ba (a 12 ko 13 layukan maimaita 3 na ƙarshe da aka riga aka kiyaye).

Bayan dabarar ta yi daidai, ko da wacce ba ta ajiye komai ba, ba a gwada dabarun da suka biyo baya. Lokacin da dabarar da ta dace ba ta adana alamun ƙididdiga ba (tsawon ÷ 4, tarawa) — misali fayil mai kama da layi na layi 25 ko ƙasa da haka, ko tsararrun JSON sama da haruffa 2,000 tare da abubuwa 7 ko ƙasan — injin mai tayar da hankali yana riƙe da ainihin sakamakon kayan aiki: duka masu kira (`compressAggressive()` da `compressAnthropicToolResultBlock()`) suna dawowa ƙasa lokacin da `saved` ya dawo ƙasa. `compressToolResult()` ta fitarwa. Matakin-sakamakon kayan aiki ba shine kalma ta ƙarshe ba: taƙaitaccen bayanin faɗuwar injin ɗin na iya gajarta saƙon `tool` ko `function` fiye da haruffa 8,192 (`maxTokensPerMessage`, 2,048, sau 4).

#### Lokacin amfani

Matsawar sakamakon kayan aiki shine mataki na 1 na injin mai ƙarfi (`compressAggressive()` a cikin `open-sse/services/compression/aggressive.ts`), don haka yana gudana a cikin yanayin tashin hankali kuma a cikin matakin `aggressive` na bututun da aka tara. Yana matsawa BuɗeAI-siffar `tool` da `function` saƙonnin da rubutu a cikin Anthropic `tool_result` tubalan. Kowace dabara tana da nata sauyawa a ƙarƙashin `aggressive.toolStrategies`, duk ta tsohuwa. A cikin dashboard, masu sauyawa suna cikin shafin Caveman** Na ci gaba** duba yayin da matsawa ke kunne kuma yanayin tsoho yana da ƙarfi.

### Bututun da aka tara

Yanayin da aka tara yana gudana** injuna da yawa a jere** — yawanci RTK na farko (60-90% tanadi akan kayan aiki), sannan Caveman akan ragowar rubutun (~ 46% tanadin shigarwa). Kunna, wato** 78-95% kewayon cancanta** (duba Lissafin Tattalin Arziki na Sama): matsakaicin `1 - (1 - 0.60..0.90) × (1 - 0.46)` ≈ 89%.

#### Yadda yake aiki

```
Input (1000 tokens)
  → RTK (command-aware filter) → 200 tokens
    → Caveman (filler removal) → 108 tokens
  → Output (108 tokens, ~89% savings)
```

#### Lokacin amfani

Yi amfani da yanayin da aka tara don:

- Kayan aiki-nauyin aiki yana gudana (ƙididdigar asali, bincike)
- sarrafa tsari mai tsada
- Lokacin da kuke buƙatar matsakaicin ajiyar alama

Ana saita bututun da aka ɗora ta hanyar saitin matsawa `stackedPipeline` na duniya, ko kuma ta hanyar haɗaɗɗiyar matsi mai suna da aka sanya wa mahaɗin kewayawa (duba Per-Combo Override a sama) — ba ta hanyar auto-combo `modePack` ba (wannan filin ne kawai ya sake yin awo na zaɓin samfurin combo, kuma `stacked` ba ingantaccen sunan fakiti bane).

## Sauya Saitunan Haɗin Matsewa

Za ka iya sauya yanayin matsewa na gaba ɗaya **ga kowane haɗi** don daidaita halayya
ga lokutan amfani daban-daban:

```json
{
  "id": "coding-combo",
  "strategy": "priority",
  "config": {
    "weights": { "taskFit": 0.5 },
    "modePack": "quality-first"
  },
  "compressionOverride": "aggressive"
}
```

Wannan yana da amfani ga:

- **Haɗin rubuta lamba**: Yi amfani da yanayin `aggressive` don zaman aiki masu tsawo
- **Haɗin tambaya da amsa cikin sauri**: Yi amfani da yanayin `lite` don amsoshi masu sauri
- **Haɗin da suka dogara sosai da kayan aiki**: Yi amfani da yanayin `stacked` don samun mafi girman tanadi
- **Haɗin samarwa**: bar sauyin saitin a kashe ga masu samar da caching — daidaitawar da ke la'akari da cache wadda take aiki koyaushe tana saukar da `aggressive`/`ultra` zuwa `standard` kai tsaye
  (babu yanayin `cache-aware` da za a iya zaɓa)
