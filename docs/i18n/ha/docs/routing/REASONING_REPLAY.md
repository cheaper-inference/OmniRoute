> **Tushen gaskiya:** `src/lib/db/reasoningCache.ts`, `open-sse/services/reasoningCache.ts`** An sabunta ta ƙarshe:** 2026-06-28 — v3.8.40

OmniRoute yana ɗaukar mataimakiyar `reasoning_content` wanda aka samar ta hanyar ƙirar yanayin tunani kuma yana sake kunna shi a bayyane akan buƙatun juyi da yawa lokacin da mai samarwa na sama ya buƙaci shi. Wannan yana kawar da kurakuran HTTP 400 waɗanda tsauraran masu samarwa ke ɗagawa lokacin da tarihin tattaunawar abokin ciniki ya ɓace tunanin farko.

## Me Yasa Wannan Akwai

Yawancin masu samar da yanayin tunani sun ƙi bibiya sai dai idan** Saƙon mataimaki na baya ya haɗa da ainihin `reasoning_content`** . Mai zuwa yana dawo da 400 tare da saƙonni kamar:

```
Param Incorrect: The reasoning_content in the thinking mode must be passed back to the API.
```

Amma abokan ciniki na yau da kullun (Cursor, Cline, Roo Code, OpenAI SDK) cire `reasoning_content` daga tarihin da suke sake kunnawa. OmniRoute yana dawo da shi daga ma'ajiyar gefen uwar garken don haka buƙatar da ake gani a sama ta daidaita. Fitowa ta #1628 ta gabatar da juriya ga matasan memory/SQLite don haka cache ya tsira daga tsarin sake farawa.

## Gine-gine

```
Turn N (assistant generates):
  → response contains reasoning_content + tool_calls
  → if requiresReasoningReplay(provider, model): cacheReasoningFromAssistantMessage()
      writes (memory + DB), one entry per tool_call.id within the request scope
  → forward response to client (which may or may not retain reasoning)

Turn N+1 (client sends follow-up):
  → translator detects: requiresReasoningReplay(provider, model) === true
  → for each assistant message with tool_calls and no reasoning_content:
      lookupReasoning(buildToolCallCacheKey(scope, toolCalls[0].id)) → memory → DB
      hit  → msg.reasoning_content = cached; recordReplay()
      miss → msg.reasoning_content = "" (legacy fallback for older DeepSeek)
  → upstream sees consistent history → no 400
```

Kamawa yana faruwa a cikin `open-sse/handlers/chatCore.ts` (shafukan biyu, a wuraren kiran `cacheReasoningFromAssistantMessage` guda biyu). Sake kunnawa yana faruwa a cikin `open-sse/translator/index.ts` bayan tilasta makirci amma kafin aikawa.

Ana adana shigarwar-kiran kayan aiki a ƙarƙashin `buildToolCallCacheKey()`, narkar da iyakar buƙatu da id ɗin kiran kayan aiki, don haka id iri ɗaya da wani maɓalli API ko zaman ke amfani da shi baya zaɓi shigarwar. Iyalin ya fito daga `buildReasoningCacheScope()`: maɓalli API, da maɓallin zaman lokacin da buƙatar tana da ɗaya. Rukunin `tool_call_id` da dashboard don haka suna nuna wannan narkar da waɗancan shigarwar.

Maɓalli (ba kayan aiki ba) maɓallai maɓallai ne daban-daban: `buildAssistantMessageCacheKey()` yana narkar da iyakar zaman tare da daidaitaccen kwafin tsarin OpenAI-har zuwa wancan lokacin, saboda DeepSeek yana buƙatar dalilin _kowane_ gaba ɗaya sau ɗaya `tools` ya kasance. Don Maƙasudin Manufofin-API (misali `opencode-go/deepseek-v4-flash`, wanda aka zagaya zuwa `/responses`) jiki na sama yana ɗaukar `input`, ba `messages`, don haka `translateRequest()` (`open-sse/translator/index.ts`) ya ba da rahoton kwafin pivot ɗin da ya narkar da shi ta hanyar zaɓin sake kira kuma wuraren da aka kama suna narkar da wannan kwafin. Fas ɗin sake kunna martani yana gudana akan pivot OpenAI don kowane tsari na tushe, don haka ana sake kunna abokan cinikin Anthropic Saƙonnin (Claude → OpenAI → Responses).

## Adana — Hybrid Memory + SQLite

Hanya mai zafi tana amfani da `Map` a cikin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya (LRU-by-halitta) mai goyan bayan tebur SQLite don dawo da haɗari da ganuwa dashboard.

| Layer            | Aiwatarwa                                      | Manufar                                  |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya | `Map` in `open-sse/services/reasoningCache.ts` | Binciken sauri, an kori mafi tsufa a 200 |
| DB               | tebur `reasoning_cache` (`src/lib/db/`)        | Naci gaba da farawa, ƙididdigar tuƙi     |

Rubuce-rubuce tafi zuwa duka biyu. Ya karanta tuntuɓar ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya da farko, sannan komawa zuwa DB (DB hits ana inganta su zuwa ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya). gazawar DB ba mai mutuwa ba ce — cache a cikin ƙwaƙwalwar tana ci gaba da hidimar hanya mai zafi.

**Matsaloli:**

- TTL: `2h` (`TTL_MS = 2 * 60 * 60 * 1000`)
- Matsakaicin shigarwar ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya: `200` (`MAX_MEMORY_ENTRIES`)
- Korar: mafi tsufa `createdAt` farko

## Bayanan Ayyuka

- **Tsaftacewa:** `cleanupReasoningCache()` yana goge bayanan da suka ƙare kuma yana gudanar da `DELETE FROM reasoning_cache WHERE expires_at <= unixepoch('now')`. Ma'aikatan kiwon lafiya suna kiran wannan lokaci-lokaci.
- **Farfadowa da hatsari:** Bayan sake kunnawa, ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya ba ta da komai amma har yanzu DB tana riƙe da shigarwar da ba ta ƙare ba. Binciken farko na `tool_call_id` da aka ba shi shine bugun DB ; dubawa na gaba shine bugun ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya.
- **Babu dalili, babu cache:** `cacheReasoningFromAssistantMessage` yana dawo da `0` lokacin da saƙon mataimakin ba shi da filin `reasoning_content` / `reasoning`, don haka martanin da ba a yi tunani ba bai biya komai ba.
- **Rubutun kuma an gated:** duka wuraren kira a cikin `chatCore.ts` (marasa yawo da yawo) suna kiran `cacheReasoningFromAssistantMessage()` kawai lokacin da `requiresReasoningReplay(provider, model)` yake `true` — iri ɗaya ne ke tantance bayanan gefen karantawa. Shigar da ba ta taɓa mai ba da maimaitawa ba ta daina biyan kuɗi don rubutawa, sabuntawar fihirisa, da try/catch akan kowane martani mai ɗaukar hankali.
- **Masu ba da ƙayyadaddun ayyuka:** Lokacin da `requiresReasoningReplay` shine `false` kuma tsarin manufa shine OpenAI, mai fassara** tsiri** kowane filin `reasoning_content` daga saƙonnin masu fita — OpenAI Ƙarshen Taɗi baya karɓa.

## Muse Opaque-Mallakar Dalili

Muse (`muse-code`) ya dawo da tunani mara tushe (`encrypted_content`). Sake kunna shi a ƙarƙashin wani asusu na daban ko bayanan da aka ɗauka ya gaza sama, don haka buƙatun OAuth na asali suna amfani da ikon matakin-zaman maimakon jujjuyawar kowane-buƙata (`src/sse/services/museSessionOwnership.ts`, tilastawa a `src/sse/handlers/chat.ts`). Haɗin maɓallin maɓalli na API da aka zaɓa da ƙayyadaddun maɓalli-kawai API suna riƙe da hanyoyin da suke da su ba tare da wannan yanayin mallakar ba.

- Bayar da tsayayyen zaman ID akan kowane juyi: `x-omniroute-session-id`, `x-omniroute-session`, `x-session-id`, ko `x-codex-session-id`; a madadin amfani da `prompt_cache_key`, `session_id`, `conversation_id`, ko `metadata.session_id` a cikin jiki (a cikin wannan tsari na gaba). IDs bata ko mara komai ya dawo 400 lokacin da asusun OAuth biyu ko fiye na asali ke aiki; tare da asusun OAuth guda ɗaya babu wani abin da za a juya zuwa gare shi, don haka irin wannan buƙatar yana ci gaba da buɗewa. Iyalin ya haɗa da maɓallan API-key OmniRoute ID mai kira.
- Sabbin zama masu zaman kansu zagaye-robin a cikin asusun OAuth na asali masu aiki da tsarin haɗin maɓalli na API ya ba da izini, wanda aka yi oda ta hanyar haɗin ID. Round-robin skips asusu dangane da sanyi, tare da matsayi na ƙarshe (`banned`, `expired`, `credits_exhausted`), ko ƙirar ƙira don ƙirar da aka nema; Rashin gazawar Muse na sama yana rikodin sanyaya da kuma ɓarnar yanayi kamar sauran masu samarwa. Haɗin da aka tilastawa zahiri ya ƙetare zagaye-zagaye don sabon zama amma ba zai iya ƙetare mai shi ba.
- Har sai mai shi ya ba da amsa mai nasara, zaman wanda babu mai shi ya zama babu shi za a sake yin da'awar zuwa asusu mai lafiya. Bayan amsawar farko ta nasara, zaman yana jingina ga mai shi har sai ya yi kwanaki 30 ba aiki (`MUSE_SESSION_IDLE_TTL_MS`).
- Kowane ci gaba da sakamakon-sakamakon kayan aiki yana tsayawa ga mai zaman: haɗin kai, ainihin asusu, da ƙididdiga-ƙirƙirar ƙira, sake dubawa kafin yunƙurin sama, gami da sake sabunta bayanan shaidarka. Amsoshi suna kiran nassoshi IDs/item da Ƙirar Kammala Taɗi kayan aiki-kiran IDs dole ne an yi rikodin wannan zaman da tsara.
- rikodin SQLite `key_value` a cikin sararin sunan `muse_session_ownership` (`src/lib/db/museSessionOwnership.ts`) ya ci gaba da mallakar mallaka da siginan jujjuyawa a duk sake farawa. Kowane iƙirari da mayar da martani yana sabunta tambarin ayyukan zaman; zaman da ba shi da aiki fiye da `MUSE_SESSION_IDLE_TTL_MS` ana yanke shi ( fil ɗin mai shi, tuta mai aiki da rikodi na opaque/reference hashes), aƙalla sau ɗaya kowane minti 10 kuma a cikin batches masu iyaka, don haka daga baya sake kunna tarihinsa ya kasa rufe a matsayin wanda ba a san mai shi ba. Rubuce-rubucen sun ƙunshi haɗin IDs da hashes na ainihin asusu, takaddun shaida, abun ciki mara kyau, da nassoshi na ci gaba, ba asiri ko tarihin tattaunawa ba. Abokan ciniki dole ne su riƙe su aika da cikakken tarihin su da kuma dalilan da ba su da tushe.
- Dalilin da ba a sani ba ko na waje, mai bacewar mai shi, asusun mai shi babu, ko canjin tsara tare da rikodi na tarihi ya gaza rufewa tare da fayyace 4xx/503 — bai taɓa yin shuru ba gaɓar gazawar lissafi kuma bai taɓa barin tunani ba.
- Ana ɗaukar maɓallin maɓalli iri ɗaya kawai lokacin da zaman ba a yi rikodin tarihin sake kunnawa ba; zaman rikodi yana ci gaba da kasa rufewa har sai an fara sabo.
- Amsar da ba komai a sama (`upstream_empty_response` / `empty_response`, babu abin da aka fitar ga mai kira) ana sake gwadawa a mafi yawan sau biyu akan mai wannan, bayan 0.5 s sannan 1.5 s (`MUSE_EMPTY_RESPONSE_RETRY_DELAYS_MS`). Idan duk yunƙurin ba komai bane, ana dawo da ainihin 502 ba tare da sanyaya ko alama ba, don haka amsa mara kyau ba ta kulle mai shi ba.
- Duk buƙatun `muse-code` ta ƙetare karatun ma'auni-cache yana karantawa da rubutawa, gami da rubuce-rubucen yawo, don haka abin da aka ɓoye ɓoye ba zai iya samun maigidan zama na daban ba.

Masu gadin koma baya: `tests/unit/muse-session-ownership.test.ts`, `tests/unit/muse-session-ownership-retention.test.ts`, `tests/unit/muse-empty-response-retry.test.ts`, da `tests/unit/chatcore-semantic-cache.test.ts`.
