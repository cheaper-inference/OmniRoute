## Bayani

Injin Raba Ƙayyadadden Amfani yana rarraba ƙayyadadden amfani na mai samarwa wanda ya danganci lokaci (misali, taga ta awa 5 ta Codex, buƙatu 1500/h na Kimi) cikin adalci tsakanin maɓallan API da yawa waɗanda ke amfani da haɗin guda.

**Matsalar da yake warwarewa:** OmniRoute yana wakiltar maɓallan API da yawa zuwa asusun mai samarwa na sama ɗaya. Ba tare da tsarin rabawa ba, tarin buƙatu kwatsam daga maɓalli A zai iya cinye ƙayyadadden amfanin mai samarwa na wannan awa, ya bar maɓallan B da C a toshe har sai tagar ta sake farawa. Injin yana hana hakan ta hanyar:

1. Bibiyar yawan amfani mai motsi na kowane maɓalli bisa kowane ma'auni (%, buƙatu, token, $).
2. Aiwatar da tsarin rabon adalci mai amfani da duk ƙarfin da ake da shi: maɓalli na iya aro daga rabon da ba a amfani da shi muddin jimillar tafkin ba ta kai cikar amfani ba.
3. Tilasta sakamakon a cikin hanyar aiwatarwa mai sauri (`chatCore.ts`) kafin buƙatar ta isa ga mai aiwatarwa na sama.

## Algorithm: Daidaita-Share Aiki-Kiyaye

An aiwatar a cikin `src/lib/quota/fairShare.ts`.

### Hanyoyi

| Sharadi                                    | Yanayin     | Hali                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------ | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `globalUsedPercent < saturationThreshold`  | **Karimci** | Maɓalli na iya aro har zuwa iyakacin duniya ban da jimlar cinyewa |
| `globalUsedPercent >= saturationThreshold` | **Tsanani** | Aiwatar da daidaitaccen rabon adalci                              |

Default `saturationThreshold = 0.5` (env `QUOTA_SATURATION_THRESHOLD`).

Waɗannan hanyoyi guda biyu suna bayyana tafkin a cikin tsohuwar yanayin aro, `saturation`. Wurin ruwa a yanayin `consent` koyaushe yana aiki da tsauraran ƙa'idodi, komai jikewa. Dubi [Bayar da Lamuni (per-pool opt-in)](#consented-lending-per-pool-opt-in).

### Shawarar kowane-girma

Ga kowane nau'i mai aiki a cikin tafkin, injin yana lissafta:

```
fairShareAllowed = poolLimit × (allocationWeight / 100)
consumed        = current rolling value for this key (from QuotaStore.peek)
remaining       = fairShareAllowed - consumed
```

Don ma'aunin `percent`, `consumed` ya fito ne daga littafin sifa maimakon (duba [Kashi na maɓalli: sifa ta kowane maɓalli](#percent-dimensions-per-key-attribution) ).

Sannan:

- **`policy = hard`** : idan `consumed > fairShareAllowed` da yanayin ya kasance m →** toshe** .
- **`policy = soft`** : idan `consumed > fairShareAllowed` da yanayin ya kasance m →** hukunta** (rasa fifiko a cikin haɗin gwiwa; kada a taɓa toshewa).
- **`policy = burst`** : ba da izini yayin da babban ɗakin zama na duniya ya kasance ba tare da la'akari da rabo mai kyau ba.

### Cap absoluto

`capValue` + `capUnit` akan rabon rufin rufi ne mai zaman kansa ba tare da tsari ko tsari ba. Duk wani girma inda `consumed >= capValue` koyaushe** tubalan** bukatar. Don hular `percent`, `consumed` shine kawai ɓangaren da aka dangana ga maɓalli.

### Duban girman girma

Ana toshe roƙon idan** kowane** girma a cikin tafkin zai toshe shi. Girma masu zaman kansu — 5h% gajiyawa baya shafar girman % mako.

### Lamuni

A cikin yanayin karimci, maɓalli wanda rabonsa ba a cinye shi ba zai iya amfani da ragi daga hannun jarin wasu maɓallan da ba a keɓe ba. Tsarin tsari shine:

```
maxAllowed = globalLimit - consumedByOtherKeys
```

ku `consumedByOtherKeys = consumedTotal - consumedByThisKey`. Teto na duniya (pool `limit` don wannan girman) koyaushe shine rufin rufi mai wuya.

### Bayar da lamuni mai izini (ciba da shiga kowane-pool)

Kowane tafkin yana da yanayin aro. `saturation` shine tsoho kuma yana kiyaye dokoki a sama. A cikin yanayin `consent` maɓallin `hard` ya wuce rabonsa kawai a cikin ƙarfin da sauran maɓallan suka amince su ba da rance. Mai gudanarwa yana saita yanayin kowane tafkin. Kowane mai riƙe maɓalli yana saita nasu izinin ta hanyar [ba da izini](#lending-consent).

Izinin kashi ne daga 0 zuwa 100 da kuma lokacin ƙarshe na zaɓi a cikin milliseconds na zamanin. Maɓalli yana ba da rancen kashi dari na rabonsa mara amfani yayin da ranar ƙarshe ke nan gaba.

Don girma ɗaya, `L` shine iyaka mai tasiri: ƙayyadaddun tsarin ƙayyadaddun lokutan asusun a cikin tafkin. Ƙofar tana ƙididdigewa, don maɓallin buƙata:

```
share_k         = w_k / 100 × L
idle_k          = max(0, share_k − consumed_k)
lent            = Σ over the other keys of lend_k / 100 × idle_k
taken           = Σ over the other keys of max(0, consumed_k − share_k)
borrowAvailable = max(0, lent − taken)
```

`w_k` shine ma'aunin maɓalli, ko `100 / n` ga kowane maɓalli lokacin da babu nauyi sama da 0. `lend_k` shine adadin maɓalli na aiki, kuma 0 da zarar lokacin ƙarshe ya wuce. Maɓallin neman izinin kansa ba ya ƙidaya. Ana ƙi maɓallin `hard` da zarar cinsa ya kai rabonsa da `borrowAvailable`.

Ƙarfin da babu da'awar rabo (100 a debe jimlar ma'auni) ba shi da lamuni. Babu maɓalli da aka yarda ya ba da rance.

**Misali.** `L` 1000.

| Maɓalli | Nauyi | Raba | cinyewa | Lamuni |
| ------- | ----- | ---- | ------- | ------ |
| A       | 50    | 500  | 530     | 0%     |
| B       | 30    | 300  | 100     | 50%    |
| C       | 0     | 0    | 60      | 0%     |

- B yana ba da rancen kashi 50% na 200 mara aiki, wanda shine 100. C ya riga ya ɗauki 60 sama da rabonsa. A iya aro 40.
- Ana yin amfani da A a 530 kuma an ƙi da zarar amfaninsa ya kai 540.
- Kafin C yayi amfani da wani abu, tare da A a ko ƙarƙashin rabonsa, C na iya aro 100.
- Kafin C yayi amfani da wani abu, tare da A a 530, C na iya aro 70.

**Nauyin sifili.** Maɓalli tare da nauyin 0 yana da rabo na 0. Ana amfani da shi ne kawai yayin amfani da shi a ƙasa da abin da wasu ke ba da rance. Lokacin da babu wanda ya ba da rance, an ƙi shi daga buƙatunsa na farko.

**Ranar ƙarshe.** Babu aikin baya da ya ƙare izini. Kowace bukata tana kwatanta ranar ƙarshe da lokacinta, kuma wa'adin da ya yi daidai da wancan lokacin ya wuce. Ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙayyadaddun ƙididdiga waɗanda ba iyakataccen lamba ba, ko kuma ya wuce mafi girman kwanan wata da `Date` za ta iya riƙe, ƙidaya kamar wucewa.

**`soft` dan `burst`.** Bayar da lamuni baya iyakance su. Ana cire maɓalli `soft` sau ɗaya ya wuce rabonsa. Maɓallin `burst` ya wuce yayin da tafkin ke da ɗaki. Har yanzu rufin tafkin yana ƙi kowane maɓalli da zarar jimlar tafkin ya kai iyakarsa.

**Farashin akan hanyar nema.** Tafkin `saturation` baya karantawa fiye da da. A cikin tafkin `consent`, girman da za a iya ƙididdigewa yana karanta sauran maɓallan kawai don maɓallin `hard` a ko sama da rabonsa yayin da wani maɓalli ya ba da rance. Sannan yana sanya daya karanta kowane maɓalli, a layi daya. Karatun da bai yi nasara ba yana ƙidaya azaman amfani da 0 don maɓallin. Girman `percent` yana ƙara rashin karantawa, saboda littafin sifa yana riƙe kowane maɓalli.

**Gyara wurin waha.** Gyara yana kiyaye izinin maɓallan da suka tsaya. Wannan yana riƙe don gyare-gyare na kasafi, maye gurbin rukuni-rukuni da kiran `ensure`. Maɓallin da aka cire yana rasa izininsa, kuma maɓallin da aka ƙara baya yana farawa a 0. Hanyoyin gudanarwa suna mayar da izinin kowane maɓalli kuma kada a rubuta shi. Canja wurin tafki baya zuwa `saturation` yana kiyaye kowane izini da aka adana, wanda ba shi da wani tasiri a wannan yanayin. Canja wurin tafki zuwa `consent` yana sake sake kunna izinin da aka adana wanda lokacin ƙarshe bai wuce ba. Mai riƙe maɓalli na iya share izinin da aka adana a kowane yanayi.

**Haɓakawa.** Idan ginshiƙan `lending_percent` da `lending_until` sun riga sun wanzu, ƙaura na ƙara `borrow_mode` kawai, kuma adadin da aka adana ya tsaya yadda suke. Kowane tafkin yana farawa a yanayin `saturation`. Kunna `consent` kowane tafkin a cikin mayen tafkin. Babu wani abu da aka fahimce daga ƙimar da aka adana.

**Iyakance.**

- Mai ba da rance zai iya samun tafkin a rufinsa kafin ya yi amfani da nasa rabon. Ƙarfin da wasu suka aro ya ƙidaya zuwa jimlar tafkin.
- Duban yana gudana kafin buƙatar kuma bai ajiye komai ba. Buƙatun lokaci guda ana karanta amfani kafin a yi rikodin kowane, don haka ana iya kashe ƙarfin lamuni fiye da sau ɗaya, an iyakance shi da buƙatun cikin jirgin. Binciken rabon yanayin `saturation` yana da dukiya iri ɗaya.

## Ma'aunin Taga Mai Motsi

An aiwatar da shi a `src/lib/quota/sqliteQuotaStore.ts` da `redisQuotaStore.ts`.

Akwai bokiti biyu ga kowane `(apiKeyId, dimensionKey)`:

- `curr`: bokiti na yanzu (`floor(nowMs / windowMs)`)
- `prev`: bokiti na baya (`curr - 1`)

Ƙimar motsi mai tasiri:

```
effectiveBucketIndex = floor(nowMs / windowMs)
bucketStartMs        = effectiveBucketIndex × windowMs
elapsed              = nowMs - bucketStartMs
weight               = 1 - elapsed / windowMs

effective = prev × weight + curr
```

**Daidaito**: kusan 99% daidai. Kuskuren bai wuce 1% na girman tagar ba a iyakar da ke tsakanin bokiti (wannan yana tattare da tsarin kimantawa na bokiti 2).

### Aiki Lokaci Guda

Direban SQLite: mutex na cikin ƙwaƙwalwa ga kowane maɓallin `(apiKeyId | dimensionKey)` yana hana karo na karanta-gyara-rubuta. Tsarin yana kama da kariyar anti-thundering-herd ta `src/sse/services/auth.ts`.

Direban Redis: rubutun Lua EVAL don ƙari na atomik — yana gudana a matsayin umarnin Redis guda ɗaya.

- Ana adana ƙididdigai a Redis; metadata (pools/allocations) har yanzu suna cikin SQLite.


Saitin DB yana da fifiko a kan env. Idan `driver=redis` amma babu URL ko kuma
ba a shigar da `ioredis` ba, factory zai koma SQLite kuma ya rubuta gargaɗi a log.

Tsarin zaɓin direba:

1. Saitin DB `quotaStore.driver`
2. Env `QUOTA_STORE_DRIVER`

## Multi-Dimension

Pool na iya samun girma dabam dabam. Kowane girma yana da zaman kansa:

```ts
QuotaDimension {
  unit: "percent" | "requests" | "tokens" | "usd",
  window: "5h" | "hourly" | "daily" | "weekly" | "monthly",
  limit: number,  // global pool ceiling for this dimension
}
```

**Misali: tsarin Codex** (5h% + mako-mako%):

```json
[
  { "unit": "percent", "window": "5h", "limit": 100 },
  { "unit": "percent", "window": "weekly", "limit": 100 }
]
```

Dole ne buƙatu ta gamsar da duk matakan da za a ba da izini.

### Girman kashi: sifa ta kowane maɓalli

Tsari na kashi yana ba da rahoton adadi ɗaya na amfanin kowane asusu da taga shirin. Mai bayarwa ba ya ba da ƙidayar kowane maɓallin API.

Don raba wannan adadi tsakanin maɓallai, kowane tafkin yana riƙe da littafin ƙididdiga kowane asusu da taga shirin. `src/lib/quota/percentLedger.ts` tana riƙe da ƙa'idodin littafin. `src/lib/quota/percentAttribution.ts` yana juya littafin zuwa lambobin da tilasta yin amfani da su. Sakamakon shine ƙididdigewa da aka gina daga alamomin kowace hanya maɓalli.

#### Karatun da ke motsa littafin

Littafin yana motsawa ne kawai akan adadi na mai bada don taga shirin da aka nema. Waɗannan haɗin gwiwar sun ba da rahoton ɗaya:

| Mai bayarwa                   | Tsara tagogi   |
| ----------------------------- | -------------- |
| Claude, ta hanyar haɗin OAuth | `5h`, `weekly` |
| Codex                         | `5h`, `weekly` |

Waɗannan ba a ƙidaya su azaman karatu:

- amsa da abokin ciniki mai bayarwa yayi alama a matsayin stale
- taga Claude wanda kashinsa mai bayarwa bai bayar da rahoto ba
- wani siffa ta taga, ko mafi munin yanayi a fadin tagogin
- faɗuwar farashin-iyakan buga kai
- a kasa karantawa

Sauran masu samarwa, gami da Claude mai maɓallin API, ba su da littafi. Ana cajin kowane maɓalli 0 akan girmansu na kashi, kamar kafin wannan canjin.

Karatu yana raba cache na daƙiƙa 30 na siginar jikewa. An rubuta karatun Claude lokacin da aka lura da adadi a cikin taken amsa ko karanta daga ƙarshen amfani, lokacin da aka san lokacin. Sauran karatun an yi kwanan watan lokacin da aka gina wannan ma'ajin.

#### Abin da littafin ya adana

Kowace buƙatun da aka yi rikodi yana ƙara alamun sa, sau ɗaya, zuwa ma'auni a kowane tafkin, asusu da maɓalli. A counter kawai girma. Littafin ya tanadi abubuwa uku:

- tushe: adadi lokacin da littafin ya buɗe ko taga ƙarshe ya juya
- adadi na ƙarshe da aka yi amfani da shi
- kowane maɓalli, alamar alamar sa a daidai wannan lokacin, ya kira alamar sa

An sabunta littafin lokacin da tilastawa ko hanyar amfani ke ƙididdige sifa. Ana kwatanta rubuce-rubuce-da-saita akan lambar bita. Marubucin da ya rasa ya sake gwadawa sau ɗaya a sabon karatu.

#### Yadda ake lissafin sashin maɓalli

Ana lissafta kowane ɓangaren maɓalli lokacin da aka karanta littafin. Girman adadi tun lokacin da aka raba tushe tsakanin maɓallai daidai da alamomin kowane ɗayan da aka ci tun lokacin alamarsa.

Together, waɗannan alamun suna bayyana a mafi yawan aya ɗaya na adadi a kowace alamar `QUOTA_PERCENT_MIN_TOKENS_PER_POINT` (tsoho 1,000). Girman da ba su bayyana ba na waje ne. Tushen yana waje kuma.

Misali, littafi yana buɗewa a 0. Maɓalli A sannan yana hanyoyi 900,000 tokens da maɓallin B 100,000. A adadi na 60, ɓangaren A shine maki 54 kuma ɓangaren B shine 6.

Ma'aunin da ke ƙasa da alamar maɓalli ya sake farawa kuma yana ƙirga gabaɗaya. Maɓalli marar alama, kamar maɓalli da ke haɗa tafkin a tsakiyar taga, yana ƙirga duk alamunsa. Hoton da ke nuna alamun yana haifar da kuskuren da ke tafiya a karatu na gaba. Ba ya tarawa.

ana karanta `QUOTA_PERCENT_MIN_TOKENS_PER_POINT` akan kowace sifa. Ƙimar da ba iyakataccen adadin aƙalla 1 ba ta koma 1,000.

#### Yadda karatu ke canza littafin

Dokar daidaitawa ta farko ta shafi:

1. **Babu littafi tukuna:** karatun yana buɗe littafin. Adadinsa shine tushen tushe kuma kowane ma'auni yana da alama.
2. **Tsohon karatu:** karatun kwanan watan kafin na karshe da aka yi amfani da shi ba a yi watsi da shi ba. Haka kuma karatun wanda lokacin sake saiti ya fi mintuna 30 kafin wanda aka adana.
3. **Sabuwar taga mai bayarwa:** lokacin sake saiti fiye da mintuna 30 bayan wanda aka adana ya juya littafin. Adadin ya zama tushe kuma kowane ma'auni ana sake yiwa alama alama. Maɓallai ba tare da counter ba daga littafin.
4. **Tagar da aka ƙare:** lokacin sake saitin da aka adana wanda baya bayan lokacin bayanan karatun yana nufin taga ya ƙare. Karatun yana juya littafin, ko menene siffarsa.
5. **adadi mafi girma:** kawai adadi na ƙarshe yana motsawa. Tushen da alamomin suna tsayawa.
6. **Siffa ɗaya:** adadi, tushe da alamomi suna tsayawa. Sai kawai lokacin sake saitin littafin ya ɓace ana rubutawa.
7. **Ƙananan adadi:** ba a taɓa yin amfani da ƙananan adadi kamar haka ba. Yana juya littafin ne kawai lokacin da abubuwa uku suka riƙe. Aƙalla minti biyu ya fi sabon karatun da aka yi amfani da shi na ƙarshe. Ba abinda yace taga iri daya bude. Ya fi ƙasa da maki ɗaya.

Tagan yana ƙidaya kamar buɗe ɗaya idan abubuwa uku suka riƙe. Dukansu lokutan sake saiti an san su. Suna cikin mintuna 30 da juna. Wanda aka adana har yanzu yana gaban karatun. Ƙananan adadi a cikin wannan taga ba zai canza kome ba.

Ana adana lokacin sake saiti ne kawai yayin da yake gaba da lokacin karatun da ke ɗauke da shi. Wani adadi mafi girma yana adana lokacin sake saitin karatun sa lokacin da ake amfani da shi, kuma yana adana wanda aka adana in ba haka ba. Daidaitaccen adadi yana yin rikodin lokacin sake saitin karatun sa kawai lokacin da littafin ba shi da ko ɗaya. Ba komai ya canza. Ƙananan adadi wanda ba ya juya littafin yana yin rikodin lokacin sake saiti.

#### Lokacin amfani da littafin

Bayan karantawa, ana amfani da littafin sai dai idan lokacin sake saitinsa da aka adana baya bayan lokacin da ake ciki. Wannan yanayin yana tasowa ne lokacin da aka duba karatun da aka ɗauka kafin sake saiti, kamar wanda aka yi aiki daga ma'ajiyar, bayansa.

Ba tare da karantawa mai amfani ba, ana amfani da littafin da aka adana kawai yayin da har yanzu zai iya zama littafin taga na yanzu. Wannan yana faruwa a lokuta biyu:

- an san lokacin sake saiti kuma har yanzu yana gaba
- ba shi da lokacin sake saiti, kuma karatun ƙarshe wanda ya canza shi shine aƙalla mintuna 15

In ba haka ba ba a dangana komai, kamar lokacin da babu littafi. Littafin wanda lokacin sake saitin da aka adana ya wuce ba a taɓa cajin shi ba.

#### tilastawa

Amfanin maɓalli akan girman kashi shine ɓangaren da aka danganta shi da rabon ɓangaren waje. Bangaren waje na farko yana amfani da iya aiki babu da'awar rabo (100 ban da jimlar ma'auni). Abin da ya wuce kawai ana raba tsakanin maɓallan ta nauyi.

An kwatanta wannan amfani da daidaitaccen rabon maɓalli kamar na kowane nau'i. Yanayi mai tsauri har yanzu yana farawa lokacin da siginar jikewa ya kai `QUOTA_SATURATION_THRESHOLD` (tsoho 0.5). Kowane lamba ana ƙididdige shi ta hanyar ingantacciyar iyaka: iyakar kowane-asusun adadin adadin asusun a cikin tafkin.

Cikakken hula tare da `capUnit: "percent"` yana ƙirga ɓangaren da aka dangana ga maɓalli kawai, wanda aka sike shi daidai. Ɗauki iyakar asusu na 100 da k asusu. Ana isa iyakar N lokacin da maɓallin maɓalli na adadi na asusun ajiyar ya kai maki N/k. Ana duba kowane asusu akan littafinsa. Misali, tare da asusu guda biyu, ana samun iyakar 10 a maki 5 na adadi ɗaya.

Lokacin da ba a iya karanta kantin sayar da kayayyaki ba, ba a danganta komai kuma kowane maɓalli ana cajin 0 akan wannan girman. Lokacin da rubutun littafi ya kasa, wannan sifa yana amfani da littafin kamar yadda aka karanta shi. Rubutun alamar da ya gaza ya bar alamun buƙatun daga cikin littafin. Babu ɗayan waɗannan gazawar da ke toshe buƙata.

A cikin tafkin `consent`, tsarin ba da lamuni yana amfani da amfani da kowane maɓalli akan girman kashi kamar yadda aka bayyana a sama. Lokacin da wani maɓalli ya ba da rance, ana karanta littafin sau ɗaya kuma ana danganta shi ga kowane maɓalli. Ba tare da littafi mai amfani ba kowane maɓalli ana cajin 0. Maɓalli mai nauyi sannan ya wuce, kuma maɓalli mai nauyi 0 yana wucewa kawai lokacin da wani maɓalli ya ba da rance.

#### Hanyar amfani

`GET /api/quota/pools/{id}/usage` yana ba da rahoton lambobi iri ɗaya ta hanyar aiki iri ɗaya. Girman kashi yana da shigarwa ɗaya kowane haɗin tafkin a `accounts`. Babban matakinsa `consumedTotal` da `perKey` madubin shigar farko.

Hanyar kuma ta shafi karatun na yanzu, don haka zai iya ciyar da littafin gaba kamar yadda buƙata ta yi. `internal + external` na iya bambanta da `consumedTotal` lokacin da babu karatu mai amfani, ko lokacin da ba a yi amfani da ƙananan adadi ba.

#### Adana da riƙewa

Dukansu shagunan keɓaɓɓun suna ajiye littafin. A kan SQLite, tsaftace bayanai ta atomatik, lokacin da aka kunna, yana cire layuka da ba a sabunta su ba har tsawon kwanaki 60. A kan Redis, littattafai da masu lissafin haɗin tafkin suna raba maɓalli ɗaya. Yana ƙare kwanaki 60 bayan rubutun sa na ƙarshe ko canjin littafi. Littafin da ba ya canzawa ba a sake rubuta shi ba, don haka an saita yanke yanke sama da taga mafi tsawo. Sake saitin tarihin amfani da hannu kuma yana share littattafai akan SQLite (duba Iyaka).

## Haɗin Bututun

### PRE hook (`open-sse/handlers/chatCore.ts`)

Yana gudana a gaban mai zartarwa na sama, bayan tantancewa da ƙa'idodi:

```
resolveComboTargets / handleSingleModel
  → enforceQuotaShare(apiKeyId, connectionId, provider, estimatedCost)
      → getQuotaStore().peek() per dimension
      → fairShare.decideFairShare()
      → if block → return 429 (buildErrorBody, Hard Rule #12)
      → if allow + deprioritize → set quotaSoftPenalty=true on candidate
  → executor.execute()
```

**Kasa-bude** : idan `enforceQuotaShare` ta jefa, ana ba da izinin buƙatun ta tare da gunkin `pino.warn`. Wannan yana hana kwaro-injin keɓaɓɓu toshe duk zirga-zirga.

**Lokacin da ƙarfin ya dawo.** Ƙi a rabon maɓalli ko a rufin tafkin ya ƙare da ɗaya daga cikin jimloli biyu, a cikin yanayin aro duka:

- taga `percent` : `. The <window> window resets at <time>.` Lokaci shine sake saita rahoton mai badawa.
- Naúrar ƙidaya: `. Enough <window> usage is expected to clear by <time>.` Lokaci yana da iyaka na sama daga taga mai zamewa, idan maɓalli bai aika da komai ba.

`<time>` shine ISO 8601 nan take a cikin UTC. Ƙimar kuma tana ɗaukar `Retry-After`: dukan daƙiƙa har zuwa wannan lokacin, da ɗaya. Abokin ciniki wanda ke jiran dogon lokacin yana sake gwadawa aƙalla daƙiƙa ɗaya bayan nan take. Lokacin da ba a san ta nan take ba, ƙi ba ta ɗauke jumlar ko taken ba. Cikakkar kin hula ba ta ɗauka. Lambar jama'a ta tsaya `quota_share_blocked`.

Ƙin rabo baya taɓa haɗin haɗin gwiwa. Ba ya saita sanyi, babu kulle ƙirar ƙira kuma babu gazawar mai bayarwa, kuma ana ci gaba da ba da sauran maɓallan tafkin. Lokacin da kowane maƙasudin haɗuwa ya ƙi maɓalli don rabonsa, haɗin yana amsa 429 ba tare da jira ba. Amsar sa tana ɗauke da alamar kamar `Retry-After`. Hanyar sakawa ta ƙare ƙinsa da jumla ɗaya kuma ba ta aika `Retry-After` ba.

### ƙugiya POST (cibin rikodin)

Bayan amsa mai nasara:

```
executor returns success
  → spendRecorder.recordConsumption(apiKeyId, connectionId, provider, actualCost)
      → getQuotaStore().consume() per dimension
      → fail-open: errors logged as pino.warn, never propagated to client
```

**Bayanan kula** : idan `consume` ta kasa amsawa bayan amsawa, mai jujjuyawa yana ƙidaya. Siginar jikewa daga mai bayarwa (misali `anthropic-ratelimit-unified-5h-utilization`) yana gyara ƙimancin duniya akan buƙatu na gaba.

### Combo soft penalty (`open-sse/services/combo.ts`)

Lokacin `decision.deprioritize === true`:

```ts
if (candidate.quotaSoftPenalty) {
  score *= QUOTA_SOFT_DEPRIORITIZE_FACTOR; // default 0.7
}
```

Ana amfani da hukuncin ne bayan duk wasu abubuwan da aka zira kwallaye. Yana rage yuwuwar haɗakarwa ta atomatik na zaɓi cikakken maɓalli ba tare da toshe shi ba.

## Ƙotancin Sabis na API (ficewa)

`GET /api/v1/me/quota/{provider}` (kuma ana yin hidima a `/v1/me/quota/{provider}`) yana barin maɓalli API ya karanta, tare da nasu maɓalli, nawa rabon da aka raba nasu da abin da ke toshe su. Hanya ce siririn harsashi a kan `src/server/selfService/quotaHandler.ts`; An gina ra'ayi ta hanyar `src/lib/quota/selfView.ts` kuma kwangilar amsawa shine `src/shared/schemas/selfQuota.ts` (`SelfQuotaView` a cikin `docs/openapi.yaml`).

### Kunnawa da tabbatarwa

- Feature flag `QUOTA_SELF_PANEL`, tsoho `false`. Yayin da yake kashewa, buƙatun da ya isa hanyar yana samun 404. Tare da `REQUIRE_API_KEY=true`, manufar API abokin ciniki ya ƙi maɓallin da ya ɓace ko mara kyau tare da 401 kafin hanya ta gudana, duk abin da tutar. Buƙatar da ba ta da maɓalli sai zaman dashboard mai shiga ya wuce waccan manufar kuma ya sami amsar kanta. Tuta tana warwarewa kamar kowace tuta (dashboard ko REST override, sannan canjin yanayi na suna iri ɗaya); ku [`FEATURE_FLAGS.md`](../reference/FEATURE_FLAGS.md).
- Ana mayar da sunan nunin kowane tafkin da kowane tambarin jadawalin zuwa mabuɗan mabuɗin tafkin kamar yadda mai gudanarwa ya rubuta su. Kafin kunna tuta, duba cewa sun dace da waɗannan masu riƙe da maɓalli su karanta.
- Ana karanta maɓalli daga rubutun kai kawai: `Authorization: Bearer <key>` ko `x-api-key: <key>`. Lokacin da aka aika duka biyun, ana amfani da na farko da ya inganta. An yi watsi da maɓalli a cikin layin tambaya ko kuki. An ƙi maɓallin mahalli na uwar garken (401).
- Maɓallin yana buƙatar iyakar `self:usage` (403 ba tare da shi ba).
- An gyara sashin mai ba da kaya, ƙananan akwati kuma an duba shi da `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9._-]{0,63}$` (400 in ba haka ba), sannan a warware shi ta wurin rajistar mai badawa, don haka wanda aka fi sani da `cc` yana karanta wuraren tafkunan `claude`. `claude` da `anthropic` karanta tafkunan ruwa guda.

| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rabon kansa     | `self:usage`                                                                                  | `pools[]` (ref, suna, mai bayarwa, manufofin, nauyi, hula), `dimensions[].yourShare`, `accounts[]` da `yourShare` (a kan taga `percent` ba tare da `attributed` da `external`), `blocks`, `availableModels`, `expiresAt`, `apiBaseUrl`                       |
| matakin lissafi | `self:usage` + `self:account-quota`, da mai bayarwa a cikin maɓalli na `sharedQuotaProviders` | yana ƙara `dimensions[].total`, `accounts[].dimensions[].account` da `accounts[].modelWindows`, `attributed` da `external` akan `yourShare` na taga `percent`, da `members` akan tafki tare da amfanin membobi akan (duba [amfanin membobi](#member-usage) ) |

- `sharedQuotaProviders` saitin maɓalli ne (`PUT /api/keys/[id]/self-service`): `null`, tsoho, yana nufin kowane mai bada maɓalli ya kai; `[]` yana nufin babu. Kowane `accountVisible` na tafkin yana faɗin wane ganuwa da aka yi amfani da shi.
- Ana nuna sandunan asusu da tagogin ƙirar ƙira don asusun kawai waɗanda maɓalli na iya amfani da su. Lokacin da maɓalli yana da jerin hanyoyin haɗin da aka yarda, asusu a wajensa yana kiyaye shigarwar `Account N` ba tare da `account` ko `modelWindows`. Jerin fanko yana nufin babu ƙuntatawa, dangane da `/v1/me/status`. Jimlar tafkin (`dimensions[].total`) bai dogara da wannan jeri ba.
- Lambobi biyu da aka nuna tare da `self:usage` kadai sun fito daga amfani da wasu maɓallai. Jadawalin kasafin kudin `exhausted` yana nuna amfani da kowane maɓalli a ƙarƙashin wannan jadawalin. A cikin tafki wanda nauyinsa duka sifili ne, `weight` ɗin maɓalli shine `100 / number of members`. Dukansu sune abin da tilastawa ya shafi maɓalli.
- Ana gano wurin tafki ta `ref` kawai, ƙima mara kyau da ke ɗaure zuwa maɓallin kira. Sunan nuni (`name`) an haɗa shi kamar yadda mai gudanarwa ya rubuta shi. An samo `ref` daga maɓalli da tafkin tare da sirrin maɓallin API na uwar garken (`API_KEY_SECRET`). Dole ne abokan ciniki kada su dage da shi: yana canzawa idan wannan asirin ya juya.
- An jera asusu a matsayin `Account 1`, `Account 2`, … a cikin tsarin tafkin. Ba a dawo da id na wurin ruwa, id na haɗi, imel na asusu ko sunan asusu ba da aka dawo, kuma amsa ta shiga cikin tsarin kwangilar, wanda ke sauke kowane filin a waje da shi.
- An ba da rahoton raka'a masu ƙididdigewa (`requests`, `tokens`, `usd`) yayin da guga tafki ƙofar keɓaɓɓiyar ke tilastawa: `limit` = shirin iyaka asusun × a cikin tafkin, `fairShare` = nauyi / 100 × `limit` (daidai raba lokacin da kowane nauyin 0), `consumed` = maɓalli na kansa, a cikin ajiyar − `fairShare` cikin ma'ajin `remaining` = `consumed` ku 0.
- `blocks.schedules` yana lissafin jadawali windows (`open`/`closed`, `blackout` ko `outside-window`, `retryAfterSeconds`, ajiye benaye, da kuma ko an kashe kasafin kuɗin taga); `blocks.modelCaps` yana lissafin iyakoki na sa'a guda ɗaya tare da amfani.
- Maɓalli a waje kowane tafkin mai bayarwa yana samun 200 tare da `pools: []`; `availableModels` (mai maɓalli na `qtSd/` yana gani a `/v1/models`), `expiresAt` da `apiBaseUrl` har yanzu suna cike.
- `apiBaseUrl` shine `OMNIROUTE_PUBLIC_API_BASE_URL`, sa'an nan `OMNIROUTE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`, da dai sauransu `null`. Ba shine asalin buƙatar kansa ba.

### Izinin ba da lamuni

`PATCH /api/v1/me/quota/{provider}` tana rubuta izinin maɓalli na kira don tafkin ruwa ɗaya. Yana amfani da tuta, masu kai da ka'idojin samar da `GET`. Ƙimar `self:usage` ta ba da izinin rubuta wannan, kodayake sunanta yana karantawa azaman karantawa kawai.

```json
{
  "pool": "qpr_0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef",
  "lendingPercent": 30,
  "lendingUntil": 1893456000000
}
```

- `pool` shine `ref` ɗin da `GET` ke dawowa don wannan tafkin.
- `lendingPercent` lamba ce daga 0 zuwa 100. Ƙimar 0 tana share yarda da ranar ƙarshe. Jiki mai `lendingPercent` 0 ana karɓa ta kowane yanayi.
- `lendingUntil` na zaɓi ne: epoch milliseconds, ko `null` don babu ranar ƙarshe. Dole ne ya kasance a nan gaba kuma a mafi yawan 864000000000000. Ƙimar da ke ƙasa da 10000000000 yana karantawa azaman seconds kuma an ƙi.
- An ƙi duk wani filin. An ƙi jikin da ya fi 4096 bytes. Karatun yana tsayawa a wannan girman, don haka jiki mai tsayi ba ya kasancewa gaba ɗaya.

Ana gudanar da cak ɗin a cikin tsari na wannan tebur. Kowane ƙin yana amfani da daidaitaccen jikin kuskure tare da kafaffen saƙo. Ƙin ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan cak ɗin bai rubuta komai ba.

| Matsayi | Sako                                                                                               | Yaushe                                                                        |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 404     | `Not found`                                                                                        | Tutar `QUOTA_SELF_PANEL` tana kashe.                                          |
| 401     | `Valid API key required`                                                                           | Maɓallin ya ɓace ko mara aiki, ko kuma shine maɓallin mahalli na uwar garken. |
| 403     | `API key lacks the self:usage scope`                                                               | Makullin ya rasa `self:usage`.                                                |
| 400     | `Invalid provider`                                                                                 | Bangaren mai ba da aiki ba shi da inganci.                                    |
| 429     | `Too many lending changes. Retry later.`                                                           | Makullin ya wuce iyakarsa. Amsar tana dauke da `Retry-After`.                 |
| 400     | `Body must be { pool, lendingPercent, lendingUntil }. lendingPercent is an integer from 0 to 100.` | Jikin ba JSON bane, yayi tsayi da yawa, ko bai dace da tsarin ba.             |
| 400     | `lendingUntil must be a Unix time in milliseconds`                                                 | Ranar ƙarshe tana karanta kamar daƙiƙa.                                       |
| 400     | `lendingUntil must be in the future`                                                               | Ranar ƙarshe shine a ko kafin lokacin buƙatar.                                |
| 404     | `Pool not found`                                                                                   | `ref` ba ta ambaci sunan tafkin wannan maɓalli na wannan mai bayarwa ba.      |
| 403     | `Lending is not enabled for this pool`                                                             | Wurin yana cikin yanayin `saturation` kuma `lendingPercent` yana sama da 0.   |
| 500     | `Failed to build quota view`                                                                       | Duk wani gazawar. An shigar da dalilinsa akan uwar garken.                    |

Iyaka shine buƙatun 20 a cikin daƙiƙa 60 na kowane maɓalli. Duk buƙatun da ta wuce rajistan mai bada ƙidaya, ko da ɗaya ya ƙi daga baya ko wanda bai canza komai ba. Tana da nata counter, baya ga iyakar ƙimar buƙatun maɓalli.

Buƙatun da ke canza izinin da aka adana yana rubuta rikodin binciken `quota.pool.lending.updated` ɗaya. Yana suna maɓalli tare da tsofaffi da sababbin dabi'u. Jiki ɗaya da aka aika sau biyu ya rubuta sau ɗaya kuma yana duba sau ɗaya. Amsar ita ce 200 tare da cikakken `GET` view, gina bayan rubuta.

`GET` ta duba rahoton bada lamuni kamar haka:

- Kowane tafkin yana ɗaukar `borrowMode` (`saturation` ko `consent`), da maɓalli nasa `lendingPercent` da `lendingUntil`.
- `lendingActive` `true` ne kawai a cikin tafkin `consent`, tare da kashi sama da 0 kuma ba a kai ga ƙarshe ba.
- A cikin tafkin `consent`, kowane `dimensions[].yourShare` mai ƙididdigewa yana ɗaukar `borrowAvailable`. Shi ne abin da maɓalli zai iya amfani da shi a sama da rabonsa a yanzu, wanda sauran maɓallan suka ba da rance.
- `borrowAvailable` tana amfani da dabarar kofa. Yana karanta kowane maɓalli, kuma yayin da maɓallin ke ƙarƙashin rabonsa. Don maɓalli `hard` wanda shine ƙarin karantawa kowane maɓalli da girma mai ƙididdigewa, a layi daya.
- `borrowAvailable` shine `null` don maɓalli `soft` ko `burst`, kuma `null` lokacin da karatu ya kasa.
- `borrowAvailable` adadi ɗaya ne ga duk sauran maɓallan tare. A cikin tafkin maɓallai biyu, siffa ce ta ɗayan.
- ba a bayar da rahoton `borrowAvailable` don windows `percent` ko a cikin `accounts[]` ba tukuna.
- A cikin tafkin `saturation` ra'ayi yana ba da rahoton yarda da aka adana tare da `lendingActive: false` kuma babu `borrowAvailable`.

### Kurakurai

Kowane martani na wannan hanya, nasara ko kuskure, yana ɗaukar `Cache-Control: no-store`. Ƙimar da ta faru kafin hanya ba ta ɗaukar shi, misali abokin ciniki API manufofin 401 ko IP filter's 403. Kurakurai na hanya suna amfani da daidaitaccen jikin kuskure tare da kafaffen saƙo: 400 (mai ba da aiki mara inganci), 401 (maɓallin ɓacewa ko mara inganci), 403 (bace `self:usage`), 0g0 (flash) da 40 (`Failed to build quota view`) . 500 yana rufe duk wani gazawa da zarar buƙatar ta isa kan hanya, gami da bincika maɓalli da kanta; dalilinsa yana shiga akan uwar garken kuma bai dawo ba. An jera kurakurai na `PATCH` a cikin [izinin ba da lamuni](#lending-consent).

### Iyakance

- `remaining` shine ɓangaren rabon maɓalli na gaskiya wanda bai yi amfani da shi ba. Ba garanti ba ne. Ana iya ba da maɓalli fiye da shi yayin da tafkin ke da ɗaki. Da zarar jimlar tafkin ta kai `limit`, kowane maɓalli na tafkin an ƙi, ko menene nasa `remaining`, kuma maɓalli ba tare da `self:account-quota` ba ba zai iya ganin wannan jimlar ba.
- `remaining` baya la'akari da cikakken `cap` na rabon. Ƙaddamarwa yana ƙi maɓalli da zarar cin nasa a cikin taga na sashin hular ya kai darajar hular.
- Mashigin matakin-asusu ya fito ne daga ma'ajiyar mai ba da iyaka-iyakance kuma yana iya jinkirta yanke shawarar tilastawa da 'yan mintoci kaɗan. An ba da rahoton karatun da lokacin sake saiti ya wuce kamar `stale`.
- Per-model windows (`modelWindows`) ana bayar da rahoton ne kawai ga masu samarwa waɗanda ke da taswirar taga-taga: Claude da Codex a yau. Ga sauran masu samar da `modelWindows` har yanzu yana nan lokacin da aka nuna bayanan asusu, tare da fanko jerin `windows`.
- Maɓalli na kansa rabon taga `percent` shine kimantawa. Ya fito daga littafin sifa kuma yana ɗaukar kowane iyakance da aka jera a ƙarƙashin "Ma'auni Kashi: sifa ta kowane maɓalli" a sama.
- `consumed` ita ce tilasta lamba idan aka kwatanta da rabo mai kyau. Yana da `attributed` da `external`, daidai. `attributed` shine sashin da littafin ya ba da maɓalli na alamun da aka fatattake shi. `external` shine maɓalli na ɓangaren amfani da asusun wanda maɓallan tafkin ba sa lissafinsu.
- Dukkan sassan biyu ana aika su ne kawai lokacin da `accountVisible` na tafkin gaskiya ne: maɓalli yana da `self:account-quota` kuma mai bayarwa yana cikin `sharedQuotaProviders` ɗin sa. Maɓalli ba tare da shi yana samun `consumed` kaɗai, tare da `status` ɗaya da `reason`.
- Tare da sassan biyu da nauyinsa, mai riƙewa zai iya yin amfani da amfani da aka yi a wajen tafkin da aka caje zuwa maɓallan tafkin. Mai riƙe da iyakar asusu ya riga ya ga jimlar asusun.
- Makullin da bai ci komai ba a cikin taga har yanzu yana iya karanta cajin sa na waje daga `consumed`, saboda sashin da aka siffanta shi shine 0.
- Misali, littafi yana buɗewa a 0, tare da maɓallan A da B a nauyin 50 kowanne. Maɓalli A hanyoyi 900,000 alamu da maɓalli B 100,000. Sannan asusun ya kai kashi 60 cikin 100 na iyaka 100. Sannan `attributed` shine 54 akan makullin A da 6 akan maballin B, kuma `external` shine 0 ga duka biyun.
- Ra'ayi yana karanta littafin kuma baya rubuta yanayinsa. Ba ya buɗe littafi, kuma bai taɓa tambayar mai bayarwa don karantawa ba. Tare da kantin Redis, karantawa yana cire wuraren bita da aka maye gurbinsu a baya, kamar yadda karatun gate ke yi.
- Rabon `ok` ne kawai yayin da ma'ajiyar gate ke riƙe da karatun asusu. Ba tare da ɗaya ba yana da `stale`, don haka a cikin tafkin da babu buƙatar kwanan nan yana nuna kamar yadda ya ƙare.
- Har sai buƙatar ta wuce ta tafkin, babu rabon rabon. Wannan buƙatar ta buɗe littafin, kuma adadi na asusun a lokacin yana ƙidaya kamar yadda aka yi amfani da shi a wajen tafkin.
- A cikin tafkin da ke da asusu da yawa, kowane shigarwar asusun yana amfani da littafin kansa da iyakar asusu ɗaya. Ƙaddamar da aiwatar da ma'auni na ɓangarorin biyu ta adadin asusun, don haka rabon daidai yake.
- Tagar-samfurin-samfurin ba ya ɗaukar rabo don maɓalli. Littafin ba ya adana bayanan kowane samfuri.
- Ba a kimanta benaye na jadawali ta hanyar ra'ayi: jadawalin zai iya nunawa a matsayin `open` yayin da aka ƙi buƙatu da dalili `schedule-reserve`.
- Lokacin da wuraren tafki guda biyu na maɓalli ɗaya suka raba haɗin gwiwa, tilasta aiwatar da ɗayansu, kuma ra'ayi ya lissafa duka biyun.
- Babu iyaka ga kowane adireshi akan yunƙurin maɓalli da suka gaza: a bayan masu kira na baya suna iya raba adireshi ɗaya (ya dogara da ko an amince da adiresoshin abokin ciniki da aka tura), don haka kullewa zai iya ƙin hanyar zuwa kowane maɓalli. Tare da `REQUIRE_API_KEY=true`, manufar API abokin ciniki yana ƙi da maɓalli mara inganci kafin hanya ta gudana, tare da jikin 401 na manufofin maimakon wannan hanyar.

### Amfanin membobi

Mai gudanarwa na iya barin masu riƙon maɓalli su ga yadda amfanin taga ke raba tsakanin membobin tafkin. Zaɓin shine `memberBreakdown` na tafkin, kashe ta tsohuwa. An saita shi a cikin mayen tafkin ko tare da `PATCH /api/quota/pools/{id}`. `GET /api/quota/pools` da `GET /api/quota/pools/{id}` suka ruwaito shi.

Girman ra'ayi yana ɗaukar `members` kawai lokacin da waɗannan duka ke riƙe:

- zabin tafkin yana kunne
- tafkin yana da aƙalla maɓallai 20 da aka ware, kuma aƙalla uku daga cikinsu na iya yin buƙatu: aiki, ba a soke ba, ba a dakatar da su ba kuma ba su wuce ƙarshen su ba.
- `accountVisible` na tafkin gaskiya ne: maɓalli yana da `self:account-quota` kuma mai bayarwa yana cikin `sharedQuotaProviders`
- akan taga `percent`, haɗin asusun shine maɓalli na iya amfani da shi
- Kason mabuɗin na wancan taga an karanta: `status` ba `unavailable` ba ce

In ba haka `members` ba ta nan. Ba wani fanni ba ne.

- Kowane yanki `{ slot, consumed, isYou }`. Tsarin amsawa ya ƙi duk wani kadara akan yanki, da jerin fiye da yanka 20. An jera yanka a cikin tsarin ramuka.
- Akwai yanki guda ɗaya a kowane maɓalli da aka keɓe, gami da maɓallin da ba zai iya yin buƙatun ba. Memba mara amfani yana nuna sifili na gaske.
- Ramin 1 koyaushe shine maɓallin tambaya, tare da `isYou` gaskiya. Sauran lambobin ramummuka sune matsayi na maɓalli na zanta. An saka shi da `API_KEY_SECRET` kuma yana rufe tafkin, maɓallin tambaya da memba.
- Don haka lambobin ba sa layi tsakanin masu riƙewa. Ga mariƙi ɗaya suna zama iri ɗaya tsakanin ziyara. Suna iya canzawa lokacin da maɓalli ya shiga ko barin tafkin, ko lokacin da wannan sirrin ya juya.
- A kan taga mai ƙididdigewa yanki shine ma'aunin memba a cikin shagon keɓe, ma'auni ɗaya da nasa `consumed`.
- A kan taga `percent` yanki yanki ne na `attributed` na memba, daga littafin ɗaya karanta kamar rabon maɓalli. Amfanin da aka yi a wajen tafkin ba a taɓa raba zuwa yanka ba. An raba wannan cajin da nauyi, don haka yankan da ke ɗauke da shi zai nuna ma'aunin nauyi.
- Kowane ra'ayi yana karanta ƙima ɗaya ga kowane memba da taga mai ƙididdigewa, a layi daya. A `percent` taga tsabar kudin yankan ba a karanta ba fiye da littafin rabon da aka karanta. Lokacin da ba za a iya karanta ƙima ɗaya ba, taga ɗin ba ta da `members`, kuma rabon maɓalli yana tsayawa.
- Zabin da adadin maɓallan da za a iya amfani da su ana karanta sau ɗaya a kowane tafkin, kuma kawai don maɓallin da zai iya ganin bayanan asusun. Rashin karatu na zaɓi ko na ƙidayar maɓalli mai amfani yana kashe sandunan don tafkin. Sabar ta yi rikodin gargadi, kuma an gina sauran ra'ayi.

Sandunan ba su ɗauke da sunaye ko ids. Har yanzu suna gaya wa mabuɗin abubuwa game da sauran membobin:

- Masu riƙon suna iya daidaita yanki ta ƙimar su.
- Tsayayyen lamba yana barin mai riƙewa ya bi memba ɗaya akan lokaci.
- Adadin yanka yana nuna adadin maɓallai da aka ware a cikin tafkin.
- A cikin tafkin maɓallai uku, wanda ya san abin da maɓalli ɗaya da aka yi amfani da shi zai iya faɗi abin da maɓallin ƙarshe ya yi amfani da shi.
- Lokacin da tafkin ya faɗi ƙasa da maɓalli uku masu amfani, sandunan sun ɓace. Ra'ayoyin da aka ɗauka a baya har yanzu suna nuna abin da kowane memba ya yi amfani da shi.
- A kan taga mai ƙididdigewa, jimlar tafkin ban da yankan na iya nuna amfani da maɓallan da ba a keɓe su ba.
- Memba wanda ke nuna sifili akan asusu ɗaya kuma yayi amfani da shi akan wani na iya nuna memba ke amfani da wane asusu.
- A cikin tafki mai ƙididdigewa tare da manufar `hard`, yanki da ke daina girma a daidai rabon memba yana nuna nauyin wannan memba.

### Jerin amfani (`GET /api/v1/me/usage`)

`GET /api/v1/me/usage?range=<range>` (wanda kuma aka yi aiki a `/v1/me/usage`) yana dawo da amfani da farashi na buƙatun maɓallin kira: jimlar, shigarwa ɗaya a kowace rana UTC, kowace rana da ƙira, kowane ƙira da kowane mai bayarwa. Yana raba tuta, ƙa'idodin tabbatarwa, iyakar `self:usage`, `Cache-Control: no-store` da kuma kuskuren hanyar keɓaɓɓiyar ke sama. Ana duba maɓalli kafin `range`. Kurakurai sune 400 (`Invalid range`), 401, 403, 404 da 500 (`Failed to build usage`).

- `range` ɗaya ne daga `1d`, `7d`, `30d` (tsofaffin) ko `90d`. Duk wata ƙima, mara komai ko siga mai maimaitawa shine 400. Saitin yana tsayawa a `90d` don ɗaure farashin kira. Duban yana karanta ɗanyen tarihin amfani kawai, ba taƙaitaccen bayanin yau da kullun ba, waɗanda ba su ɗauke da maɓalli ba.
- Ana zaɓar layuka ta ID ɗin maɓallin kira. Maɓallin sunan maɓalli bai taɓa daidaitawa ba, kuma layuka da aka yi rikodin ba tare da id ɗin maɓalli ba ba a taɓa ƙidaya su ba.
- Gina jeri yana gudanar da tambayoyin rukuni biyar akan layuka na maɓalli a cikin taga. Ana sake amfani da jerin da aka gina na tsawon daƙiƙa 60 a kowane maɓalli da kewayo, kuma kiran da aka sake amfani da shi ba ya gudanar da ko ɗaya. `generatedAt` tana bada shekarunta.
- Tagan yana ƙare lokacin da aka gina jerin. Jere da aka buga daga baya, misali ta skew agogo, ba a ƙidaya shi.
- `coverage.retentionDays` shine saitin riƙon tarihin amfani da ɗanyen mai aiki (`retention.usageHistory` a cikin saitunan bayanai). `coverage.truncatedByRetention` gaskiya ne lokacin da kewayon da ake buƙata ya fi tsayin wannan saitin. Yana kwatanta lambobin biyu kuma baya duba ko ainihin layuka sun ɓace.
- Ana mayar da mai bayarwa kamar yadda aka yi rikodin don buƙatun maɓalli na kansa. Samfura shine sunan da aka rubuta bayan `/` na ƙarshe , kuma ana haɗa layuka da shi. Ana yin rikodin buƙatun haɗaɗɗiyar wacce aka ƙi kowace manufa tare da alamar tuƙi a matsayin mai bayarwa (sunan haɗaɗɗiyar, `auto` ko `combo`) da kuma kirtani samfurin da abokin ciniki ya aiko. Sauran buƙatun da aka ƙi, misali ta hanyar buɗaɗɗen da'ira, sanyin ƙira ko tsallake-tsallake-ƙididdigar ƙididdigewa, kiyaye mai samarwa da ƙira.
- Bayan kirga da farashi, martanin yana ɗaukar matsakaicin jinkiri, ƙimar nasara da lokutan buƙatun farko da na ƙarshe.
- `byModel` ya lissafa nau'i-nau'i-nau'i-nau'i-na-baki-da-samfurin 50 tare da mafi yawan buƙatun. Tare da ƙarin nau'i-nau'i shigarwar sa ba su ƙara zuwa `summary` ba, kuma `summary.uniqueModels` yana ƙididdige kowane rubutaccen rubutun samfurin. `byModelTruncated` to gaskiya ne, kuma samfurin wanda nau'ikan sa suka faɗi a ɓangarorin biyu na yanke yana nuna jimlar juzu'i.
- An bayar da rahoton farashi biyu. `costUsd` shine kudin da aka biya, kamar yadda nazarin amfani ya nuna. Masu ba da kuɗaɗen ƙira, kamar tsarin lambar Claude, ƙidaya $0 a wurin. `listPriceUsd` iri ɗaya ne mai amfani a farashin jeri, an haɗa biyan kuɗi. Buƙatar da ƙirar ta ba ta da layin farashi tana ƙididdige kuɗin da aka adana: farashin da mai bayarwa ya bayar, ko wanda aka ƙididdige lokacin da aka yi rikodin buƙatar. Buƙatar kawai ba tare da ƙidaya 0 ba, ba tare da alamar alama ba, don haka jimillar na iya zama bangaranci.
- Kwangilar amsawa shine `src/shared/schemas/selfUsage.ts` (`SelfUsageSeries` a cikin `docs/openapi.yaml`).

### Shafin sabis na kai

Tare da kunna `QUOTA_SELF_PANEL`, `/quota/{provider}` shafi ne na jama'a. Mai mariƙin maɓalli yana rubuta maɓallin API a wurin kuma yana ganin wuraren tafkunan maɓalli na mai bayarwa. Kowane tafkin yana nuna kati ɗaya a kowane taga tafkin, tare da amfani da tafkin don kowa da kuma rabon maɓalli. Kowane asusu na tafkin yana da katunan kansa, tare da amfani da asusun da rabon maɓalli. Amfani da asusun yana bayyana ne kawai lokacin da maɓalli na iya ganin bayanan asusu. Shafin kuma ya lissafta iyakoki na kowane-iri na kowane asusu da kuma amfanin mabuɗin. Sanarwa yana bayyana lokacin da jadawalin ya toshe maɓalli a yanzu. Kati yana lissafin kowane maɓalli na kowane samfuri tare da amfani da shi, kuma yana yiwa waɗanda aka kai alama. `{provider}` id ne mai bayarwa ko kuma laƙabin sa (`/quota/claude`, `/quota/cc`). Wani mai bada da ba a sani ba, da kowane hanyar `/quota/*` yayin da tuta ke kashe, amsa 404. Pools a kan buɗaɗɗen buɗaɗɗen al'ada- ko kumburin Anthropic ba su da shafi. Har yanzu API ya amsa musu.

Shafin ba shi da zama. Ana aika maɓalli kawai a cikin taken `Authorization` na `GET /api/v1/me/quota/{provider}`, `PATCH /api/v1/me/quota/{provider}` da `GET /api/v1/me/usage`, ba a cikin URL ba . Maɓallin da uwar garken ke karɓa ana adana shi a cikin `sessionStorage` ɗin shafin. Tare da "Ka tuna akan wannan na'urar" an adana shi a cikin `localStorage` maimakon, kuma "Maɓallin Manta" yana cire shi daga duka biyun. Maɓallin da uwar garken ya ƙi an cire shi daga ma'adana.

Wurin ruwa a yanayin `consent` yana nuna katin lamuni. A can mai maɓalli yana saita cikakken kashi daga 0 zuwa 100 na rabon maɓalli mara amfani don rance. Har ila yau, mai riƙewa yana zaɓar tsawon lokacin da izinin zai kasance: har sai an kashe, awa 1, awanni 8, awanni 24, kwanaki 7 ko kwanaki 30. Tsawon lokaci yana ƙidaya daga lokacin adanawa. Yayin da ranar ƙarshe ke aiki, mai riƙewa zai iya ajiye shi a maimakon haka. Idan ranar ƙarshe ta wuce kafin Ajiye, katin ba zai aika komai ba kuma yana neman wani zaɓi. Tare da 0% ba a aika ranar ƙarshe ba. Katin yana nuna ranar da aka adana tare da yankin lokacin sa mai suna. Yarjejeniyar wacce ranar ƙarshe ta wuce ana nuna kamar yadda ya ƙare.

Don maɓallin `hard` katin kuma ya lissafta, kowane taga tafkin, menene sauran maɓallan ke ba da rance ga maɓalli a yanzu. Kowane jere adadi ɗaya ne don duk sauran maɓallan tare. A cikin tafkin maɓallai biyu, siffa ce ta ɗayan. Adadin da ra'ayi ba ya bayar da rahoto yana karanta kamar babu shi, ba a matsayin sifili ba. Don maɓallin `soft` ko `burst` katin yana cewa babu adadi da ya shafi.

Wurin ruwa a yanayin `saturation` wanda maɓallinsa ke adana kashi sama da 0 yana nuna cirewa kawai. Ya ce adadin da aka adana ba shi da wani tasiri a can. Maɓallin janyewar sa yana aika 0 ba tare da ƙarewa ba. Wurin ruwa a yanayin `saturation` tare da adana 0 yana nuna babu katin lamuni. Bayan adanawa, katin da duban da aka dawo baya buƙata yana tsayawa tare da saƙon sa har sai yanayin shafin ya sake canzawa.

Babu wani abu da aka ajiye sai mai mariƙin ya danna Ajiye ko Janye. Kowane latsa yana aika buƙatar `PATCH /api/v1/me/quota/{provider}` ɗaya, kuma latsa na biyu ba ya aika komai yayin da na farko yana jiran. Jikin yana ɗaukan `ref` maras kyau na tafkin daga gani, kashi da ranar ƙarshe. Amsar ita ce ra'ayin rabon da aka sake ginawa, kuma shafin yana nuna shi ba tare da karantawa na biyu ba. Lokacin da ajiya guda biyu suka zo juna, shafin yana karanta adadin sau ɗaya bayan amsa ta ƙarshe. Shafin baya kiyaye yarda a ma'ajiyar burauza.

Ana bayyana canjin da aka ƙi a katin ta hanyar rubutu da aka zaɓa daga matsayin HTTP. Ba a karanta jikin kuskure. A 401 ya ce ba a karɓi maɓalli ba kuma ya sake shigar da adadin. A 403 ya ce tafkin baya ɗaukar canje-canjen lamuni, kuma 404 ya ce tafkin ya tafi. Dukansu suna ba da maɓalli wanda ke sake loda adadin. Amsar 400 ko wata 4xx ta tambayi mai riƙewa don duba kashi da ranar ƙarshe. A 429 ya nemi mariƙin ya jira, tare da daƙiƙa na `Retry-After` lokacin da amsar ta ba su. Amsar 5xx, 408 ko gazawar hanyar sadarwa ta ce mai yuwuwa ba a ajiye canjin ba, kuma yana kiyaye ƙimar da aka buga. Amsa a wajen kwangilar tana sake shigar da adadin. Ajiyar da ta gaza ba ta cire maɓallin da kanta. Lokacin da aka sake kunnawa bayan an ƙi, shafin yana cire maɓallin kamar yadda aka ƙi karantawa.

Da zarar an karɓi maɓalli, shafin kuma yana nuna katin "Set up your tools" mai shafuka uku. "Claude Code" yana ba da toshe `env` don `~/.claude/settings.json`. "Shell" yana ba da masu canji iri ɗaya kamar layin `export`. "Claude Desktop" yana ba da ma'auni don tagar tantancewar ɓangare na uku. Samfuran sune samfuran rabon rabon da maɓalli na iya kira ga mai badawa. Lokacin da babu irin wannan jeri da ya zo tare da maɓalli, toshe yana kunna gano ƙirar ƙofa maimakon. Adireshin ƙofa shine asalin API na jama'a wanda uwar garken ya ba da rahoton (`OMNIROUTE_PUBLIC_API_BASE_URL`, sannan `OMNIROUTE_PUBLIC_BASE_URL`). Lokacin da ba a saita ɗaya ba, katin yana nuna mai riƙewa kuma ya gaya wa mai riƙe ya tambayi mai gudanarwa. Ana nuna maɓalli azaman mai riƙewa. "Include my key" yana sanya maɓalli gaba ɗaya a cikin rubutun akan allo kuma a cikin abin da maɓallin kwafi. Wannan yana dawwama har sai akwatin ya share, an manta da maɓallin ko canza shi, ko kuma a bar shafin. Katin ba ya aika buƙatu kuma yana ba da saukar da fayil.

Ana nuna karatun API alamomin `stale` kamar yadda zai yiwu ya ƙare. Ana nuna karatun sa alamar `unavailable` tare da dalilinsa, ba a matsayin sifili ba.

A kan tsarin kashi dari shafin yana zana rabon maɓalli lokacin da API ya ba da rahotonsa. Ya ce adadin kiyasi ne kuma ya sanya sunayen sassansa guda biyu. Iyakoki na kowane samfuri suna nuna asusun kawai.

Lokacin da taga yana ɗaukar `members`, katinsa kuma yana nuna mashaya mai kashi ɗaya akan kowane memba, a cikin tsari. Bangaren maɓallin tambayar yana amfani da launi na lafazin. Sauran sassan suna zagaya ta cikin launukan ginshiƙi na jigon. Tatsuniyar rubutu yana jera kowane bangare tare da ƙimar sa, don haka babu abin da ya dogara da launi. Ana yiwa sassan lakabin `You` ko `Member N`, inda N shine ramin.

### Sadaukarwa tashar jiragen ruwa don shafin

`QUOTA_PANEL_PORT` yana buɗe mai sauraro na biyu wanda ke ba da sabis na kai kawai. Yana kashe sai dai idan an saita canjin. `QUOTA_PANEL_HOST` shine adreshin daure kuma ya sabawa `127.0.0.1`.

Mai sauraro yana ba da waɗannan hanyoyin zuwa babban tashar jiragen ruwa kuma yana amsa 404 ga kowace hanya:

| Hanya                                                                    | Hidima                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| `/quota/{provider}`                                                      | Shafin                               |
| `/api/v1/me/quota/{provider}`, `/v1/me/quota/{provider}`                 | Duban keɓaɓɓun shafin yana karantawa |
| `/api/v1/me/usage`, `/v1/me/usage`                                       | Jerin amfanin shafin yana karantawa  |
| `/_next/static/`                                                         | Rubutun, salo da kuma rubutun ginin  |
| `/favicon.ico`, `/favicon.svg`, `/icon-512.png`, `/apple-touch-icon.png` | Gumaka                               |

ana `GET` da `HEAD` ba tare da jiki ba. Ana kuma aika buƙatu ɗaya tare da jiki: `PATCH` na hanyar duba keɓaɓɓu, wanda shafin ya aika don adana zaɓin lamuni. Duk wata hanya ta amsa 404, sai dai buƙatar CONNECT, wanda haɗinsa ke rufe ba tare da amsa ba. Duk wani buƙatar da ke bayyana jiki yana amsa 404 kuma an rufe haɗin sa. An ƙi buƙatun haɓakawa kuma an rufe haɗin su. An ƙi hanyar da ke da ɓangaren digo ko alamar guntu, kamar a tashar API. Haka ma buƙatun buƙatun tare da hali a waje da za'a iya bugawa ASCII. Ana amsa da ɗanyen ikon sarrafawa a cikin maƙasudi 400 ta ma'aunin HTTP, kafin cak ɗin wannan tashar jiragen ruwa. Bukatar kanun labarai waɗanda tsarin ya tanadi don amfanin kansa ba a sake su: sunayen da suka fara da `x-middleware-`, `x-invoke-`, `x-nextjs-`, `x-now-` ko `x-next-`, da sunayen `x-matched-path` da `next-resume`. Ana isar da buƙatar ba tare da su ba. Ba za a iya isa ga dashboard, shafin shiga, API na gudanarwa da kuma hanyoyin da za a iya isa ta wannan tashar jiragen ruwa ba.

Dole ne `PATCH` ta ɗauki `Content-Type: application/json`, ba tare da siga ba ko tare da `charset=utf-8` kawai. Dole ne ya ɗauki `Content-Length` ɗaya daga 1 zuwa 4096, wanda aka rubuta azaman lambobi a sarari, kuma babu `Transfer-Encoding`. `PATCH` wanda ya karya ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan ƙa'idodin yana amsa 404. Wasu tsayin da ba su da kyau, kamar sa hannu ko mai maimaita, ana amsawa 400 ta hanyar HTTP parser kafin rajistan kansa ta tashar jiragen ruwa. Tashar jiragen ruwa tana karanta bytes da aka ayyana kafin ta tuntuɓar babban tashar jiragen ruwa, kuma tana jira aƙalla daƙiƙa 5 a gare su. Jikin da bai cika ba sai ya amsa 404 kuma an rufe haɗinsa. Babu wani abu da ya isa babban tashar jiragen ruwa a wannan yanayin, ko lokacin da abokin ciniki ya fara fita. Tashar tashar jiragen ruwa tana isar da daidaitattun bytes da aka ayyana, tare da `Content-Length` iri ɗaya. Ana karanta Bytes bayan su azaman buƙatun na gaba akan haɗin. Wakili na baya na TLS a gaba dole ne ya tura `Content-Length` kuma kada ya sake yanke jikin buƙatar. Babban tashar jiragen ruwa yana duba maɓallin API a cikin taken `Authorization`, dangane da `GET`.

Jiki 404 ne `{"error":"not_found","message":"Not found."}`. Relay da ya gaza amsa 502 (`api_bridge_unavailable`) ko 504 (`api_bridge_timeout`) ba tare da filin `detail` ba.

Mai sauraro baya karanta `QUOTA_SELF_PANEL`, kuma baya kunna shafin. Yayin da aka kashe tuta, shafin da API ɗinsa suna amsa 404 akan babbar tashar jiragen ruwa, kuma mai sauraro yana ba da wannan amsar.

Ƙarƙashin uwar garken ci gaba (`next dev`), shafin ba ya zama m ta wannan tashar jiragen ruwa. Ci gaban soket-sake lodi mai zafi buƙatun haɓakawa ne, kuma tashar jiragen ruwa ta ƙi shi. Yi amfani da babban tashar jiragen ruwa yayin haɓakawa.

Tashar tashar jiragen ruwa tana hidimar HTTP a sarari kuma shafin yana aika maɓallan API. Ajiye adireshin daurin tsoho kuma sanya wakili na baya tare da TLS a gabansa. Adireshin daure ban da loopback yana rubuta wannan sanarwar:

```text
[Quota Panel] Bound to <host>. This port serves plain HTTP and the page sends API keys. Put a TLS reverse proxy in front of it.
```

Takwarorin dawo da baya wanda ba ya aika da kai mai turawa yana ƙidaya azaman na gida a babban tashar jiragen ruwa. Don haka dole ne wakili a kan mai gida ɗaya ya aika `X-Forwarded-For`. An amince da wakili akan adireshi na sirri don wannan taken kawai tare da `OMNIROUTE_TRUST_PROXY=private` (ko `lan`). Duk wani takwarorinsu ana bayar da rahoton ta hanyar adireshin haɗin yanar gizonsa, kuma ana jefar da rubutun turawa da ya aika.

Mai sauraro yana riƙe da mafi yawan haɗin kai 256 a lokaci guda. Abokin ciniki yana da daƙiƙa 10 don aika kan buƙatun da daƙiƙa 30 don aika buƙatun gabaɗaya. Haɗin da ba shi da rai yana rufewa bayan kamar daƙiƙa 5. An rufe haɗin da babu aiki na daƙiƙa 60. Waɗannan dabi'u an gyara su. Saita haɗin haɗi da ƙimar ƙima akan wakili na baya a gaba shima. Wakili na baya a gaba yakamata ya kiyaye haɗin kai sama da kasa da daƙiƙa 5 mara amfani, ko kar a sake amfani da su. Relay zuwa babban tashar jiragen ruwa yana amfani da lokacin ƙarewar tashar tashar API (`API_BRIDGE_*`).

Mai saurare kuma yana farawa lokacin da ba a saita `API_PORT` ba. `QUOTA_PANEL_PORT` dole ne ya zama tashar jiragen ruwa daga 1 zuwa 65535, wanda aka rubuta cikin lambobi kawai. Dole ne ya bambanta da tashar dashboard da tashar API. Yayin da uwar garken WebSocket ke kunne, dole ne kuma ya bambanta da `LIVE_WS_PORT` (tsoho 20132). Duk wata ƙima ba ta buɗe komai kuma tana yin rajistar gargaɗi. Gargadi game da ƙima da aka ƙi suna suna mai canzawa kuma baya maimaita ƙimar. Tashar jiragen ruwa da aka riga aka fara amfani da ita ba ta buɗe komai kuma ta yi rajistar wannan gargaɗin:

```text
[Quota Panel] Port <port> is already in use. The quota panel port stays off.
```

Tare da saita `OMNIROUTE_BASE_PATH`, mai sauraro yana tsammanin kowace hanya a sama ƙarƙashin wannan prefix. Yi amfani da `OMNIROUTE_BASE_PATH` iri ɗaya a lokacin gini da lokacin gudu. Rubuta shi tare da slash mai jagora kuma ba tare da slash ba, misali `/omniroute`. Wani siffar, ciki har da `/` da `/omniroute/`, ya bar tashar jiragen ruwa tare da gargadi, ko da yake ginin yana karɓar waɗannan siffofin. Haka kuma hanyar tushe wadda sashin farko ya sanya sunan hanyar aikace-aikacen: `api`, `v1`, `v1beta`, `_next`, `quota`, `dashboard`, `docs`, `login`, `codex`, `responses`, `anthropic`, `openai`, `v0` ko `connect`, a kowane hali.

Iyakoki:

- `/quota/{provider}/` tare da slash amsa 404 akan wannan tashar jiragen ruwa.
- Ba a ba da ma'aikacin sabis da bayanan bayanan yanar gizo akan wannan tashar jiragen ruwa ba.
- Ba a nuna saitin favicon na al'ada a cikin dashboard akan wannan tashar jiragen ruwa.
- Ba a nuna gumaka lokacin da aikace-aikacen ke gudana ƙarƙashin hanyar tushe.
- Tashar jiragen ruwa na magana a fili HTTP/1.1.
- Ba a sake gwada ɗaurin da ya gaza ba har sai an sake farawa.
- Mai saurare ba shi da haɗi ko ƙimar ƙimar kowane adireshin abokin ciniki.
- Wannan sashe ba ya rufe saitin kwantena.

## Magance Matsaloli

### An saita Redis amma baya haɗuwa

Tabbatar cewa an girka `ioredis` (`npm ls ioredis`) kuma ana iya isa ga
`QUOTA_STORE_REDIS_URL`. Idan haɗin ya gaza, factory zai koma amfani da SQLite
(an rubuta shi a matakin `warn`).

### `peek` yana dawo da tsohon bayani / fail-open

Idan `peek` ya jefa kuskure, `enforceQuotaShare` yana ɗaukar sakamakon a matsayin
"ba da izini" (fail-open). Duba logs na `pino` don shigarwar `quota:enforce` da
`quota:factory` domin gano ainihin dalilin.

### Karkacewar ƙididdigar amfani

Idan ainihin amfanin mai samarwa ya bambanta da ƙididdigun, hakan abin tsammani
ne — taga mai zamewa ta rukuni 2 tana da kusan kuskuren 1% a iyakokin taga, kuma
`consume` yana gudana bayan amsa ba tare da jira ba. Alamar cikewa
(`saturationSignals.ts`) tana karanta ainihin matakin amfani na mai samarwa tare
da TTL na daƙiƙa 30 sannan ta daidaita `globalUsedPercent` yadda ya dace.

### Rukuni yana nuna "babu bayanai" don saurin amfani

`computeBurnRate` yana buƙatar aƙalla samfuran tarihi 2. Sababbin rukunoni da ba su
da kiraye-kirayen `consume` na baya za su nuna `tokensPerSecond: 0` da
`timeToExhaustionMs: null`.

## Ƙaura daga localStorage

Lokacin da `/dashboard/costs/quota-share` ya fara lodawa, hook ɗin `useLocalStoragePoolMigration`
yana bincika:

1. `localStorage.getItem("omniroute:quota-share:pools")` ba fanko ba ne.
2. `GET /api/quota/pools` yana dawo da `[]` (DB fanko ne).

Idan duka biyun gaskiya ne, yana aika kowane tsohon pool zuwa `POST /api/quota/pools` a rukuni,
sannan ya cire maɓallin localStorage. Ƙaurar idempotent ce: sharadi na 2 yana hana
sake yin ƙaura.

## Rarraba Dabarun Ciki

`quota-share` dabarar routing ce ta **ciki-kawai** (`INTERNAL_ROUTING_STRATEGY_VALUES` a cikin
`src/shared/constants/routingStrategies.ts`). Combos na pool na `qtSd/` da tsarin ya ƙirƙira ne
kawai suke amfani da ita, kuma da gangan aka cire ta daga `ROUTING_STRATEGY_VALUES` domin kada
ta taɓa bayyana a matsayin zaɓin da mai amfani zai iya zaɓa a cikin UI ko API.

## DB Schema Summary

Tebura guda uku da aka ƙara ta ƙaura `078`, `079` da `085`:

- `quota_pools` + `quota_allocations` — ma'anonin tafkin da kuma rabon maɓalli.
- `quota_consumption` — mirgina 2-guga counters per `(apiKeyId, dimensionKey)`.
- `provider_plans` — tsarin mai bada jagora ya soke (girman JSON kowace connectionId).

Duk allunan da aka ƙara ta hanyar ƙauran `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` masu ƙarfi.

Bayar da lamuni yana ƙara ginshiƙai uku: `quota_allocations.lending_percent` ( lamba, tsoho 0), `quota_allocations.lending_until` (epoch milliseconds, ko `NULL` don babu ƙayyadaddun lokaci) da `quota_pools.borrow_mode` ( `saturation` tsoho). Ƙauransa yana ƙara waɗannan ginshiƙai ne kawai kuma ba ya rubuta wani layi na yanzu.

Littafin sifa na kashi yana ƙara `quota_percent_ledger` (jeri ɗaya a kowane tafkin, asusu da taga shirin) da `quota_percent_ledger_tokens` (ƙirar alama ɗaya a kowane tafkin, asusu da maɓallin API ). `src/lib/db/quotaPercentLedger.ts` yana karantawa ya rubuta su.
