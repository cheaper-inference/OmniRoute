> **Matsayi:** an gina dashboard akan **Keak CI UI Kit** (`@keak/cheaper-inference-ui-kit`). An haɗa kit ɗin a cikin wannan ma'ajiyar azaman sararin aiki `packages/ui-kit` (`@omniroute/ui-kit`). Hoton da ke sama yana liƙa a cikin `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` kuma `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` yana kiyaye shi. Jigon tunani shine yanayin haske; fifikon tsoho shine `system`. Tsohuwar palette na kit's ne **Editions** bayyanar: dumi-dumin takarda-launuka masu tsaka-tsaki da launi na jan karfe `#e5ac82` (`packages/ui-kit/src/ci-editions.css`). Saitunan → Bayyanar → Launi yana tayi **Classic** , Alamu na ƙirar ƙirar kit ɗin tare da lafazin lemun tsami-kore. Switzer shine farkon dangin rubutu don UI da rubutu mai nuni (`--ci-font-ui`; `--ci-font-display` da `--ci-font-mono` ƙuduri zuwa gare shi). Ana ba da font ɗin daga fayilolin rubutu na gida da aka haɗa a cikin kit (`packages/ui-kit/src/ci-fonts.css`, wanda `ci-tokens.css` ya shigo da shi). Kowane gunki ya fito daga Lucide.
>
> Inter koma baya ce ga glyphs da suka ɓace daga Switzer; taswirorin `--font-mono` zuwa jerin tsayayyen fonts na tsarin `--ci-font-code`.
>
> `src/app/globals.css` tana zaɓar rubutun baya a kowane glyph lokacin da `Switzer` ya rasa glyph: `Inter` don Cyrillic, Greek da Vietnamese, da `Vazirmatn` don rubutun Larabci kawai a ƙarƙashin `html[dir="rtl"]`.

`src/app/globals.css`:

```css
:root,
.ci-theme {
  --ci-font-ui:
    "Switzer", "Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif;
  --ci-font-code: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", monospace;
  --font-mono: var(--ci-font-code);
}
```

## 1. Inda abubuwa suke rayuwa

- **Kit.** `packages/ui-kit/src` kwafin byte-for-byte ne na `src/` na sama a ƙaddamarwa a cikin `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` (wanda kuma ke rikodin izinin lasisi); `PATCHES.md` yana lissafin faci na gida. Ana cinye shi azaman tushe, ba tare da ginawa ba: `next.config.mjs` yana jujjuya shi, kuma taswirar `tsconfig.json` `@omniroute/ui-kit` → `packages/ui-kit/src` da `@omniroute/ui-kit/*` → `packages/ui-kit/src/*` (`vitest.config.ts` da `vitest.mcp.config.ts` suna ɗauke da laƙabi ɗaya). Shigo da kayan kit ta hanyar ƙarƙashinsa (`@omniroute/ui-kit/table`, `@omniroute/ui-kit/page-header`…), ba ta hanyar ganga `@omniroute/ui-kit` ba: ganga tana jan kowane sashi (ciki har da mai ɗaukar kwanan wata) cikin kowane mai shigo da kaya. Salon kit ɗin yana shiga ta hanyar `src/app/globals.css` (`@import "../../packages/ui-kit/src/ci-styles.css"`, cikakken takardar: ainihin `ci-ui.css` tare da kowane dangi).
- **Alamu.** `packages/ui-kit/src/ci-tokens.css` yana bayyana alamun `--ci-*` ; `src/app/globals.css` yana haɗa su zuwa sunayen OmniRoute na gado (`--color-*`, `--radius-*`, `--font-sans`/`--font-mono`, `--table-*`, `--orch-status-*`, `--color-fd-*`), don haka `var(--color-*)` na yanzu yana karantawa kuma kayan aikin Tailwind suna ci gaba da aiki. Sabuwar lambar tana amfani da kayan aikin Tailwind (`bg-surface`, `text-text-muted`, `border-border`, `text-success`, `bg-success-bg`, `bg-chart-1`…) ko azuzuwan kit — ba zai taɓa darajar hex ba (`tests/unit/design-kit-bridge.test.ts` yana tilasta gada). `text-primary` yana ƙaddamarwa zuwa `--color-primary-ink` ta hanyar `--text-color-primary`, don haka rubutun farko yana kasancewa mai karantawa yayin da `bg-primary`/`border-primary` ke kiyaye farfajiyar lafazin: a ƙarƙashin Editions shi ne `--ci-color-accent-active` na kit (zurfin `#99562f` na jan karfe a cikin haske, lafazin jan karfe a duhu); karkashin Classic, lime-800 a cikin haske da kuma lemun tsami a cikin duhu. Jerin ginshiƙi suna amfani da `--color-chart-1` … `--color-chart-8`, kowannensu yana da `--color-chart-N-ink` mai aminci da rubutu da guntuwar guntu `--color-chart-N-subtle` mara kyau, wanda aka sanya a cikin jerin kit ɗin (`ciChartSeriesOrder`).
- **Jigogi.** `<html className="ci-theme">` (`src/app/layout.tsx`) ya zaɓi daftarin aiki a cikin jigon kit. Abin da ake so shine `light`, `dark` ko `system` (`THEME_CONFIG.defaultTheme = "system"` a cikin `src/shared/constants/appConfig.ts`; `system` yana bin `prefers-color-scheme`). `applyTheme()` a cikin `src/store/themeStore.ts` (wanda `THEME_BOOT_SCRIPT` ya yi madubi, wanda aka sa a cikin `src/app/layout.tsx`, wanda ke gudana kafin ruwa) yana saita `data-theme="dark"|"light"` kuma yana jujjuya ajin `.dark` akan `<html>` tare. Saitin alamar duhun kit ɗin yana da maɓalli akan `data-theme="dark"` (`packages/ui-kit/src/ci-tokens.css`); OmniRoute nasa duhu ya ƙetare (tushe `.dark` a cikin `globals.css`) da maɓallin kayan aikin Tailwind `dark:` na gado akan ajin `.dark`. Babu saitattun launi na lafazin: lafazin ko da yaushe yana fitowa daga kit ɗin, kuma saitaccen saiti daga tsofaffin ginin yana raguwa lokacin da kantin sayar da kaya ya yi lodi.
- **Palettes.** Biyu, duka daga kit (`src/shared/constants/appearancePalette.ts`): `editions`, tsoho, da `classic`. Editions shine sifa na `data-ci-appearance="editions"` na kit akan `<html>`, wanda `ci-editions.css` (wanda `ci-ui.css` ya shigo da shi) ya haɗu da `data-theme`; Classic shine rashin sifa. Tushen shimfidar uwar garken - yana ba da sifa, don haka baƙo ba tare da komai ba (ko babu JS) yana samun Editions akan kowane saman dashboard —, shiga, `/status`, shafukan kuskure, `/docs` da PWA. Zaɓin yana dagewa kusa da jigon a cikin kantin sayar `theme` guda ɗaya (`palette`, `setPalette()`, `applyPalette()` a `src/store/themeStore.ts`) kuma ana amfani dashi kafin zane ta `THEME_BOOT_SCRIPT` (`src/shared/utils/themeBootScript.ts`, wanda aka sanya a cikin `<head>` na layout), wanda ke cire sifa kawai lokacin da palette da aka adana shine `"classic"`; `src/app/global-error.tsx`, a waje da tushen tushen, yana amfani da shi daga kantin sayar da. Kada a sanya `data-ci-appearance` akan kowane nau'i: kayan aikin ya dace da shi da `html:root:has([data-ci-appearance="editions"])`, don haka zuriya ɗaya za ta canza duk takaddun. Bambancin Editions `data-ci-editions-variant="landing"` don shafukan talla ne, ba dashboard ba. chrome Browser: uwar garken yana ba da tsoho, Editions, a cikin `viewport.themeColor` (tag ɗaya akan `prefers-color-scheme`) da kuma bayanan yanar gizon — `PWA_THEME_COLORS` a cikin `src/shared/constants/pwaThemeColors.ts`, madubi ɗaya na Editions `--ci-color-background` (`#f3f1e9` / `#11120f`), wanda aka lika zuwa `ci-editions.css` ta `tests/unit/pwa-theme-colors.test.ts`. `<meta name="theme-color">` mai rai sannan ya bi palette da yanayin da aka adana: `THEME_BOOT_SCRIPT` ya sake rubuta alamun kafin fenti kuma ayyukan kantin jigo suna yin shi akan kowane canji (`resolveThemeColor()` / `applyThemeColor()` a cikin `src/shared/utils/themeColorMeta.ts`, tare da karanta `CLASSIC_PAGE_BACKGROUND` daga kit's `ciTokens`). Taken light/dark bayyananne yana saita kowane alamar zuwa wannan yanayin; `system` yana kiyaye launi ɗaya kowane tsari. Bayanin yana tsayawa Editions: ana samun shi sau ɗaya a kowane shigarwa, ba tare da fifiko ba. Monaco tana karanta launuka daga binciken (`monacoKitTheme.ts`); A karkashin Editions binciken da aka karanta yana jujjuya tushen `data-theme` don fasfo guda ɗaya na aiki tare, saboda kit ɗin yana warware Editions light/dark daga tushen.
- **Abubuwan da aka gyara (adapters).** `src/shared/components/index.tsx` kiyaye da OmniRoute bangaren API (sunaye iri ɗaya, fitar da tsoho da abubuwan haɓakawa), da kuma abubuwan da ke bayan sa adaftan akan kit: siffofi (`Button`, `Input`, `Textarea`, `Checkbox`, `Select`, `Toggle`, `Badge`, `Card`), overlays da feedback (`Modal`/`ConfirmModal`, `Tooltip`/`InfoTooltip`, `NotificationToast`, `EmptyState`, `Loading`), tsari (`Collapsible`, `CollapsibleSection`, `SegmentedControl`, `Breadcrumbs`, `Avatar`) da tebur (`DataTable`, `FilterBar`, `ColumnToggle`, `FilterPills`, `PresetSlider`). Adafta yana shigo da kayan kit ta hanyar ƙarƙashin ƙasa kuma yana tsara abubuwan abubuwan gado akansa — misali `Button variant="primary"` kit ne `default` button (`--ci-button-primary-*`: juzu'in saman haske, lafazin tare da ita `--ci-color-accent-foreground` tawada a cikin duhu), `danger` shine `destructive`, kuma `warning` kit ne `warning` maballin. `Badge` bambance-bambancen matsayi sune sautin kit, da `Select` tare da `<option>` yara suna yin kit `NativeSelect`. `tests/unit/ui/adapters/*.test.tsx` fil kowacce adaftar.
- **Shell** `src/app/(dashboard)/layout.tsx` yana fassara `DashboardLayout`, wanda ke fassara `src/shared/components/layouts/DashboardShell.tsx`: kit ɗin `SidebarProvider` / `Sidebar` / `SidebarInset` (`packages/ui-kit/src/sidebar.tsx`), tare da dabarun kewayawa OmniRoute a cikin `src/shared/components/Sidebar.tsx`. Hanyar hanyar "tsalle zuwa abun ciki" zuwa `#main-content` shine kashi na farko da ake mayar da hankali. `src/shared/components/Header.tsx` yana riƙe da kit ɗin `SidebarTrigger` (maɓallin gefen harsashi kawai), Keak `ProductSwitcher` da mabuɗin palette; yana nuna taken hanya ne kawai yayin da babu kit `PageHeader` (`.ci-page-header`) da aka dora a cikin `#main-content`, don haka shafi mai `PageHeader` nasa yana da `h1` guda daya . `src/shared/components/CommandPalette.tsx` shine kit `Dialog`.
- **Gumaka** `<Icon name="material_name">` (`src/shared/components/Icon.tsx`) yana warware sunan ( ƙamus na Alamomin Material da allon ke amfani da shi) ta hanyar `ICON_MAP` (`src/shared/constants/iconMap.ts`) zuwa glyph Lucide. Rubutun Alamomin Material da kunshin sun ɓace: sunan da ya ɓace daga taswirar yana sanya glyph mai riƙe tsaka tsaki (`data-icon-missing="true"`) kuma yana yin rajistar gargaɗin na'urar bidiyo a wajen samarwa. `npm run gen:icon-map` yana buga sunayen da ake amfani da su da suka ɓace daga taswira (`tests/unit/icon-map-coverage.test.ts` ya gaza akan kowane suna ba tare da alamar Lucide ba; `tests/unit/icon-map-object-values.test.ts` yana rufe sunaye da aka adana a cikin abubuwan bincike); `scripts/dev/codemod-material-icons.mjs <dir|file>... [--write]` yana sake rubutawa na zahiri na `<span className="material-symbols-outlined">` zuwa `<Icon>`.
- **Alamar.** `src/shared/components/OmniRouteLogo.tsx` ta nannade kit ɗin: tsohuwar fitarwa `OmniRouteLogo` tana ba da `ProductIcon product="omniroute" variant="glyph"` ( SVG na layi, `basePath` -safe) kuma `OmniRouteLockup` yana sanya `ProductLockup product="omniroute"` (suna mai dama "Keak OmniRoute") ko, ga masu aiki da alamar farar, nasu `name`/`logoSrc`. Wuraren sauya samfura suna rayuwa a cikin `src/shared/constants/keakProducts.ts` (`KEAK_PRODUCT_LINKS`, `KEAK_ACTIVE_PRODUCT`). Fuskokin da ke tsaye — `src/app/login/page.tsx`, `src/app/status/page.tsx`, `src/app/maintenance/page.tsx`, `src/app/offline/page.tsx`, `src/app/not-found.tsx` da `src/shared/components/ErrorPageScaffold.tsx` — suna nuna makullin; `tests/unit/brand-pages-static.test.ts` ya kiyaye shi.

## 2. Dokoki

1. Tokens, ba ƙimomin kai-tsaye ba. Keɓantattun abubuwan da aka bar da gangan: terminal ɗin console mai duhu koyaushe da
   `STATUS_HEX` (`src/shared/constants/statusColors.ts`), madubin resolved-hex na palette ɗin matsayin kit
   ga masu kira waɗanda ke buƙatar ƙimar hex kai-tsaye. Palettes na rukunai ma ba keɓantattu ba ne yanzu:
   suna amfani da tokens takwas na chart na kit (`--color-chart-1..8`, tare da `-ink`/`-subtle`), bisa
   jerin kit series, ta hanyar
   `src/shared/utils/categoricalPalette.ts` kuma, don dabarun routing,
   `src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/combos/strategyPalette.ts`; ReactFlow edges da orchestration
   canvas suna karanta variables masu bin theme na `--orch-status-*` (`src/shared/components/flow/edgeStyles.ts`).
2. Kada a taɓa gyara `packages/ui-kit/src` da hannu: yi sync da `scripts/dev/sync-ui-kit.sh <commit>`; ƙofar
   pre-commit ta `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` (`.husky/pre-commit`) tana fāɗuwa idan akwai ko byte ɗaya
   da ya bambanta da pinned upstream commit (tana tsallakewa tare da gargaɗi idan upstream clone,
   `UI_KIT_CLONE` ko `_references/cheaper-inference-ui-kit`, ba ya nan). Idan kit ba shi da wani abu,
   nemo mafita a adapter ko screen sannan a nemi upstream ya samar da shi.
3. Tsoffin rubutun kit Turanci ne (`label = "Loading"`, `loadingLabel = "Loading table"`…) — tura rubutun da aka
   fassara daga `useTranslations(...)` duk lokacin da wani kit component ke nuna rubutu.
4. Tailwind utilities suna rinjayar classes na kit component (kit yana ayyana
   `@layer theme, base, components, utilities` kuma dokokinsa suna cikin `components`) — yi amfani da utilities don
   spacing da layout, ba don sake canza launin kit components ba.
5. Headings: `.ci-heading` don taken shafi da sashe; `.ci-dashboard-display` kawai don babban rubutun display
   mai sarari, kamar status code na shafin kuskure. Dukansu suna amfani da Switzer.
6. `tests/unit/dashboard-kit-sweep.test.ts` yana tsare karɓar kit: babu Material ligature span a ƙarƙashin
   `src/app` ko `src/shared/components`; a cikin bishiyar dashboard (`src/app/(dashboard)` da
   `src/shared/components`) babu ɗanyen `<select>`, `<textarea>` ko `<table>`, kuma babu
   `role="dialog"` da aka gina da hannu a wajen keɓantattun wuraren da aka lissafa (native branch na
   `Select` adapter, `RequestLoggerDetail`); kowane ɗanyen `<button>` ko `<input>` a wurin yana buƙatar comment mai ɗauke da
   `kit-exception:` (tare da dalili) wanda zai ƙare a mafi yawan layuka 3 a samansa; palette hues da `dark:` variants
   kawai a cikin files da test ya lissafa.
7. Tabbatar da themes ɗin biyu da viewport na 390 px kafin PR (screenshots); `tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts` yana gudanar da
   axe.

## 3. Ƙara allo

1. Fara shafin da `PageHeader` na kit (`@omniroute/ui-kit/page-header`, `compact` a cikin
   dashboard) — take, bayani da `actions`. Sannan header ɗin shell yana bar taken kansa.
2. Gina controls daga `@/shared/components` (adapters ɗin) kuma yi amfani da kit components ta subpath inda babu
   adapter (`Table`/`TableScrollArea`/`DataTableShell`, `Field`, `Alert`, `Tabs`, `RadioGroup`,
   `Slider`…).
3. Icons: `<Icon name="…" />`. Sabon suna yana shiga `ICON_MAP` tare da Lucide component ɗin da ke dace da shi a ma'ana;
   `npm run gen:icon-map` yana jera sunayen da har yanzu ba a ƙara ba.
4. Launuka: semantic utilities (`text-success`, `bg-warning-bg`, `border-border`…) da
   `var(--color-chart-N)` don jerin chart; babu ƙimar hex kuma babu `dark:` variants da ke maimaita launi kawai.
5. Rubutu: kowanne string, gami da labels ɗin da kake wucewa zuwa kit components, yana fita daga `useTranslations`. Sababbin
   keys suna shiga `src/i18n/messages/en.json`, `pt-BR.json` da `vi.json` (gwajin daidaito na Vietnamese yana kafa
   kowanne key na Turanci); sauran locales ana cika su ta sync ɗin i18n.
6. Gudanar da guards ɗin kafin PR: `tests/unit/dashboard-kit-sweep.test.ts`,
   `tests/unit/icon-map-coverage.test.ts`, `tests/unit/icon-map-object-values.test.ts`,
   `npm run check:dashboard-typecheck` (kawai gwajin da ke kama import da aka maimaita a cikin `iconMap.ts`),
   `npm run check:ui-icu`, da gwaje-gwajen allon kanta.

## 4. Ƙara ko canza adapter

1. Kiyaye API na jama'a na `src/shared/components/index.tsx`: sunayen export iri ɗaya, default exports da props da aka karɓa. Daidaita legacy props a kan kit a cikin adapter (duba `Button.tsx`: `VARIANT_TO_KIT`,
   `SIZE_TO_KIT`).
2. Shigo da kit ta subpath (`@omniroute/ui-kit/<component>`).
3. Hanyoyin kaucewa ga gibunan kit suna zaune a cikin adapter (ko a cikin `globals.css` `@layer components` idan gyaran salon ne), ba taɓa a `packages/ui-kit/src` ba.
4. Ƙara ko sabunta `tests/unit/ui/adapters/<name>.test.tsx`. Ga adapter mai masu kira da yawa, kwatanta markup da aka render kafin da bayan canjin — canje-canjen padding ko wrapper suna yaɗuwa zuwa kowane allo.

## 5. Sabunta kit

`npm run check:ui-kit-upstream` yana kwatanta sigar da aka liƙa a cikin `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` tare da sabuwar alamar `v*` na repo na sama kuma yana buga umarnin daidaitawa lokacin da sakin ya fito.

Gudun aikin `UI Kit upstream watch` na yau da kullun (`.github/workflows/ui-kit-upstream-watch.yml`) yana gudanar da bincike iri ɗaya. Yayin da fil ɗin yana baya, `scripts/ci/ui-kit-sync-pr.sh` yana buɗe PR guda ɗaya a buɗe tare da mafi girman reshe `release/vX.Y.Z`, akan reshe na birgima `chore/ui-kit-sync-latest`. Ba zai taɓa haɗuwa ba: kowane sakin kit har yanzu yana buƙatar dubawa na gani akan VPS na gida. Ana sake gina reshen lokacin da sabon sakin ya fito kuma a bar shi shi kaɗai. Buɗaɗɗen PR tare da ayyukan turawa da hannu ba a sake rubutawa ba, kuma PR na sigar da mai kula ya rufe ba a haɗa shi ba yana zama a rufe har sai an sake sabon saki.

Lokacin da ba za a iya buɗe PR ba (sakin da aka yi niyya ya daskare, PR ya aikata da hannu, an rufe PR, ko kuma ba a saita alamar PR ) aikin yana buɗe batun bin diddigi guda ɗaya wanda ke faɗi dalilin.

Tsarin aiki yana amfani da sirri guda biyu:

- `secrets.UI_KIT_READ_TOKEN`: alamar karantawa kawai ga mai zaman kansa na sama. Lokacin da binciken sama ba zai iya karanta bayanai ba (ba alama, alamar da ta daina aiki, ko GitHub ba za a iya kaiwa ba), yana ba da rahoton `unknown` kuma yana fita tare da lambar `0`. Gudun aikin yana rubuta gargaɗi kuma yana ƙoƙarin buɗe batun bin diddigin guda ɗaya; gudu mai nasara daga baya yana rufe shi bayan dawowar shiga. Rashin gazawar batun GitHub API ya kasa aiki.
- `secrets.UI_KIT_SYNC_PR_TOKEN`: alama ce mai kyau akan wannan ma'ajiyar tare da Abun ciki da Buƙatun jawo rubuta damar shiga. Wannan aikin yana buɗewa ko sabunta aiki tare PRs kawai lokacin da aka saita alamar sadaukarwa; ba tare da shi ba, aikin aiki yana tura reshe kuma ya ba da rahoton cewa ba a buɗe PR ba.

`scripts/dev/sync-ui-kit.sh <commit|latest> [clone]` tana kwafin `src/` na sama a waccan ƙaddamarwa (`latest` = sabuwar `v*` tag) zuwa `packages/ui-kit/src`, tana aiwatar da sauyi ɗaya da aka yarda (yana cire ƙarin `.js` daga shigo da kayayyaki ta hanyoyin dangi), kuma ya saita sigar filin aiki a cikin `packages/ui-kit/package.json` da `package-lock.json` zuwa na sama. Sa'an nan: sake duba `git diff packages/ui-kit`, yi duban gani akan masu samarwa, saituna, combos, amfani da ƙarshen duka a cikin jigogi biyu, gudanar da `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` da `npm run test:vitest:ui`, kuma buɗe PR tare da canjin canji na sama.

## 6. Zane-zanen README

Zane-zane goma sha biyu da tushen `README.md` yake haɗawa (`docs/diagrams/*.svg`) su ne kit ɗin da aka yi amfani da shi a kan hoto marar motsi, kuma ana samar da su ne, ba a zana su da hannu ba: fayilolin samarwa 13 a `scripts/docs/gen-readme-*.mjs` (zane-zane goma sha biyu tare da direban `gen-readme-diagrams.mjs`) da gwaje-gwajen tsufa 12 a `tests/unit/docs/readme-*-svg.test.ts`, ɗaya ga kowane zane, inda kowannensu yake kwatanta fayil ɗin da aka commit baiti-da-baiti da abin da na'urar samar da shi ta fitar (aikin `test-unit`).

- **Tokens.** `scripts/docs/readme-diagram-theme.mjs` yana kwafe palet ɗin Editions (siffar tsoho) daga
  `packages/ui-kit/src/ci-editions.css` (wanda aka ɗaure da wannan fayil ta hanyar
  `tests/unit/docs/readme-diagram-theme.test.ts`, wanda kuma yake duba WCAG AA ga kowane launin rubutu a kan kowace
  fuska a cikin tsare-tsaren biyu; banda guda ɗaya, `warn` na haske, shi ne gargaɗin kit da aka ƙara masa duhu da 10 % saboda ƙimar kit
  ita ce 4.46:1 a kan canvas) kuma yana ayyana rawar rubutu bisa ma'aunin nau'in kit (`ci-tokens.css`): Switzer bisa nauyin kit (400/500/600, nunawa a 500) da tazara tsakanin haruffa ga
  komai, ban da rawar lamba (`.cmd`, `.term`, `.term-lg`, `.mono`, `.mono-sm`, `.mono-xs`), waɗanda
  suke amfani da IBM Plex Mono kuma ana amfani da su ne kawai ga umarni, URLs, masu ganowa da allon terminal.
  Ƙimomin haske suna kan `:root`, ƙimomin duhu kuma suna bayan `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, a cikin fayil ɗin, don haka kowane
  hoto yana bin tsarin mai kallo a GitHub.
- **Fonts.** GitHub Markdown ba ya ɗauke da CSS, kuma camo proxy yana toshe duk wani ɗauko na waje, saboda haka nau'in kit
  dole ne ya kasance a cikin fayil ɗin. `scripts/docs/readme-outline.mjs`, mataki na ƙarshe na kowace na'urar samarwa, yana fasalta
  rawar Switzer da HarfBuzz kuma yana zana su a matsayin siffofin vector (`<defs>` paths da `<use>`), tare da kibiyoyi
  da alamomin lissafin siffa da Switzer ba shi da su waɗanda aka zana bisa ma'aunansa. Siffofin vector ne, ba font da aka saka a ciki ba: lasisin Switzer
  (`packages/ui-kit/src/fonts/FFL.txt`) yana hana rage font da sauya tsari ba tare da rubutacciyar
  izini ba kuma yana ba da damar zane-zanen vector, don haka kada a sake shigar da Switzer `@font-face` ko `data:` URI. Font guda ɗaya
  da ya rage a cikin fayil shi ne ƙaramin ɓangaren IBM Plex Mono na OFL don abin da ya ci gaba da kasancewa rubutun lamba na gaske
  (`scripts/docs/readme-fonts.mjs`), wanda aka gina daga waɗannan haruffa; duk wani glyph da ba zai iya sakawa ba yana jefa kuskure.
- **Sake samarwa.** `npm run docs:readme-diagrams` yana sake rubuta duk goma sha biyun bisa jerin README kuma yana buga layi ɗaya ga kowane
  fayil tare da girmansa. Haka kuma ana iya gudanar da na'urar samarwa guda ɗaya da kanta (`node scripts/docs/gen-readme-<name>.mjs`;
  `free-tier-budget` yana buƙatar `node --import tsx/esm` saboda alkalumansa sun fito daga mai loda katalog mai aiki a
  `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.ts`; sakamakon CI na sauyin katalog yana cikin
  `docs/architecture/QUALITY_GATES.md` → `docs-sync-strict`). Yi commit na na'urar samarwa da SVG tare.
- **Shaida.** `scripts/docs/readme-svg-shots.mjs <file.svg> <outdir>` yana nuna fayil a gwajin gida (tag `<img>`, `Content-Security-Policy` mai takura da amsar `image/svg+xml`), ya ɗauki hoton haske da na duhu, kuma ya bayar da rahoton buƙatun font na waje da shafin ya yi; dole adadinsu ya zama 0.

- **Kasafin girma.** Kasa da 200 KB ga kowane fayil (kowane gwajin tsufa yana tabbatar da hakan); 500 KB ita ce iyakar ƙarshe ga kowane hoton README
  (dokar mai tantance SVG studio).
- **Rubutu na bai ɗaya, adadi daga gate.** kowane janareta yana ɗaukar kwafin zanensa a cikin madaidaicin `*_COPY` (ko ya gina shi daga kasida mai rai) wanda shine duka rubutun da aka zana da shigar da matakin fayyace. Lissafi na canonical suna tsayawa a cikin nodes `<text>` (wanda ba a iya gani da zarar an tsara shi, `fill-opacity:0`), karanta ta `check:docs-counts` (`scripts/check/check-docs-counts-sync.mjs`); Gwaje-gwajen rashin daidaituwa na zane-zanen da ƙofar ke karantawa suma suna gudanar da masu tabbatarwa akan fayil ɗin da aka samar, don haka adadi da ya tashi daga lambar ya kasa gwajin — ya gyara tushen da ƙofar ke karantawa, ba lambar da ke cikin janareta ba.
