> **Matsayi:** an gina dashboard akan **Keak CI UI Kit** (`@keak/cheaper-inference-ui-kit`). An haɗa kit ɗin a cikin wannan ma'ajiyar azaman sararin aiki `packages/ui-kit` (`@omniroute/ui-kit`). Hoton da ke sama yana liƙa a cikin `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` kuma `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` yana kiyaye shi. Jigon tunani shine yanayin haske; fifikon tsoho shine `system`. Tsohuwar palette na kit's ne **Editions** bayyanar: dumi-dumin takarda-launuka masu tsaka-tsaki da launi na jan karfe `#e5ac82` (`packages/ui-kit/src/ci-editions.css`). Saitunan → Bayyanar → Launi yana tayi **Classic** , Alamu na ƙirar ƙirar kit ɗin tare da lafazin lemun tsami-kore. Switzer shine farkon dangin rubutu don UI da rubutu mai nuni (`--ci-font-ui`; `--ci-font-display` da `--ci-font-mono` ƙuduri zuwa gare shi). Ana ba da font ɗin daga fayilolin rubutu na gida da aka haɗa a cikin kit (`packages/ui-kit/src/ci-fonts.css`, wanda `ci-tokens.css` ya shigo da shi). Kowane gunki ya fito daga Lucide.
>
> Inter koma baya ce ga glyphs da suka ɓace daga Switzer; taswirorin `--font-mono` zuwa jerin tsayayyen fonts na tsarin `--ci-font-code`.
>
> `src/app/globals.css` tana zaɓar rubutun baya a kowane glyph lokacin da `Switzer` ya rasa glyph: `Inter` don Cyrillic, Greek da Vietnamese, da `Vazirmatn` don rubutun Larabci kawai a ƙarƙashin `html[dir="rtl"]`.

`src/app/globals.css`:

```css
:root,
.ci-theme {
  --ci-font-ui:
    "Switzer", "Inter", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif;
  --ci-font-code: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Consolas, "Liberation Mono", monospace;
  --font-mono: var(--ci-font-code);
}
```

## 1. Inda abubuwa suke

- **Kit.** `packages/ui-kit/src` kwafi ne iri ɗaya byte-da-byte na upstream `src/` a commit ɗin da aka ƙayyade a
  `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` (wanda kuma yake rubuta izinin lasisi); `PATCHES.md` yana lissafa gyare-gyaren
  gida. Ana amfani da shi kai tsaye a matsayin source, ba tare da build ba: `next.config.mjs` yana transpile ɗinsa, sannan
  `tsconfig.json` yana daidaita `@omniroute/ui-kit` → `packages/ui-kit/src` da `@omniroute/ui-kit/*` →
  `packages/ui-kit/src/*` (`vitest.config.ts` da `vitest.mcp.config.ts` suna ɗauke da alias iri ɗaya). Shigo da
  wani component na kit ta subpath ɗinsa (`@omniroute/ui-kit/table`, `@omniroute/ui-kit/page-header`…), kada a taɓa
  yin hakan ta barrel ɗin `@omniroute/ui-kit`: barrel ɗin yana jawo dukkan components (har da date picker)
  cikin kowane importer. Stylesheet na kit yana shiga ta `src/app/globals.css`
  (`@import "../../packages/ui-kit/src/ci-styles.css"`, cikakken sheet ɗin: babban `ci-ui.css` tare da kowane rukunin pattern).
- **Tokens.** `packages/ui-kit/src/ci-tokens.css` yana ayyana tokens na `--ci-*`; `src/app/globals.css`
  yana haɗa su da tsoffin sunayen OmniRoute (`--color-*`, `--radius-*`, `--font-sans`/`--font-mono`,
  `--table-*`, `--orch-status-*`, `--color-fd-*`), domin karatun `var(--color-*)` da ke akwai da utilities na Tailwind
  su ci gaba da aiki. Sabon code yana amfani da utilities na Tailwind (`bg-surface`, `text-text-muted`,
  `border-border`, `text-success`, `bg-success-bg`, `bg-chart-1`…) ko classes na kit — ba a taɓa amfani da hex value ba
  (`tests/unit/design-kit-bridge.test.ts` yana tabbatar da bridge ɗin). `text-primary` yana warwarewa zuwa
  `--color-primary-ink` ta hanyar `--text-color-primary`, don haka primary text yana kasancewa mai sauƙin karantawa yayin da
  `bg-primary`/`border-primary` suke riƙe accent surface: a ƙarƙashin Editions shi ne
  `--ci-color-accent-active` na kit (deep copper `#99562f` a light, copper accent a dark); a ƙarƙashin Classic,
  lime-800 a light da lime accent a dark. Series na chart suna amfani da
  `--color-chart-1` … `--color-chart-5`.
- **Themes.** `<html className="ci-theme">` (`src/app/layout.tsx`) yana shigar da document cikin theme na kit.
  Zaɓin da aka fi so shi ne `light`, `dark` ko `system` (`THEME_CONFIG.defaultTheme = "system"` a cikin
  `src/shared/constants/appConfig.ts`; `system` yana bin `prefers-color-scheme`). `applyTheme()` a cikin
  `src/store/themeStore.ts` (wanda `THEME_BOOT_SCRIPT` yake kwaikwaya, an saka shi kai tsaye a `src/app/layout.tsx`, kuma yana gudana
  kafin hydration) yana saita `data-theme="dark"|"light"` kuma yana kunna ko kashe class na `.dark` a kan `<html>` tare.
  Dark token set na kit yana dogara da `data-theme="dark"` (`packages/ui-kit/src/ci-tokens.css`);
  dark overrides na OmniRoute kansa (block na `.dark` a cikin `globals.css`) da tsoffin utilities na Tailwind `dark:`
  suna dogara da class na `.dark`. Babu presets na accent color: accent koyaushe yana fitowa daga
  kit, kuma ana cire persisted preset daga tsohon build lokacin da store ya loda.
- **Palettes.** Guda biyu ne, dukkansu daga kit (`src/shared/constants/appearancePalette.ts`): `editions`, wanda shi ne
  default, da `classic`. Editions shi ne attribute na kit `data-ci-appearance="editions"` a kan `<html>`, wanda
  `ci-editions.css` (wanda `ci-ui.css` ya shigo da shi) yake haɗawa da `data-theme`; Classic kuwa shi ne rashin wannan
  attribute. Root layout yana server-render attribute ɗin, don haka baƙon da ba shi da wani abu da aka adana (ko ba shi da JS) yana samun
  Editions a kowane surface — dashboard, login, `/status`, shafukan error, `/docs` da PWA. Ana adana zaɓin
  kusa da theme a cikin store ɗin `theme` iri ɗaya (`palette`, `setPalette()`, `applyPalette()` a cikin
  `src/store/themeStore.ts`) kuma `THEME_BOOT_SCRIPT` yana aiwatar da shi kafin paint
  (`src/shared/utils/themeBootScript.ts`, an saka shi kai tsaye a cikin `<head>` na layout), wanda yake cire attribute ɗin
  kawai idan palette da aka adana shi ne `"classic"`; `src/app/global-error.tsx`, wanda yake wajen root layout, yana aiwatar
  da shi daga store iri ɗaya. Kada a sa `data-ci-appearance` a kan wani element dabam: kit yana matching ɗinsa da
  `html:root:has([data-ci-appearance="editions"])`, don haka descendant guda ɗaya zai sauya document gaba ɗaya.
  Variant na Editions `data-ci-editions-variant="landing"` na shafukan marketing ne, ba na dashboard ba.
  Browser chrome (`viewport.themeColor`, web manifest) ba zai iya karanta CSS ko zaɓin da aka adana ba, saboda haka yana
  bin default: `PWA_THEME_COLORS` a cikin `src/shared/constants/pwaThemeColors.ts` shi ne madubi guda ɗaya na
  `--ci-color-background` na Editions (`#f3f1e9` / `#11120f`), wanda
  `tests/unit/pwa-theme-colors.test.ts` ya ɗaure da `ci-editions.css`. Monaco yana karanta colours ɗinsa daga probe (`monacoKitTheme.ts`);
  a ƙarƙashin Editions, karatun probe yana jujjuya root `data-theme` na synchronous pass guda ɗaya, saboda kit yana
  warware Editions light/dark daga root.
- **Components (adapters).** `src/shared/components/index.tsx` yana riƙe API na component na OmniRoute (sunaye iri ɗaya,
  default exports da props), kuma primitives da ke bayansa adapters ne a kan kit: forms
  (`Button`, `Input`, `Textarea`, `Checkbox`, `Select`, `Toggle`, `Badge`, `Card`), overlays da feedback
  (`Modal`/`ConfirmModal`, `Tooltip`/`InfoTooltip`, `NotificationToast`, `EmptyState`, `Loading`),
  structure (`Collapsible`, `CollapsibleSection`, `SegmentedControl`, `Breadcrumbs`, `Avatar`) da tables
  (`DataTable`, `FilterBar`, `ColumnToggle`, `FilterPills`, `PresetSlider`). Adapter yana shigo da component na kit
  ta subpath sannan ya daidaita tsoffin props da shi — misali `Button variant="primary"` shi ne
  button na kit `default` (`--ci-button-primary-*`: inverse surface a light, accent tare da
  ink na `--ci-color-accent-foreground` a dark), `danger` shi ne `destructive`, sannan `warning` shi ne `outline` tare da
  extension na `.ci-button--warning`. Rules na `.ci-button--warning` da `.ci-badge[data-tone]` da ke
  ƙarshen `globals.css` (`@layer components`) extensions ne na OmniRoute a saman classes na kit.
  `tests/unit/ui/adapters/*.test.tsx` suna ɗaure kowane adapter.
- **Shell.** `src/app/(dashboard)/layout.tsx` yana render `DashboardLayout`, wanda yake render
  `src/shared/components/layouts/DashboardShell.tsx`: `SidebarProvider` / `Sidebar` /
  `SidebarInset` na kit (`packages/ui-kit/src/sidebar.tsx`), tare da navigation logic na OmniRoute a cikin
  `src/shared/components/Sidebar.tsx`. Link na "tsallaka zuwa content" zuwa `#main-content` shi ne element na farko
  da za a iya mayar da focus a kansa. `src/shared/components/Header.tsx` yana ɗauke da `SidebarTrigger` na kit (shi kaɗai ne
  sidebar toggle na shell), `ProductSwitcher` na Keak da mai buɗe command-palette; yana nuna route title ne kawai yayin da
  babu `PageHeader` na kit (`.ci-page-header`) da aka mount a cikin `#main-content`, don haka shafin da yake da nasa
  `PageHeader` yana da `h1` guda ɗaya. `src/shared/components/CommandPalette.tsx` `Dialog` ne na kit.
- **Gumaka.** `<Icon name="material_name">` (`src/shared/components/Icon.tsx`) yana daidaita sunan (kalmomin
  Material Symbols da shafukan suke amfani da su) ta hanyar `ICON_MAP` (`src/shared/constants/iconMap.ts`) zuwa
  alamar Lucide. An cire font da kunshin Material Symbols: sunan da babu a taswirar yana nuna
  alamar madadin tsaka-tsaki (`data-icon-missing="true"`) kuma yana rubuta gargadi a console a wajen production.
  `npm run gen:icon-map` yana fitar da sunayen da ake amfani da su waɗanda babu su a taswirar
  (`tests/unit/icon-map-coverage.test.ts` yana gaza kan duk sunan da ba shi da gunkin Lucide;
  `tests/unit/icon-map-object-values.test.ts` yana gwada sunayen da aka adana a cikin abubuwan lookup);
  `scripts/dev/codemod-material-icons.mjs <dir|file>... [--write]` yana sake rubuta spans na zahiri na
  `<span className="material-symbols-outlined">` zuwa `<Icon>`.
- **Alama.** `src/shared/components/OmniRouteLogo.tsx` yana naɗe kit ɗin: default export `OmniRouteLogo`
  yana nuna `ProductIcon product="omniroute" variant="glyph"` (SVG na inline, mai aminci da `basePath`) sannan
  `OmniRouteLockup` yana nuna `ProductLockup product="omniroute"` (sunan isa “Keak OmniRoute”) ko kuma, ga
  masu gudanarwa na white-label, nasu `name`/`logoSrc`. Wuraren zuwa na product-switcher suna cikin
  `src/shared/constants/keakProducts.ts` (`KEAK_PRODUCT_LINKS`, `KEAK_ACTIVE_PRODUCT`). Shafukan masu zaman kansu
  — `src/app/login/page.tsx`, `src/app/status/page.tsx`, `src/app/maintenance/page.tsx`,
  `src/app/offline/page.tsx`, `src/app/not-found.tsx` da `src/shared/components/ErrorPageScaffold.tsx`
  — suna nuna lockup ɗin; `tests/unit/brand-pages-static.test.ts` yana tabbatar da hakan.

## 2. Dokoki

1. Tokens kawai, ba a amfani da literals ba. An riƙe keɓancewa da gangan: tashar console da take
   duhu a kowane lokaci da `STATUS_HEX` (`src/shared/constants/statusColors.ts`), madubin hex da aka
   warware na palette na matsayin kit ga masu kira da ke buƙatar hex na zahiri. Palettes na rukuni
   ba su cikin keɓancewa kuma: suna tafiya a kan tokens biyar na chart na kit (`--color-chart-1..5`)
   ta hanyar `src/shared/utils/categoricalPalette.ts` kuma, ga dabarun routing,
   `src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/combos/strategyPalette.ts`; gefunan ReactFlow da canvas na
   orchestration suna karanta masu canji `--orch-status-*` da suka fahimci jigo
   (`src/shared/components/flow/edgeStyles.ts`).
2. Kada a taɓa gyara `packages/ui-kit/src` da hannu: yi sync da `scripts/dev/sync-ui-kit.sh <commit>`; matakin binciken pre-commit `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` (`.husky/pre-commit`) yana gaza idan akwai ko byte ɗaya da ya bambanta da pinned upstream commit (yana tsallakewa tare da gargaɗi idan upstream clone,
   `UI_KIT_CLONE` ko `_references/cheaper-inference-ui-kit`, ba ya nan). Idan kit ɗin ba shi da wani abu,
   nemo hanyar kewaye da hakan a adapter ko screen sannan a nemi upstream ya samar da shi.
3. Tsoffin rubutun kit suna cikin Turanci (`label = "Loading"`, `loadingLabel = "Loading table"`…) — aika strings da aka fassara daga `useTranslations(...)` a duk lokacin da kit component yake render ɗin rubutu.
4. Tailwind utilities suna rinjayar classes na kit component (kit ɗin yana ayyana
   `@layer theme, base, components, utilities` kuma dokokinsa suna cikin `components`) — yi amfani da utilities don
   spacing da layout, ba don sake canza launin kit components ba.
5. Kanun labarai: `.ci-heading` don taken shafi da sashe; `.ci-dashboard-display` kawai don babba, rubutun nuni mara ƙarfi kamar lambar matsayi na shafin kuskure. Dukansu suna amfani da Switzer.
6. `tests/unit/dashboard-kit-sweep.test.ts` yana tabbatar da rungumar kit ɗin: babu Material ligature span a ƙarƙashin
   `src/app` ko `src/shared/components`; a cikin dashboard tree (`src/app/(dashboard)` da
   `src/shared/components`) babu raw `<select>`, `<textarea>` ko `<table>`, kuma babu `role="dialog"` da aka ƙirƙira da hannu
   a wajen keɓancewar da aka lissafa (`Select` adapter's native branch,
   `RequestLoggerDetail`); kowane raw `<button>` ko `<input>` a wurin yana buƙatar comment mai ɗauke da
   `kit-exception:` (tare da dalili) wanda yake ƙarewa a mafi yawan layuka 3 a samansa; palette hues da `dark:` variants
   suna cikin files da test ɗin ya lissafa kawai.
7. Tabbatar da themes biyu da viewport na 390 px kafin PR (screenshots); `tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts` yana gudanar da
   axe.

## 3. Ƙara allo

1. Fara shafin da `PageHeader` na kit (`@omniroute/ui-kit/page-header`, `compact` a cikin
   dashboard) — take, bayani da `actions`. Sannan header ɗin shell yana bar taken kansa.
2. Gina controls daga `@/shared/components` (adapters ɗin) kuma yi amfani da kit components ta subpath inda babu
   adapter (`Table`/`TableScrollArea`/`DataTableShell`, `Field`, `Alert`, `Tabs`, `RadioGroup`,
   `Slider`…).
3. Icons: `<Icon name="…" />`. Sabon suna yana shiga `ICON_MAP` tare da Lucide component ɗin da ke dace da shi a ma'ana;
   `npm run gen:icon-map` yana jera sunayen da har yanzu ba a ƙara ba.
4. Launuka: semantic utilities (`text-success`, `bg-warning-bg`, `border-border`…) da
   `var(--color-chart-N)` don jerin chart; babu ƙimar hex kuma babu `dark:` variants da ke maimaita launi kawai.
5. Rubutu: kowanne string, gami da labels ɗin da kake wucewa zuwa kit components, yana fita daga `useTranslations`. Sababbin
   keys suna shiga `src/i18n/messages/en.json`, `pt-BR.json` da `vi.json` (gwajin daidaito na Vietnamese yana kafa
   kowanne key na Turanci); sauran locales ana cika su ta sync ɗin i18n.
6. Gudanar da guards ɗin kafin PR: `tests/unit/dashboard-kit-sweep.test.ts`,
   `tests/unit/icon-map-coverage.test.ts`, `tests/unit/icon-map-object-values.test.ts`,
   `npm run check:dashboard-typecheck` (kawai gwajin da ke kama import da aka maimaita a cikin `iconMap.ts`),
   `npm run check:ui-icu`, da gwaje-gwajen allon kanta.

## 4. Ƙara ko canza adapter

1. Kiyaye API na jama'a na `src/shared/components/index.tsx`: sunayen export iri ɗaya, default exports da props da aka karɓa. Daidaita legacy props a kan kit a cikin adapter (duba `Button.tsx`: `VARIANT_TO_KIT`,
   `SIZE_TO_KIT`).
2. Shigo da kit ta subpath (`@omniroute/ui-kit/<component>`).
3. Hanyoyin kaucewa ga gibunan kit suna zaune a cikin adapter (ko a cikin `globals.css` `@layer components` idan gyaran salon ne), ba taɓa a `packages/ui-kit/src` ba.
4. Ƙara ko sabunta `tests/unit/ui/adapters/<name>.test.tsx`. Ga adapter mai masu kira da yawa, kwatanta markup da aka render kafin da bayan canjin — canje-canjen padding ko wrapper suna yaɗuwa zuwa kowane allo.

## 5. Sabunta kit

`npm run check:ui-kit-upstream` yana kwatanta sigar da aka liƙa a cikin `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` tare da sabuwar alamar `v*` na repo na sama kuma yana buga umarnin daidaitawa lokacin da sakin ya fito.

Gudun aikin `UI Kit upstream watch` na yau da kullun (`.github/workflows/ui-kit-upstream-watch.yml`) yana gudanar da bincike iri ɗaya. Yayin da fil ɗin yana baya, `scripts/ci/ui-kit-sync-pr.sh` yana buɗe PR guda ɗaya a buɗe tare da mafi girman reshe `release/vX.Y.Z`, akan reshe na birgima `chore/ui-kit-sync-latest`. Ba zai taɓa haɗuwa ba: kowane sakin kit har yanzu yana buƙatar dubawa na gani akan VPS na gida. Ana sake gina reshen lokacin da sabon sakin ya fito kuma a bar shi shi kaɗai. Buɗaɗɗen PR tare da ayyukan turawa da hannu ba a sake rubutawa ba, kuma PR na sigar da mai kula ya rufe ba a haɗa shi ba yana zama a rufe har sai an sake sabon saki.

Lokacin da ba za a iya buɗe PR ba (sakin da aka yi niyya ya daskare, PR ya aikata da hannu, an rufe PR, ko kuma ba a saita alamar PR ) aikin yana buɗe batun bin diddigi guda ɗaya wanda ke faɗi dalilin.

Tsarin aiki yana amfani da sirri guda biyu:

- `secrets.UI_KIT_READ_TOKEN`: alamar karantawa kawai ga mai zaman kansa na sama. Lokacin da binciken sama ba zai iya karanta bayanai ba (ba alama, alamar da ta daina aiki, ko GitHub ba za a iya kaiwa ba), yana ba da rahoton `unknown` kuma yana fita tare da lambar `0`. Gudun aikin yana rubuta gargaɗi kuma yana ƙoƙarin buɗe batun bin diddigin guda ɗaya; gudu mai nasara daga baya yana rufe shi bayan dawowar shiga. Rashin gazawar batun GitHub API ya kasa aiki.
- `secrets.UI_KIT_SYNC_PR_TOKEN`: alama ce mai kyau akan wannan ma'ajiyar tare da Abun ciki da Buƙatun jawo rubuta damar shiga. Wannan aikin yana buɗewa ko sabunta aiki tare PRs kawai lokacin da aka saita alamar sadaukarwa; ba tare da shi ba, aikin aiki yana tura reshe kuma ya ba da rahoton cewa ba a buɗe PR ba.

`scripts/dev/sync-ui-kit.sh <commit|latest> [clone]` tana kwafin `src/` na sama a waccan ƙaddamarwa (`latest` = sabuwar `v*` tag) zuwa `packages/ui-kit/src`, tana aiwatar da sauyi ɗaya da aka yarda (yana cire ƙarin `.js` daga shigo da kayayyaki ta hanyoyin dangi), kuma ya saita sigar filin aiki a cikin `packages/ui-kit/package.json` da `package-lock.json` zuwa na sama. Sa'an nan: sake duba `git diff packages/ui-kit`, yi duban gani akan masu samarwa, saituna, combos, amfani da ƙarshen duka a cikin jigogi biyu, gudanar da `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` da `npm run test:vitest:ui`, kuma buɗe PR tare da canjin canji na sama.

## 6. Zane-zanen README

Zane-zane goma sha biyu da tushen `README.md` yake haɗawa (`docs/diagrams/*.svg`) su ne kit ɗin da aka yi amfani da shi a kan hoto marar motsi, kuma ana samar da su ne, ba a zana su da hannu ba: fayilolin samarwa 13 a `scripts/docs/gen-readme-*.mjs` (zane-zane goma sha biyu tare da direban `gen-readme-diagrams.mjs`) da gwaje-gwajen tsufa 12 a `tests/unit/docs/readme-*-svg.test.ts`, ɗaya ga kowane zane, inda kowannensu yake kwatanta fayil ɗin da aka commit baiti-da-baiti da abin da na'urar samar da shi ta fitar (aikin `test-unit`).

- **Tokens.** `scripts/docs/readme-diagram-theme.mjs` yana kwafe palet ɗin Editions (siffar tsoho) daga
  `packages/ui-kit/src/ci-editions.css` (wanda aka ɗaure da wannan fayil ta hanyar
  `tests/unit/docs/readme-diagram-theme.test.ts`, wanda kuma yake duba WCAG AA ga kowane launin rubutu a kan kowace
  fuska a cikin tsare-tsaren biyu; banda guda ɗaya, `warn` na haske, shi ne gargaɗin kit da aka ƙara masa duhu da 10 % saboda ƙimar kit
  ita ce 4.46:1 a kan canvas) kuma yana ayyana rawar rubutu bisa ma'aunin nau'in kit (`ci-tokens.css`): Switzer bisa nauyin kit (400/500/600, nunawa a 500) da tazara tsakanin haruffa ga
  komai, ban da rawar lamba (`.cmd`, `.term`, `.term-lg`, `.mono`, `.mono-sm`, `.mono-xs`), waɗanda
  suke amfani da IBM Plex Mono kuma ana amfani da su ne kawai ga umarni, URLs, masu ganowa da allon terminal.
  Ƙimomin haske suna kan `:root`, ƙimomin duhu kuma suna bayan `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, a cikin fayil ɗin, don haka kowane
  hoto yana bin tsarin mai kallo a GitHub.
- **Fonts.** GitHub Markdown ba ya ɗauke da CSS, kuma camo proxy yana toshe duk wani ɗauko na waje, saboda haka nau'in kit
  dole ne ya kasance a cikin fayil ɗin. `scripts/docs/readme-outline.mjs`, mataki na ƙarshe na kowace na'urar samarwa, yana fasalta
  rawar Switzer da HarfBuzz kuma yana zana su a matsayin siffofin vector (`<defs>` paths da `<use>`), tare da kibiyoyi
  da alamomin lissafin siffa da Switzer ba shi da su waɗanda aka zana bisa ma'aunansa. Siffofin vector ne, ba font da aka saka a ciki ba: lasisin Switzer
  (`packages/ui-kit/src/fonts/FFL.txt`) yana hana rage font da sauya tsari ba tare da rubutacciyar
  izini ba kuma yana ba da damar zane-zanen vector, don haka kada a sake shigar da Switzer `@font-face` ko `data:` URI. Font guda ɗaya
  da ya rage a cikin fayil shi ne ƙaramin ɓangaren IBM Plex Mono na OFL don abin da ya ci gaba da kasancewa rubutun lamba na gaske
  (`scripts/docs/readme-fonts.mjs`), wanda aka gina daga waɗannan haruffa; duk wani glyph da ba zai iya sakawa ba yana jefa kuskure.
- **Sake samarwa.** `npm run docs:readme-diagrams` yana sake rubuta duk goma sha biyun bisa jerin README kuma yana buga layi ɗaya ga kowane
  fayil tare da girmansa. Haka kuma ana iya gudanar da na'urar samarwa guda ɗaya da kanta (`node scripts/docs/gen-readme-<name>.mjs`;
  `free-tier-budget` yana buƙatar `node --import tsx/esm` saboda alkalumansa sun fito daga mai loda katalog mai aiki a
  `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.ts`; sakamakon CI na sauyin katalog yana cikin
  `docs/architecture/QUALITY_GATES.md` → `docs-sync-strict`). Yi commit na na'urar samarwa da SVG tare.
- **Shaida.** `scripts/docs/readme-svg-shots.mjs <file.svg> <outdir>` yana nuna fayil a gwajin gida (tag `<img>`, `Content-Security-Policy` mai takura da amsar `image/svg+xml`), ya ɗauki hoton haske da na duhu, kuma ya bayar da rahoton buƙatun font na waje da shafin ya yi; dole adadinsu ya zama 0.

- **Kasafin girma.** Kasa da 200 KB ga kowane fayil (kowane gwajin tsufa yana tabbatar da hakan); 500 KB ita ce iyakar ƙarshe ga kowane hoton README
  (dokar mai tantance SVG studio).
- **Rubutu na bai ɗaya, adadi daga gate.** kowane janareta yana ɗaukar kwafin zanensa a cikin madaidaicin `*_COPY` (ko ya gina shi daga kasida mai rai) wanda shine duka rubutun da aka zana da shigar da matakin fayyace. Lissafi na canonical suna tsayawa a cikin nodes `<text>` (wanda ba a iya gani da zarar an tsara shi, `fill-opacity:0`), karanta ta `check:docs-counts` (`scripts/check/check-docs-counts-sync.mjs`); Gwaje-gwajen rashin daidaituwa na zane-zanen da ƙofar ke karantawa suma suna gudanar da masu tabbatarwa akan fayil ɗin da aka samar, don haka adadi da ya tashi daga lambar ya kasa gwajin — ya gyara tushen da ƙofar ke karantawa, ba lambar da ke cikin janareta ba.
