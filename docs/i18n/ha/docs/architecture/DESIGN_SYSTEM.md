> **Matsayi:** an gina dashboard ɗin a kan **Keak CI UI Kit** (`@keak/cheaper-inference-ui-kit`), wanda aka saka shi
> a matsayin workspace `packages/ui-kit` (`@omniroute/ui-kit`; an kafa snapshot na upstream a cikin
> `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` kuma ana kare shi da `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot`). Light shi ne
> theme ɗin asali; zaɓin da aka sanya a matsayin default shi ne `system`. Accent ɗin shi ne Keak lime
> (`--ci-color-accent`). Inter (letter-spacing 0, `--ci-letter-spacing-ui`) shi ne iyalin rubutun UI,
> Instrument Serif kawai don manyan rubutu masu tsira na nuni (`.ci-dashboard-display`), IBM Plex Mono
> don code — duk ana masaukar su da kansu ta hanyar `@fontsource/*` imports da ke saman
> `src/app/globals.css`. Kowane icon glyph ne na Lucide.

## 1. Inda abubuwa suke

- **Kit.** `packages/ui-kit/src` kwafi ne baiti-da-baiti (byte-for-byte) na `src/` na upstream a commit ɗin da aka manda a `packages/ui-kit/UPSTREAM.md` (wanda kuma yana riƙe da izinin lasisi); `PATCHES.md` yana jere gyare-gyare na gida (babu). Ana amfani da shi a matsayin tushe (source), ba tare da build ba: `next.config.mjs` ne yake transpile da shi, kuma `tsconfig.json` yana ƙaddara `@omniroute/ui-kit` → `packages/ui-kit/src` da `@omniroute/ui-kit/*` → `packages/ui-kit/src/*` (`vitest.config.ts` da `vitest.mcp.config.ts` suna ɗauke da wannan alias ɗin guda). Shigo da component ɗin kit ta subpath ɗinsa (`@omniroute/ui-kit/table`, `@omniroute/ui-kit/page-header`…), kada a taɓa ta barrel ɗin `@omniroute/ui-kit`: barrel yana ja dukkan components (ciki har da mai zaɓin kwanan wata) zuwa kowane mai shigo da su. Stylesheet ɗin kit yana shiga ta `src/app/globals.css` (`@import "../../packages/ui-kit/src/ci-ui.css"`).
- **Tokens.** `packages/ui-kit/src/ci-tokens.css` yana bayyana tokens na `--ci-*`; `src/app/globals.css` yana haɗa su zuwa tsofaffin sunayen OmniRoute (`--color-*`, `--radius-*`, `--font-sans`/`--font-mono`, `--table-*`, `--orch-status-*`, `--color-fd-*`), don haka karatun `var(--color-*)` da ake da su da Tailwind utilities su ci gaba da yin aiki. Sabon code yana amfani da Tailwind utilities (`bg-surface`, `text-text-muted`, `border-border`, `text-success`, `bg-success-bg`, `bg-chart-1`…) ko classes na kit — ba taɓa ƙimar hex ba (`tests/unit/design-kit-bridge.test.ts` yana tilasta wannan haɗin). `text-primary` yana warwara zuwa `--color-primary-ink` ta `--text-color-primary` (lime-800 a light, accent ɗin lime a dark), don haka rubutun primary yana kasancewa mai iya karantawa yayin da `bg-primary`/`border-primary` suke riƙe da lime surface. Chart series suna amfani da `--color-chart-1` … `--color-chart-5`.
- **Themes.** `<html className="ci-theme">` (`src/app/layout.tsx`) yana sa takardar ta shiga theme ɗin kit. Zaɓin shine `light`, `dark` ko `system` (`THEME_CONFIG.defaultTheme = "system"` a `src/shared/constants/appConfig.ts`; `system` yana bin `prefers-color-scheme`). `applyTheme()` a `src/store/themeStore.ts` (wanda inline bootstrap script ɗin `src/app/layout.tsx` yake kwafinsa, wanda yana gudu kafin hydration) yana saita `data-theme="dark"|"light"` kuma yana canza `.dark` class a kan `<html>` a tare. Dark token set ɗin kit an ɗaure shi da `data-theme="dark"` (`packages/ui-kit/src/ci-tokens.css`); dark overrides na OmniRoute kanta (`.dark` block ɗin `globals.css`) da tsohuwar Tailwind `dark:` utilities suna ɗaure da `.dark` class. Babu presets na launin accent: accent ko da yaushe yana fitowa daga kit ɗin, kuma ana watsar da preset da aka adana daga tsohon build sa'ad da store yake lodi.
- **Components (adapters).** `src/shared/components/index.tsx` yana riƙe da component API ɗin OmniRoute (sunaye iri ɗaya, default exports da props), kuma primitives da ke bayansa adapters ne a kan kit: forms (`Button`, `Input`, `Textarea`, `Checkbox`, `Select`, `Toggle`, `Badge`, `Card`), overlays da feedback (`Modal`/`ConfirmModal`, `Tooltip`/`InfoTooltip`, `NotificationToast`, `EmptyState`, `Loading`), tsari (`Collapsible`, `CollapsibleSection`, `SegmentedControl`, `Breadcrumbs`, `Avatar`) da tables (`DataTable`, `FilterBar`, `ColumnToggle`, `FilterPills`, `PresetSlider`). Adapter yana shigo da component ɗin kit ta subpath kuma yana ƙaddara tsofaffin props a kansa — misali `Button variant="primary"` shine button ɗin kit na `default` (`--ci-button-primary-*`: inverse surface a light, accent tare da ink ɗin `--ci-color-accent-foreground` a dark), `danger` shine `destructive`, kuma `warning` shine `outline` tare da ƙarin `.ci-button--warning`. Dokokin `.ci-button--warning` da `.ci-badge[data-tone]` a ƙarshen `globals.css` (`@layer components`) ƙarin OmniRoute ne a kan classes na kit. `tests/unit/ui/adapters/*.test.tsx` yana tabbatar da kowanne adapter.
- **Shell.** `src/app/(dashboard)/layout.tsx` yana render `DashboardLayout`, wanda yake render `src/shared/components/layouts/DashboardShell.tsx`: kit `SidebarProvider` / `Sidebar` / `SidebarInset` (`packages/ui-kit/src/sidebar.tsx`), tare da dabarar kewayawa ta OmniRoute a `src/shared/components/Sidebar.tsx`. Haɗin "skip to content" zuwa `#main-content` shine farkon element da ake iya mai da hankali a kai. `src/shared/components/Header.tsx` yana riƙe da kit `SidebarTrigger` (kadai toggle na sidebar na shell), Keak `ProductSwitcher` da mai buɗe command-palette; yana nuna taken route ne kawai muddin babu kit `PageHeader` (`.ci-page-header`) da aka mount a cikin `#main-content`, don haka shafi mai nasa `PageHeader` yana da `h1` guda ɗaya. `src/shared/components/CommandPalette.tsx` kit `Dialog` ne.
- **Icons.** `<Icon name="material_name">` (`src/shared/components/Icon.tsx`) yana warware sunan (kalmomin Material Symbols da allunan ke amfani da su) ta `ICON_MAP` (`src/shared/constants/iconMap.ts`) zuwa glyph na Lucide. An cire font ɗin Material Symbols da package ɗinsa: suna da ba a cikin taswirar (map) ba yana nuna glyph ɗin riƙo wuri mai tsaka-tsaki (`data-icon-missing="true"`) kuma yana rubuta gargaɗi a console a waje da production. `npm run gen:icon-map` yana buga sunayen da ake amfani da su waɗanda ba su cikin taswirar ba (`tests/unit/icon-map-coverage.test.ts` yana gazawa kan kowane suna marar Lucide icon; `tests/unit/icon-map-object-values.test.ts` yana rufe sunayen da aka adana a cikin lookup objects); `scripts/dev/codemod-material-icons.mjs <dir|file>... [--write]` yana sake rubuta literal spans na `<span className="material-symbols-outlined">` zuwa `<Icon>`.
- **Brand.** `src/shared/components/OmniRouteLogo.tsx` yana nannade kit ɗin: default export `OmniRouteLogo` yana nuna `ProductIcon product="omniroute" variant="glyph"` (inline SVG, mai aminci ga `basePath`) kuma `OmniRouteLockup` yana nuna `ProductLockup product="omniroute"` (accessible name "Keak OmniRoute") ko, ga masu sarrafa white-label, nasu `name`/`logoSrc`. Manufofin da product-switcher ke nufi suna a `src/shared/constants/keakProducts.ts` (`KEAK_PRODUCT_LINKS`, `KEAK_ACTIVE_PRODUCT`). Allunan da ke kaɗai — `src/app/login/page.tsx`, `src/app/status/page.tsx`, `src/app/maintenance/page.tsx`, `src/app/offline/page.tsx`, `src/app/not-found.tsx` da `src/shared/components/ErrorPageScaffold.tsx` — suna nuna lockup ɗin; `tests/unit/brand-pages-static.test.ts` yana tsare shi.

## 2. Dokoki

1. Token, ba literals ba. An riƙe keɓancewa da gangan: tashar console da ke duhu koyaushe, layukan
   ReactFlow SVG ta `STATUS_HEX` (`src/shared/constants/statusColors.ts`), da kuma palettes na
   rarrabuwa `STRATEGY_COLORS` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/combos/live/nodes/StrategyNode.tsx`) da
   `LAYER_COLORS` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/compression/studio/nodes/EngineNode.tsx`).
2. Kada a taɓa faca `packages/ui-kit/src` da hannu: daidaita da `scripts/dev/sync-ui-kit.sh <commit>`;
   ƙofar pre-commit `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` (`.husky/pre-commit`) tana gaza a kan kowane byte
   da ya bambanta da commit na upstream da aka kafa (tana tsallake da gargaɗi idan clone na upstream,
   `UI_KIT_CLONE` ko `_references/cheaper-inference-ui-kit`, baya nan). Idan kit ɗin ya rasa wani abu,
   kewaye shi a cikin adapter ko allon kuma a tambayi upstream don shi.
3. Tsoffin rubutun kit Turanci ne (`label = "Loading"`, `loadingLabel = "Loading table"`…) — watsa
   kirtani da aka fassara daga `useTranslations(...)` a duk lokacin da wani sashi na kit yake nuna
   rubutu.
4. Utilities na Tailwind suna rinjayar classes na sassa na kit (kit yana bayyana
   `@layer theme, base, components, utilities` kuma dokokinsa suna cikin `components`) — yi amfani da
   utilities don tazara da tsari, ba don sake launawa na sassa na kit ba.
5. Kanun labarai: `.ci-heading` (Inter, ba tare da tracking ba) don taken shafi da sashe;
   `.ci-dashboard-display` (Instrument Serif) kawai don manyan rubutun nuni masu kaɗan kamar lambar
   matsayin shafin kuskure.
6. `tests/unit/dashboard-kit-sweep.test.ts` yana tsare karɓar kit: babu span na Material ligature a
   ƙarƙashin `src/app` ko `src/shared/components`; a cikin bishiyar dashboard (`src/app/(dashboard)` da
   `src/shared/components`) babu `<select>`, `<textarea>` ko `<table>` kai tsaye kuma babu
   `role="dialog"` da aka yi da hannu a waje da keɓancewa da aka jera (reshe na asali na adapter
   `Select`, `RequestLoggerDetail`); kowane `<button>` ko `<input>` kai tsaye a can yana buƙatar sharhi
   da ke ɗauke da `kit-exception:` (tare da dalilin) da ke ƙarewa mafi yawa layuka 3 a sama da shi;
   launukan palette da bambancewar `dark:` kawai a cikin fayilolin da gwajin ya jera.
7. Tabbatar da duka jigogi biyu da viewport na 390 px kafin PR (hotunan allo); `tests/e2e/a11y.spec.ts`
   yana gudanar da axe.

## 3. Ƙara allo

1. Buɗe shafin da `PageHeader` na kit (`@omniroute/ui-kit/page-header`, `compact` a cikin dashboard) —
   take, bayani da `actions`. Sai kai na shell ya cire takensa.
2. Gina sarrafawa daga `@/shared/components` (adapters) kuma yi amfani da sassa na kit ta subpath inda
   babu adapter (`Table`/`TableScrollArea`/`DataTableShell`, `Field`, `Alert`, `Tabs`, `RadioGroup`,
   `Slider`…).
3. Alamomi: `<Icon name="…" />`. Sabon suna yana shiga cikin `ICON_MAP` tare da sashin Lucide da ya
   dace da ma'ana; `npm run gen:icon-map` yana jera sunayen da har yanzu ke rasa.
4. Launuka: utilities masu ma'ana (`text-success`, `bg-warning-bg`, `border-border`…) da
   `var(--color-chart-N)` don jerin sigina; babu ƙimar hex kuma babu bambancewar `dark:` da ke maimaita
   launi kawai.
5. Rubutu: kowace kirtani, gami da lakabin da kake watsawa ga sassa na kit, tana zuwa daga
   `useTranslations`. Sababbin maɓalli suna shiga cikin `src/i18n/messages/en.json`, `pt-BR.json` da
   `vi.json` (gwajin daidaito na Vietnamese yana kafa kowane maɓalli na Turanci); sauran harsuna ana
   cika su ta daidaitawar i18n.
6. Gudanar da masu tsaro kafin PR: `tests/unit/dashboard-kit-sweep.test.ts`,
   `tests/unit/icon-map-coverage.test.ts`, `tests/unit/icon-map-object-values.test.ts`,
   `npm run check:dashboard-typecheck` (kawai binciken da ke kama import da aka maimaita a cikin
   `iconMap.ts`), `npm run check:ui-icu`, da gwaje-gwajen allon kanta.

## 4. Ƙara ko canza adapter

1. Kiyaye API na jama'a na `src/shared/components/index.tsx`: same export names, default exports da
   props da aka karɓa. Danganta legacy props zuwa kit a cikin adapter (duba `Button.tsx`: `VARIANT_TO_KIT`,
   `SIZE_TO_KIT`).
2. Shigo da kit ta subpath (`@omniroute/ui-kit/<component>`).
3. Maganganu na wucewa (workarounds) don gibin kit suna rayuwa a cikin adapter (ko a cikin `globals.css` `@layer components` idan gyaran shine
   style), ba a taɓa a `packages/ui-kit/src`.
4. Ƙara ko sabunta `tests/unit/ui/adapters/<name>.test.tsx`. Ga adapter da ke da masu kira da yawa, kwatanta
   markup da aka samar kafin da bayan canjin — canje-canjen padding ko wrapper suna yaduwa zuwa kowane allon.

## 5. Sabunta kit

`scripts/dev/sync-ui-kit.sh <commit> [clone]` yana kwafin `src/` na upstream a wannan commit zuwa
`packages/ui-kit/src` kuma yana aiwatar da canjin da aka yarda da shi guda ɗaya (yana sauke suffix na `.js` daga
relative imports). Sannan: duba `git diff packages/ui-kit`, yi duba-gani akan providers, settings, combos,
usage da endpoint a cikin themes biyu, gudanar da `npm run check:ui-kit-snapshot` da `npm run test:vitest:ui`, kuma
buɗe PR tare da changelog na upstream.
