## TL;DR — a zahiri, nawa ne jimillar inference kyauta da OmniRoute ke tattarawa?

Waɗannan alkaluma suna bayyana asusun ɓangarori na uku daban-daban da sharuɗɗan cancanta a duk faɗin kundin. OmniRoute ba ya ba ku wannan kasafi: dole ne ku sami asusu ko bayanan shaida da ake buƙata, ku haɗa masu samarwa da suka cancanta, sannan ku mutunta iyakokinsu. Ainihin ƙarfin da za a iya amfani da shi zai iya zama ƙasa, har ma ya zama sifili a sabon shigarwa wanda ba shi da wata hanya da ta cancanta. Masu samarwa da aka yiwa alamar `tos: avoid` ba a haɗa su cikin zaɓin hanya ta atomatik ta asali.

**An duba 2026-10-08:** [Cerebras](https://www.cerebras.ai/pricing) yana bayar da kiredit na talla na $5 sau ɗaya wanda ke buƙatar hanyar biyan kuɗi, kuma zai ƙare bayan kwanaki 30. ## Sabuntawar 2026-06-17 — abin da ya canza tun daga 2026-06-05

Binciken yanar gizo da wakilai 50 suka gudanar (takardun hukuma + labaran kwanaki 7 da suka gabata, tare da tsauraran matakan tantancewa) ya sabunta kundin gaba ɗaya. Muhimman bayanai:

- **An cire / babu matakin kyauta (2026):** `chutes` (matakin kyauta ya ƙare 2026-03), `phind` (kamfanin ya rufe 2026-01), `kluster` (an dakatar 2026-06-09 → MITO), `gitlawb` + `gitlawb-gmi` (an soke kyautar MiMo 2026-05-24, tallan Nemotron ya ƙare 2026-06 — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `aimlapi` (an dakatar da matakin kyauta — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `yi` (an yi ritayar Yi-Light, yanzu biyan kuɗi gwargwadon amfani — an sake tabbatarwa 2026-06-18), `featherless-ai` (babu matakin kyauta a halin yanzu), `chipotle` ("Pepper AI" na Chipotle — widget ɗin tattaunawa na Amelia da aka gano yadda yake aiki, wato `amelia.chipotle.com`, wanda mai samarwar ke sadarwa da shi yanzu yana mayar da 404 a kowace hanya, har da root; an daina amfani da shi/an matsar da backend, an tabbatar 2026-09-15 — an cire shi gaba ɗaya daga kundin, #13131/#4037). `iflytek` / `sparkdesk` sun ci gaba da kasancewa a jeri amma suna ɗauke da bayanin taka-tsantsan game da ToS (Spark Lite kyauta ne; ToS ɗin yana ƙuntata amfani da proxy/relay).
- **Gemini** — an rufe `2.0 Flash` / `2.0 Flash-Lite` a 2026-06-01, kuma `2.5 Pro` ya fita daga matakin kyauta (2026-04); yanzu matakin kyauta ya taƙaita ga **iyalan Flash kawai** (2.5/3/3.1/3.5 Flash + Gemma). Yanzu kundin yana **haɗa** iyalan Flash cikin rukuni guda (a baya an kumbura adadin ta hanyar ƙirga kowane nau'i dabam: 462M → 60M).
- **Adadin da aka gyara:** `cloudflare-ai` 122M → **30M** (ainihin Neurons 10k/rana), an sake rarraba `doubao` a matsayin kiredit na rajista na sau ɗaya (ba mai maimaituwa ba), `llm7` 4M → **150M** (tokens 5M/rana kamar yadda takardu suka nuna), an dakatar da endpoints na "-Free" na `together` → an kuma cire kiredit ɗin rajista na $25 (ana buƙatar mafi ƙarancin saye na $5, babu gwaji kyauta), lokacin Preview na `longcat` ya ƙare + an yi ritayar samfurorin Flash → **LongCat-2.0** kawai, an sake rarraba shi a matsayin kiredit ɗin rajista na **10M** tokens na sau ɗaya (yana buƙatar KYC, ba mai maimaituwa ba).
- **Sabbin masu samar da sabis na kyauta da aka gano:** ⭐ **Kilo Code** (`kilo-gateway` — jerin "Auto Free" mai sauyawa: iyalan NVIDIA Nemotron 3, StepFun, Poolside, Nex-N2-Pro), ⭐ **OpenCode Zen** (`opencode-zen` — samfurorin coding kyauta guda 10 masu sauyawa, an sabunta 2026-10-20), ⭐ **Z.AI / Zhipu** (`glm-cn` — GLM-4-Flash / 4.5-Flash / 4.7-Flash kyauta na dindindin + kyautar rajista ta 20M), da `arcee-ai` Trinity Large Preview.
- **Sabbin matakai na gaskiya** (duba Hanyar Aiki): nau'in _kyauta-na-dindindin-amma-ba-shinge_ (ainihin damar amfani mai maimaituwa, babu iyakar token da za a ƙirga) da _ƙarin-ƙarfi-ta-buɗewa-da-ajiya_ (OpenRouter $10 → +24M/wata), an nuna su **dabam-dabam** don kada su taɓa kumbura babban adadin.

> Cikakken jadawalin kowane mai samarwa da ke ƙasa shi ne **hoton yanayin 2026-06-05**; sauye-sauyen da ke sama sun maye gurbinsa. Madogara mai rai kuma ta hukuma ita ce kundin kowane samfurin `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.ts`.

## Gyaran samfuran kyauta na SiliconFlow na 2026-10-18 (#17028)

An fitar da manyan samfuran SiliconFlow guda goma waɗanda ke da tabbatattun farashin kowane token
a matsayin damar kyauta ta `recurring-uncapped` bisa kuskure. An **cire** waɗannan layuka goma daga
kundin tsayayye, wanda yanzu yake da **shigarwa 467**. SiliconFlow ba ya ƙara samar da
layin samfuri kyauta mara sharadi ko mai samarwa na kyauta-na-dindindin ga jimillar kundin tsayayye.

Wannan **ba** yana nufin SiliconFlow ba shi da samfuran kyauta ba. Shigo da samfuran kyauta kawai da alamar Free
yanzu suna buƙatar **tabbatacciyar shaida ta kowane samfuri daga payload ɗin samfuran mai samarwa na kai-tsaye**, kamar
alamar boolean ta kyauta a sarari ko kuma duk farashin token biyu su kasance sifili. Alamar biya a sarari ko kowane
tabbataccen farashi na lamba yana da fifiko a kan alamar kyauta ta zato mai kyau. Samfurin da ba shi da
wadatacciyar shaida ta kai-tsaye **ba** za a ɗauka kyauta ba; kuɗin kyautar sabon asusu na sau ɗaya ba zai iya
tabbatar da tallafin kyauta mai maimaituwa ba. Idan API na mai samarwa na yanzu ba zai iya bayar da ingantaccen
farashi a matakin samfuri ba, tabbatar da shi a console na mai samarwar kafin tura buƙatu. Wannan zaɓi na taka-tsantsan
yana hana cajin bazata ta hanyar shigo da samfuran kyauta kawai da haɗuwa ta atomatik.

Teburin masu samarwa na tarihi da ke ƙasa ba alkawarin samuwa na kai-tsaye ba ne. Tsohuwar
shigarwar SiliconFlow ta uncapped/samfura 10 ba ta nan da gangan saboda ba ta ƙara
wakiltar tabbataccen tallafin kyauta mai maimaituwa ba.

## Gyaran Bytez da Nous na 2026-10-15

[Tsarin biyan kuɗin Bytez](https://docs.bytez.com/model-api/docs/billing) yana iyakance samfuran buɗaɗɗen tushe na tsarinsa na Kyauta
zuwa ma'aunai 7B. Shigarwar 70B da 72B suna buƙatar a sayi kuɗin-kiredit
don buɗe su, saboda haka an cire su. Mistral-7B ya ci gaba da kasancewa `recurring-credit`: kiredit
suna sabuntawa duk bayan makonni huɗu, amma ana lissafin kuɗi ne bisa daƙiƙar inference kuma ba a
wallafa wani adadin token ba. Ma'aunan token ɗinsa sifili ne don wakiltar adadin da
ba a sani ba, a maimakon hasashen token miliyan 1 da ba shi da tushe; wannan ba yana nufin babu
kiredit na kyauta ba. An yi cikakken bayani game da sauye-sauyen Nous a bayanin mai samarwa da ke ƙasa.

## Tsaruka biyu — ƙidayawa da yanke shawara

OmniRoute yana amsa tambayar "kyauta ne?" ta hanyar tsaruka biyu waɗanda da gangan suke karanta
mabambantan tushe:

| Tsari                 | Tushen gaskiya                                                                                                                                                                                            | Wuraren da ya shafa                                                                                                            |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Ƙidayawa / nunawa** | Kundin da aka warware — kundin asali da aka tura wanda bayanan Radar suka ɗora masa sabuntawa (`getRadarCatalog`)                                                                                         | Jimillar matakin kyauta, katin kasafin kuɗi, allunan bayanai                                                                   |
| **Yanke shawara**     | Kundin da aka tura kawai (`FREE_MODEL_BUDGETS` a cikin `open-sse/config/freeModelCatalog.data.ts`) tare da dabarun tantancewa na gida (ƙarin `:free` a ƙarshen suna, farashin sifili, `grantsFreeAccess`) | Duk masu amfani da `src/shared/utils/freeModels.ts`: shigo da samfuri, turawar `auto/*`, `GET /v1/models`, da samfotin burauza |

Ƙidayawa na iya inganta a duk lokacin da ake samun tushen bayanai. Yanke shawara yana ci gaba da dogaro da
kayan sakin, don haka amsar tana zama iri ɗaya a burauza da sabar,
ana iya maimaita ta ba tare da intanet ba, kuma ana iya gwada ta ba tare da rumbun bayanai ba. Barin samfotin
burauza ya karanta tushe ɗaya yayin da shigo da bayanai na sabar yake karanta wani zai haifar da
samfotin da bai yi daidai da abin da ke faruwa bayan dannawa ba — an bar wannan rabuwar
da gangan.

## Hanyar aiki da abubuwan lura

- Lambobin **hasashen iyaka mafi girma** ne daga iyakokin matakin kyauta da kowanne mai samarwa ya rubuta zuwa **2026-06-17**, waɗanda aka tattara ta binciken yanar gizo. Matakan kyauta suna canzawa koyaushe — sake tabbatarwa kafin dogaro da kowace ƙima.
- **Abin da kowace shigarwa take tabbatarwa a zahiri.** Babu shigarwar da ke ɗauke da ma'aunin amincewa na kowane layi, kuma API ba ya bayar da ko ɗaya — ɗauki kowace ƙimar da ke sama a matsayin hasashe mai inganci iri ɗaya wanda ba a bayyana ba. Abubuwa biyu sun bambanta, domin ana tsara su da hannu maimakon a fitar da su ta hasashe: shigarwa 44 suna da tsayayyen wurin dakatarwa da aka rubuta daga tushe mai zaman kansa (39 daga cikinsu layukan xKiro ne, waɗanda duk suke amfani da adadin yau da kullum guda ɗaya), sannan shigarwa 12 suna da bayanin bayyana horarwa da prompt. Ana saita `hardStopGuaranteed` ne kawai idan sharuɗɗan mai samarwar da kansa suka ce wuce adadin kyauta zai sa a ƙi buƙatar maimakon fara cajin kuɗi a ɓoye, tare da tushe a cikin sharhi kusa da shigarwar; ba a taɓa saita shi zuwa `true` ta tsohuwa ba, kuma shigarwar da babu wanda ya tabbatar da ita tana kasancewa ba a saita ba. Saboda haka, rashin tutar tsayayyen dakatarwa yana nufin "ba a tabbatar ba", ba "an san zai caje ka" ba. **Yanayin STRICT** (matakin kariyar turawa na zaɓi `freeAccessPolicy=strict`) yana amincewa ne kawai da shigarwar da ke ɗauke da tutar; ana cire duk sauran matakan kyauta a matsayin `no-hard-stop` (duba `open-sse/services/autoCombo/strictZeroCostFilter.ts`). Lokacin da matakin kariyar ya tace tarin, log ɗin da ma'aikaci zai iya gani yana bayar da rahoton adadin abubuwan da aka cire da kuma kaso na `no-hard-stop` a cikinsu.
- `estMonthlyFreeTokens` = token masu maimaituwa na wata-wata kawai. **Kiredit na rajista na sau ɗaya ba sa maimaituwa** kuma ana lissafa su a matsayin 0. Matakan da aka dakatar su ma 0 ne.
- Iyakar token ta yau da kullum → `monthly = daily × 30`. Idan RPD kaɗai aka rubuta → `RPD × ~800 output tokens × 30`. Idan RPM/TPM kaɗai ne (babu iyakar yau da kullum) → **ba shi da iyaka** (duba ƙasa).
- **Kyauta ta dindindin, amma babu iyakar token da aka wallafa** (`glm-cn`, `tencent`, `baidu`, `kilo-gateway`, `opencode-zen`, `gemini`, `ollama-cloud`): waɗannan samun dama na kyauta ne na gaske kuma mai maimaituwa, amma an iyakance su ta adadin buƙatu/aiwatarwa a lokaci guda. Muna rarraba su a matsayin `recurring-uncapped` kuma **ba ma taɓa haɗa su cikin jimla** — ninka `RPM × 24/7 × 30d` zai samar da iyakar ƙarya (kumburin ƙididdigar da muke ƙin yarda da shi). An jera su ne domin ka san suna nan.
- **Ƙarin adadi bayan saka kuɗi don buɗewa:** ƙaramin ƙarin kuɗi na sau ɗaya wanda yake ɗaga adadin kyauta har abada (OpenRouter: $10 → buƙatu 1000/rana ≈ +24M/wata). Ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin ƙima daban, kuma ba a haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin.
- **Adadin da ya dogara da cancanta** (`eligibilityGate: "regional-identity"`): adadin gaske ne mai maimaituwa wanda yake buɗewa ne kawai bayan tantance shaidar mutum da ke da alaƙa da yanki (a halin yanzu, tantance sunan gaskiya a babban yankin China). Ana ƙidaya shi da ƙa'idar cire maimaituwar tari guda ɗaya zuwa cikin ƙima daban (`gatedRecurringTokens`), kuma ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin babban adadin dindindin. Tsarin (`freeType`) bai canza ba, don haka turawa ma bai canza ba.
- **Rukunonin hujja.** Ƙa'idar ita ce: lamba a cikin kundin tana ambaton tushenta a cikin sharhin `// evidence:` kusa da shigarwar — `public-page` (shafin mai samarwa da kowa zai iya karantawa), `api-public` (endpoint na mai samarwa da baya buƙatar tantancewa, misali endpoint ɗin tsare-tsaren jama'a na NaraRouter a router.bynara.id), ko `console-verified <date> por <who>` (ana iya ganin ƙimar ne kawai a cikin na'ura mai kula da asusu; sharhin yana rubuta wanda ya gan ta da lokacin da ya gan ta, da kuma shafin jama'a da ya ce iyakar tana wanzuwa). Halin da ake ciki a yau: rukuni biyar da aka sake bincikawa a 2026-09-02 suna ɗauke da shi (`gemini`, `groq`, `mistral`, `ollama-cloud`, `nara`); shigarwar da suka gabaci sake binciken 2026-09-02 suna gādon binciken da ya gabata har sai an sabunta su; duk wata lamba **sabuwa ko da aka canza** da ba ta da sharhin hujja kuskure ce. A yau `mistral` kaɗai ne aka tabbatar ta na'ura mai kula da asusu.

## Me ya sa adadinmu ya fi na sauran masu tarawa ƙanƙanta

Yawancin alkaluman "free tokens per month" a wannan fanni jimillar lambobin da aka ware wa kowane model ne. Namu ba haka yake ba, kuma da gangan muka yi hakan:

- **Ana ƙirga kowane shared pool sau ɗaya ne kawai.** Tsarin kyauta na Mistral yana da kaso guda na 1B/wata ga kowace ƙungiya; jera shi a ƙarƙashin model guda biyar ba ya sa ya zama 5B. Idan aka tara bisa kowane model, catalog ɗinmu zai nuna **~7.4B** (an sake lissafawa a 2026-09-03; wannan adadi ba ya ƙarƙashin kulawar CI — a sake auna shi duk lokacin da catalog ya canza) — babban taken yana cewa **~1.47B** domin wannan ne ainihin abin da account guda na kowane provider zai iya amfani da shi.
- **Ana sauya daily caps zuwa na wata, amma ba rates ba.** Iyakar tokens/day da aka rubuta tana zama `× 30`; iyakar requests/day da aka rubuta tana zama `RPD × ~800 tokens × 30`; provider da ke wallafa requests-per-minute kawai ba shi da adadin wata kuma ana jera shi a matsayin _ba shi da iyaka_, ba a taɓa haɗa shi cikin jimilla ba. Ninka rate limit da 24/7 shi ne ƙarin da ba mu yarda da shi ba.
- **Ana nuna quotas da ke buƙatar tantance asalin yanki dabam** (`+~6M behind regional identity verification`), domin yawancin masu karatu ba za su iya amfani da su ba.
- **Signup credits na watan farko ne kawai** kuma ana nuna su a matsayin adadi na biyu, ba a taɓa haɗa su da adadin dindindin ba.
- **CI ne ke tabbatar da adadin.** `npm run check:docs-counts` yana sake lissafa jimillar daga catalog kuma yana hana build ɗin kammalawa idan wannan fayil, README ko budget card sun saɓa da ita.

## Teburin abubuwan lura na ToS

> **Alamar ToS shawara ce, ba shingen routing ba.** Har yanzu providers da aka yi wa alamar `tos` suna cikin routing da combo/fallback ta tsohuwa; alamar tana bayyana ne kawai a `/dashboard/free-tiers` da `/api/free-tier/summary`. Query parameter na `excludeTosAvoid` yana shafar summary view ne kawai, ba global routing ba. Hukuncin yana cikin `open-sse/config/freeTierCatalog.ts` (na bayani ne, routing engines ba sa karanta shi).

> Takaitaccen bayani kan sharuɗɗan kowane provider game da proxy na kai wanda aka self-host kuma mutum ɗaya ke amfani da shi. `caution` = tanadin personal-use ko proxy da ya dace a bincika; `ambiguous` = ba a fayyace ba; `ok` = an ba da izini a sarari. Bayani ne kawai, ba shawarar doka ba — kai ne za ka yanke hukunci.
