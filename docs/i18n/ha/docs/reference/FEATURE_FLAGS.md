> Maɓallan lokacin aiki ne da ke sauya halayen OmniRoute **ba tare da sake tura manhajar ba**.
> Kowace alama da aka jera a nan an bayyana ta a cikin
> [`src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts`](../../src/shared/constants/featureFlagDefinitions.ts)
> — tushen gaskiya guda ɗaya. Dashboard da REST API dukansu suna karantawa daga
> wannan fayil, don haka an samar da jadawalin da ke ƙasa domin ya yi daidai da shi 1:1.

## Mene Ne Feature Flags

Feature flag wani maɓalli ne mai suna (boolean ko enum) wanda za a iya sauya ƙimarsa a
lokacin aiki kuma a adana ta a ma'ajiyar bayanai, ba tare da buƙatar sake tura tsarin ba. Ana
bayyana kowace alama ta hanyar `FeatureFlagDefinition` mai `key`, `label`,
`description`, `category`, `defaultValue`, `type`, da alamar `requiresRestart`.

### Jerin Warware Ƙima

Ana tantance **ƙima mai aiki** ta wata alama ta hanyar
[`resolveFeatureFlag()`](../../src/shared/utils/featureFlags.ts) bisa wannan
jerin fifiko (mafi girman fifiko ne ke rinjaya):

1. **Sauyin DB** — ƙimar da aka adana a teburin `key_value` ƙarƙashin
   namespace na `feature_flags` (wanda aka saita ta dashboard ko REST API).
2. **Environment variable** — `process.env[<KEY>]`, idan an saita shi kuma ba komai ba ne.
3. **Ƙimar tsoho ta bayani** — `defaultValue` daga `featureFlagDefinitions.ts`.

Ana ɗaukar alamar boolean a matsayin **mai aiki** idan ƙimarta mai aiki ita ce `"true"`,
`"1"`, ko `"yes"` (duba `isFeatureFlagEnabled()`).

> [!NOTE]
> Yawancin alamomi kuma suna da environment variable mai dacewa da su mai **suna iri ɗaya**
> wanda aka bayyana a cikin [`ENVIRONMENT.md`](./ENVIRONMENT.md). Sauyin DB na alamar
> yana da fifiko a kan wannan environment variable. Alamar da ke da
> `requiresRestart: true` ana adana ta nan take, amma sai an sake karanta ta yayin fara
> tsarin — sauya ta yana nuna banner na **"Sake Fara Server"** a dashboard.

## Tutoci masu juyawa

### Dashboard

Kewaya zuwa** Dashboard → Saitunan → Feature Tutocin** (`/dashboard/settings/feature-flags`). Grid (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/settings/components/FeatureFlagsGrid.tsx`) yana goyan bayan:

- **Bincika** ta maɓalli ko bayanin, kuma** tace** ta rukuni (da na roba** Yana buƙatar sake farawa** duba).
- A** juya** don tutocin boolean da a** zazzagewa** ga tutocin enum (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/settings/components/FeatureFlagCard.tsx`).
- A** lambar tushe** kowane tuta — `DB`, `ENV`, ko `DEF` — yana nuna inda inganci mai inganci ya fito.
- A** Sake saiti** maɓalli (wanda aka nuna don tutocin `DB` -sourced kawai) don jefar da abin rufewa, da a** Sake saita Duk Saukewa** button a kasa.
- A** Sake kunna uwar garken** tuta lokacin da aka canza tutar `requiresRestart`.

### REST API

Duk ayyuka suna tafiya ta hanya ɗaya: [`src/app/api/settings/feature-flags/route.ts`](../../src/app/api/settings/feature-flags/route.ts). Kowace hanya tana buƙatar ingantaccen zaman dashboard (`401` in ba haka ba).

Tare da nakasasshen shiga (`requireLogin=false`) hanya a buɗe take, sai dai ga tutoci masu kariya: kowace tuta a cikin rukunin Tsaro da `OMNIROUTE_ALLOW_PRIVATE_PROVIDER_URLS`, `OMNIROUTE_ALLOW_LOCAL_PROVIDER_URLS`, `MITM_DISABLE_TLS_VERIFY`, `OMNIROUTE_MCP_ENFORCE_SCOPES` da `SKILLS_SANDBOX_NETWORK_ENABLED` (`isProtectedFeatureFlag()` a `protectedFeatureFlags.ts`). A `PUT` akan ɗayansu, da `DELETE` yayin da ɗayansu yana da jujjuyawar DB, har yanzu yana buƙatar zaman dashboard, maɓalli na `manage` -scope API, alamar shiga `oma_` tare da rubutu ko mafi girma, ko alamar CLI ko alamar sabis na cikin gida da aka aika daga madauki. Wannan saitin ya fi fadi fiye da shiga a kunne, inda hanyar ke karɓar zama kawai ko maɓallin `manage` -scope.

#### `GET /api/settings/feature-flags`

Yana dawo da kowace tuta tare da ingantaccen ƙimarta, tushenta, da taƙaice.

```jsonc
{
  "flags": [
    {
      "key": "REQUIRE_API_KEY",
      "label": "Require API Key",
      "description": "Require an API key for all incoming requests",
      "category": "security",
      "type": "boolean",
      "enumValues": null,
      "defaultValue": "false",
      "effectiveValue": "false",
      "source": "default", // "db" | "env" | "default"
      "requiresRestart": false,
      "warningLevel": "caution",
    },
    // ... all 121 flags
  ],
  "summary": {
    "total": 121,
    "active": 0,
    "inactive": 0,
    "overriddenByDb": 0,
    "overriddenByEnv": 0,
  },
}
```

#### `PUT /api/settings/feature-flags`

Saita ko cire wuce gona da iri. Jiki: `{ key: string; value?: string }`. Yin watsi da `value` yana kawar da abin rufewa (sake dawo da env / tsoho).

```bash
# Set a DB override
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/settings/feature-flags \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"key":"REQUIRE_API_KEY","value":"true"}'

# Remove the override (no "value")
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/settings/feature-flags \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"key":"REQUIRE_API_KEY"}'
```

Amsar tana sake maimaita sabon `effectiveValue`/`source`, da `previousValue`/ `previousSource`, da `requiresRestart`. Maɓallan da ba a san su ba da ƙimar ƙima ta ƙima tare da `400`.

#### `DELETE /api/settings/feature-flags`

Yana sharewa** duka** DB ya soke lokaci guda, yana maido da kowace tuta zuwa ƙimar env/tsoho. Yana dawo `{ cleared: <count>, message: "..." }`.

> [!NOTE]
> Tutoci masu `requiresRestart: true` suna aiki ne kawai bayan an sake lodawa. Sake kunna dashboard ɗin yana kiran `POST /api/restart` sannan ya yi `GET /api/health/ping` har sai uwar garken ya dawo.

## Madadin Kasafin Kuɗi na Gaggawa

`OMNIROUTE_EMERGENCY_FALLBACK` (nau'in `runtime`, tsoho `true`) yana sarrafa
hanyar madadin kyauta ta gaggawa a cikin
[`open-sse/services/emergencyFallback.ts`](../../open-sse/services/emergencyFallback.ts).
Idan an kunna shi, buƙatun da suka ƙare kasafin kuɗinsu za a tura su zuwa wani
mai samarwa/samfuri na madadin kyauta maimakon su gaza kai tsaye. Saita shi zuwa
`false` (ko `0`) — ta hanyar maɓallin dashboard, saitin maye gurbi na DB, ko
environment variable na `OMNIROUTE_EMERGENCY_FALLBACK` — don kashe wannan
ɗabi'ar kuma a bar buƙatun da suka ƙare kasafin kuɗinsu su gaza. (An bayyana shi
a matsayin maɓallin dashboard a cikin PRs #3741 / #3752.)

Amsar da aka bayar ta wannan madadin tana ɗauke da
`X-OmniRoute-Emergency-Fallback: from=<provider/model>; to=<provider/model>`, don
haka abokin hulɗa zai iya gane cewa an sake tura ta ba tare da kwatanta
`X-OmniRoute-Provider` da buƙatarsa ba. Wannan header ba ya kasance a cikin kowace
amsa daban.
