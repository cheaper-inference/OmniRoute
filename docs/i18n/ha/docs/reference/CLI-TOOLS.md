## Saita ta atomatik tare da `setup-*`

Ba sai ka rubuta saitin kowane kayan aiki da hannu ba. OmniRoute tana aika umarni `setup-*` kowane CLI mai goyan baya wanda ya karanta** rayuwa** katalojin samfurin daga OmniRoute mai gudana (na gida ko na nesa) kuma ya rubuta tsarin na'urar ta kan injin ku:

```bash
omniroute setup-codex        omniroute setup-claude       omniroute setup-opencode
omniroute setup-cline        omniroute setup-kilo         omniroute setup-continue
omniroute setup-cursor       omniroute setup-roo          omniroute setup-crush
omniroute setup-goose        omniroute setup-qwen         omniroute setup-aider
omniroute setup-5dive        omniroute setup-zed
omniroute setup-whycodes
```

Kowannensu yana karɓar `--remote <url> --api-key <key>` (daidaita kayan aiki na gida da OmniRoute mai nisa), `--dry-run` (samfoti ba tare da rubutu ba), da `--port`. Kayan aiki ba tare da samfurin ganowar atomatik ba (Cline, Kilo, Roo, Goose, Aider, Qwen, 5dive, WhyCodes) suna ɗaukar `--model <id>` (da `--yes` don gudanar da ba tare da haɗin gwiwa ba). `setup-5dive` shine girke-girke daya wanda baya rubuta a karkashin `$HOME`: yana daidaita rukunin wakilai na 5dive ta hanyar rubuta bayanan asalin tushen tushen akan rundunar jiragen ruwa, don haka yana sake aiwatarwa ta hanyar `sudo` kuma ba shi da yanayin nesa na kansa. Don ƙaddamar da CLI tare da alluran env na dama kuma ba a rubuta saiti kwata-kwata, yi amfani da ƙaddamar da `omniroute run <target>` gabaɗaya (claude, codex, aider, goose, opencode, qwen, gemini, whycodes — hari da laƙabi sun fito daga `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs`); Abubuwan ƙaddamar da kayan aiki na gadar `omniroute launch` (Lambar Claude ) da `omniroute launch-codex` (Codex) suna nan. Gemini CLI ƙaddamar-kawai: manufa ce ta `omniroute run` amma ba ta da girke-girke na `setup-*`/`configure`.

> **Cikakken magana:** babban tebur - abin da kowane umarni ya rubuta, kowane tuta, na gida vs nesa, da waɗanne kayan aikin ke son ƙarar `/v1` - yana zaune a ciki** [CLI Haɗin kai](../guides/CLI-INTEGRATIONS.md)** .

### Gudun waɗannan a cikin akwati

Umurnin `setup-*` da aka aiwatar a cikin kwandon OmniRoute yana rubutawa cikin kwantenan gidan kansa, wanda babu mai masaukin CLI ya karanta kuma ya ɓace tare da kwantena. OmniRoute yana gano hakan kuma ya fita `2` tare da umarni maimakon rubutu. Hanyoyi guda biyu masu goyan bayan gaba - shigar da CLI akan mai watsa shiri da `omniroute connect` zuwa akwati, ko ɗaure saitin dirs kuma saita `CLI_CONFIG_HOME` (ƙirƙirar bayanin martabar `host` ). Kowane umarni `setup-*`, da `omniroute configure` da `omniroute config set`, suna karɓar `--allow-container-write` yayin daidaita CLIs ɗin kwandon shine ainihin abin da kuke nufi; `OMNIROUTE_ALLOW_CONTAINER_CONFIG_WRITE=true` yayi haka don uwar garken. Duba [jagorar Docker → Yana daidaita kayan aikin CLI mai masaukin baki](../guides/DOCKER_GUIDE.md#configuring-host-cli-tools-when-omniroute-runs-in-docker).

Dashboard's** shafi karshen** (`POST /api/cli-tools/apply`) yana tilasta tsaro iri ɗaya: a cikin akwati, rubuta wanda ba a ɗaure makasudinsa ba daga mai masaukin ya ba da amsa.**`422`** tare da `containerEphemeralTarget: true`, rubutun kuskuren aminci da kuma - don kayan aiki tare da girke-girke (claude, codex, opencode, cline, kilo, ci gaba) - a `hostSetupCommand` (misali `omniroute setup-opencode`) don gudu a kan mai watsa shiri maimakon; babu abin da aka rubuta. `dryRun: true` yana ci gaba da aiki a yanayin kwantena kuma yana dawo da hanyar samfoti da aka gyara ta hanyar + ba tare da taɓa faifai ba. Abubuwan samfoti ba ƙaƙƙarfan tsari ba ne don kwafi ko shigo da su. Aiwatar tare da ainihin bayanan tool/base URL/API key/model akan mai watsa shiri, ko yi amfani da umarnin saitin gefen rundunar da aka nuna. Dubi [tsaro na sanyi na CLI](../security/CLI-CONFIGURATION.md) don taken samfoti da neman kwangila. Wannan hali na ganganci ne da koma baya ta hanyar `tests/unit/api/cli-tools/apply-container-guard.test.ts` - kar a taɓa "gyara" a 422 ta hanyar cire mai gadi.

## Tushen Gaskiya

Haɗaɗɗen kundin yana cikin `src/shared/constants/cliTools.ts` a matsayin `CLI_TOOLS: Record<string, CliCatalogEntry>`.

Kowace shigarwa tana da waɗannan filaye (an ayyana su a cikin `src/shared/schemas/cliCatalog.ts`):

| Fili                                            | Nau'i                                                        | Bayani                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `category`                                      | `"code" \| "agent"`                                          | Shafin da kayan aikin yake bayyana a kai                              |
| `vendor`                                        | `string`                                                     | Asalin kayan aikin ("Anthropic", "OSS (P. Gauthier)")                 |
| `acpSpawnable`                                  | `boolean`                                                    | Haka kuma ana iya amfani da shi a matsayin ACP Agent (ana nuna lamba) |
| `baseUrlSupport`                                | `"full" \| "partial" \| "none"`                              | Matakin goyon bayan endpoint na musamman. `"none"` = jerin jiran MITM |
| `configType`                                    | `"env" \| "custom" \| "guide" \| "custom-builder" \| "mitm"` | Tsarin daidaitawa                                                     |
| `id`, `name`, `color`, `description`, `docsUrl` | daidaitacce                                                  | Muhimman filayen nunawa                                               |

Shigarwar da suke da `baseUrlSupport: "none"` **ba a nuna su** a shafukan dashboard — an yi musu rajista a jerin jiran MITM don shiri na 11 (duba `_tasks/features-v3.8.6/refactorpages/_orchestration/_plan11-mitm-backlog.md`).

### Matakan iyawa (na-kundi × mai-gano × mai-daidaitawa × mai-ƙaddamarwa)

Ba kowane kayan aikin da ke cikin kundin ne ake iya ganowa, daidaitawa ko ƙaddamarwa ba. Kowane mataki yana da tushe guda
da ke ayyana shi, kuma gwajin karkacewa yana tabbatar da cewa sun kasance daidai:

| Mataki             | Ma'ana                                                                          | An ayyana a cikin                                                   |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Na-kundi**       | Yana bayyana a kundin dashboard (suna, mai samarwa, takardu, nau'in daidaitawa) | `src/shared/constants/cliTools.ts` (`CLI_TOOLS`)                    |
| **Mai-gano**       | Gano binary/config, binciken lafiya, hanyoyin config                            | `src/shared/services/cliRuntime.ts` (kundin runtime na `CLI_TOOLS`) |
| **Mai-daidaitawa** | `omniroute configure <cli>` yana goyon bayansa (akwai tsarin saiti)             | `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` (`configure: true`)                      |
| **Mai-ƙaddamarwa** | `omniroute run <target>` yana goyon bayansa (an ayyana shigar da env/args)      | `bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` (`run: true`)                            |

`bin/cli/cli-manifest.mjs` shi ne tabbataccen manifest mai aiwatarwa don hanyoyin umarnin CLI:
`run`, `configure` da masu samar da kammala-umarnin shell duk suna samo jerin
target ɗinsu, warware alias (misali `kilocode`/`kilo-code`/`kilo_cli` → `kilo`)
da haɗa flag na `--model` daga gare shi. Mai tsaron karkacewa
`tests/unit/cli/cli-manifest-drift.test.ts` yana tabbatar da cewa manifest, kundin runtime,
kundin UI da kowace hanyar amfani suna kasancewa daidai — target da aka ƙara zuwa
hanya guda ba tare da sauran ba zai sa test suite ɗin ya gaza maimakon ya karkace a ɓoye.

## 5. API na Gano Kayan Aiki Gaba Ɗaya

Ana tattara duk gano kayan aiki ta hanyar endpoint guda ɗaya:

**`GET /api/cli-tools/all-statuses`**

- Tantancewa: `requireCliToolsAuth(request)` (daidai yake da sauran hanyoyin `/api/cli-tools/`)
- Yana dawo da: `Record<toolId, ToolBatchStatus>` (nau'i: `src/shared/types/cliBatchStatus.ts`)
- Dabara: `Promise.all` a kan duk kayan aiki, lokacin jira na 5s ga kowane kayan aiki
- Ma'aji: LRU na cikin ƙwaƙwalwa wanda aka yi masa fihirisa ta `mtime` na fayil ɗin saituna. Ana soke ma'ajin idan mtime ya canza. Ana sake saita shi idan aka sake kunna sabar.

Tsarin amsa ga kowane kayan aiki:

```ts
interface ToolBatchStatus {
  detection: {
    installed: boolean;
    runnable: boolean;
    version?: string;
    command?: string;
    commandPath?: string;
    reason?: string;
  };
  config: {
    status: "configured" | "not_configured" | "not_installed" | "unknown" | "other";
    endpoint?: string | null;
    lastConfiguredAt?: string | null;
  };
  error?: string; // an tsabtace shi, babu bayanan bin diddigin stack
}
```

## 10. CLI na Cikin Gida na OmniRoute

Fayil ɗin binary na `omniroute` yana samar da umarni don zagayen rayuwar sabar, saitawa, binciken matsaloli, da sarrafa masu samarwa. Wurin farawa: `bin/omniroute.mjs`.

```bash
omniroute                              # Fara saba (tsohuwar tashar 20128)
omniroute setup                        # Jagoran saitawa mai mu'amala
omniroute doctor                       # Duba tsari, DB, tashoshi, da yanayin gudanarwa
omniroute providers list               # Haɗin masu samarwa da aka tsara
omniroute providers test-all           # Gwada kowane haɗi mai aiki
omniroute reset-password               # Sake saita kalmar sirrin mai gudanarwa
omniroute logs                         # Watsa rajistan buƙatu kai tsaye
omniroute health                       # Cikakken bayani kan lafiya (breakers, cache, ƙwaƙwalwa)
omniroute --version                    # Nuna sigar
omniroute --help                       # Nuna duk umarni
```

### Saitawa da Farawa

```bash
omniroute setup                        # Jagoran saitawa mai mu'amala
omniroute setup --non-interactive      # Yanayin CI/aiki da kai (yana karanta env vars + flags)
omniroute setup --password '<value>'   # Saita kalmar sirrin mai gudanarwa kai tsaye
omniroute setup --add-provider \
  --provider openai \
  --api-key '<value>' \
  --test-provider                      # Ƙara kuma gwada mai samarwa lokaci guda
```

Sauye-sauyen muhalli da ake gane su don saitawa marar mu'amala:

| Var                 | Manufa                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` | Maɓallin API na mai samarwa (an ɗaure shi da `--api-key` ta Commander `.env()`) |
| `DATA_DIR`          | Sauya kundin bayanan OmniRoute                                                  |

Ana miƙa duk sauran bayanan shigarwa marasa mu'amala a matsayin flags, ba sauye-sauyen muhalli ba:
`--password`, `--provider`, `--provider-name`, `--provider-base-url`, `--default-model`
(duba zaɓuɓɓukan `omniroute setup` da ke sama).

### Binciken Matsaloli

```bash
omniroute doctor                       # Duba tsari, DB, tashoshi, yanayin gudanarwa, ƙwaƙwalwa, da kasancewar sabar a raye
omniroute doctor --json                # JSON da na'ura za ta iya karantawa
omniroute doctor --no-liveness         # Tsallake gwajin lafiyar HTTP
omniroute doctor --host 0.0.0.0        # Sauya host na gwajin kasancewa a raye
omniroute doctor --liveness-url <url>  # Sauya cikakken URL na endpoint ɗin lafiya
```

`doctor` yana gudanar da waɗannan bincike: `Config`, `Database`, `Storage/encryption`,
`Port availability`, `Node runtime`, `Native binary` (better-sqlite3),
`Memory`, da `Server liveness`. Yana fita da lamba marar sifili idan kowane bincike ya kasance `fail`.

### Sarrafa Masu Samarwa

```bash
omniroute providers available                       # Kasidar masu samarwa ta OmniRoute
omniroute providers available --search openai       # Tace kasida ta id/suna/alias/rukuni
omniroute providers available --category api-key    # Tace ta rukuni (api-key, oauth, free, ...)
omniroute providers available --json                # JSON da na'ura za ta iya karantawa

omniroute providers list                            # Haɗin masu samarwa da aka tsara
omniroute providers list --json

omniroute providers test <id|name>                  # Gwada haɗi guda da aka tsara
omniroute providers test-all                        # Gwada kowane haɗi mai aiki
omniroute providers validate                        # Tabbatar da tsari a cikin gida kawai
omniroute providers add <provider> --credential-env PROVIDER_KEY
omniroute providers import ./providers.json --dry-run --json
omniroute providers auth <provider>                 # Tsarin OAuth da ake da shi
omniroute providers edit <id|name> --default-model <model>
omniroute providers remove <id|name> --yes
```

`providers add/import/auth/edit/remove` suna fifita API, saboda haka suna aiki da
mahallin gida ko na nesa da ke aiki. Ya kamata shigar da bayanan shaida ya yi amfani da
`--credential-stdin` ko `--credential-env`; `--dry-run --json` yana bayar da rahoto ne kawai
kan kasancewa/siffa da aka ɓoye. `providers available` yana karanta kasidar OmniRoute;
`providers list/test/test-all/validate` suna ci gaba da halayensu na SQLite na cikin gida kuma
ba sa buƙatar sabar ta kasance tana aiki.

Don node na musamman mai dacewa da OpenAI ko Anthropic, haɗa bayanan shaida da
ID na node ɗin da `omniroute nodes add` ya dawo da shi, ta amfani da `omniroute keys add "$NODE_ID" --stdin`.
Wannan yana buƙatar saba mai aiki da kuma tantancewar gudanarwa don mahallin da ke aiki.
CLI yana amfani da `POST /api/providers`, wanda ke tabbatar da node ɗin kuma ya kwafi saitunan endpoint
ɗinsa zuwa cikin haɗin. Rashin node, gazawar izini, ko rashin samuwar
saba yana mayar da kuskure ba tare da ƙirƙirar madadin bayanan shaida na cikin gida ba.

`nodes add --base-url` yana saita endpoint na node; ya bambanta da adireshin saba
da ke cikin `OMNIROUTE_BASE_URL`. Don fayilolin OpenAPI, yi amfani da
`omniroute openapi dump --format json --out ./openapi.json`; `--output` na gama-gari
yana zaɓar tsarin nunawar CLI, ba sunan fayil ɗin manufa ba.

### Fitar da Bayanan Shaida (⚠ a kula sosai)

```bash
omniroute auth export                                 # Nuna gargaɗi + matakin tabbatarwa — babu shiga DB
omniroute auth export --force                          # Fitar da bayanan shaida da AKA WARWARE na DUK haɗi zuwa stdout a matsayin JSON
omniroute auth export --force --id <id>                 # Fitar da haɗin da ya dace kawai
omniroute auth export --force --format env               # Fitar da layukan OMNIROUTE_<PROVIDER>_<FIELD>=<value>
omniroute auth export --force --out creds.json           # Rubuta zuwa fayil (wanda aka ƙirƙira da izinin 0600)
```

`auth export` yana aiki ne **a cikin na’ura kawai** (yana karanta SQLite kai tsaye, babu hanyar HTTP) kuma da gangan yake bugawa/rubutawa
ƙimomin **rubutu bayyananne** na `apiKey`/`accessToken`/`refreshToken`/`idToken` — wannan fasali ne, ba
matsala ba. Ba a karanta komai daga ma’ajiyar bayanai, kuma ba a warware ɓoyayyen komai, ba tare da `--force` ba. Kullum ana
nuna tutar gargaɗi a stderr kafin a fitar da kowane rubutu bayyananne. Yana buƙatar a saita `STORAGE_ENCRYPTION_KEY`.
Filin da ba a iya warware ɓoyewarsa ba (tsohuwar maɓalli, gurɓataccen ciphertext) ana bayar da rahotonsa a matsayin
`<field>DecryptFailed: true` maimakon dakatar da duk aikin fitarwa ko fallasa kuskuren da ke ƙarƙashinsa.

## Sarrafa manufofin inference-key da OmniRoute CLI

Yi amfani da mahallin gudanarwa mai tantancewa (duba [Yanayin Nesa](../guides/REMOTE-MODE.md))
don bincika ko sabunta inference key da ke akwai ta amfani da ID ɗinsa:

```bash
omniroute --output json keys policy show KEY_ID
omniroute keys policy set KEY_ID --unrestricted
omniroute --output json keys policy show KEY_ID
```

`--unrestricted` wata madadin alama ce ta `--model-access-mode all`. Sabuntawar tana share
jerin samfuran da aka yarda da su. Tana kiyaye scopes, dokokin haɗi, samfuran da aka toshe, iyakokin yawan buƙata
da iyakokin kuɗi, don haka waɗannan sauran matakan sarrafawa za su iya ci gaba da ƙin karɓar buƙata. Bayyanannen
`--allowed-models ""` shi ma yana zaɓar `all`; haɗa shi da
`--model-access-mode restricted` don riƙe maɓalli mai ƙuntatawa wanda ba shi da samfuran da aka yarda.
Jerin da ba fanko ba, wanda waƙafi ya raba, yana zaɓar `restricted` sai dai idan an fayyace akasin haka.
Ana ƙin karɓar haɗin `all` mai cin karo da juna tare da jerin izini marar fanko kafin a aika buƙata.

`--rate-limit 30` yana saita adadin buƙatu a minti ɗaya kuma yana kiyaye dokokin sauran
lokutan aunawa. `--max-cost 5` yana kunna iyakar USD ta yau da kullum ba tare da canza iyakar mako-mako ba;
`--max-cost 0` yana cire iyakar yau da kullum kuma yana kiyaye maɓallin kunna iyakar amfani
da iyakar mako-mako da suke akwai. Filayen manufofin da aka tsallake ba sa canzawa. Canje-canjen adadin buƙata suna karanta
manufofin yanzu da farko; guji gyare-gyare lokaci guda ga jerin rate-limit na maɓalli ɗaya.

Umarnin manufofi suna amfani da `GET`/`PATCH /api/keys/{id}` mai tantancewa kuma suna bin mahallin da ke aiki
da zaɓin `--api-key` na gama-gari. Fitarwar JSON tana ɗauke da filayen manufofi kawai,
ba tare da maɓallin da aka adana ko wakilcinsa da aka ɓoye ba. Shaidar da aka keɓe ga inference kawai
ba za ta iya ba da izinin canje-canjen gudanarwa ba. 401/403 yana buƙatar a bincika shaidar gudanarwa
da mahallin; ba ya tabbatar da gazawar CLI-token ko salt na cikin gida.
Duba [Token na CLI](../security/CLI_TOKEN.md) don keɓantaccen ƙa’idar tantancewa ta cikin gida.
