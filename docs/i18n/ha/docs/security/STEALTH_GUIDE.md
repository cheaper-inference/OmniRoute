## Sanarwar Doka da Ɗa'a

An samar da fasalolin ɓoye-sawu ne domin OmniRoute ya iya aiki a matsayin shimfiɗar daidaitawa tsakanin asusun hukuma mallakar masu amfani (Claude Code CLI, Codex, Antigravity, Cursor, da sauransu) da haɗaɗɗiyar API ta OmniRoute. **Ba** a yi su domin kauce wa gano zamba, raba bayanan shiga, ko karya Sharuɗɗan Sabis na masu samarwa ba. Masu kula da tsarin suna sa ran masu gudanarwa su bi ToS na tushe da suka amince da su lokacin ƙirƙirar asusu.

## Matakin Gano Sawun TLS

### `open-sse/utils/tlsClient.ts` — wreq-js (Chrome 124)

Ana ƙirƙirar zaman `wreq-js` masu ɗorewa ne kawai lokacin da ake buƙata ga kowane iyakar asusu da proxy da aka warware. `TlsClient` na gaba ɗayan tsari yana tara zaman da ba su wuce 128 ba waɗanda suke kwaikwayon **Chrome 124 a kan macOS** don sabis na sama da ke bayan Cloudflare. `TlsClient.fetch()` yana dakatar da aiki cikin aminci idan native runtime ba ya samuwa; mai kira zai iya zaɓar madadin a wajen wannan wrapper a sarari.

- Bayanan zaman: `browser: "chrome_124", os: "macos"`
- Warware proxy (bisa fifiko): `HTTPS_PROXY` → `HTTP_PROXY` → `ALL_PROXY` (har da masu ƙananan haruffa)
- Wa'adin lokaci: `TLS_CLIENT_TIMEOUT_MS` (yana gado daga `FETCH_TIMEOUT_MS`, tsoho 600000)
- Response na `wreq-js` ya dace da fetch (`headers`, `text()`, `json()`, `clone()`, `body`).
- Mai sa ido kan byte na farko (`open-sse/utils/tlsFirstByteWatchdog.ts`, #12656): `TlsClient.fetch()`
  yana kammala warwarewa da zarar headers na sabis na sama sun iso, saboda haka `TLS_CLIENT_TIMEOUT_MS` kaɗai ba zai iya iyakance
  body da bai taɓa samar da byte na farko ba. `guardTlsFirstByte()` yana fafata da `read()` na farko na body
  a kan `TLS_FIRST_BYTE_WATCHDOG_MS` (tsoho `10000`, `0` yana kashe shi); body mai lafiya ba ya
  shafuwa, yayin da body da ya tsaya cak ke soke wreq reader kuma ya bar dabarar komawa-baya ta TLS
  da ke cikin `proxyFetch` ta wuce zuwa mai tura buƙata kai tsaye/ta proxy (buƙatar da ba ta da aminci ga sake kunnawa, misali
  POST mai body, har yanzu tana jefa kuskure maimakon a sake gwada ta a ɓoye).

### Sufurin mai samar da web-cookie — wreq-js 3.2.0

`open-sse/services/tlsClientBase.ts` shi ne adapter ɗin gama-gari don takamaiman
sufurin web-cookie guda biyar da ke ƙasa. Kowane siririn wrapper na mai samarwa yana zaɓar bayanan browser/OS. Adapter ɗin
yana amfani da runtime loader guda ɗaya na wreq da tarin sufuri da ke cikin `open-sse/utils/tlsClient.ts`, waɗanda aka tsara maɓallinsu da
profile + OS + proxy da aka warware, yayin da kowace buƙata ke amfani da `cookieMode: "ephemeral"`. Saboda haka, asusu da
buƙatu suna raba haɗin matakin-sufuri, amma ba sa taɓa raba zaman wreq ko ma'ajin cookie.

| Mai samarwa | Profile       | OS da aka kwaikwaya | Manufar EOF ta stream                 |
| ----------- | ------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Claude      | `chrome_146`  | Linux               | haɗa da `[DONE]`                      |
| Perplexity  | `firefox_151` | macOS               | haɗa da `event: end_of_stream`        |
| Grok        | `chrome_146`  | Linux               | cire `[DONE]`                         |
| Notion      | `chrome_146`  | Windows             | haɗa da `[DONE]`                      |
| LMArena     | `chrome_146`  | Windows             | babu alamar ƙarshe; rufe a native EOF |

- Streaming yana amfani da `ReadableStream` na native response kai tsaye; ba a ƙirƙirar fayil na ɗan lokaci ko sidecar.
- Ana bincika har zuwa bytes 256 na farko kafin a bayyana stream. Masu samar da SSE suna adana kurakuran da ba na SSE ba a buffer;
  Grok/LMArena suna mayar da ƙalubalen Cloudflare zuwa `403`, sannan shafukan HTML na tsaka-tsaki zuwa `502`.
- Har yanzu ana naɗe wa'adin lokacin native request da cikakken wa'adin ƙarshe na JS. Tsayawar aiki tana soke inganci
  kuma tana rufe sufuri na profile/OS/proxy da abin ya shafa kaɗai, kafin buƙata ta gaba ta sake ƙirƙirarsa.
- Fifikon warware proxy shi ne `proxyUrl` na kowane kira → mahallin asusu/dashboard na takamaiman buƙata →
  `HTTPS_PROXY`/`HTTP_PROXY`/`ALL_PROXY` (har da sigogin ƙananan haruffa). Kurakuran warwarewa suna dakatar da aiki
  cikin aminci maimakon su fallasa haɗin kai tsaye. LMArena da gangan yana warwarewa bisa `arena.ai`.
- `byteResponse` yana dawo da URL na `data:` mai nau'in abun ciki ba tare da lalata UTF-8 ba.
- Kurakuran su ne `TlsClientUnavailableError` (package/addon ba ya samuwa), `TlsClientHangError`
  (an wuce wa'adin ƙarshe), da `WreqTransportCapacityError` (lambar kuskuren ƙarfin zaman da aka raba)
  lokacin da duk iyakantattun guraben profile/OS/proxy guda 128 suke aiki ko ana rufe su.

Zaman `TlsClient` na gama-gari da ke sama yana ci gaba da kasancewa na musamman don yanayin cookie mai ɗorewa
wanda browser ke tallafawa. Duk hanyoyin biyu suna sake amfani da cached wreq module loader guda ɗaya da process lifecycle hook; tarukansu suna
ci gaba da kasancewa daban saboda tsawon rayuwar cookies ɗinsu ya bambanta da gangan.

Pinned package ɗin yana goyon bayan profiles ɗin, amma karɓuwar WAF ta ainihi na iya sauyawa ba tare da la'akari
da gwaje-gwajen kwangilar cikin gida ba. Tabbatar da sauye-sauyen fingerprint ta amfani da live account da aka ba da izini a sarari
kafin a yi iƙirarin daidaito da browser na sabis na sama.

## Kunshin Ɓoye-Ɓoye na Claude Code

Lokacin da aka kunna `cliCompatMode`, OmniRoute yana sake fasalta buƙatun Claude masu fita ta yadda ba za a iya bambanta su da zirga-zirgar `claude-cli` ba. Module uku ne suke haɗin gwiwa:

### `claudeCodeFingerprint.ts`

Yana ƙididdige alamar yatsa mai haruffa 3 ta `cc_version` da aka saka a cikin taken lissafin kuɗi:

```
SHA256(SALT + msg[4] + msg[7] + msg[20] + version)[:3]
```

- `FINGERPRINT_SALT = "59cf53e54c78"` (an saka kai tsaye; ya yi daidai da client na hukuma)
- Abubuwan shigarwa: haruffan da ke index 4, 7, 20 na rubutun saƙon mai amfani na farko + igiyar version
- Sakamako: farkon hex mai haruffa 3

### `claudeCodeCCH.ts` (Hash ɗin Abun Cikin Client)

Binciken inganci a ɓangaren server wanda Claude Code CLI na hukuma ke ƙididdigewa ta Bun/Zig. OmniRoute ya sake aiwatar da shi da `xxhash-wasm`:

1. Mayar da body zuwa tsari tare da madadin `cch=00000;`
2. `xxhash64(bytes, seed) & 0xFFFFF`
3. Ƙaramin hex mai haruffa 5 wanda aka cika da sifili a gaba
4. Maye gurbin `cch=00000;` da token ɗin da aka ƙididdige

Ƙa'idodi:

- Seed: `0x6e52736ac806831e`
- Pattern: `/\bcch=([0-9a-f]{5});/`

### `claudeCodeObfuscation.ts`

Yana saka Unicode **zero-width joiner** (`U+200D`) bayan harafin farko na sunayen client masu "hankali" domin matatan upstream ba za su iya gano su da grep ba. Jerin kalmomin tsoho:

```
opencode, open-code, cline, roo-cline, roo_cline, cursor, windsurf,
aider, continue.dev, copilot, avante, codecompanion
```

Ana amfani da shi ga: tubalan `system`, duk `messages[].content`, da `tools[].description` / `tools[].function.description`. Mai gudanarwa zai iya sauya shi ta `setSensitiveWords()`.

### `claudeCodeCompatible.ts` — masu samar da `anthropic-compatible-cc-*`

Ga relay na Anthropic na ɓangare na uku waɗanda kawai suke karɓar zirga-zirgar "Claude Code na gaske":

- `CLAUDE_CODE_COMPATIBLE_USER_AGENT = "claude-cli/2.1.280 (external, sdk-cli)"`
- `CLAUDE_CODE_COMPATIBLE_STAINLESS_PACKAGE_VERSION = "0.112.1"`
- `CLAUDE_CODE_COMPATIBLE_STAINLESS_RUNTIME_VERSION = "v26.3.0"`
- `anthropic-beta = "claude-code-20250219,interleaved-thinking-2025-05-14,effort-2025-11-24"` ne ta tsohuwa
- Maɓallin kowane haɗi na "Enable redact-thinking beta" yana ƙara `redact-thinking-2026-02-12` lokacin da wani CC Compatible upstream yake buƙatar rafukan tunani da aka ɓoye musamman
- Maɓallin kowane haɗi na "Enable summarized thinking display" yana adana `providerSpecificData.requestDefaults.summarizeThinking` kuma yana ƙara `display: "summarized"` ga buƙatun tunani na CC Compatible waɗanda ba su riga sun saita yanayin nuni ba
- `CONTEXT_1M_BETA_HEADER = "context-1m-2025-08-07"` (iyalan Opus/Sonnet 4.x)
- Path na tsoho: `/v1/messages?beta=true`

Module masu alaƙa a cikin wannan kunshi:

- `claudeCodeConstraints.ts` — ƙa'idodin temperature + cache-control
- `claudeCodeToolRemapper.ts` — sake daidaita sunayen tool
- `claudeCodeExtraRemap.ts` — ƙarin daidaita payload

## Antigravity Stealth

Buƙatun Antigravity yana adana rubutun mai kira byte-for-byte. OmniRoute baya saka haruffa masu faɗin sifili cikin faɗakarwa ko kayan aikin rename/inject don yin koyi da abokin ciniki IDE.

### `antigravityHeaderScrub.ts`

Zaɓuɓɓuka Bakin SDK alamomi (`x-stainless-lang`, `x-stainless-package-version`, `x-stainless-os`, `x-stainless-arch`, `x-stainless-runtime`, `x-stainless-runtime-version`, `x-stainless-timeout`, `x-stainless-retry-count`, `x-stainless-helper-method`) kafin turawa.

### Hadarin ⚠️ : `ANTIGRAVITY_CREDITS=always` (wajen da aka hana asusu)

Hanyoyin `ANTIGRAVITY_CREDITS=always` (wanda `open-sse/executors/antigravity.ts` ke cinyewa).**kowane** nema ta hanyar Antigravity AI Credit Overages (biya Google credits) maimakon barin Google's kyauta ga keɓaɓɓen ƙofa abubuwa. An rubuta wannan a matsayin sifa, amma yana da **rahoton cin zarafin ToS guda ɗaya da muke gani** An dakatar da asusun — da yawa Google Ultra tare da `403 / "service disabled for ToS violation" / insufficient_quota` bayan ya gudana na 'yan sa'o'i tare da `=always`.

Ana ci gaba da aiwatar da aikin **Bangaren Google** , ba wani abu da OmniRoute zai iya hanawa ba. Sunan env var da takaddun da ke akwai suna sa ya zama kamar kulli mai aminci don juyawa; ba haka ba.

**Me yasa wannan ke jawo gano cin zarafi fiye da amfani da matakin kyauta kawai:**

- Ci gaba da ciyarwa ta atomatik akan tutocin asusun Google guda ɗaya daban fiye da matakin hits-ke-da-tsayawa.
- Ƙididdigar ƙima ba ta da rufin ƙima, don haka abokin ciniki da ba daidai ba zai iya ƙone ta cikin USD ɗari da yawa a cikin mintuna kuma yayi kama da sake siyarwar API-key ko zirga-zirgar bot.
- Masu amfani da OmniRoute da yawa suna bugun ƙididdiga masu yawa a layi daya daga IP na waje guda suna haɗa siginar.

**Matsayin da aka ba da shawarar:**

1. Ajiye tsohuwar `ANTIGRAVITY_CREDITS=off` sai dai idan mai aiki ya karɓi ƙididdiga da aka biya da haɗarin tilasta aiwatar da asusu. `retry` yana aika buƙatun al'ada da farko kuma yana allurar ƙididdigewa sau ɗaya bayan adadin da ya cancanta 429; `always` tana allurar ƙididdiga akan buƙatun farko.
2. **Yada kaya a kan masu samarwa ta hanyar Auto-Combo** (`model: "auto"` ko `kr/glm/etc` -combo) maimakon yin cikakken asusun Antigravity guda ɗaya.
3. **Saita iyakoki RPM kowane-haɗi** a cikin shafin gyare-gyare na mai bada Antigravity (Dashboard → Providers → Antigravity → connection → rate limit). 30-60 RPM shine babban abin kariya don amfani mai dorewa.
4. **Yi amfani da tsayayye, hanyar sadarwar sama mai sarrafa afareta** kuma a guji raba asusu ɗaya a tsakanin masu amfani ko nauyin aiki marasa alaƙa.
5. **Idan an hana** : roko ta hanyar `support.google.com` → "Mayar da damar Workspace/Account " tare da ainihin `quota_exceeded` / `service disabled` amsa jikin Google da aka aiko. Ba a da garantin maidowa.

Bayanin yanayi yana rubuta asusu kuma yana kashe tasirin kowane yanayin ƙididdigewa.

#### Ba kawai `=always` ba: Antigravity OAuth routing gabaɗaya

Dakatarwar ba ta iyakance ga yawan kiredit ba. Rahotanni a cikin Nuwamba 2026 (#19223, #19266) sun bayyana wasu asusun Google da aka dakatar yayin da suke tafiya ta hanyar masu samar da `antigravity` da `agy` (Antigravity CLI), tare da:

```
403: This service has been disabled in this account for violation of Terms of Service.
Please submit an appeal to continue using this product.
```

Dukansu masu samarwa sun tabbatar da zaman Antigravity samfurin OAuth, wanda shine yanayin sanarwar haɗarin dashboard ("Wannan mai bada yana amfani da samfurin ku na hukuma session/OAuth, wanda ba a ba da izini don amfani da proxy/router ") yayi kashedin kafin a haɗa haɗin. Babu ɗayan shawarwarin da ke sama da ya ba da izinin amfani; suna rage yawan zirga-zirgar ababen hawa ne kawai.

**Abubuwan musaya na API masu izini don ƙirar Gemini.** Google tana ba da damar yin amfani da shirye-shirye zuwa Gemini ta hanyar APIs ɗin sa, wanda OmniRoute ke goyan bayan a matsayin masu samar da maɓallin API na yau da kullun:

| Id mai bayarwa | Dashboard sunan           | Takaddun shaida                                                                        |
| -------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `gemini`       | Gemini (Google AI Studio) | Maɓallin Gemini API daga Google AI Studio (akwai matakin kyauta)                       |
| `vertex`       | Farashin AI               | Asusun Sabis JSON, alamar samun damar OAuth ko maɓallin API na Vertex Express (Gemini) |

Waɗannan masu samarwa suna amfani da al'amurran Google na takaddun shaida don samun damar API, sabanin zaman samfurin Antigravity. Ba su sa kowane amfani ya dace ba ko cire haɗarin tilastawa: kowanne ana sarrafa shi da nasa sharuɗɗan, wanda ma'aikacin dole ne ya bincika jigilar su. Misali, [Ƙarin Sharuɗɗan Sabis Gemini API](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms) yana ba da damar Sabis na Biya kawai lokacin da aka samar da abokan ciniki API ga masu amfani a Yankin Tattalin Arziki na Turai, Switzerland ko United Kingdom, kuma ana iya amfani da abun ciki da aka aika zuwa Sabis ɗin da ba a biya ba don haɓaka samfuran Google. Vertex AI ya faɗi ƙarƙashin Google Cloud sharuɗɗan maimakon ( [Sharuɗɗan Takaddun Sabis](https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms) ). Farashi da ƙididdiga suna bin waɗannan sharuɗɗan API maimakon biyan kuɗin Antigravity.

Masu aiki waɗanda ke son guje wa dakatarwar Antigravity da aka kwatanta a sama yakamata su bi hanyar Gemini ta ɗayan waɗannan masu samarwa maimakon `antigravity` / `agy`, bayan sun duba cewa amfani da su ya yi daidai da sharuɗɗan mai bayarwa.

Abubuwan taɓawa:

- `open-sse/executors/antigravity.ts` — yana karanta `process.env.ANTIGRAVITY_CREDITS`
- `src/lib/oauth/providers/antigravity.ts` — bututun bututu
- Rahoton abin da ya faru na asali: Tattaunawa [#1183](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/discussions/1183)

## Rijistar Sawun Yatsa na CLI — `open-sse/config/cliFingerprints.ts`

Teburin kowane mai samarwa wanda ke kayyade **ainihin** jerin headers da jerin filayen jikin JSON da aka samo daga mitmproxy traces na CLI na hukuma. Waɗanda aka yi wa rajista a halin yanzu: `codex`, `claude`, tare da profiles da ake samarwa yayin aiki a cikin `providerHeaderProfiles.ts` don `antigravity` da `github`.

```ts
interface CliFingerprint {
  headerOrder: string[]; // yana bambanta bisa manyan da ƙananan haruffa
  bodyFieldOrder: string[]; // maɓallan JSON na matakin sama
  userAgent?: string | (() => string);
  extraHeaders?: Record<string, string>;
}
```

Kunna ko kashe ga kowane mai samarwa ta env (duba ƙasa). Lokacin da aka kashe, headers/maɓallan body za su bayyana a duk jerin da Node/JSON ya bayar — wanda ke sa gano sawun yatsarsu ya zama mai sauƙi.

## MITM Proxy (Antigravity, Linux/macOS/Windows)

Ga CLI waɗanda ba za a iya karkatar da binaries ɗinsu ta `OPENAI_BASE_URL` ba, OmniRoute yana gudanar da proxy na cikin gida mai ƙare TLS. Endpoints suna ƙarƙashin `src/app/api/cli-tools/antigravity-mitm/`.

| Hanya  | Endpoint                                | Manufa                                              |
| ------ | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| GET    | `/api/cli-tools/antigravity-mitm`       | Matsayi — yana aiki, pid, dnsConfigured, certExists |
| POST   | `/api/cli-tools/antigravity-mitm`       | Fara MITM (yana buƙatar `apiKey` + `sudoPassword`)  |
| DELETE | `/api/cli-tools/antigravity-mitm`       | Dakatar da MITM                                     |
| GET    | `/api/cli-tools/antigravity-mitm/alias` | Jera laƙabban model                                 |
| PUT    | `/api/cli-tools/antigravity-mitm/alias` | Ajiye laƙabban model don wani tool                  |

Host ɗin da ake kutsewa: **`daily-cloudcode-pa.googleapis.com`** (upstream na Antigravity).

### Jerin farawa (`src/mitm/manager.ts::startMitm`)

1. Samar da self-signed cert ta `selfsigned` (RSA-2048, SHA-256, shekara 1) — `cert/generate.ts`
2. Shigar da cert cikin system trust store — `cert/install.ts`
3. Ƙara hosts entry `127.0.0.1 daily-cloudcode-pa.googleapis.com` — `dns/dnsConfig.ts`
4. Ƙaddamar da `src/mitm/server.cjs` tare da `ROUTER_API_KEY` + `MITM_LOCAL_PORT` (tsoho `443`)
5. Adana PID zuwa `<DATA_DIR>/mitm/.mitm.pid`

### Gano trust-store mai canzawa na Linux — `cert/install.ts`

`getLinuxCertConfig()` yana bi cikin jerin fifiko kuma ya zaɓi directory na farko da yake akwai:

| Iyalin distro            | Directory                                   | Umarnin sabuntawa        |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Debian / Ubuntu          | `/usr/local/share/ca-certificates`          | `update-ca-certificates` |
| Arch / CachyOS / Manjaro | `/etc/ca-certificates/trust-source/anchors` | `update-ca-trust`        |
| Fedora / RHEL / CentOS   | `/etc/pki/ca-trust/source/anchors`          | `update-ca-trust`        |
| openSUSE                 | `/etc/pki/trust/anchors`                    | `update-ca-certificates` |

Sunan fayil ɗin cert: `omniroute-mitm.crt`. Daidaita fingerprint ta `getCertFingerprint()` (SHA-1 na DER).

Bugu da ƙari, `updateNssDatabases()` yana shigarwa cikin NSS DBs na kowane mai amfani idan `certutil` yana akwai: `~/.pki/nssdb`, `~/snap/chromium/.../nssdb`, duk Firefox profiles (har da snap), ƙarƙashin laƙabin **`OmniRoute MITM Root CA`**.

### macOS / Windows

- **macOS:** `security add-trusted-cert -d -r trustRoot -k /Library/Keychains/System.keychain`
- **Windows:** PowerShell mai elevated privileges → `certutil -addstore Root`

### Tantancewa

Duk MITM endpoints suna buƙatar management auth (`requireCliToolsAuth`). Ana cache ɗin kalmar sirrin sudo a module scope (ba a taɓa amfani da `globalThis` ba) kuma ana share ta a `stopMitm()`.

## Sabunta Sawun Yatsa Idan Mai Samarwa Ya Sauya Shi

1. Ɗauki zirga-zirgar CLI na hukuma da `mitmproxy` (tsoma bakin TLS + zubar da bayanai)
2. Ciro JA3/JA4 da ainihin jerin kanun bayanan
3. Sabunta shigarwar `CLI_FINGERPRINTS[...]` da ta dace
4. Ƙara tsohuwar ƙimar `*_USER_AGENT` da ta dace a cikin `.env.example`
5. Idan musayar gaisuwar TLS da kanta ta sauya, sabunta marufin mai samarwar da ya dace ko zaɓin wreq-js `browser:`
6. Gudanar da gwaje-gwajen TLS na musamman ga mai samarwar da gwajin canary na hannu a kan mai samarwar kai-tsaye
7. Fitar da shi a cikin sakin faci; rubuta bayaninsa a `CHANGELOG.md`
