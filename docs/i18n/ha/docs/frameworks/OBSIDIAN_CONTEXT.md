## Mene ne

OmniRoute yana haɗuwa da rumbun **Obsidian** a matsayin **tushen mahallin bayani** — ma'ajiyar
ilimin Markdown ta gida wadda wakilai ke karantawa da rubutawa ta hanyar sabar MCP da aka
gina a ciki. Haɗin yana sadarwa da plugin ɗin al'umma na **Obsidian Local REST API** wanda
ke gudana a cikin manhajar tebur, don haka wakilai za su iya bincika bayanan rubutu,
karanta/rubuta/gyara fayiloli, jera abubuwan rumbun, aiki da bayanan rubutu na yau da kullum
ko na mako-mako, sarrafa tags, gudanar da umarnin Obsidian, da kuma (idan ana so) daidaita
rumbun ta hanyoyi biyu tsakanin tebur↔wayar hannu.

Abokin cinikin (`src/lib/obsidian/api.ts`) yana lulluɓe Local REST API da:

- **Sake gwadawa tare da jinkiri mai ƙaruwa** don kurakuran wucin gadi na `5xx`, da **lokacin jira na daƙiƙa 30** ta hanyar `AbortController`.
- **Rarraba kurakurai bisa nau'i** — `ObsidianAuthError` (401/403),
  `ObsidianNotFoundError` (404), `ObsidianServerError` (5xx), `ObsidianTimeoutError`.
- **Shawara mai sauƙin fahimta ta "ba za a iya isa ga Obsidian ba"** wadda ke nuna kuskuren
  tashar sadarwa da ake yawan yi (HTTP a `27123`, **ba** ƙarshen MCP da ke `27124` ba) da tsarin Tailscale.
- **Tsarawa ta musamman ga hanyoyin fayil** masu dangantaka da rumbu, domin hanyoyin bayanan rubutu masu sarari/slashes su kasance amintattu.

## Saitawa

Babu **environment variable** don token na Obsidian ko base URL — dukansu ana
adana su a teburin SQLite `key_value` (namespace `obsidian`) ta hanyar
`src/lib/db/obsidian.ts`. Ana **rufe token ɗin yayin ajiya** (AES-256-GCM, tare da
madadin dacewa da tsohon tsarin plaintext). Yi saitin daga shafin **Context Sources** na
dashboard ɗin Endpoint (`ObsidianSourceCard`), ko ta hanyar REST API na saituna.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Dole ne a girka plugin ɗin **Obsidian Local REST API** kuma yana aiki. REST
> interface ɗinsa yana sauraro a **HTTP `127.0.0.1:27123`** (tsohon base URL). Port `27124`
> endpoint ne na MCP/HTTPS _na daban_ kuma hanyar saituna tana ƙin karɓarsa kai tsaye.
> Idan ana haɗawa daga wata na'ura, yi amfani da `http://<tailscale-ip>:27123`.

### Maɓallan saiti (SQLite `key_value`, namespace `obsidian`)

| Maɓalli           | Manufa                                                   | An rufe |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| `api_key`         | Bearer token na Local REST API                           | eh      |
| `base_url`        | REST base URL (tsoho `http://127.0.0.1:27123`)           | a'a     |
| `vault_path`      | Cikakkiyar hanya zuwa kundin vault (don daidaitawa)      | a'a     |
| `webdav_username` | Sunan mai amfani na WebDAV da aka samar (daidaita vault) | a'a     |
| `webdav_password` | Kalmar sirrin WebDAV da aka samar (daidaita vault)       | eh      |
| `webdav_enabled`  | Ko an kunna daidaita vault ta WebDAV                     | a'a     |

### Saitawa ta hanyar REST

```bash
# Ajiye + tabbatar da token na Local REST API (POST yana tabbatarwa ta hanyar duba matsayi)
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/settings/obsidian \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"token":"<obsidian-rest-api-key>","baseUrl":"http://127.0.0.1:27123"}'

# Duba matsayin haɗin kai (yana dawo da connected, hasToken, baseUrl, vaultPath)
curl http://localhost:20128/api/settings/obsidian

# Cire haɗin kai (yana share token ɗin da aka adana)
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:20128/api/settings/obsidian
```

Duk hanyoyin suna buƙatar tantancewar dashboard. `POST` yana ƙin duk wani URL da ke kan port `27124`
kuma yana tabbatar da token ta hanyar kiran status endpoint na Local REST API kafin adanawa.

### Daidaita vault ta WebDAV

`src/app/api/settings/obsidian/webdav/route.ts` yana sarrafa daidaita vault na zaɓi mai amfani da WebDAV
(wanda `src/lib/obsidianSync.ts` ke tafiyarwa). Kunna shi yana nuna OmniRoute zuwa
kundin vault na gida kuma yana ƙirƙirar bazuwar sunan mai amfani/kalmar sirri na WebDAV:

```bash
# Kunna daidaita WebDAV don kundin vault (yana ƙirƙirar sunan mai amfani/kalmar sirri)
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/settings/obsidian/webdav \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"vaultPath":"/home/me/MyVault"}'

# Samo matsayin daidaita WebDAV (ana dawo da bayanan shiga ne kawai yayin da aka kunna)
curl http://localhost:20128/api/settings/obsidian/webdav

# Kashe daidaita WebDAV (yana share bayanan shiga + .stignore da ake sarrafawa)
curl -X DELETE http://localhost:20128/api/settings/obsidian/webdav
```

### Tsaro & Sufuri

Tabbatar da WebDAV yana tilasta bin diddigin gazawar kowane-IP da kullewa (yunƙurin gazawar 10 a cikin taga na daƙiƙa 60 yana haifar da kullewar minti 5 tare da `429 Too Many Requests` da `Retry-After`). Saboda WebDAV yana watsa ainihin shaidar shaidar, ƙare TLS (ko kewayawa a kan amintaccen rami kamar Tailscale ko Cloudflare Tunnel) ana bada shawarar sosai lokacin samun damar ƙarshen WebDAV a waje da madauki.

### Tushen mahalli na kowane API key (na zaɓi)

Ana iya keɓance saitin Obsidian **ga kowane API key** ta hanyar teburin `api_key_context_sources`
(`src/lib/db/apiKeyContextSources.ts`). Lokacin da kiran MCP ya ƙunshi id na API
key da aka tantance, `getObsidianConfigForApiKey()` yana fifita token/base-URL/vault-path
na wannan maɓallin (`source: "api_key"`), in ba haka ba ya koma ga saitin gama-gari (`source: "global"`).

Kayan aikin MCP waɗanda ba na daidaitawa ba suna buƙatar sanannen asalin mai kira kafin karanta wannan saiti.
Mai kira da ba shi da asali yana karɓar `Obsidian API token not configured`, kuma ba a aika wani buƙatar
vault ba. Ana tantance masu kiran HTTP ta hanyar bayanan shaidar buƙatarsu; ana iya tantance masu kiran stdio
ta hanyar `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` ko `ROUTER_API_KEY`. Mai kiran HTTP da ba a iya tantancewa ba
ba zai iya gaji asalin environment na uwar garken ba. Placeholder IDs kaɗai ba sa ba da izinin
samun dama; ingantaccen bayanin shaidar environment da ke kaiwa zuwa `env-key` har yanzu ana tallafa masa.
Maɓallan da aka tantance waɗanda ba su da saitin Obsidian na musamman ga maɓalli suna ci gaba da amfani da saitin gama-gari a matsayin madadi.
Kayan aikin `obsidian_sync_*` guda huɗu suna ci gaba da amfani da halayen sync-token nasu na daban.

## MCP aiki (22)

An bayyana a cikin `open-sse/mcp-server/tools/obsidianTools.ts`. Ana warware token/base-URL kowane kira (kowace-API-key na farko, sannan na duniya). Kayan aikin da suka buga OmniRoute** sync uwar garken** (kayan aikin `obsidian_sync_*` huɗu) kuma suna buƙatar alamar daidaitawa da aka saita a cikin saitunan OmniRoute.

### Kayan aikin karanta (`read:obsidian`)

| Kayan aiki                   | Bayani                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `obsidian_check_status`      | Bincika ko Local REST API ana iya kaiwa kuma an inganta shi.                                                |
| `obsidian_search_simple`     | Binciken cikakken rubutu na abun ciki na bayanin kula; yana dawo da snippets tare da hanyoyin fayil.        |
| `obsidian_search_structured` | Bincika ta amfani da kalmar ma'ana ta JSON ( filtar and/or/regex/path ).                                    |
| `obsidian_read_note`         | Karanta bayanin kula ta hanyar vault-dangi; na zaɓi takamaiman heading/block/frontmatter.                   |
| `obsidian_list_vault`        | Yi lissafin fayiloli da kundayen adireshi a cikin vault (itacen shigarwa).                                  |
| `obsidian_get_document_map`  | Samo tsarin taken bayanin kula azaman taswirar taken lambobi layin →.                                       |
| `obsidian_get_note_metadata` | Sami gaba-gaba, tags, links, ƙidaya char/word ba tare da cikakken abun ciki ba.                             |
| `obsidian_get_active_file`   | Samun hanyar + abun ciki na fayil mai aiki a halin yanzu a cikin Obsidian.                                  |
| `obsidian_get_periodic_note` | Samu bayanin lokaci-lokaci daily/weekly/monthly na kwanan wata (yau idan an cire shi).                      |
| `obsidian_get_tags`          | Lissafin duk alamun vault tare da mitocin su.                                                               |
| `obsidian_list_commands`     | Jerin akwai umarnin Obsidian IDs (amfani da `obsidian_execute_command`).                                    |
| `obsidian_sync_status`       | Matsayin sabar sync OmniRoute : Gudu, sunan vault, tashar jiragen ruwa, lokacin aiki, daidaitawa ta ƙarshe. |
| `obsidian_sync_conflicts`    | Lissafin rikice-rikicen aiki tare da ba a warware su ba (hanya, hanyar rikici, gano-a).                     |

### Rubutun kayan aikin (`write:obsidian`)

| Kayan aiki                       | Bayani                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `obsidian_write_note`            | Ƙirƙiri ko sake rubuta bayanin kula tare da abun cikin Markdown da aka bayar.             |
| `obsidian_append_note`           | Sanya abun ciki zuwa bayanin kula; na zaɓi zuwa takamaiman heading/block.                 |
| `obsidian_patch_note`            | Tiyata append/prepend/replace a kan gaba, toshe, ko filin gaba.                           |
| `obsidian_delete_note`           | Share rubutu na dindindin daga rumbun ajiya.                                              |
| `obsidian_move_note`             | Matsar ko sake suna bayanin kula a cikin rumbun ajiya.                                    |
| `obsidian_execute_command`       | Yi umarnin Obsidian ta umarninsa ID.                                                      |
| `obsidian_open_file`             | Bude fayil a cikin Obsidian (yana ƙirƙira shi idan babu shi).                             |
| `obsidian_sync_trigger`          | Ƙirƙirar aiki tare da ↔ ta hannu vault na tebur na biyu kai tsaye.                        |
| `obsidian_sync_resolve_conflict` | Yanke rikicin daidaitawa: kiyaye `local` (wayar hannu), `remote` (tebur), ko `keep-both`. |

> [!NOTE]
> Makasudin `obsidian_patch_note` sun karɓi `targetType` na `heading | block | frontmatter` da `operation` na `append | prepend | replace`, tare da `createTargetIfMissing` na zaɓi . Kayan aikin `obsidian_sync_*` guda huɗu suna magana da uwar garken daidaitawa na gida (`http://127.0.0.1:27781` ta tsohuwa) kuma suna buƙatar alamar daidaitawa.

### Iyaka

Kayayyakin karatu suna buƙatar `read:obsidian`; kayan aikin rubuta suna buƙatar `write:obsidian`. Yin tilastawa yayi kama da Notion — wanda `withScopeEnforcement()` ke sarrafa shi a cikin `open-sse/mcp-server/server.ts`, wanda aka ɗora akan `OMNIROUTE_MCP_ENFORCE_SCOPES=true` kuma ana tilastawa ga masu kiran HTTP/SSE waɗanda maɓallan API basu da `manage`/`admin`, tare da iyakoki masu izini waɗanda aka samo daga `OMNIROUTE_MCP_SCOPES` ko mahallin mahallin maɓalli na API. Duba [MCP-SERVER.md](./MCP-SERVER.md).

## Wuraren Ƙarshe

| Hanya    | Tafarki                         | Manufa                                                            |
| -------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `GET`    | `/api/settings/obsidian`        | Mayar da `{ connected, hasToken, baseUrl, vaultPath }`.           |
| `POST`   | `/api/settings/obsidian`        | Ajiye + tabbatar da token (yana ƙin port `27124`).                |
| `DELETE` | `/api/settings/obsidian`        | Cire haɗin (goge token da aka adana).                             |
| `GET`    | `/api/settings/obsidian/webdav` | Matsayin daidaitawar WebDAV + bayanan shiga (yayin da aka kunna). |
| `POST`   | `/api/settings/obsidian/webdav` | Kunna daidaitawar WebDAV don kundin adireshin vault.              |
| `DELETE` | `/api/settings/obsidian/webdav` | Kashe daidaitawar WebDAV.                                         |

> Waɗannan su ne hanyoyin saitunan dashboard. Ana isa ga vault ɗin kansa ta Obsidian
> Local REST API (`base_url` da aka saita) da kuma ta kayan aikin MCP da ke sama — babu
> endpoint na wakilin Obsidian na `/v1` na jama'a.

## Yanayin amfani

- **Amsoshi masu tushe daga vault** — `obsidian_search_simple` / `obsidian_search_structured`
  sannan `obsidian_read_note` domin wakili ya amsa daga ainihin bayanan rubutunka.
- **Rubuta bayanai / kundin yau da kullum** — `obsidian_write_note`, `obsidian_append_note`, ko
  `obsidian_patch_note` mai yin gyara daidai domin rubuta sakamakon wakili, taƙaitawa, ko bayanan yau da kullum
  (`obsidian_get_periodic_note`) cikin vault.
- **Kewaya vault** — `obsidian_list_vault`, `obsidian_get_document_map`, da
  `obsidian_get_tags` don bincika tsari kafin karantawa/rubutawa.
- **Sarrafawar Obsidian ta atomatik** — `obsidian_list_commands` + `obsidian_execute_command` don
  sarrafa plugins/commands daga wakili; `obsidian_open_file` don nuna wani bayani a cikin UI.
- **Daidaitawar wayar hannu** — kunna daidaitawar WebDAV, sannan `obsidian_sync_trigger` /
  `obsidian_sync_status` / `obsidian_sync_conflicts` / `obsidian_sync_resolve_conflict`
  don daidaita desktop↔mobile da warware rikice-rikice.
