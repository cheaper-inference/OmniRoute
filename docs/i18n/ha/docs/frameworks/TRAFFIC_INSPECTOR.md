Traffic Inspector shi ne mai gyara matsalolin zirga-zirgar HTTPS da aka gina a cikin OmniRoute — kayan aiki irin na Charles Proxy / mitmweb / HTTP Toolkit wanda yake **fahimtar LLM** kuma yake **fahimtar wakilai**. Yana nan a `/dashboard/tools/traffic-inspector` kuma yana karɓar zirga-zirga kai-tsaye daga hanyoyin ɗaukar bayanai har guda 5 a lokaci guda.

**Wurin dashboard:** `/dashboard/tools/traffic-inspector`
**Rukunin sidebar:** Tools (bayan AgentBridge)
**Duba kuma:** [`AGENTBRIDGE.md`](./AGENTBRIDGE.md) — AgentBridge shi ne yanayin ɗaukar bayanai na 1.

## §2 Hanyoyin kama zirga-zirga

Traffic Inspector yana goyon bayan **tushen kama guda 5 a lokaci guda**. Ana iya kunna ko kashe kowannensu daban. Filin `source` a kowane `InterceptedRequest` (`src/mitm/inspector/types.ts`) yana ɗaya daga cikin `"agent-bridge"`, `"custom-host"`, `"http-proxy"`, `"system-proxy"`, ko `"tproxy"`.

### Hanya ta 1 — AgentBridge (ta asali, kullum a kunne)

**Tushe:** Masu sarrafa AgentBridge (`src/mitm/handlers/base.ts`)
**Tsari:** Kowane kiran `intercept()` a cikin `MitmHandlerBase` yana kiran `hookBufferStart()` kafin tura buƙatar, sannan ya kira `hookBufferUpdate()` bayan kammalawa. Ba a buƙatar ƙarin saiti — yana aiki da zarar AgentBridge yana gudana.
**Iyakar aiki:** Wakilan IDE guda 9 da aka saita a AgentBridge
**Lura:** Filin `source` a cikin `InterceptedRequest` = `"agent-bridge"`

### Hanya ta 2 — Custom Hosts (juya alkiblar DNS)

**Tushe:** Jerin host da mai amfani ya ayyana (teburin `inspector_custom_hosts`)
**Tsari:** Ƙara host ta UI yana ƙara `127.0.0.1 <host>` zuwa `/etc/hosts` (yana buƙatar sudo). Sabar MITM ta AgentBridge da ake da ita (port 443) tana samar da takardar shaidar SNI kai tsaye don sabon host.
**Iyakar aiki:** Duk wani application da ke amfani da host ɗin da aka ƙara — ba a buƙatar canza saitin application
**Lura:** `source` = `"custom-host"`

Misalan yanayin amfani:

- Sa ido kan `api.openai.com` daga rubutun Python
- Gano matsala a `my-internal-llm.company.com`
- Kama zirga-zirga daga na'urorin hannu da ke kan network ɗaya (ta hanyar kwaikwayon ARP — na ƙwararru)

### Hanya ta 3 — Mai sauraron HTTP_PROXY (port 8080)

**Tushe:** Applications masu amfani da environment variables na `HTTP_PROXY`/`HTTPS_PROXY`
**Tsari:** Mai sauraro na biyu a port 8080 (`src/mitm/inspector/httpProxyServer.ts`) wanda ke aiki a matsayin daidaitaccen explicit proxy na HTTP/HTTPS. Yana karɓar tunnels na `CONNECT` (HTTPS) da buƙatun HTTP kai tsaye.
**Iyakar aiki:** Duk wani application da ke bin env na `HTTP_PROXY` — babu canjin DNS, babu sudo
**Lura:** `source` = `"http-proxy"`

```bash
# Kama zirga-zirga cikin sauri don umarni guda:
HTTPS_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8080 curl https://api.openai.com/v1/models

# Kama zirga-zirga na dindindin a zaman shell:
export HTTP_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8080
export HTTPS_PROXY=http://127.0.0.1:8080
```

**Iyakancin TLS:** HTTPS yana ratsawa ta tunnel na `CONNECT` mara sarrafawa: mai sauraron yana isar da encrypted bytes ba tare da taɓa su ba, kuma yana yin rikodin metadata kawai (host, port, lokaci). Ba ya taɓa decrypting HTTPS, kuma babu wani saiti da ke kunna decryption a wannan hanyar. Don kama bodies na HTTPS, bi da host ɗin ta Hanya ta 1 (AgentBridge), Hanya ta 2 (Custom Hosts), ko Hanya ta 5 (TPROXY decrypt).

**Rikicin port:** Idan ana amfani da port 8080, AgentBridge yana mayar da 409 tare da kuskure mai tsari. Canza port ɗin ta hanyar env var na `INSPECTOR_HTTP_PROXY_PORT`.

### Hanya ta 4 — Proxy na dukkan system (na ƙwararru, sai an zaɓa)

**Tushe:** Saitunan proxy na matakin OS (yana aiki ga duk applications da ke kan na'urar)
**Tsari:** Yana amfani da APIs na OS don karkatar da duk zirga-zirgar HTTP/HTTPS ta mai sauraron HTTP_PROXY:

- **macOS:** `networksetup -setwebproxy / -setsecurewebproxy`
- **Linux:** `gsettings set org.gnome.system.proxy` + `/etc/environment`
- **Windows:** `netsh winhttp set proxy 127.0.0.1:8080`
  **Iyakar aiki:** Kowane application da ke kan na'urar wanda ke bin saitunan system proxy
  **Lura:** `source` = `"system-proxy"`

**Matakan kariya:**

- Mai ƙidayar lokacin kashewa kai tsaye (minti 30 ta asali, ana iya saitawa ta `INSPECTOR_SYSTEM_PROXY_GUARD_MINUTES`)
- Ana adana yanayin system proxy na baya a DB kuma a dawo da shi yayin mayarwa
- Dashboard yana nuna saƙon "Ana mayar da system proxy" idan mai amfani ya bar shafin yayin da yake aiki
- UI yana nuna alamar `⚠ Na ƙwararru` + akwatin tabbatarwa a sarari

### Hanya ta 5 — TPROXY transparent decrypt (Linux, root, sai an zaɓa)

**Tushe:** Kernel TPROXY + policy routing (`src/mitm/tproxy/`)
**Tsari:** Yana yi wa sabbin local outbound TCP connections zuwa target port (ta asali `443`) alama a cikin `mangle OUTPUT`, sai `ip rule` ya sake karkatar da packets masu alamar zuwa local delivery, sannan target na `TPROXY` na `mangle PREROUTING` ya miƙa su ga transparent listener (**IP_TRANSPARENT**) (port na asali `8443`). Mai sauraron yana ƙare TLS da leaf certificate da dynamic CA ta bayar **ga kowane SNI hostname lokacin da ake buƙata**, yana kama decrypted exchange, sannan ya tura buƙatar bayan sake encrypting ɗinta zuwa asalin destination.
**Iyakar aiki:** Hosts na destination **ko waɗanne iri** a target port — babu kwaikwayon `/etc/hosts`, babu env na `HTTP_PROXY`, babu sauya system-wide proxy. Process ɗin da ake intercepting ba ya buƙatar canjin saiti, amma dole ne ya amince da dynamic CA.
**Lura:** `source` = `"tproxy"`

**Abubuwan da ake buƙata:** Linux kawai (**IP_TRANSPARENT** na Linux ne kawai), damar **CAP_NET_ADMIN** (root), da native N-API addon wanda dole ne a gina shi da C toolchain (`npm run build:native:tproxy`). Idan babu su, ana kashe toggle na dashboard tare da tooltip mai cewa "TPROXY decrypt yana buƙatar Linux + root + native addon". Ana aiwatarwa da mayar da firewall rules a matsayin transaction (crash ba zai taɓa barin `mangle` rule a baya ba), kuma ana share su lokacin reboot. Tsarin anti-loop mai amfani da SO_MARK yana hana proxy sake intercepting nasa re-encrypted forward.

Wannan babban subsystem ne mai nasa keɓantaccen jagorar operator — duba `docs/security/MITM-TPROXY-DECRYPT.md` (git; ba a compile shi cikin `/docs` ba) don cikakken tsarin firewall, per-SNI dynamic CA + trust-store installer, local-only route, bayanan anti-loop, da configuration schema. `GET / POST / DELETE /api/tools/agent-bridge/tproxy` ne ke sarrafa toggle ɗin (lura: route ɗin yana ƙarƙashin prefix na AgentBridge, ba prefix na Traffic Inspector ba).

## §4 Fasallolin da suka fahimci LLM

### 4.1 Mai gano nau'i (`src/mitm/inspector/kindDetector.ts`)

Yana rarraba kowace buƙata a matsayin `"llm"`, `"app"`, ko `"unknown"` ta amfani da alamomi 4:

1. **Rajistar runduna** — kusan sunayen rundunar API na LLM guda 18 da aka sani (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, Mistral, Together, Fireworks, Cohere, Perplexity, Hugging Face, OpenRouter, xAI, Moonshot, da sauransu.)
2. **Tsarukan hanya** — `/v1/chat/completions`, `/v1/messages`, `/generateContent`, `/v1/responses`, da sauransu.
3. **Siffar jiki** — yana gano filayen `messages[]` (OpenAI/Claude), `contents[]` (Gemini), `prompt`, `input`
4. **Alamomin user-agent** — `codex`, `claude`, `gemini`, `antigravity`, `kiro`, `copilot`, `cursor` a cikin zaren UA

Rundunonin al'ada da aka ƙara ta Mode 2 suna gādon `kind` ɗinsu daga shigarwar fom (tsohon ƙimarsa ita ce `"custom"`).

### 4.2 Mai haɗa SSE (`src/mitm/inspector/sseMerger.ts`)

**Aiwatarwa mai zaman kanta da aka gina daga tushe.** Tantance aukuwar tana bin
[ka'idar WHATWG ta abubuwan da uwar garke ke aikowa](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/server-sent-events.html#parsing-an-event-stream),
yayin da sake ginawa ke bin tsare-tsaren yawo na jama'a na [OpenAI](https://platform.openai.com/docs/api-reference/chat/create),
[Anthropic](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/streaming), da
[Gemini](https://ai.google.dev/api/generate-content#method:-models.streamgeneratecontent).

Yana sake gina saƙon mataimaki na ƙarshe daga ɗanyen aukuwar delta na SSE:

- **Anthropic**: yana tara `content_block_delta` bisa ga fihirisa; yana sarrafa `text_delta`, `input_json_delta` (kiran kayan aiki), `thinking_delta`
- **OpenAI**: yana tara zaɓuɓɓukan Chat Completions/kiran kayan aiki da abubuwan fitarwa na Responses API
  bisa ga fihirisa
- **Gemini**: yana tara `candidates[i].content.parts`
- **Unknown**: yana mayar da ɗanyen aukuwar yadda suke

Shafin Response yana nuna maɓallin sauyawa: **"Ɗanyen aukuwar ↔ Haɗaɗɗe"**.

### 4.3 Mai daidaita tattaunawa (`src/mitm/inspector/conversationNormalizer.ts`)

**Aiwatarwa mai zaman kanta da aka gina daga tushe.** Ana ayyana daidaitawa ta hanyar kwangilolin black-box
na cikin gida da tsare-tsaren saƙonni na jama'a na OpenAI, Anthropic, da Gemini; ba a yi amfani da
lambar tushe ta upstream ba.

Yana sauya tsarin saƙonnin OpenAI, Anthropic, da Gemini zuwa `NormalizedConversation` guda ɗaya kafin nunawa:

```ts
interface NormalizedConversation {
  request: NormalizedTurn[]; // saƙonni / abubuwan ciki / umarni daga jikin buƙata
  response: NormalizedTurn[]; // martanin mataimaki (wanda aka haɗa ta sseMerger)
  contextKey: string | null; // sawun SHA-256 na umarnin tsarin
}
```

Nau'ikan tubali: `text`, `tool_use`, `tool_result`. Shafin Conversation yana amfani da wannan siffa ba tare da la'akari da mai samarwa ba.

### 4.4 Ba wa maɓallin mahallin launi (`src/mitm/inspector/contextKey.ts`)

- Yana lissafa `SHA-256` na umarnin tsarin (saƙon farko mai `role:system`, ko filin `system`, ko `systemInstruction` na Gemini)
- Yana mayar da farkon hex mai haruffa 12 (`"a3f9c2..."`)
- Frontend yana danganta maɓallin da tabbataccen launin HSL don sandar kan iyakar hagu
- **Tacewar "mahalli iri ɗaya"**: danna alamar `ctx #a3f` yana ƙara tacewa don nuna buƙatun da suke da sawun iri ɗaya kawai

Wannan yana sauƙaƙa bambance “halaye” ko ayyuka daban-daban ta gani waɗanda ke gudana a zaman agent guda ɗaya.

### 4.5 Ciro metadata na LLM

Ga buƙatun LLM, shafin LLM Details yana ciro:

```ts
interface LlmMetadata {
  provider: string | null; // "openai" | "anthropic" | "gemini" | ...
  apiKind: string | null; // "chat.completions" | "messages" | "embeddings" | ...
  model: string | null; // daga jikin buƙata ko martani
  messages: number; // yawan zagayen tattaunawa
  tokensIn: number | null; // usage.prompt_tokens / usage.input_tokens
  tokensOut: number | null; // usage.completion_tokens / usage.output_tokens
  streamed: boolean; // true idan martanin SSE ne
  mappedTo: string | null; // taken x-omniroute-mapped
  costEstimateUsd: number | null; // kimanta kuɗi bisa farashin OmniRoute
}
```

### 4.6 Tacewar buƙatun da ke gudana kai tsaye

Filin `status` na buƙata shi ne `number | "in-flight" | "error"` — ana
tura shigarwa a matsayin `"in-flight"` da zarar buƙatar ta fara kuma ana **sabunta ta a wurin**
lokacin da martani (ko kuskure) ya iso. Maɓallin sauyawa na **"Kai tsaye"** a toolbar
(`liveOnly`, maɓallin i18n `trafficInspector.liveOnly`) yana takaita jerin ga shigarwar
da `status === "in-flight"` kawai, yana ba ka damar kallon buɗaɗɗun haɗi a ainihin lokaci.

Tacewar tsantsar predicate ce ta ɓangaren client a
`src/lib/inspector/matchesTrafficFilter.ts`:

```ts
if (f.liveOnly && req.status !== "in-flight") return false;
```

Halin maɓallin sauyawa yana cikin `useTrafficFilters` (hooks na dashboard ɗin inspector) kuma
yana haɗuwa da sauran tacewa (profile, host, agent, source, status, context).

### 4.7 Danganta tsari (Linux)

A Linux, ana iya danganta kowace buƙatar da aka katse da **tsarin cikin gida
da ya samo asali**. Ana ƙara filaye biyu na zaɓi zuwa `InterceptedRequest`:

```ts
pid?: number;          // id na tsarin da ya samo asali (Linux kawai)
processName?: string;  // sunan tsarin da ya samo asali (Linux kawai)
```

`src/mitm/inspector/processAttribution.ts` yana danganta ephemeral port na _client_
na haɗin zuwa PID + suna ta hanyar:

1. Karanta `/proc/net/tcp` da `/proc/net/tcp6` don nemo inode na socket na
   port ɗin (`parseProcNetTcpForInode`, parser tsantsa wanda za a iya gwadawa da fixture).
2. Bincika `/proc/<pid>/fd/` don neman symlink zuwa `socket:[<inode>]`.
3. Karanta sunan tsari daga `/proc/<pid>/comm`.

Cache mai TTL na daƙiƙa 1 yana iyakance kuɗin binciken procfs ƙarƙashin nauyi. Dangantawar
**ƙoƙari ne gwargwadon iko** — duk wata gazawa tana komawa `null` kuma ba ta taɓa hana kamawa ba. A
macOS/Windows aikin yana mayar da `null` (stub; tallafin `lsof`/`GetExtendedTcpTable`
za a ƙara shi daga baya).

## §5 Zaman zama

### 5.1 Yin rikodin zaman zama

1. Danna **"● Yi rikodin zaman zama"** a maƙallan kayan aiki → shigar da suna (na zaɓi)
2. Bibiyar kai-tsaye za ta ci gaba kamar yadda aka saba; alama ja mai walƙiya za ta nuna `◉ REC · <name> · 00:42 · 23 reqs`
3. Danna **"⏹ Tsaya"** → za a adana hoton yanayin zaman zama zuwa `inspector_sessions` + `inspector_session_requests`

### 5.2 Duba zaman zama da aka yi rikodi

Jerin zaɓen **Zaman zama** da ke maƙallan kayan aiki yana jera zaman zama da aka adana. Zaɓar ɗaya zai:

- Loda hoton yanayin zaman zama (yanayi daskararre)
- Nuna sanarwa: `Ana duba zaman zama da aka yi rikodi "<name>" — [Koma kai-tsaye]`
- Sa shafin Ƙididdiga ya zama mai samuwa tare da tarin bayanan Recharts

### 5.3 Tsare-tsaren fitarwa

Ana iya fitar da kowane zaman zama a matsayin:

| Tsari                      | Amfani                                                                                        |
| -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **HAR** (HTTP Archive 1.2) | Ya dace da Chrome DevTools, Charles, Fiddler — shigo da shi don nazarin ba tare da intanet ba |
| **JSONL**                  | `InterceptedRequest` ɗaya a kowane layi — ya dace da tsarin `llm-interceptor`                 |

Yi fitarwa ta `GET /api/tools/traffic-inspector/sessions/{id}/export.har` ko maɓallin ⬇ da ke cikin jerin zaɓen Zaman zama.

## §6 Tsaro

Traffic Inspector yana nuna **duk zirga-zirgar HTTPS da aka kama**, ciki har da kanun izini da jikin buƙatu. Ana amfani da matakan kariya masu zuwa:

| Matakin kariya              | Cikakkun bayanai                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **LOCAL_ONLY**              | Duk hanyoyi da endpoint na WebSocket suna karɓar haɗi ne kawai daga loopback (ana tilasta hakan a cikin `routeGuard.ts` kafin tantancewa)                                                    |
| **Ɓoye sirrika**            | Na'urar binciken `maskSecret()` mai layi tana ɓoye bayanan shaidar Bearer na RFC 6750, maɓallan da ke da prefix na mai samarwa, da dogayen token marasa bayyana kafin `TrafficBuffer.push()` |
| **Iyakar girman jiki**      | Ana taƙaita jikin da ya haura `INSPECTOR_MAX_BODY_KB` (tsoho 1024 KB) tare da sanarwar `"(an taƙaita saboda inganci)"`                                                                       |
| **Tsabtace kanun bayanai**  | Ana mayar da sunaye zuwa ƙananan haruffa; ana cire kanun framing/hop-by-hop da proxy-auth; ana ɓoye cookies gaba ɗaya; ana miƙa ƙimomin bayanan shaida zuwa `maskSecret()`                   |
| **CSP**                     | Ana amfani da tsauraran Content Security Policy a shafukan Traffic Inspector don hana XSS ta hanyar jikin amsa da aka shigar                                                                 |
| **Babu adanawa ta tsohuwa** | `TrafficBuffer` yana cikin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya kuma yana ɓacewa idan aka sake kunna uwar garken. Ana adana zaman ne kawai idan an yi rikodinsu a fili                                           |

### Tsauraran Ka'idojin da ake amfani da su

| Ka'ida                            | Yadda ake amfani da ita                                                                      |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **#12** `sanitizeErrorMessage`    | Ana tsabtace duk amsoshin kuskuren HTTP daga hanyoyin Traffic Inspector                      |
| **#15 + #17** `isLocalOnlyPath()` | `/api/tools/traffic-inspector/` yana da LOCAL_ONLY + SPAWN_CAPABLE (umarnin proxy na tsarin) |

### Sanannun iyakoki

- **Yanayin proxy na faɗin tsarin** yana shafar duk manhajojin da ke kan na'urar, ciki har da abokan hulɗar VPN da SSO. A koyaushe a yi amfani da shi tare da mai ƙidayar lokaci na kashewa ta atomatik. Kada a yi amfani da shi a kan na'urorin da ake amfani da su tare.
- **CONNECT tunnel HTTPS**: Mode 3 (HTTP_PROXY) yana kama bayanan metadata na tunnel kawai don wuraren HTTPS, kuma ba ya taɓa warware ɓoyewarsu. Haka aka tsara shi — warware ɓoyewar zai buƙaci kowane abokin hulɗa ya amince da takardar shaidar MITM, saboda haka listener yana barin HTTPS da ɓoyewar end-to-end. Yi amfani da AgentBridge, Custom Hosts ko TPROXY decrypt lokacin da kake buƙatar jikin HTTPS.
- **Rubutun da aka dasa kai tsaye a wasu components**: Wasu UI components (F7/F8) suna da ƴan rubutu da aka dasa kai tsaye waɗanda maɓallan i18n ba su rufe ba tukuna. An rubuta bayaninsu a matsayin Sanannen Iyaka a cikin rahoton gibin i18n; za a mayar da su a wani zagaye na gaba. Rubutun da abin ya shafa alamomin ado ne na UI waɗanda ba sa buƙatar fassara don amfani na aiki.

## §7 Warware matsaloli

### Katsewar haɗin WebSocket

Idan bibiyar kai tsaye ta nuna "An katse haɗi":

1. Tabbatar cewa uwar garken tana ci gaba da aiki: `GET /api/tools/traffic-inspector/capture-modes`
2. Sake loda shafin — WebSocket zai sake haɗuwa kuma ya karɓi sabon hoton halin yanzu
3. Idan an sake kunna uwar garken, an share ma’ajiyar wucin gadi ta ƙwaƙwalwa — tsofaffin bayanai sun ɓace sai dai idan an yi rikodin zaman

### Rikicin tashar 8080

Idan yanayin HTTP_PROXY ya kasa farawa:

```bash
lsof -i :8080    # nemo tsarin da ke aiki
```

Canja tashar:

```bash
# .env
INSPECTOR_HTTP_PROXY_PORT=8888
```

### Ba a mayar da wakilin tsarin yadda yake ba

Idan OmniRoute ya samu matsala yayin da yanayin wakilin gabaɗayan tsarin yake aiki:

**macOS:**

```bash
networksetup -setwebproxystate Wi-Fi off
networksetup -setsecurewebproxystate Wi-Fi off
```

**Linux (GNOME):**

```bash
gsettings set org.gnome.system.proxy mode 'none'
```

**Windows:**

```cmd
netsh winhttp reset proxy
```

Allon sarrafawa zai kuma ba da zaɓin "Mayar da wakilin tsarin yadda yake" a lodawa ta gaba idan ya gano cewa halin DB yana nuna wakilin yana aiki.

### Ma’ajiyar wucin gadi ta cika

Lokacin da ma’ajiyar wucin gadi ta kai `INSPECTOR_BUFFER_SIZE` (tsoho 1000), sabbin bayanai za su cire mafi tsofaffin. Idan ana rasa muhimman buƙatu:

- Ƙara `INSPECTOR_BUFFER_SIZE` (misali, 5000) — wannan yana musanya ƙarin amfani da ƙwaƙwalwa da tsawaita riƙe bayanai
- Yi rikodin zaman don adana sashen da ya dace a DB
