> **Tushen sahihancin bayanai:** `src/lib/plugins/` (`marketplace.ts`, `manager.ts`, `manifest.ts`,
> `scanner.ts`, `loader.ts`), `src/app/api/plugins/`, da
> `src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/plugins/`
> **Sabuntawa ta ƙarshe:** 2026-06-28 — v3.8.40

OmniRoute na zuwa da tsarin plugin mai irin na WordPress. Plugins kundin adireshi ne
masu zaman kansu — kowanne yana da manifest na `plugin.json` da fayil ɗin shigarwa — waɗanda ke haɗuwa
da jerin sarrafa buƙata (`onRequest` / `onResponse` / `onError`) da kuma
abubuwan zagayowar rayuwa (`onInstall` / `onActivate` / `onDeactivate` / `onUninstall`).

**Kasuwar Plugin** ita ce shimfiɗar ganowa da ke saman wannan tsarin. Tana
bayar da kundin plugins da za a iya lilo da girkawa. A tsari na asali, kundin ƙaramin
rijistar farko ce da aka gina a ciki; mai gudanarwa zai iya nuna masa URL na rijista
mai nisa na musamman, wanda a wannan yanayin ake ƙarfafa aikin ɗauko bayanan da kariyar SSRF
mai warware DNS (duba [Tsaro](#security)).

Kowace hanyar plugin **loopback-only** ce (Tier 1 — `LOCAL_ONLY`): plugins suna lodawa
kuma suna aiwatar da lamba a cikin child processes, don haka ba za a iya isa ga hanyoyin
daga tushen da ba na loopback ba, ba tare da la’akari da auth ba. Duba
[`docs/security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md`](../security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md).

## Yadda Ya Dace Together

```
Dashboard (/dashboard/plugins)
  ├─ "Installed" tab  → GET /api/plugins            (listPlugins)
  │                     POST /api/plugins/scan      (pluginManager.scan)
  │                     POST /api/plugins/{name}/activate|deactivate
  │                     DELETE /api/plugins/{name}   (uninstall)
  └─ "Marketplace" tab → GET /api/plugins/marketplace
                          → listMarketplacePlugins()
                            ├─ no custom URL → built-in SEED_REGISTRY
                            └─ custom URL → isSafeMarketplaceUrl() SSRF guard
                                          → safeOutboundFetch(guard:"public-only")
```

- **Layer Registry** — `src/lib/plugins/marketplace.ts`: jeri/bincike kasidar, fadowa baya ga rijistar iri akan kowace gazawa.
- **Layer na rayuwa** — `src/lib/plugins/manager.ts` (`pluginManager` singleton): shigarwa, haɓakawa, kunnawa, kashewa, cirewa, duba, ɗaukar nauyi.
- **Bayyanar Layer** — `src/lib/plugins/manifest.ts`: Zod schema + tsoho don `plugin.json`.
- **Scanner** — `src/lib/plugins/scanner.ts`: yana gano plugins akan faifai a ƙarƙashin jagorar plugin.
- **Mai lodi** — `src/lib/plugins/loader.ts`: yana haifar da kowane plugin a cikin wani tsari na yara Node.js daban kuma dillalai suna yin kira akan IPC. Yaron ba akwatin yashi bane (duba "keɓancewar aiwatar da plugin" a ƙasa).

## Kundin Kasuwa

`listMarketplacePlugins()` (`src/lib/plugins/marketplace.ts`) yana mayar da jerin
abubuwan `MarketplaceEntry`:

| Fili          | Nau’i    | Bayani                                      |
| ------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `name`        | string   | Sunan plugin na kebab-case                  |
| `version`     | string   | semver                                      |
| `description` | string   | Taƙaitaccen bayani                          |
| `author`      | string   | Marubuci / ƙungiya                          |
| `license`     | string   | ID na lasisi mai tsarin SPDX                |
| `downloadUrl` | string   | URL na sauke tushe (yana iya zama fanko)    |
| `repository`  | string?  | URL na repository na zaɓi                   |
| `tags`        | string[] | Alamomin bincike/tacewa                     |
| `downloads`   | number   | Adadin saukewa                              |
| `rating`      | number   | 0–5                                         |
| `verified`    | boolean  | Ko an yi wa shigarwar alamar tabbatarwa     |
| `lastUpdated` | string   | Kirtanin kwanan wata mai kama da tsarin ISO |

Idan ba a saita URL na rijista na musamman ba, kundin zai zama
`SEED_REGISTRY` da aka gina a ciki (a halin yanzu `request-logger`, `rate-limiter`, `cost-tracker`, da
`theme-manager`). Rijistar farko tana samuwa koyaushe — idan ba a iya isa ga rijista
mai nisa da aka saita ba, ta mayar da matsayin da ba `200` ba, ko ta mayar da
bayanan da ba a gane su ba, `listMarketplacePlugins()` zai rubuta gargaɗi sannan ya koma
ga jerin farko.

> Lura: an haɗa **kundin** kasuwa (lilowa/bincike) daga farko har ƙarshe, amma
> **girkawa** da dannawa sau ɗaya daga kundin kasuwa ba a aiwatar da shi ba tukuna — maɓallin
> "Install" na dashboard a kan shigarwar kasuwa a halin yanzu yana nuna sanarwar
> "coming soon". A yau, ana yin girkawa ta hanyar tsarin girkawa daga hanyar gida
> (`POST /api/plugins`) da ganowa a kan faifai (`POST /api/plugins/scan`).

## REST API

Duk wuraren ƙarewa suna buƙatar auth gudanarwa (`requireManagementAuth`)** kuma** An jera madogara-kawai — `/api/plugins` da `/api/plugins/` a cikin `LOCAL_ONLY_API_PREFIXES` (`src/server/authz/routeGuard.ts`).

| Ƙarshen Ƙarshe                     | Hanya  | Bayani                                                              |
| ---------------------------------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/api/plugins`                     | GET    | Jerin abubuwan da aka shigar (fitar `?status=` na zaɓi)             |
| `/api/plugins`                     | POST   | Shigar da plugin daga kundin tushen tushen plugin                   |
| `/api/plugins/scan`                | POST   | Bincika kundin adireshin plugin ɗin kuma yi rajistar sabbin plugins |
| `/api/plugins/marketplace`         | GET    | Lissafin shigarwar kasida ta kasuwa                                 |
| `/api/plugins/marketplace/install` | POST   | Sanya plugin ɗin kasuwa da suna                                     |
| `/api/plugins/[name]`              | GET    | Samun bayanan plugin ɗin da aka shigar                              |
| `/api/plugins/[name]`              | DELETE | Cire plugin                                                         |
| `/api/plugins/[name]/activate`     | POST   | Kunna (saukar da ƙugiya masu rijistar + )                           |
| `/api/plugins/[name]/deactivate`   | POST   | Kashe (wuta `onDeactivate`, unregister hooks)                       |
| `/api/plugins/[name]/config`       | GET    | Samun plugin config + config schema                                 |
| `/api/plugins/[name]/config`       | PUT    | Sabunta saitin plugin (an inganta shi da tsari)                     |

Tacewar `GET /api/plugins` `status` tana karɓar ɗayan `installed` / `active` / `inactive` / `error`. Ƙimar mara aiki tana mayar da `400`.

### Jerin abubuwan da aka shigar

```bash
curl http://localhost:20128/api/plugins \
  -H "Cookie: auth_token=..."
```

### Shigar daga hanyar gida

```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:20128/api/plugins \
  -H "Cookie: auth_token=..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "path": "/absolute/path/to/my-plugin" }'
```

Dole ne `path` ta kasance** cikakke** hanyar zuwa directory** a cikin kundin tushen tushen plugin** : `plugin-sources` kusa da tushen plugin, don haka `~/.omniroute/plugin-sources/` ta tsohuwa, ko `<parent of OMNIROUTE_PLUGINS_DIR>/plugin-sources/` lokacin da aka saita wannan canjin (`getPluginSourcesDir()` a `src/lib/plugins/installSource.ts`). Kwafi ko clone da plugin a can farko. Ana ƙi shigarwar tare da `400` lokacin da hanyar ta ƙunshi `..` ko babu byte, yana kwance a wajen waccan directory, yana warwarewa a wajensa ta hanyar alamar alama, yana ƙunshe da alamar alamar wanda manufarsa ke wajensa, ko lokacin da directory ɗin ba ya wanzu. Dole ne littafin tushen tushen ya ƙunshi ingantaccen `plugin.json` (ko kuma ya zama iyayen ɗaya). A cikin nasara amsar ita ce `201` tare da layin plugin ɗin da aka shigar.

Ba kamar sauran hanyoyin plugin ɗin ba, wannan yana buƙatar takardar shaidar gudanarwa (zaman dashboard ko maɓallin API mai sarrafa-scoped) koda lokacin da aka kashe shiga (`requireLogin=false`): shigar da lambar ba wani aiki ne da ba a san shi ba. Hakanan ya shafi `POST /api/plugins/marketplace/install` (jiki `{ "name": "<plugin>" }`).

### Yi lilo a kasuwa

```bash
curl http://localhost:20128/api/plugins/marketplace \
  -H "Cookie: auth_token=..."
```

### Sabunta saitin plugin

```bash
curl -X PUT http://localhost:20128/api/plugins/my-plugin/config \
  -H "Cookie: auth_token=..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "config": { "level": "debug", "maxItems": 100 } }'
```

`PUT .../config` yana inganta jiki akan `configSchema` na plugin (an bayyana a cikin bayyani) tare da `validatePluginConfig()` daga `src/lib/plugins/manifest.ts`: kowane maɓalli dole ne a bayyana shi a cikin tsari, ƙimar dole ne ta dace da nau'in da aka ayyana, filayen `number` suna girmama `min`/`max`, kuma filayen `select` dole ne su dace da ayyana `enum`. Duk wani cin zarafi ya dawo `400` kuma ba a adana komai. Plugin da ke bayyana babu `configSchema` ba shi da maɓallai masu daidaitawa, don haka an ƙi buƙatar da `400` ("Plugin baya ayyana kowane maɓallan daidaitawa").

## Saitawa

### Kundin plugin

Plugins suna ƙarƙashin kundin bayanan OmniRoute:

```
~/.omniroute/plugins/<plugin-name>/
  ├─ plugin.json
  └─ index.js          # (ko duk abin da manifest.main ke nunawa)
```

`getDefaultPluginDir()` (`src/lib/plugins/scanner.ts`) yana tantance wannan kundin a
matakai uku:

1. **`OMNIROUTE_PLUGINS_DIR`**, idan an saita shi — ana amfani da shi kai tsaye, ko mene ne
   `HOME` ya nuna. Wannan shi ne madaidaicin saitin Docker/K8s, inda ake bind-mount na
   tsarin plugins a wata hanya wadda yawanci ba ta da alaƙa da kundin gida na container
   (#11827).
2. `<home>/.omniroute/plugins`, inda `<home>` yake fitowa daga environment variables
   na `HOME` / `USERPROFILE`.
3. `/tmp/.omniroute/plugins`, idan process ɗin bai fitar da wani kundin gida ba kwata-kwata.

Ana rubuta kundin da aka tantance sau ɗaya a log yayin farawa a matsayin
`scanner.dir_resolved`, tare da ambaton input ɗin da aka zaɓa (`OMNIROUTE_PLUGINS_DIR`,
`home`, ko `no-home-fallback`) — don haka image da ya koma mataki na 3 ba tare da sanarwa
ba zai bayyana hakan, maimakon kawai bayar da rahoton jerin plugins marar komai.
`POST /api/plugins/scan` yana gano duk wani ƙaramin kundi a wurin da ke ɗauke da
ingantaccen `plugin.json` sannan ya yi masa rajista; wannan kundin shi ne kuma root ɗin
da `pluginManager.install()` ke kwafar plugins zuwa cikinsa, saboda haka override yana
matsar da ganowa da girkawa tare.

> **`OMNIROUTE_PLUGINS_DIR` ba `OMNIROUTE_PLUGIN_PATH` ba ne.** Na biyun CLI
> command-plugin loader (`bin/cli/plugins.mjs`) ne kawai ke karantawa domin nemo
> packages na npm na `omniroute-cmd-*` waɗanda ke ƙara subcommands na `omniroute` —
> ba shi da wani tasiri ga runtime scanner da aka bayyana a nan. Duba
> [PLUGINS.md](./PLUGINS.md) don wannan ɓangaren.

### URL na musamman na registry na kasuwa

Ana karanta tushen katalog na kasuwa daga saitin `pluginMarketplaceUrl`
(`src/lib/plugins/marketplace.ts` yana karanta `settings.pluginMarketplaceUrl`). Idan
an saita shi zuwa URL na `http(s)`, `listMarketplacePlugins()` yana ɗebo wannan URL
kuma yana karɓar ko dai JSON array na entries a matakin sama, ko object mai array na
`plugins`; ana tace entries da ba su da `name` na nau'in string. Idan ba a saita shi ba
(ko idan ɗebo bayanan ya kasa gwajin kariyar SSRF / ya dawo da amsa marar kyau), ana
amfani da built-in seed registry.

Shafin "Marketplace" na dashboard yana samar da field don wannan URL (ana sake karanta
shi daga `GET /api/settings`).

> Bayanin aiwatarwa: aikin "Save" na dashboard yana aika
> `pluginMarketplaceUrl` zuwa `PATCH /api/settings`. A lokacin rubuta wannan,
> ba a ayyana wannan key a cikin `updateSettingsSchema` ba
> (`src/shared/validation/settingsSchemas.ts`), saboda haka tabbatar da dorewar saitin
> a release ɗinku kafin dogaro da shi — hanyar **karantawa** (`getSettings()` →
> `listMarketplacePlugins()`) tana amfani da key ɗin da zarar yana cikin ma'ajiyar
> saituna.

## Tsaro

### Hanyar tier — loopback kawai

Plugins suna aiwatar da lamba a cikin tsarin yara da aka haifa, don haka gabaɗayan saman `/api/plugins` an rarraba `LOCAL_ONLY` (Tier 1). Aiwatar da Loopback yana gudana ba tare da sharadi ba** kafin** duk wani tambarin auth, don haka alamar gudanarwar leaked ta isa akwatin da ke kan rami har yanzu ba zai iya shigar, kunnawa, ko cire plugin ɗin ba. Duba [`docs/security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md`](../security/ROUTE_GUARD_TIERS.md) da Dokokin Hard #15 / #17.

### Rajistar kasuwa SSRF guard

Tsarin rajista na al'ada URL yana da tasiri mai tasiri, don haka kafin a ɗauko shi `listMarketplacePlugins()` yana tafiyar da shi ta hanyoyi biyu:

1. **`isSafeMarketplaceUrl(url)`** (`src/lib/plugins/marketplace.ts`):
   - Ya ƙi duk wani abu da ba `http:` / `https:` ba.
   - Ya ƙi rundunonin private/loopback/link-local/ULA na zahiri (IPv4** kuma** IPv6, gami da IPv4-mapped) ta hanyar canonical `isPrivateHost` (`src/shared/network/outboundUrlGuard.ts`).
   - Yana warwarewa** duka biyu** `A` da `AAAA` ya rubuta kuma ya ƙi idan** kowane** adireshin da aka warware — na sirri ne yana rufe sunan mai masaukin jama'a → keɓaɓɓen-IP wucewa.
   - **Ya kasa rufe** : gazawar ƙudurin DNS ya ƙi URL.
2. **`safeOutboundFetch(url, { guard: "public-only", timeoutMs: 5000 })`** (`src/shared/network/safeOutboundFetch.ts`): sake amfani da jama'a-kawai gadin URL a lokacin kawo da** toshe turawa** (babu → na jama'a mai zaman kansa `30x` pivot).

URL da ta gaza ko wanne Layer baya soke buƙatun — wurin kasuwa a shiru ya faɗi baya ga ginanniyar rajistar iri kuma ya yi rajistar gargaɗi.

> Wannan mai gadin ya taurare a cikin PR #3774 musamman don warware A + AAAA da kuma amfani da `isPrivateHost` na canonical maimakon cakin IPv4-only.

### Warewa aiwatar da plugin

> **Plugins ba a cikin akwati ta tsohuwa.** Yaro tsari ne Node.js na yau da kullun yana gudana azaman mai amfani OmniRoute, ba tare da mahallin VM ba kuma babu tutocin izini Node.js, don haka lambar plugin ɗin zata iya karantawa da rubuta kowane fayil ɗin da mai amfani zai iya, gudanar da shirye-shirye kuma isa hanyar sadarwa. `requires.permissions` kawai ya yanke shawarar waɗanne canjin yanayi ne yaron ya karɓa; sauran izini ba a tilasta su. Tutar fasalin ficewa na `PLUGIN_PERMISSION_MODEL_ENABLED` (kashe ta tsohuwa) tana gudanar da kowane plugin ƙarƙashin ƙirar izinin Node.js da aka gina daga waɗannan izini, ban da `network`, wanda Node.js ba zai iya ƙuntatawa ba (duba [`PLUGIN_SDK.md`](./PLUGIN_SDK.md#permission-system) ). Sanya plugins ɗin da kuka dogara kawai. Iyakar aikin da ke ƙasa ya ƙunshi hadarurruka da rataye; ba iyakar tsaro ba ce.

- **Tsari warewa** — `loadPlugin()` (`src/lib/plugins/loader.ts`) yana haifar da kowane plugins a cikin wani tsari na Node.js na daban kuma yana sadarwa akan IPC. Kiran ƙugiya yana da lokacin ƙarewa tare da haɓaka `SIGTERM` → `SIGKILL`.
- **Jerin izini na Env** — yaron yana karɓar jeri kawai na masu canjin yanayi; Ana ba da mafi girman saiti ne kawai lokacin da bayyananniyar ta nemi izinin `env`.
- **Shigar da tushen tushen** — `POST /api/plugins` kawai yana karantawa daga kundin adireshi na tushen plugin (duba "Shigar daga hanyar gida"): hanyar tana canonicalized tare da `realpath`, dole ne ta tsaya a cikin wannan kundin adireshin, kuma itacen tushen da ke ɗauke da alamar alamar da ke tserewa an ƙi kafin a kwafi komai.
- **Tsarewar hanya** — install/upgrade/uninstall sun tabbatar da cewa plugin directory da `manifest.main` sun warware** ciki** Tushen plugin ɗin da aka sarrafa kafin kowane kwafi ko sharewa mai maimaitawa (masu kariya daga ɓarna hanyoyin DB da `../` traversal a `manifest.main`). Kunnawa yana warware alamomi ta hanyar `realpath` kuma ya ƙi ɗaukan wurin shigarwa wanda ya tsere wa kundin adireshi.
- **fil ɗin mutunci na zaɓi** — bayyananne zai iya bayyana filin `integrity` (`sha256-<base64>`, tsarin SRI ). Lokacin da ake ciki, mai ɗaukar kaya yana tabbatar da hash ɗin fayil ɗin shigarwa a lokacin ɗauka kuma ya ƙi kunna rashin daidaituwa. Yana da ficewa-in-tamper-ganewa,** ba** iyakar tsaro: baya iyakance abin da kayan aikin da aka ɗora zai iya yi.
- **Babu takardun shaidar abokin ciniki a cikin ƙugiya** — mahallin da aka wuce zuwa `onRequest`, `onResponse` da `onError` an cire maɓallan `authorization`, `x-api-key`, `proxy-authorization` da `cookie`, kuma rikodin maɓalli API ba shi da ƙima mai mahimmanci (`redactPluginContext()` a `src/lib/plugins/hooks.ts`).

## Bayyanawa (`plugin.json`)

Ya inganta daga `PluginManifestSchema` (`src/lib/plugins/manifest.ts`):

| Filin              | Nau'in   | Bayanan kula                                                       |
| ------------------ | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `name`             | kirtani  | Da ake bukata; kebab-case (`^[a-z0-9-]+$`), 1-100 chars            |
| `version`          | kirtani  | Da ake bukata; semver (`MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`)                        |
| `description`      | kirtani? | ≤ 500 char                                                         |
| `author`           | kirtani? | ≤ 200 chars                                                        |
| `license`          | kirtani? | Default to `MIT`                                                   |
| `main`             | kirtani? | Fayil ɗin shigarwa; gazawa zuwa `index.js`                         |
| `source`           | enum?    | `local` \| `marketplace` (tsoho zuwa `local`)                      |
| `tags`             | zaren[]? | Bincika tags                                                       |
| `requires`         | abu?     | `{ omniroute?, permissions[] }`                                    |
| `hooks`            | abu?     | Booleans yana bayyana abin da ke haɗa plugin ɗin                   |
| `skills`           | abu[]?   | Ma'anar fasaha na zaɓi                                             |
| `enabledByDefault` | boolean? | Kunna ta atomatik akan shigarwa                                    |
| `configSchema`     | abu?     | Taswirar filayen daidaitawa (`string`/`number`/`boolean`/`select`) |

Ana samun izini daga enum `network` / `file-read` / `file-write` / `env` / `exec`. `env` ne kawai yana da tasirin lokacin aiki (wani ɗan ƙaramin yanayi mafi girma-jerin ba da izini ga yaro); sauran sanarwar ce kuma ba a aiwatar da su ba.

## Gudanarwar Zagayowar Rayuwa

```
install (POST /api/plugins, path)
  → bincika/tabbatar da manifest → kwafa zuwa staging → tabbatar main yana cikin kundin adireshi
  → sauya suna ta atomatik zuwa ~/.omniroute/plugins/<name> → saka layi a DB
  → kunna onInstall → idan enabledByDefault: activate

activate (POST /api/plugins/{name}/activate)
  → binciken realpath don tabbatar da yana ciki → loadPlugin() (ƙaddamar da child process)
  → yi rajistar hooks da aka ayyana → status = "active" → kunna onActivate

deactivate (POST /api/plugins/{name}/deactivate)
  → kunna onDeactivate (KAFIN cire rajista) → cire rajistar hooks
  → kashe child process → status = "inactive"

uninstall (DELETE /api/plugins/{name})
  → deactivate idan yana active → kunna onUninstall
  → gogewa mai maimaitawa ta kundin plugin bayan binciken kasancewa ciki → goge layin DB
```

Sake gudanar da `install` a kan kundin adireshi wanda sigar manifest ɗinsa ta fi
sigar da aka girka **sabuwa sosai** yana yin haɓakawa ta atomatik (sake girkawa
mai tsabta; config yana komawa ƙimomin tsoho). Ana ƙin sigar da ta yi daidai ko
ta fi tsufa.

## Dashboard

Shafin dashboard a `/dashboard/plugins` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/plugins/page.tsx`) yana ba da shafuka biyu:

- **An shigar** — yana lissafin abubuwan da aka shigar tare da ƙugiya da aka ayyana, maɓallin activate/deactivate, maɓallin cirewa, da aikin "Scan for plugins" (`POST /api/plugins/scan`).
- **Kasuwa** — yana nuna kas ɗin daga `GET /api/plugins/marketplace` tare da fili don saita URL rajista na al'ada.

Shafin koyaushe yana nuna gargaɗin da plugins ke gudana tare da cikakken gata na tsarin OmniRoute.

Shafin saiti na kowane-plugin yana rayuwa a `/dashboard/plugins/[name]/config` (`src/app/(dashboard)/dashboard/plugins/[name]/config/page.tsx`).
