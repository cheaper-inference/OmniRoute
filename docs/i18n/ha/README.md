## Alamu Kyauta a Wata: ~ 11.3B kamar yadda Wasu ke ƙidaya · ~ 90M Gaskiya

</div>

> **~ 11.3B alamun kyauta / wata**<br/> <sub> ya ƙidaya yadda sauran kasidu ke ƙirga: kowane jere da aka haɗa, ƙarewar tayi ya haɗa da </sub>
>
> **~ 90M gaske a yau**<br/> <sub> kowace adadin da aka raba an ƙidaya sau ɗaya: abin da za ku iya amfani da </sub> a zahiri
>
> **Komai a nan kyauta ne. Babu paywall.**<br/> ★ [Like it? Tauraro mu akan GitHub](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute)

Sauran catalogs suna cajin daga US$ 19 a shekara zuwa US$ 49 don samun damar rayuwa ta rayuwa. Anan komai kyauta ne. <sub> Farashin da aka duba akan 2026-11-18.</sub>

<sub>Catalog na ƙarshe an duba shi akan 2026-11-23. [Yadda muke kirga →](docs/reference/FREE_TIERS.md#how-we-count)</sub>

> **Kawo asusun mai bada naka.** Waɗannan matakan kyauta ne na ɓangare na uku, ba kyautar alama daga OmniRoute ba. Yi rajista, sami takaddun shaida inda mai bada sabis ya tambaye su, kuma haɗa waɗanda za ku iya amfani da su. Kowane mai bayarwa yana saita iyakoki, samuwa da sharuddan.
>
> Tsara matakan kyauta da hannu yana nufin ɗimbin SDKs, da yawa na iyakoki kuma babu fayyace ra'ayin abin da kuka bari. OmniRoute yana adana layuka 214 na kasida tare da damar shiga kyauta kuma yana ƙididdige ainihin adadi daga wuraren tafki 12 tare da adadin da aka buga, kowane tafkin da aka raba an ƙidaya sau ɗaya. Ƙididdigar da kawai ke buɗewa bayan bincike na ainihi na yanki (yau, ModelScope) ana nuna su daban, +~ 6M a bayan tabbatar da asalin yanki, kuma ba a ƙara zuwa ainihin adadi ba. Abin da ya rage yana nunawa akan dashboard (`/dashboard/free-tiers`).

### Zero-config: kawai amfani da `auto`

```bash
curl http://localhost:20128/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <your-omniroute-key>" \
```

### An gina juriya a cikin: 3 yadudduka masu zaman kansu

Ana sarrafa gazawar ta hanyoyi guda uku, don haka mugun samfuri ɗaya ba ya ɗaukar asusunsa, kuma mugun asusu ɗaya ba ya saukar da mai samar da shi.

<img src="./docs/diagrams/resilience-layers.svg" width="100%" alt="OmniRoute resilience: 3 independent self-healing layers, the right layer for the right failure. Layer 1, provider circuit breaker (whole provider): trips only on 408/500/502/503/504, opens after 8 failures for OAuth providers or 12 for API-key providers, resets after 60s or 30s into a HALF-OPEN probe, lazy recovery; while OPEN the combo reroutes to the next provider. Layer 2, connection cooldown (one key or account): base 5s OAuth or 3s API-key, exponential backoff with an anti-thundering-herd guard, success clears the error state; one cooling key is skipped while sibling keys keep serving. Layer 3, model lockout (one model): per-model 429, 404 or access denials lock just that model, never the whole connection. Terminal states (banned, expired, credits exhausted) wait for the operator; they are not cooldowns."/>

#### Idan duk mai badawa ya kasa

The** mai ba da wutar lantarki** yana buɗewa bayan 8 (OAuth) ko 12 (API key) gazawar tare da matsayi 408, 500, 502, 503 ko 504. Combos tsallake wannan mai bada yayin buɗewa. Bayan 60s ko 30 s, buƙatun bincike ɗaya zai yanke shawarar ko ta rufe.

#### Idan maɓalli ɗaya ko asusu yana da iyaka

The** sanyi haɗi** benci kawai wannan haɗin, tare da juzu'i na baya daga 5 s (OAuth) ko 3 s (maɓallin API ). Wasu maɓallai don mai bada iri ɗaya suna ci gaba da yin hidima.

#### Idan an ƙi samfurin ɗaya

The** kulle model** toshe wannan ƙirar kawai akan wannan haɗin bayan kowane-samfurin 429, 404 ko hana samun dama. Wasu samfura akan haɗin gwiwa ɗaya suna ci gaba da hidima.

Haɗin da aka dakatar, ƙarewa da kuma rashin biyan kuɗi jihohi ne masu iyaka, ba sanyi ba: suna jiran ku don gyara su.

## Cikakken CLI, MCP da A2A

> OmniRoute yana jigilar abokin ciniki-layin umarni tare da** 91 manyan umarni da ƙungiyoyin umarni** (754 umarni da kirga ƙananan umarni), an** uwar garken MCP tare da kayan aikin 110 da scopes 33** , kuma an** wakilin A2A mai fasaha 6** . Rubutun, tashoshi da wakilan AI duk suna iya aiki iri ɗaya.

### A hakikanin CLI, ba kawai `start`

```bash
omniroute                 # start the gateway and dashboard (port 20128)
omniroute setup           # guided first-run wizard
omniroute repl            # interactive chat in the terminal (/model /combo /skill /memory)
omniroute chat "hello"    # one-shot prompt; also reads --file or --stdin
omniroute doctor          # check config, database, ports, Node runtime and native deps
```

Tutar `--output table|json|jsonl|csv` ta duniya tana yin umarni iri ɗaya masu amfani daga rubutun da CI.

### Yanayin nesa: CLI akan kwamfutar tafi-da-gidanka, OmniRoute akan sabar

Shiga sau ɗaya zuwa uwar garken nesa. CLI ajiye shi azaman a** mahallin** , kuma daga baya umarni suna aiki da wannan uwar garken har sai kun koma baya.

```bash
omniroute connect omniroute.example.com          # asks for the password, saves a scoped token as a context
omniroute models                                 # runs against the remote server
omniroute configure codex                        # picks a remote model, writes a local Codex profile
omniroute tokens create --name ci --scope read   # mint a narrower token for another machine
omniroute contexts use default                   # switch back to the local server
```

| Iyakar alama | <img width="2000" height="0" alt=""> Abin da ya halatta                           |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `read`       | Lissafi da dubawa: samfura, matsayi, rajistan ayyukan, amfani                     |
| `write`      | `read` da daidaitawa kuma yi amfani da: saitin abokin ciniki, maɓalli, daidaitawa |
| `admin`      | `write` plus sarrafa: alamu, masu samarwa, ayyuka, manufofi                       |

Mai masaukin baki yana samun `http://` da tashar jiragen ruwa 20128; wuce cikakken `https://` URL lokacin da uwar garken ke zaune a bayan TLS. Hanyoyin da za su iya haifar da tafiyar matakai suna zama mara baya-kawai, ko wane alama.

**Kafin ka haɗa:**


A kan macOS ko Windows, Podman yana amfani da injin Podman mai nisa: tsallake `podman unshare` kuma bi [jagorar jagorar bayanai na takamaiman topology](contrib/podman/README.md#data-directory-permissions-by-topology).

📖 [Jagorar Podman](contrib/podman/README.md) — Shirya gini, Injin Podman, da saitin Quadlet Linux/systemd.

**⚡ Mai Sauri / Leaner shigar (tsalle ginin ɗan ƙasa)**

Injin SQLite na asali (`better-sqlite3`) shine** na zaɓi** dogara, don haka shigarwa na duniya ba zai taɓa toshewa akan haɗawa daga tushe: yana amfani da binary da aka riga aka gina lokacin da mutum yayi daidai da platform/Node ku, kuma in ba haka ba ya faɗi a bayyane zuwa injin-JS mai tsarki (`node:sqlite` akan Node 22 +, in ba haka ba bundled `sql.js` WASM) — babu kayan aikin da ake buƙata.

Don tsallake shigarwa bayan shigarwa** na asali dumi-up** gaba ɗaya (CI, marasa kai, ko injinan jinkirin). Lura: wannan kawai ya tsallake matakin dumin SQLite na asali (`scripts/postinstall.mjs`); ƙugiya binary-copy/repair (`scripts/build/postinstall.mjs`) har yanzu tana gudana akai-akai:

```bash
OMNIROUTE_SKIP_POSTINSTALL=1 npm install -g omniroute   # CI=1 also skips it
```

Don shigarwa mafi sauri sun fi so** pnpm** (kantin sayar da abun ciki + hard links — see sama). Don rashin dashboard, lokacin aiki mara kai yi amfani da bayanin Docker `base` (a sama) ko [jagorar Termux](docs/guides/TERMUX_GUIDE.md). Ana amfani da CLI da dashboard ɗin yanar gizo ta hanyar tsari iri ɗaya akan tashar jiragen ruwa ɗaya, don haka babu wani fakitin CLI-kawai daban a yau.
