>
> Tsara matakan kyauta da hannu yana da zafi — zunzurutun SDKs, da yawa na iyaka, kuma ba ku san nawa kuke da shi ba. OmniRoute kataloji** 465 shigarwar matakin kyauta a cikin maɓallan tafkin mai maimaita 34** kuma yana ƙididdige kanun labaran alamar daga** Tafkunan ruwa 17 tare da ingantaccen kasafin kuɗi na wata-wata tare da ƙima guda biyar na kowane nau'in Groq** , cirewa ta wurin ruwa mai raba. Ƙididdigar da kawai ke buɗewa bayan binciken tantance asalin yanki (yau: ModelScope) ana nuna su daban, +~ 6M a bayan tabbatar da asalin yanki, kuma ba a taƙaita su cikin kanun labarai ba. Sakamakon yana kasancewa a bayyane akan dashboard (`/dashboard/free-tiers`).

## 🔌 Cikakken CLI + A2A & MCP

</div>

> Bayan uwar garken, OmniRoute **cikakkiyar cibiyar sarrafa layin umarni ce** mai **umarni sama da 80**, tare da buɗaɗɗun ƙa'idojin wakilai domin wakilin AI ya iya sarrafa ta **da kansa**.

### ⌨️ CLI na gaske (ba `start` kawai ba)

```bash
omniroute               # ƙaddamar da gateway + dashboard (tashar 20128)
omniroute chat          # abokin hulɗar taɗi na TUI (slash: /model /combo /skill /memory)
omniroute setup         # jagorar saitin fara amfani
omniroute doctor        # gano matsalolin masu samarwa, tashoshi, native deps
```

### 🛰️ Yanayin nesa — gudanar da CLI a nan, OmniRoute a kan VPS

OmniRoute na kan uwar garke? Sarrafa shi daga kwamfutar tafi-da-gidanka da **CLI iri ɗaya**. Shiga sau ɗaya
da scoped access token; daga nan kowane umarni zai nufi na'urar nesa.

```bash
omniroute connect 192.168.0.15            # kalmar sirri → scoped token, an adana shi a matsayin context
omniroute models                         # ← yana gudana a kan uwar garken NESA
omniroute configure codex                 # ← yana zaɓar model na nesa, yana rubuta profile na Codex na gida
omniroute tokens create --name ci --scope read   # ƙirƙiri tokens masu ƙuntataccen izini don wasu na'urori
omniroute contexts use default            # ← koma zuwa uwar garken gida
```

Tokens suna da scopes na `read` / `write` / `admin`; routes masu ƙaddamar da processes suna nan a loopback kawai.

### 🎚️ Bayan injuna — salon fitarwa, madaidaicin daidaitawa & sarrafawa ta kowace buƙata

Injunan 12 da ke sama suna rage abin da ke **shiga**. Ƙarin matakai uku suna tsara **yadda**, **yaushe**, da abin da ke **fitowa**:

- **🪄 Salon Fitarwa** _(jagorar ɓangaren fitarwa)_ — suna shigar da umarnin tsara amsa masu tabbas kuma masu aminci ga cache; ana iya haɗa su, kowannensu a ƙarfin `lite` / `full` / `ultra`. Ƙara salo abu ne na layi ɗaya a cikin rajista:
  - **Taƙaitaccen rubutu** — cire cike gurbi / kalmomin haɗi / kalmomin nuna shakka; a kiyaye ainihin ma'anar fasaha.
  - **Ƙaramin lamba** — YAGNI na "ƙwararren mai haɓakawa malalaci": ƙaramin canji mai aiki, ba tare da ƙarin tsarin da ba a nema ba.
  - **Wutsiyar doki (ƙwararren mai haɓakawa malalaci)** — hau matakan YAGNI, gyara tushen matsala, da mafi ƙaramin bambancin aiki.
  - **Ina da ADHD (aiki da farko)** — mataki na gaba ya zo farko, a ƙidaya matakai, mataki guda mai ma'ana na gaba, ba tare da gabatarwa ba.
  - **Taƙaitaccen CJK (文言)** — salon Sinanci na gargajiya mai tsananin taƙaitawa (ana iyakance shi ga `zh`).
- **🎯 Kasafin mahallin mai daidaitawa** _(madaidaicin daidaitawa)_ — maimakon ƙofa guda ta alamar kunnawa/kashewa, a ƙara injunan da suka fi araha kuma suka fi kiyaye asali ne kawai gwargwadon bukata, domin **a dace da girman mahallin samfurin**. Manufofi: `reserve-output` (na asali, mai sanin samfurin) · `percentage` · `absolute`. Yanayi: `floor` (tabbatar da dacewa) · `replace-autotrigger` (zaɓinka na bayyane ya fi ƙarfi) · `off` (ƙofar gargajiya).
- **🎛️ Inda ake yanke shawarar matsawa** _(fifiko, daga sama → ƙasa)_ — taken `x-omniroute-compression` na kowace buƙata › sauya saitin haɗin routing › bayanin martaba mai suna mai aiki › adaptive / auto-trigger › tsoffin saitunan panel › off. Tsarin da aka yi amfani da shi yana dawowa a cikin taken amsa `X-OmniRoute-Compression: <mode>; source=<source>`.

A kunna auto-trigger ta ƙofar alamomi, a juya madaidaicin daidaitawa, a kulle bayanin martaba mai suna, a saita buƙata guda, ko a ware pipeline ga kowane haɗin routing — duk wanda ya dace da nauyin aiki. Wani **eval harness** na zaɓi da ke aiki ba tare da intanet ba (`npm run eval:compression`) yana auna aminci da tanadi a kan tarin bayanai da aka kulle kafin ka fitar da canji.

### Yadda ake Ba da Gudummawa

1. Yi fork na ma'ajiyar
2. Yi branch daga **sigar `release/vX.Y.Z` mai aiki** (ba daga `main` ba) — duba [Tsarin Branch da Saki](docs/ops/BRANCHING_MODEL.md)
3. Ƙirƙiri branch ɗin fasalinka (`git checkout -b feat/amazing-feature`)
4. Yi commit na sauye-sauyenka (`git commit -m 'feat: add amazing feature'`)
5. Tura zuwa branch ɗin (`git push origin feat/amazing-feature`)
6. Buɗe Buƙatar Haɗawa tare da **base = wannan branch ɗin `release/vX.Y.Z`**

Duba [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) don cikakkun jagorori.
