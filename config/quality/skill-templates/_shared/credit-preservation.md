# Contributor credit: preserve the original contribution

Keep the original PR and its authorship when using a contributor's code, ideas or
fixes. Fixing a conflict or adding a missing test is not a reason to replace or
close that contribution.

## Repair in place when authorized

Resolve ownership and write permission before changing a contributor branch.
An active worktree owned by another session remains off limits. With authorized
access, make the adjustment in the original branch and preserve the contributor
as commit author, with the human maintainer as co-author for the adjustment.
Do not add assistant or bot attribution.

Validate and admit the original PR through merge-gates.md. Permission to prepare
a fix does not grant permission to merge, close or bypass required checks.

## Locked or unavailable contributor branches

If an authorized in-place repair cannot be pushed, retain the original PR and
record the access limitation. Ask the owner for a credit-preserving repair
strategy before creating a replacement or changing the integration path.

An approved separate repair PR must link the original contribution and preserve
the relevant human author/co-author credit. A direct push or cherry-pick to a
protected release is not a fallback authorization. Keep the original PR open
unless the owner separately approves its disposition.

## Closure and readback

Obtain the specific closure decision for a redundant, deferred or replacement
item; an empty diff or a related merged PR does not prove that no contribution
was used. State the evidence and preserve public attribution.

After an authorized merge, verify the actual merged state, landing identity and
human attribution. A thank-you or changelog credit complements the original PR;
it does not substitute for its merge or authorize closing it.
