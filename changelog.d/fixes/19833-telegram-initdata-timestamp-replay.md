- **fix(telegram):** enforce bidirectional timestamp boundaries on Mini App `initData` and add opt-in replay protection. Previously, future `auth_date` values bypassed the `now - authDate > maxAgeSec` freshness check and never expired. The in-memory replay cache (`TELEGRAM_INITDATA_ANTI_REPLAY=true`, off by default) rejects an exact repeat of an (`initData`, message) pair within the validity window ([#19833](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/issues/19833)).
