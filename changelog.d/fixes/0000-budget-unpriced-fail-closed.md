- **fix(budget):** the per-key dollar budget (`/api/usage/budget`, the Budget tab) no longer lets a metered model with no pricing row through for free. Such a call used to be charged `$0` and dropped, so a budgeted key could keep spending on that model while the budget never moved. Like the per-key usage limit (#12341), the budget now fails closed for the rest of its period once a metered call it could not price completes (non-streaming and streaming), refusing further metered requests with `BUDGET_EXCEEDED`; `USAGE_LIMIT_IGNORE_UNPRICED=true` keeps counting that usage as `$0`, and saving the budget again reopens the period. Flat-rate subscription providers and provider-reported costs (xAI, OpenRouter) are unaffected.
