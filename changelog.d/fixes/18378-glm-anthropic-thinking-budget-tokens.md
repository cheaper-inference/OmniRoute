- **fix(sse):** the GLM Anthropic transport (`glm-5.2-high` / `glm-5.2-max` effort tiers, and any GLM request routed to `/api/anthropic`) sent `thinking: { type: "enabled" }` without `budget_tokens`, and Z.ai rejected it with HTTP 400 `missing field budget_tokens`. Enabled thinking now always carries a budget. A client-supplied budget is kept; a missing one comes from the tier (high 24576, max 131072, medium 10240 for other models) and is clamped to `max_tokens - 1024` ([#18378](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/18378)) — thanks @mj10612 and @AppleVibe
