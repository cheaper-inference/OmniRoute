- **fix(quota):** Quota Share now gives each API key its own part of percent-based plan windows on Claude (OAuth connections) and Codex. `hard` and `soft` shares now take effect on those plans. Each pool keeps an attribution book per account and plan window, aligned with the provider's own window. The growth of the account figure since the window started is split between keys by the tokens each one routed since then. Those tokens explain at most one point per `QUOTA_PERCENT_MIN_TOKENS_PER_POINT` tokens (new, default 1,000). The rest is external usage. It first uses the pool's unallocated capacity, and only the excess is shared by weight. The split is an estimate. All growth since the window started counts, so usage made outside OmniRoute is charged to the keys up to what their tokens allow. With the default floor, a key that routes 60,000 tokens while outside use raises the account 60 points gets all 60 as its own part. With the floor at 50,000, its own part is 1.2 points, and the other 58.8 are external usage charged by weight. Both quota stores, SQLite and Redis, keep the book, and `GET /api/quota/pools/{id}/usage` reports the numbers enforcement uses. **Behavior change:** in existing percent-plan pools, `hard` keys can now be refused at their share once the saturation signal reaches `QUOTA_SATURATION_THRESHOLD` (default 0.5). `soft` keys at or above their share are deprioritized in combos, and percent caps count only the part attributed to the key. A percent cap applies in every mode, so it can refuse a key far below the threshold. **Upgrade:** usage already on an account when its book opens counts as external until the window turns. Each key starts with its weight share of that usage, after the capacity no allocation claims. With one account at 85% and five keys of weight 20, each key starts with 17 of its 20 used. The manual usage-history reset also clears the attribution books in the SQLite database. To keep the previous behavior in a pool, use the `burst` policy and no percent cap ([#20822](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/20822), closes [#19904](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/issues/19904))
