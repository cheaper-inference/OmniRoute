- **fix(routing):** Account selection for `fill-first`, `p2c`, `least-used`, `round-robin` and the other global strategies now skips an account that is at its `max_concurrent` cap while a sibling account has room, so a request no longer waits in the queue of a full account. A pinned session, an active lease and an API-key preference keep their account. When every account is full, or none has a cap, the pick is unchanged. ([#19469](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/19469))
