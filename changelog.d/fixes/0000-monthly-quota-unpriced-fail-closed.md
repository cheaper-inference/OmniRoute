- **fix(quota):** the per-key monthly USD quota (`/api/usage/key-quota`) no longer lets a metered model with no pricing row through for free. Such a call never reached the cost ledger the cap sums, so a capped key could keep spending on that model all month. Like the dollar budget and the per-key usage limit (#12341), the monthly quota now fails closed for the rest of the calendar month once a metered call it could not price completes (non-streaming and streaming), refusing further requests with `429`; `USAGE_LIMIT_IGNORE_UNPRICED=true` keeps counting that usage as `$0`, and saving the quota again reopens the month. Keys without a monthly cap are unaffected.
