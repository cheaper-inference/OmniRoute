- **fix(images):** `/v1/images/generations` now forwards an explicit `output_format` (png, jpeg, webp, gif, svg) with its `frame_count`, `frame_duration_ms` and `background` controls to OpenAI-compatible providers, and legacy Fal no longer downgrades a requested `gif` or `svg` to PNG. When `output_format` is requested, the response reports the container detected from the image bytes as `output_format` and per-image `mime_type`, with a warning when it differs from the request. Requests without `output_format`, Stability AI and Codex GPT Image are unchanged. Salvaged from [#20219](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/20219) by @KiaroSama.
