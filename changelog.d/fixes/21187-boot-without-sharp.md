- **fix(guardrails):** OmniRoute boots on a runtime where `sharp` cannot load, such as Android/Termux arm64. The Video Bridge drill-down store imported `sharp` at module load, and the startup hook reaches it through the guardrails, so boot failed with `Failed to load external module sharp` even with no video request. `sharp` now loads when a drill-down operation needs it ([#21187](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/21187)).
