- Contain two malformed-upstream payloads as request-scoped failures instead of provider outages: a Responses stream that aborts with a bare error frame (and never settles on a terminal or completed snapshot) now reports `status: "failed"` with that error rather than collapsing to an empty success, and non-object or unparseable tool-call arguments now raise a typed `SyntaxError` instead of escaping the non-streaming translator. Empty, missing or `null` tool-call arguments still translate to an empty `input: {}`.
