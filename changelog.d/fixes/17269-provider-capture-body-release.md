- **fix(usage):** stop the AsyncLocalStorage store from pinning the captured provider request body after its dispatch settles — `runWithCapture()` (`open-sse/utils/providerRequestLogging.ts`) now stores a small holder in the store and empties it in its `finally`, so sockets and streams that outlive the dispatch no longer keep the `Capture` and its multi-megabyte `latest` body (3–10 MB for vision traffic with base64 images) reachable. chatCore keeps the `Capture` through its own closure, so `latest()`/`body()` still return the exact wire body after the dispatch (signature-failure diagnostics, call-log request body); late callbacks from a settled dispatch cannot capture anything or overwrite a fresh retry. Refs #17268.
