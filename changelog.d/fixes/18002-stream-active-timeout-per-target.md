- **fix(streams):** with `STREAM_ACTIVE_TIMEOUT_MS` unset, the active-stream watchdog no longer cuts off a target whose own declared budget is longer than the 21-minute default (1260000). GLM declares a 50-minute provider-level `timeoutMs`, and a connection can declare its own in `providerSpecificData.timeoutMs`, but both were killed at 21 minutes mid-answer with no terminal event. Such a target now gets the larger of the default and its declared budget (connection, then per-model, then provider-level) plus the one-minute margin, clamped to the timer range; targets that declare nothing keep the 21-minute bound #12913 added for endless streams. A positive `STREAM_ACTIVE_TIMEOUT_MS` is now a hard cap for every target that declared budgets cannot raise, and `0` still disables the watchdog. ([#18002](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/18002)) — thanks @HouMinXi
