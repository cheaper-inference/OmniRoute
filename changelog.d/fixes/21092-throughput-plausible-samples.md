- **fix(usage):** tokens-per-second measurements ignore generation windows shorter than 250 ms and rates above 5,000 tok/s, so a response that arrives in one burst no longer reports hundreds of thousands of tok/s in `usage.tokens_per_second`, the `X-OmniRoute-Tokens-Per-Second` header or the model latency stats. The latency stats that feed speed-based routing now compute `avgTokensPerSecond` as total tokens over total generation time instead of a mean of per-request rates.
