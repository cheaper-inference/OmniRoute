- **fix(tls):** browser fingerprints (TLS profile, User-Agent and client hints) now come from the newest profile the installed wreq-js ships, resolved at request time, instead of literals frozen in source. The shared TLS session and the Codex Responses WebSocket move off their pinned chrome_124 / chrome_142 profiles, and grok-web, claude-web, perplexity-web, notion-web and lmarena send a User-Agent that matches their TLS fingerprint. An unusable wreq-js catalog fails the request that needs it instead of the module load. The claude-web clearance cache is keyed by the browser version, so a wreq-js upgrade drops cookies minted under the old one ([#17869](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/17869)) — thanks @HouMinXi
