- **perf(usage-analytics):** five redundant panel passes over the unified cost source collapse into one matrix read per dashboard load. `getDailyCostRows`, `getModelUsageRows`, `getProviderCostRows`, `getProviderUsageRows` and `getServiceTierUsageRows` each ran their own `GROUP BY` over the same UNION of `usage_history` and `daily_usage_summary`, so one dashboard load made five passes plus five temp B-tree sorts to answer one dataset. They are replaced by a single `getModelUsageMatrixRows` read on `(date, provider, model, serviceTier)` and the `derive*Rows` roll-ups, which are exact: every measure is additive, the latency average is recombined from the carried `latencyWeightedMs` / `latencySamples` pair, and the aggregated/raw cost legs stay separated through `aggregatedStoredCost` / `rawStoredCost`. The summary, daily, heatmap, weekly and preset passes remain - they answer questions these buckets cannot (`COUNT(DISTINCT …)` across three dimensions, an independent 364-day heatmap window, and per-preset windows).
