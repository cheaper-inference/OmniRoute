- **fix(i18n):** the scheduled mirror refresh no longer throws away every translated locale when a few fail: `run-translation.mjs` exits 3 on a partial run, keeps each failed (source, locale) mirror and state record as it was and writes them to `--failures-json`, and `i18n-mirror-refresh.yml` opens or updates a draft refresh PR with the translated mirrors and lists the failed units in its body. All five drift classes remain checked; failed units are excused only while their mirror bytes and locale records still match the selected base, including after commit hooks. The Google engine now also retries a dropped connection (`fetch failed` with a socket cause) instead of failing the locale on the first try ([#18539](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/18539), refs [#18538](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/issues/18538))
