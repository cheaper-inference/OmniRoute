- **feat(dashboard):** opt-in quota alerts in the Usage page — a toast fires once when a quota window drops below 15% (critical) or 35% (warning), and once when a reset credit is within 24 h of expiring, so a banked credit is not silently lost. Alerts are computed client-side from the snapshot the dashboard already polls (no upstream request, no quota consumed), deduped through a latch persisted in `key_value`, and off by default behind the new `codexQuotaAlerts.enabled` setting
