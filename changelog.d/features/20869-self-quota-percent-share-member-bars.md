- **feat(api):** the self-service quota view and the `/quota/{provider}` page now show the key's own share of percent plan windows (Claude by OAuth, Codex). The number is the estimate the quota gate uses, read from the attribution book without writing it. For a key that may see account usage it comes with its two parts: what the key routed, and its part of usage made outside the pool. A share that cannot be read is reported as unavailable with a reason, never as zero. A pool can also opt in to member usage bars: with 3 to 20 allocated keys, of which at least three can make requests, a holder who may see account usage gets one unnamed slice per key. The option is off by default ([#20869](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/20869))
