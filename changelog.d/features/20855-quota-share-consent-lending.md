- **feat(api):** Quota Share pools can opt into consented lending. In `consent` mode a `hard` key passes its share only within the idle share other keys agreed to lend. Key holders set a percentage and an optional deadline with `PATCH /api/v1/me/quota/{provider}`. The default mode, `saturation`, is unchanged. A refusal at a key's share or at the pool ceiling now says when capacity returns, when the reset time is known or can be estimated. A chat refusal then also carries `Retry-After`. An embeddings refusal carries the sentence only. An absolute cap refusal carries neither ([#20855](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/20855))
