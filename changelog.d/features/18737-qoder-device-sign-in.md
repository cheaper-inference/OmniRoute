- **feat(oauth):** Qoder accounts can be added with a native browser sign-in (device code) instead of hand-set `QODER_OAUTH_*` variables. These accounts are sign-in only for now: they cannot serve chat, and their `dt-` device token is never sent to DashScope or any other upstream (the executor refuses them with `qoder_sign_in_only` and credential selection skips them). Chat still needs a Qoder PAT (`pt-`, local `qodercli`) or a DashScope API key. ([#18737](https://github.com/cheaper-inference/OmniRoute/pull/18737)) — thanks @Junior-HJ
