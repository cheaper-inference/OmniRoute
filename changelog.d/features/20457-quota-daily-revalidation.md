- **feat(resilience):** a quota lock that outlives a day can now be revalidated once a day (`QUOTA_DAILY_REVALIDATION`, off by default), so a quota that resets early — or a lock derived from a bad parse — stops parking the model until the announced window ends (#20343). The probe is one minimal real request (an answer cap of 16 tokens, the smallest budget some upstreams accept — a smaller cap is rejected 400 by OpenCode Zen and the Responses-shaped upstreams) to the locked model on its connection, at most once a day per locked model and 5 per sweep: if the model answers, its lock is cleared and it returns to rotation; a quota answer keeps the lock (refreshed from the announced reset, under the lock's own scope), and any other outcome leaves it untouched. A lock scoped to a quota family rather than one model — Codex `codex`/`spark`, Antigravity `gemini`/`claude`/`argon` — stores the bucket name instead of a model id, so the probe resolves a real model of that same family from the connection's synced catalog (static registry as fallback) and skips the request when it finds none; on recovery the persisted family deadline (Codex scope cooldown, Antigravity family cooldown) is released too, or it would rehydrate the lock on the next credential read. A probe only ever affects that model's lock — never a connection-wide cooldown or the connection's other models — and nothing runs at all without the flag
