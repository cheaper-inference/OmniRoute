- **feat(resilience):** a quota lock that outlives a day can now be revalidated once a day (`QUOTA_DAILY_REVALIDATION`, off by default), so a quota that resets early — or a lock derived from a bad parse — stops parking the model until the announced window ends (#20343). A passing re-test clears the lock and the model returns to rotation; a failing or inconclusive one leaves it exactly as it was. At most once a day per lock and 5 per sweep, so one pass cannot storm upstream, and nothing runs at all without the flag
