- **feat(resilience):** a quota lock that outlives a day can now be revalidated once a day (`QUOTA_DAILY_REVALIDATION`, off by default), so a quota that resets early — or a lock derived from a bad parse — stops parking the model until the announced window ends (#20343). The probe is one minimal real request (one-token answer cap) to the locked model on its connection, at most once a day per locked model and 5 per sweep: if the model answers, its lock is cleared and it returns to rotation; a quota answer keeps the lock (refreshed from the announced reset), and any other outcome leaves it untouched. A probe only ever affects that model's lock — never a connection-wide cooldown or the connection's other models — and nothing runs at all without the flag
