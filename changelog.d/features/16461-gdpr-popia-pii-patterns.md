- **feat(guardrails):** PII masking covers GDPR and POPIA identifiers (IBAN, EU VAT, Spanish DNI/NIE, Italian Codice Fiscale, Polish PESEL, Dutch BSN, German Steuer-ID, Swedish personnummer, South African ID/phone/VAT/tax reference). Every match must pass its checksum, structure or context validator before it is redacted on the request and response sides, so order numbers, timestamps and identifiers such as `gpt4o` are left alone. The ids join the per-type selection, settings schema and dashboard card, with new `gdpr` and `popia` templates; masking stays opt-in ([#16461](https://github.com/diegosouzapw/OmniRoute/pull/16461)) — thanks @zerotraceio
